# Registration phases

Registration phases

Find out which phase you can register your child under.

For those with ties to the school[Phase 1If the child’s sibling is currently studying in your preferred school.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/registration-phases-key-dates?pt=1)[Phase 2AIf you or your child’s sibiling is a former student or meet other criteria, or your child is in an affiliated kindergarten.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/registration-phases-key-dates?pt=2A)[Phase 2BIf you’re a volunteer at your preferred school, or have been endorsed by an affiliated church or clan.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/registration-phases-key-dates?pt=2B)

For those without ties to the school[Phase 2CFor citizens or PRs who have not secured a place in a primary school.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/registration-phases-key-dates?pt=2C)[Phase 2C SupplementaryFor citizens or PRs who have not secured a place in a primary school after Phase 2C.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/registration-phases-key-dates?pt=2C%20Supplementary)[Phase 3: For international studentsAfter all citizens and PRs have been allocated a place. You must indicate your interest before the phase begins.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/international-students)

Balloting

If a primary school has more registrants than vacancies, a computerised balloting will be conducted.

[How balloting worksFind out who gets priority if your child needs to ballot for a place in your preferred school.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/understand-balloting)[How distance affects priority admissionThe closer you stay to your preferred school, the higher your chances.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/distance)[Address used for registrationLearn about the address you can use, and the requirements to meet.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/home-address)

For children with special educational needs

If you’ve applied to a special education school, you do not need to participate in the P1 registration exercise.

[Special educational needs support at mainstream primary schoolsChildren with mild special educational needs such as dyslexia and hearing loss can attend mainstream schools.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/special-educational-needs/school-support/primary-schools)[Special education (SPED) schoolsFor children with moderate-to-severe special educational needs, or have higher support needs.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/special-educational-needs/sped-schools)[Get a professional assessment for your childIf you do not know if your child can cope in a mainstream school.](https://www.moe.gov.sg/special-educational-needs/understand/assessment)
