# Support For Lower Income Workers

- [Home](https://www.life.gov.sg/)
- [Guides](https://www.life.gov.sg/guides)
- [Support for your job search](https://www.life.gov.sg/guides/support-for-your-job-search)
- Support for lower-income workers

You can learn more about financial help at Workfare while finding a job. Workfare is targeted at lower-income workers and self-employed persons with a gross monthly income of $2,300 and below.

If you’re eligible, you can get Workfare payouts in the form of:

skills training allowance

CPF top-ups for retirement

cash supplement for income
