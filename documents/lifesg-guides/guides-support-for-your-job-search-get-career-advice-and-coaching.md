# Get Career Advice And Coaching

- [Home](https://www.life.gov.sg/)
- [Guides](https://www.life.gov.sg/guides)
- [Support for your job search](https://www.life.gov.sg/guides/support-for-your-job-search)
- Get career advice and coaching

You can meet a career coach from Workforce Singapore (WSG) or Employment and Employability Institute (e2i).

By working with a career coach, you can:

personalise your career plan to your goals and interests

build confidence and receive support for job-hunting

get job search and interview skills

learn about relevant programmes, events and workshops

You can also book a consultation with a Skills Ambassador for advice on skills upgrading and suitable training courses.
