# Life In Ns

Last updated 14 April 2026

Your son or ward will start his journey as a full-time National Serviceman (NSF) with basic training. The length and type of basic training he receives will depend on:

- Whether he’s enlisted to Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) or Singapore Police Force (SPF)
- His medical fitness status
- His pre-enlistee Individual Physical Proficiency Test (IPPT) results (if applicable)
Stay-in training

During the initial phase of basic training, your son or ward must stay in camp throughout. This can be between 10 days to 19 days, depending on which service your son is enlisted to.

Booking out

After that, he will be able to return home (book out) during weekends and public holidays if he does not have any training scheduled on those days.

Basic training in the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF)- If your son or ward has enlisted to the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), he will go through basic military training.
- His camp might be at the Basic Military Training Centre (BMTC) in Pulau Tekong, BMTC School V in Kranji Camp 2 or other SAF units.
- He must stay in for the first 2 weekends, and will be able to book out and return home after 15 to 19 days, depending on his training school.

Items givenYour son or ward will be given a list of items upon his enlistment. This includes his uniform, clothes, shoes, and other training necessities. The full list of items given can be viewed under the respective service:

- [Items issued for Singapore Armed Forces (SAF)](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/life-in-ns/saf/items-issued)
- [Items issued for Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF)](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/life-in-ns/scdf/items-issued)
- [Items issued for Singapore Police Force (SPF)](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/life-in-ns/spf/items-issued)

Living arrangementsYour son or ward will live in a common bunk together with enlistees from different walks of life, race and religion. Together, they are responsible for the cleanliness of their living quarters.

Meals providedYour son or ward will be provided with 3 meals a day. If there is night training, a night snack will be given. All meals are carefully planned to provide proper nutrition for training and recovery. If your son or ward has dietary restrictions or food allergies, he should inform his officers upon enlistment.

After basic trainingAfter basic training is completed, your son or ward:

- will be posted to the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) or Singapore Police Force (SPF) unit to complete the rest of his training and to serve the rest of his full-time National Service (NS).
- may need to undergo vocation training.
- may be selected for a leadership course and undergo leadership training before he’s assigned a vocation.
Visit the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) website to learn more about life in NS in the[SAF](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/life-in-ns/saf/),[SCDF](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/life-in-ns/scdf/), and[SPF](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/life-in-ns/spf/).
