# Care services

Care services

Get help from qualified professionals at home, in a centre, or at a stay-in facility.

Singapore citizens and permanent residents (PRs) who enrol in government-funded intermediate and long-term care services may be eligible for subsidies of up to 75%.

[More information](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/subsidies-for-residential-long-term-care-services)
