About this support

Government and community-funded Special Education (SPED) schools cater to children and youths with moderate-to-severe special educational needs and require more intensive and specialised support in their schooling journey.

Different SPED schools cater to students with different special educational needs.

Visit [MOE Special Education (SPED) schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/special-educational-needs) to learn about the support for students with special educational needs and their educational pathways.

Who is this for?

These SPED schools offer the customised curriculum for students aged 7 to 18 with Moderate to Severe Intellectual Disability.

What to expect?

Please visit [MOE Special Education (SPED) schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/special-educational-needs/sped-schools) and each SPED school’s website for more information on each school.

How to apply?

Please visit [MOE Special Education (SPED) schools](https://www.moe.gov.sg/special-educational-needs) for details on the application process for SPED schools.
