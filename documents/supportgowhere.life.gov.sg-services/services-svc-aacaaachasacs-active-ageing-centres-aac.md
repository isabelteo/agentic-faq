# Active Ageing Centres (AAC) - SupportGoWhere

Active Ageing Centres (AAC)

Share link

A centre for seniors to build strong social connections, take part in recreational activities, access community health services, and contribute to the community.

SERVICE HIGHLIGHTS

- Free
- Referral not required

Expand all sections

About this support

Provide opportunities for seniors to stay physically and mentally active, explore interests and meet other seniors, such as:

- Active ageing activities to keep seniors engaged within the community

- Befriending services for vulnerable seniors who require additional social support

- Making referrals for care services when required

- Providing assistance as a social connector for social and lifestyle interventions to support seniors in adhering to their health plan recommendations

- Supporting seniors in the measurement of their vital signs in adherence to their health plan


Active Ageing Centre (Care), or AAC (Care), provides AAC services and additional care services, such as day care and community rehabilitation.

Who is this for?

Seniors aged 60 and above who are Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents.

What to expect?

- Active ageing programmes:

  - Organise a variety of activities such as karaoke, arts & craft, cooking and exercise
- Befriending & buddying:

  - Provide social support to seniors through house visits and phone calls
- Information and referral to care services:

  - Provide information on schemes, grants and support

  - Raise referrals to appropriate services

  - Management of emergency alert response calls
- Social connector for social and lifestyle interventions:

  - Assist seniors with recommendations in adherence to their health plan (e.g. registering for active ageing programmes)
- Community screening:

  - Help seniors measure their vital signs as required in adherence to their health plan

How to apply?

You may:

- Drop by an AAC in your neighbourhood to sign up

- Find out more at any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)


Visit [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Active-Ageing-Centres) to search for AACs bypostal code, address or your device’s current location.

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.

- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.
