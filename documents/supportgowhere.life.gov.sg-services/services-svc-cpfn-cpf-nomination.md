About this support

A CPF nomination allows you to decide:

- Who will receive your CPF savings (your “nominee”)

- The percentage of your CPF savings each nominee should receive

- CPF savings **cannot be distributed by a will** as CPF savings do not form part of your estate. This arrangement **protects your CPF savings from creditor claims** on any outstanding debts you may have and preserves your savings fully for your nominees.


Your CPF nomination will cover:

- CPF savings in your Ordinary, Special, MediSave and Retirement Accounts

- CPF LIFE premium balance

- Your discounted Singtel shares (if any)


Your CPF nomination does not cover:

- Properties bought using your CPF savings

- Payout from the Dependants' Protection Scheme (DPS)

- Investments under CPF Investment Scheme (CPFIS)


Who is this for?

To make a CPF nomination, you must:

- Be a CPF member

- Be aged 16 years and above

- Have the mental capacity to make your own decisions


You can **nominate any person or legal entity\*** to be the beneficiary of your CPF savings.

_\*An entity that is capable of receiving monies in its own right._

What to expect?

If your nominee falls into the following categories, here is what to expect:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **Below 18 Years Old** | The monies will be held in trust by the Public Trustee until your nominee turns 18. |
| **Unable To Make Decisions Independently** | The monies will be handled by their court appointed deputy. |
| **Bankrupt** | The monies belonging to the undischarged bankrupt nominee, will be transferred to the Official Assignee. |

To allow your CPF savings to be distributed swiftly according to your wishes when you pass on, it is important to **review your CPF nomination regularly**, especially at key milestones (e.g.):

- Starting Work

- Marriage\*

- Childbirth

- Divorce

- Death of a nominee

- When you reach your golden years\*\*


_\*Your nomination will be revoked upon marriage, and you should make a new nomination._

_\*\*Review your nomination and consider your current family circumstances, such as which family member may need your savings more._

How to apply?

You can:

- Apply [online](http://cpf.gov.sg/MakeCPFNOM) using your Singpass

- [Make an appointment](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/appt/oas/form) to apply in person at any CPF service centre if you are unable to find two witnesses

- [Book an appointment](https://www.booking.gov.sg/public/services/G4x7rneZ/list) to visit a [ServiceSG Centre](https://mylegacy.life.gov.sg/find-a-service/life-planning-service/) for in-person guidance on your online nomination application. Click [here](https://www.psd.gov.sg/servicesg/) for the list of ServiceSG Centres and their operating hours


**For online application**

You will need to have:

**Your nominees’ particulars**

1. Full Name

2. NRIC or Foreign Identification Number (FIN)

3. Mailing Address (for foreigners)

4. Email Address (optional)


**Two witnesses’ particulars**

1. Full Name

2. NRIC

3. Email address and/or Singapore-registered mobile phone number

4. Cannot be a nominee

5. He/she cannot be a nominee and must be at least 21 years of age


Upon submission of your nomination, **inform your witnesses to log in** [here](http://cpf.gov.sg/witnessNOM) **within 7 calendar days to witness your nomination using their Singpass.**

**For in-person application**

You will need to bring along and prepare:

- A copy of your NRIC/passport

- Your nominee(s)' NRIC number or Foreign Identification Number (FIN), email (optional), and mailing address (for foreigners)


As CPF Board's customer service executives or ServiceSG staff are acting as your witnesses, you do not need to prepare your witnesses' particulars.
