About this support

Trained caregiving staff provides seniors living together with basic support, such as:

- Basic care

- Help with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)

- Meal preparation

- Domestic tasks

- Social activities


Who is this for?

- Senior who is able to live mostly independently but need help with some ADLs

- Is able to make decisions about their care, or has a next-of-kin who can do so on their behalf


What to expect?

- Stay-in SCS does not provide medical or nursing care.

- Subsidies are currently not available, as it is a private service.


How to apply?

You can contact the service provider directly to find out more about their service offerings. Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Shared-Stay-in-Senior-Caregiving-Services) to search for service providers bypostal code, address or your device’s current location.

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.

- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.
