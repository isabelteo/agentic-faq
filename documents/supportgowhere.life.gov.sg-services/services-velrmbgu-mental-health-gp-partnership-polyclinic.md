# Mental Health GP Partnership/ Polyclinic - SupportGoWhere

Mental Health GP Partnership/ Polyclinic

Share link

Partnership with General Practitioners (GPs) and polyclinics to provide care for both mental and physical health with a single GP or polyclinic.

SERVICE HIGHLIGHTS

- Fees payable
- Referral not required

Expand all sections

About this support

- Manage both your physical and mental health conditions with a single GP or polyclinic

- Provides early identification, assessment, diagnosis, treatment, and stabilisation of mental health conditions and dementia at participating GP and polyclinics.

- Works with community mental health teams who can provide support such as counselling and psychotherapy.


Who is this for?

Anyone who prefers to have both their mental and physical health conditions managed by the same GP or polyclinic.

What to expect?

- Costs will vary between clinics.

- Singapore Citizens can use CHAS and Medisave for outpatient treatment at participating GPs and polyclinics.


How to apply?

Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Mental-Health-GP-Partnership-Polyclinic) to search for participating GPs and polyclinics bypostal code, address or your device’s current location.

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.

- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.
