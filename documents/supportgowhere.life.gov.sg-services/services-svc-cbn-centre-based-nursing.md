About this support

Offers basic nursing services at centres close to where seniors live, such as:

- Wound care

- Changing of feeding tube

- Caring and dressing of feeding tube (PEG)

- Caring and dressing of tracheostomy tube

- Caring and dressing of kidney drainage tube

- Helping with bowel movements (as prescribed by doctor)

- Stoma care – colostomy and ileostomy care

- Caring and changing urinary catheters

- Post-procedure medication (as prescribed by doctor)


Who is this for?

Senior who requires basic nursing care.

What to expect?

Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result.

- Only applicable to Senior Citizens and Permanent Residents


How to apply?

You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs

- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)


If you are interested to view a list of providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/care-services/centre-based-nursing).

For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.

- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.
