# Long-Term Care Subsidies - SupportGoWhere

Long-Term Care Subsidies

Share link

Provides means-tested subsidies for long-term care services by MOH-funded long-term care providers.

SCHEME HIGHLIGHTS

- For home and community long-term care services provided by MOH-funded providers: Means-tested subsidies of up to 80%
- For institutional long-term care services provided by MOH-funded providers: Means-tested subsidies of up to 75%
- Additional subsidies of up to 15% for Singapore Citizens born in or before 1969

Expand all sections

About this support

**For home and community long-term care services (e.g. Day Care, Community Rehabilitation services, Home Nursing, Home Medical)**

- Means-tested subsidies of up to 80%

- Additional 15% subsidies for Singapore Citizens born in and before 1969


**For institutional long-term care services (e.g. Nursing Home)**

- Means-tested subsidies of up to 75%

- Additional 5% subsidies for Singapore Citizens born in and before 1969


Who is this for?

- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

- Monthly per capita household income (PCHI) of less than or equal to $4,800 (or property annual value (AV) of less than or equal to $21,000 for households with no income)

- Receiving long-term care services from MOH-funded providers


How to apply?

Persons who require long-term care services can approach their doctor at the hospital, polyclinic, or Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) for assistance in applying for admission to a subsidised long-term care service.
