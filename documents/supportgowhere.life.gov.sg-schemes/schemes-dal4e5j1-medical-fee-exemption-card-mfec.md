About this support

Provides subsidised standard medical treatment for eligible stay-in care recipients at government or restructured hospitals, polyclinics and approved Community Care Services.

Who is this for?

- Singapore Citizen (SC), Permanent Resident (PR) or stateless person with valid ICA documentation

- Subsidised resident living in an approved MOH-funded institutions

- Must meet the means-test criteria for MFEC


How to apply?

You can approach the medical social worker in the institution your loved one is staying in. They will help your loved one to apply for the card.
