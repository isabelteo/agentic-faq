About this support

- Monthly cash payouts of $300 or $400 for up to 5 or 6 years upon severe disability, depending on the ElderShield plan

- Can be used for long-term care needs, such as hiring a helper, paying for care services, or daily expenses


Who is this for?

- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents who are existing ElderShield policyholders

- Assessed by an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor to be unable to perform at least 3 out of the following 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs):

  - Washing

  - Dressing

  - Feeding

  - Toileting

  - Walking or moving around

  - Transferring

How to apply?

As of 1 January 2020, applications for enrolment into ElderShield as a new policyholder have closed.

Existing policyholders can apply for claims:

- Online at the [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)

- Via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in

- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)


You will need to contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the claim application. Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment) for a list of assessors.
