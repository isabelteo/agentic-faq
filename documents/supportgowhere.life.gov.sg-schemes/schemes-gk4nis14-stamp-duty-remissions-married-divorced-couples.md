# Stamp Duty Remissions for Married and Divorced Couples - SupportGoWhere

Stamp Duty Remissions for Married and Divorced Couples

Share link

Provides various stamp duty remissions for eligible couples buying new residential property or transferring their current one(s) as part of matrimonial proceedings.

SCHEME HIGHLIGHTS

- Full Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) remission for couple’s joint purchase of first residential property
- ABSD remission for a couple’s jointly purchased second property
- Receive Buyer’s Stamp Duty (BSD), ABSD and Seller’s Stamp Duty (SSD) remissions when transferring property due to matrimonial proceedings

[Apply now](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/estamping/)

Expand all sections

About this support

- For married couples:

  - Full ABSD remission for married couple’s joint purchase of their first residential property

  - Partial ABSD remission for married couple’s joint purchase of a second residential property
- For couples undergoing matrimonial proceedings:

  - Potential remission of BSD, ABSD and SSD on property transfers resulting from matrimonial proceedings

Who is this for?

Please refer to the following table for the various conditions and scenarios where a couple may receive remissions:

| Joint purchase of first residential property by married couple | While ABSD will be applicable upon stamping, you may apply for the ABSD remission instead if you meet these conditions:<br>- Married couple with at least 1 Singapore Citizen spouse<br>  <br>- Property must be jointly purchased under couple’s names only<br>  <br>- Both spouses do not already own any residential properties at the time of purchase |
| Joint purchase of second residential property by married couple | - ABSD has already been paid on the second property<br>  <br>- Married couple with at least 1 Singapore Citizen spouse<br>  <br>- Property must be jointly purchased under couple’s names only<br>  <br>- Couple must not own or have an interest in more than 1 residential property each when purchasing second property<br>  <br>- Couple’s first property must be sold within 6 months after either:<br>  <br>  - Date of purchase of the second property (for completed property)<br>    <br>  - Issue date of the Temporary Occupation Permit (TOP) / Certificate of Statutory Completion (CSC), whichever is earlier, if the property was uncompleted at the time of purchase |
| Couples undergoing matrimonial proceedings | - Transfer of property is due to either:<br>  <br>  - Court-ordered division of matrimonial assets (e.g. a proceeding for a decree of divorce, judicial separation or nullity of marriage under the law or religion)<br>    <br>  - Result of divorce proceedings<br>- Property must be transferred from 1 party to another (both involved in the matrimonial proceedings)<br>  <br>- Property may also be transferred by any child or children of the marriage / former marriage of the parties |

How to apply?

- For partial ABSD remission for married couple’s joint purchase of a second residential property:

  - Navigate [e-Stamping Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/estamping/) to "Request" > "Apply for Refund"

  - For “Sale & Purchase – Purchase/ Acquisition” forms submitted on or after 2 Jul 2023, ABSD will be automatically refunded to the purchasers, within 6 weeks from the stamping of the first property’s sale, without needing a refund application

  - For stampings before 2 Jul 2023, you must manually provide the Stamp Duties (Spouses) (Remission of ABSD) refund application via the [e-Stamping Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/estamping/) \> "Request" > "Apply for Refund"
- For full ABSD remission for married couple’s joint purchase of their first residential property:

  - e-Stamp via the [e-Stamping Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/estamping/) to obtain the remission certificate

  - Alternatively, a legal representative, where engaged, will assist with the stamping
