# SkillsFuture Series - SupportGoWhere

SkillsFuture Series

Share link

Provides short, industry-relevant training for in-demand skills in Care, Digital, Green, and Industry 4.0 sectors.

SCHEME HIGHLIGHTS

- Offers short, industry-relevant training programmes
- Focus on emerging skills in Care, Digital, Green, and Industry 4.0 sectors
- Training across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels for different proficiencies

Expand all sections

About this support

- Training across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels to match different skill proficiencies

- Acquire foundational or advanced skills to boost career prospects and enhance employability


Who is this for?

- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents, criteria may vary depending on courses


How to apply?

To enrol in a SkillsFuture Series course:

- Visit the [MySkillsFuture portal](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/index.html)

- Log in with your Singpass to access the course directory

- Search for SkillsFuture Series courses under the respective growth areas: [Care Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1%3A%5B2022-12-15T00%3A00%3A00Z%20TO%20*%5D&fq=IsValid%3Atrue&q=care%20economy), [Digital Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1%3A%5B2022-12-15T00%3A00%3A00Z%20TO%20*%5D&fq=IsValid%3Atrue&q=digital%20economy), [Green Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1%3A%5B2022-12-15T00%3A00%3A00Z%20TO%20*%5D&fq=IsValid%3Atrue&q=green%20economyI), or [Industry 4.0](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1%3A%5B2022-12-15T00%3A00%3A00Z%20TO%20*%5D&fq=IsValid%3Atrue&q=industry%204.0)

- Select a course at the Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced level according to your proficiency

- Follow the instructions to sign up for your chosen course
