About this support

- One-off financial assistance from $100 to $400

- The amount will depend on the needs of each individual student


Who is this for?

Full-time Singaporean ITE students who require financial assistance to tide over short-term financial difficulties

How to apply?

You can apply online via the [ITE portal](https://myite.ite.edu.sg/)

If you need more help, refer to the [step by step guide](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/145/c7a7f637-986d-483a-9766-a19bccd726c0/Step%20By%20Step%20Guide%20on%20Applying%20Financial%20Assistance_Bursary_15%20Sep%2025.pdf).

Where can I find help?

[View more details](https://www.ite.edu.sg/current-full-time-students/admissions/student-services/financial-assistance)
