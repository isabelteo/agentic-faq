# Early Intervention Programme for Infants and Children - P (EIPIC-P)

## Overview
EIPIC-P provides therapy and educational intervention for children aged 0 to below 7 years who require medium to high levels of early intervention (EI) support, offered at ECDA-appointed private centres at subsidised rates as an alternative to government-funded EIPIC centres. The service is centre-based, fees are payable, and a referral is required.

## Eligibility
- From birth to 6 years old
- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents
- Assessed by a paediatrician to be at risk of a developmental, intellectual, sensory or physical disability, or a combination of disabilities
- Not concurrently enrolled in any other ECDA-funded EI programmes (i.e. EIPIC, DS-LS, DS-Plus programmes)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach a doctor at any polyclinic to obtain a referral for assessment at the Department of Child Development (DCD) at KK Women's and Children's Hospital (KKH) or Child Development Unit (CDU) at National University Hospital (NUH)
- Alternatively, approach a private paediatrician for an assessment
- If assessed suitable for EIPIC, the hospital or private paediatrician will recommend EIPIC and submit the application to SG Enable upon consent
- To be eligible for EIPIC-P specifically, the child should be referred by a paediatrician to the EI Programme, with referral made through SG Enable
- Upon receiving the referral, SG Enable will contact you within 10 working days to help you choose between a government-funded EIPIC centre or an EIPIC-P centre
- The selected EI Centre will contact you within 2 weeks of referral to arrange an interview and assessment of your child
- Enrolment is subject to availability of vacancies and approval of centre management
- More information: https://www.enablingguide.sg/im-looking-for-disability-support/therapy-intervention/early-intervention-programme-for-infants-and-children-p
