# CPF Nomination

## Overview
A CPF nomination lets a CPF member decide who receives their CPF savings and in what proportion after they pass away. It is free, centre-based support is available, and no referral is required.

## Eligibility
- Must be a CPF member
- Must be aged 16 years and above
- Must have the mental capacity to make your own decisions
- Can nominate any person or legal entity (an entity capable of receiving monies in its own right) as beneficiary

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online (https://cpf.gov.sg/MakeCPFNOM) using Singpass
- Make an appointment (https://www.cpf.gov.sg/appt/oas/form) to apply in person at any CPF service centre if unable to find two witnesses
- Book an appointment (https://www.booking.gov.sg/public/services/G4x7rneZ/list) to visit a ServiceSG Centre for in-person guidance on the online nomination application
- For online application, you need: nominees' full name, NRIC/FIN, mailing address (for foreigners), email (optional); two witnesses' full name, NRIC, email/Singapore-registered mobile number (witnesses cannot be a nominee and must be at least 21 years of age)
- After submission, witnesses must log in (http://cpf.gov.sg/witnessNOM) within 7 calendar days to witness the nomination using their Singpass
- For in-person application, bring a copy of NRIC/passport and nominee(s)' NRIC/FIN, email (optional), and mailing address (for foreigners); CPF Board or ServiceSG staff act as witnesses so witness particulars are not needed

## Support / Benefits
- CPF savings cannot be distributed by a will as they do not form part of your estate; nomination protects your CPF savings from creditor claims and preserves your savings fully for your nominees
- Covers CPF savings in Ordinary, Special, MediSave and Retirement Accounts, CPF LIFE premium balance, and discounted Singtel shares (if any)
- Does not cover properties bought using CPF savings, Dependants' Protection Scheme (DPS) payouts, or investments under CPF Investment Scheme (CPFIS)
- If nominee is below 18: monies held in trust by the Public Trustee until nominee turns 18
- If nominee is unable to make decisions independently: monies handled by their court-appointed deputy
- If nominee is bankrupt: monies transferred to the Official Assignee
- Nomination is automatically revoked upon marriage; a new nomination should be made
