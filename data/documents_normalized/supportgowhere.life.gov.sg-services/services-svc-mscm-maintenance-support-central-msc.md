# Maintenance Support Central (MSC)

## Overview
MSC empowers individuals by providing knowledge of their marital and maintenance rights and offers comprehensive support for those who have difficulty receiving spousal and/or child maintenance, in collaboration with community partners.

## Eligibility
- Individuals residing in Singapore who face financial abuse, marital issues, or have a Singapore court order and require legal support to navigate family law matters
- Includes individuals dealing with unpaid maintenance, divorce proceedings, and financial difficulties arising from marital conflicts
- Legal clinics are open to individuals residing in Singapore, with an income cap criteria

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply to file a Fresh or Enforcement of Maintenance application online via https://ifams.gov.sg/sop/#iFAMS, or contact the MSC programme's hotline at 6837 0611 to make an appointment
- Register for the online legal clinic at https://forms.office.com/r/4JucwmuQfc; if you qualify, staff will respond within 3 working days
- Register for counselling service online at https://forms.office.com/r/mvdxTJcBFF, or call the hotline at 6571 0185 / 6837 0611 (WhatsApp)
- Complete a registration form; staff confirm appointments within 3 to 5 working days of form submission (3 working days for legal clinic or counselling registrants); other services can be arranged at the time of contact
- Referral not required; centre-based

## Support / Benefits
- Maintenance Applications: assists in filing Fresh Maintenance Applications (FMO) via the Integrated Family Application Management System (iFAMS); assists in Enforcement of Maintenance Orders (EMO) through video-link filing (iFAMS); appointments must be made in advance
- Legal Clinics: Civil Family Law legal clinic on the first and third Monday evenings of each month; Syariah Family Law legal clinic on the third Monday of each month
- Court Process Guidance: legal guidance including explanation of standard legal procedures and legal terms related to family law
- Counselling Services: face-to-face and phone counselling for emotional support related to marital issues, post-divorce/separation, parenthood struggles, and similar concerns; counselling services are chargeable, based on clients' financial capabilities
- Educational Talks: legal talks on family law matters for the public, social service professionals, and community partners, conducted by guest speakers who are experts in Family and Syariah law
- Also assists in responding to enquiries by sharing information or referring to other community partners

## Related Issues & Concerns
- ex-spouse not paying child or spousal maintenance as ordered
- need help filing or enforcing a maintenance order in Singapore
- looking for affordable legal advice on divorce or family law matters
- need emotional support coping with divorce or separation
- want to understand Syariah family law processes for maintenance disputes
