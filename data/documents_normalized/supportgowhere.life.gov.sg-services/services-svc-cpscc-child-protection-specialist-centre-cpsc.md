# Child Protection Specialist Centre (CPSC)

## Overview
CPSCs provide child-centric, family-focused and community-based support to children who face abuse or neglect at home. The service is free, centre-based, and requires a referral.

## Eligibility
- Children and young persons who face abuse or neglect at home

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Referral is required
- Contact CPSC via phone call or email to enquire on the referral process
- After receiving the referral, a social worker will contact the referring person to find out more about the presenting abuse or neglect concerns
- CPSC will inform whether the referral is assessed as suitable for CPSC intervention; if not suitable, CPSC will advise on other possible services or contact points

## Support / Benefits
- Safety planning
- Casework intervention
- Counselling to address the risks posed to children and young persons
- Practical and other relevant services to children, young persons and their families

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried a child I know is being abused or neglected at home
- need to make a referral for a child protection concern
- looking for counselling support for a child affected by family violence
- unsure how to report suspected child abuse and what happens after
- need casework support for a family dealing with child safety issues
