# Advance Care Planning (ACP)

## Overview
Advance Care Planning (ACP) is an ongoing process for individuals aged 21 and above (with capacity to make decisions) to plan and communicate their personal beliefs, values, and healthcare preferences to loved ones and their healthcare team, so these can guide decisions if they later lose capacity. Fees may apply depending on provider.

## Eligibility
- Individuals aged 21 years old and above who have the capacity to make their own decisions, including those healthy or living with chronic or other medical conditions
- Individuals below 21 with a health condition should approach their healthcare team directly

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Option 1: Use the [myACP online service](https://mylegacy.life.gov.sg/find-a-service/acp/) if you have a Singpass account, are at least 21, do not have any existing serious illness, and do not have a Preferred Plan of Care (PPC) ACP or Disease Specific (DS) ACP. After completing the online form, your Nominated Healthcare Spokesperson (NHS) receives digital invitations via SMS and must accept via My Legacy/Singpass; ACP registers automatically once all NHS accept
- Option 2: [Book an appointment with an ACP facilitator](https://mylegacy.life.gov.sg/listing/acp-providers/results) if you prefer a guided conversation or have an existing serious illness (cancer, stroke, end stage renal failure, severe liver disease, COPD, congestive heart failure, dementia, chronic neurological disease). ACP registers in the NEHR upon completing all discussions; some providers may charge a nominal fee
- Option 3: [Book an appointment](https://www.booking.gov.sg/public/services/G4x7rneZ/list) or visit a [ServiceSG Centre](https://mylegacy.life.gov.sg/find-a-service/life-planning-service/) for in-person guidance
- More information: [mylegacy.life.gov.sg](https://mylegacy.life.gov.sg/end-of-life-planning/advance-care-planning/)

## Support / Benefits
- You appoint a Nominated Healthcare Spokesperson (NHS) to speak on your behalf when you cannot
- Completed ACP is available on the National Electronic Health Record (NEHR) for your healthcare team to reference

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Want to make sure my healthcare wishes are respected if I lose the ability to speak for myself
- Living with a chronic condition like diabetes or kidney disease and want to plan future care
- Need to appoint someone to make healthcare decisions on my behalf
- Not sure whether to do ACP online or with a facilitator
- Caring for a family member with dementia and want their preferences documented
