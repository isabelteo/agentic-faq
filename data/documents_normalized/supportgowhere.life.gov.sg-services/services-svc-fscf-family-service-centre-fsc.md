# Family Service Centre (FSC)

## Overview
FSCs are community-based social services that provide support for vulnerable individuals and families with social and emotional issues. Social Work Practitioners provide case management support and partner with other agencies to coordinate holistic support to help clients achieve stability, self-reliance, and social mobility. Open to families and individuals regardless of age, race, language, religion, life stage, or family structure.

## Eligibility
- Regardless of age, race, language or religion
- At different life stages (such as couples, families with young children, adolescents, or elderly members)
- Of various family structures

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Call, walk in or email the FSCs for help on family, socio-emotional, interpersonal or other social issues
- To find the FSC in your neighbourhood, call the ComCare hotline at 1800 222 0000 or visit https://www.msf.gov.sg/our-services/directories
- Upon contact, a FSC worker will assess needs and determine the type of support required, then either work with the individual/family directly or refer them to appropriate social services or community resources

## Support / Benefits
- Information and Referral: information about social services and links to appropriate resources
- Casework: holistic case management and individual/family counselling
- Group Work: purposeful group interactions to address common needs and bring about positive change
- Community Work: tapping on community resources to help individuals and families support one another
- Nominal charges may apply

## Related Issues & Concerns
- struggling with family conflict or emotional issues and don't know who to turn to
- worried about my family's financial stability and need case management support
- looking for counselling services for interpersonal or social issues near my home
- unsure which social service agency to contact for help regardless of income level
