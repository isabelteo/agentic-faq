# Day Activity Centre (DAC)

## Overview
Day Activity Centres (DACs) are community-based facilities that equip persons with disabilities aged 18 and above with daily living and community living skills to maximise independence, while also offering caregivers daytime respite. The service is centre-based, fees are payable, and a referral is required.

## Eligibility
- 18 years old and above (applicants below 21 need consent of parents/legal guardians; applicants 21 and above with no mental capacity need consent of appointed deputy(s)/donee(s))
- Singapore citizens or Permanent Residents (for PRs, at least one immediate family member — spouse, parent, child, including stepparents/stepchildren — must be a Singapore Citizen)
- Have a permanent disability based on: physical disability requiring assistance with at least 1 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living; moderate visual impairment or worse in the better eye; moderate hearing loss or worse in the better ear; intellectual disability; or autism
- Proof of disability required via the Disability Verification Form (DVF) completed by a registered healthcare professional (standardised requirement from 8 September 2025 for selected MSF disability schemes and other selected Government schemes)
- Applicants should not be suffering from any untreated infectious disease
- Students of Government and community-funded SPED schools should be encouraged to remain in school until graduation

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach a social service professional from a touchpoint (e.g. Social Service Agency, hospitals) for assistance in submitting a DAC application; if not currently supported by a touchpoint, approach SG Enable for assistance
- Before meeting a Touchpoint, check if disability status is already verified by logging into SupportGoWhere (https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/grants/pwdr/apply) with Singpass
- Step 1: Check verified disability status via SupportGoWhere/Singpass; if already verified, a DVF is not required
- Step 2: If not verified or a new disability condition exists, the registered Touchpoint Assessor/Admin must submit a DVF completed by a registered Healthcare Professional together with the application
- Step 3: Touchpoints' Assessor/Admin submit the application and DVF online via CorpPass (recommended) or hardcopy (download the AD application form, print and send with required documents)
- SG Enable will contact the applicant within 10 working days of receiving the completed application and required documents, and update the referring social worker
- Acceptance into the programme is subject to availability of vacancies and approval of centre management
- For help checking disability status, call SG Enable at 1800-8585-885

## Support / Benefits
- Programme designed to equip persons with disabilities with daily living and community living skills to maximise independence
- Meaningfully occupies persons with disabilities with social and recreational activities
- Therapeutic and behavioural intervention services may be provided at some centres
- Provides a form of daytime respite for caregivers
