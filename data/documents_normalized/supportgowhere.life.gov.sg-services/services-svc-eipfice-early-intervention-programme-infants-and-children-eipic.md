# Early Intervention Programme for Infants & Children (EIPIC)

## Overview
EIPIC is a centre-based programme that provides therapy and educational intervention for children aged 0 to 6 years who require medium to high levels of early intervention (EI) support. The continuum of EI services includes EIPIC Under-2s (trains parents/caregivers to carry out intervention strategies at home), EIPIC@Centre (therapy and educational intervention, typically in small groups), and DS-Plus (provided in preschools for children aged 2 to 6 requiring low levels of EI support).

## Eligibility
- From birth to 6 years old
- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents
- Assessed by a paediatrician to be at risk of a developmental, intellectual, sensory or physical disability, or a combination of disabilities
- Not concurrently enrolled in any other ECDA-funded EI programmes (i.e. DS-LS, DS-Plus programmes)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach a doctor at any polyclinic to obtain a referral for assessment at the Department of Child Development (DCD) at KK Women's and Children's Hospital (KKH) or Child Development Unit (CDU) at National University Hospital (NUH)
- Alternatively, approach a private paediatrician for assessment and recommendation for EIPIC
- If your child is assessed to be suitable for EIPIC, the hospital or private paediatrician will recommend EIPIC and submit the application to SG Enable upon your consent
- Upon receiving the referral, SG Enable will contact you within 10 working days to help you make an informed decision on your choice of EI Centre
- Your selected EI Centre will contact you within 2 weeks of referral to arrange an interview and assessment of your child
- Enrolment is subject to availability of vacancies at the centre and approval of the respective centre management
- More information: [Enabling Guide](https://www.enablingguide.sg/im-looking-for-disability-support/therapy-intervention/early-intervention-programme-for-infants-children)
