# Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP)

## Overview
Eligible foreigners can stay in Singapore for an extended period of time via a Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP). The pass is issued digitally and viewable via the Singpass app, MyICA, or FileSG. Singapore Citizens with foreign spouses may also be eligible for LTVP Plus (LTVP+), offering longer residency and additional employment and healthcare benefits.

## Eligibility
- Spouse of a Singapore citizen (must be sponsored by the Singapore citizen spouse)
- Foreign spouse of a Singapore Permanent Resident (must be sponsored by the Permanent Resident spouse)
- Unmarried child under 21, born within a legal marriage to / legally adopted by a Singapore citizen or Permanent Resident (must be sponsored by parents)
- Parent of a Singapore citizen or Permanent Resident (sponsor must be above 21)
- Graduate from an Institute of Higher Learning seeking employment in Singapore
- Parent or grandparent of a Student's Pass holder studying in Singapore (requires a local sponsor above 21 years old)
- Visitor seeking to give birth in Singapore (requires a local sponsor above 21 years old)
- Note: criteria are not exhaustive; eligibility is subject to ICA's final approval
- Couples consisting of a Singapore Citizen and a Non-Resident are strongly encouraged to complete the Pre-Marriage LTVP Assessment (PMLA) before marriage ([learn more](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/pre-marriage-long-term-visit-pass-assessment), [apply for PMLA](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/evpaa))
- For LTVP+: no separate application is required — applicants are automatically considered for LTVP+ (if eligible) when applying for LTVP

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Submit application online using the [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/evp)
- After receiving in-principle approval, refer to the [procedures relating to the Completion of Formalities and Issuance of Long-Term Visit Pass](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP/cof-and-issue-new) for next steps
- For further assistance, contact ICA at [6391 6100](tel:6391-6100) or [submit an enquiry](https://go.gov.sg/ica-ltvp-enquiry)

## Support / Benefits
- A S$45 non-refundable processing fee applies for each application
- Additional fees apply if the LTVP application is granted in-principle approval
- Applications are generally processed within six weeks if all required documents are submitted and in order
- May take longer (up to 6 months) for couples who did not complete the PMLA
- Application outcome informed via email; status can be checked via [MyICA](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/icsbip) or the [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/evp)
- LTVP+ provides a longer period of residency and additional employment and healthcare benefits ([more info](https://www.ica.gov.sg/news-and-publications/newsroom/media-release/351))
