# Centre-based Nursing

## Overview
Centre-based Nursing offers basic nursing services at centres close to where seniors live. Fees apply and a referral is required.

## Eligibility
- Senior who requires basic nursing care
- Subsidies (means-tested) only applicable to Senior Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic, or GP familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Or apply at any [AIC Link](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- View a list of providers on [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/care-services/centre-based-nursing)
- For further assistance, contact AIC via hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg

## Support / Benefits
- Wound care
- Changing of feeding tube; caring and dressing of feeding tube (PEG)
- Caring and dressing of tracheostomy tube and kidney drainage tube
- Help with bowel movements (as prescribed by doctor)
- Stoma care (colostomy and ileostomy)
- Caring and changing urinary catheters
- Post-procedure medication (as prescribed by doctor)
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (Senior Citizens/PR only)

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Elderly parent needs regular wound care or catheter changes but going to hospital is difficult
- Looking for nursing support near home rather than a home visit
- Need help managing a feeding tube or tracheostomy for a senior family member
- Wondering if there's financial help/subsidy for centre-based nursing
