# Dementia Day Care

## Overview
Dementia day care centres help seniors with mild to advanced dementia continue living in the community and delay institutionalisation, through mind-stimulating activities, exercises and personal care. Fees are payable and a referral is required.

## Eligibility
- Senior diagnosed with mild to advanced dementia, and/or requiring supervision during the day while caregivers are at work
- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links (https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- View the list of service providers at AIC's website (https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Day-Care)
- For further assistance, contact AIC via hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry (mailto:enquiries@aic.sg); airtime charges apply for mobile calls, calls free from regular land lines; calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Group and individual activities to slow deterioration of physical and mental functions, based on ability
- Individualised care plans with suitable maintenance exercises
- Assistance with daily personal care
- Caregiver support activities (support groups, talks, care demonstrations)
- Offered on weekdays; some centres also provide weekend support
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq)
