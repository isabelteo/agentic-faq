# Experiential Learning and Mentoring Programme (ELMP)

## Overview
ELMP supports youths through mentoring and experiential learning, providing a safe and conducive place to hang out after school and empowering them with pro-social skills and resilience. The programme engages youth through centre-based drop-in activities (e.g. tchoukball training, small group tuition), experiential learning activities and workshops, and 1-to-1 mentoring sessions, conducted once a week.

## Eligibility
- Youths aged 11 to 19 years old who are enrolled in school social work programmes, as well as those referred from school counsellors and parents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Youths or their parents can approach or call the centre directly
- An intake assessment will be conducted, after which youths will be matched to a suitable mentor

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried my teenager has nowhere safe to go after school and is getting into trouble
- looking for a mentor for my child who was referred by the school counsellor
- want structured after-school activities to build my child's confidence and social skills
- unsure how to get my at-risk teenager connected to youth support services
