# Counselling

## Overview
Provides counselling and psychotherapy to individuals, couples and families facing interpersonal, intrapersonal, and mental health issues, delivered through a network of service providers across Singapore.

## Eligibility
- Individuals, couples and families with issues such as marital and relationship issues, family and parenting issues, grief and loss, mental health, personal challenges, stress, or trauma

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Call or email the respective service provider for enquiries or appointment booking
- Helplines available: Care Corner Counselling Centre's toll-free Mandarin Counselling Hotline at 1800 353 5800 (daily, 10am–10pm, excluding public holidays); SAMH Insight Centre toll-free helpline at [1800 287 7019](tel:18002837019) (9am–6pm weekdays except 1pm–2pm, excluding public holidays)
- Sessions can be conducted in-person or online via Zoom

## Support / Benefits
- Services include Family Therapy, Couple/Marital Therapy, Individual Psychotherapy, pre-marital counselling and marriage preparation, and counselling on sexuality and sexual issues
- The provider assesses presenting issues and preferred day/time, and arranges a slot
- Intake and assessment conducted at the first appointment; client sees the same counsellor until case is closed or transferred
- A session takes around 1 hour; most issues need more than 1 session
- Fees payable for all service providers except SAMH Insight Centre

## Related Issues & Concerns
- struggling with marital or relationship problems and needing professional counselling
- looking for family therapy to deal with parenting conflicts
- coping with grief, trauma, or stress and unsure where to get help
- need premarital counselling before getting married
- looking for a free mental health helpline
