# Community Mental Health Services

## Overview
Over 100 community mental health teams islandwide, with at least one in every neighbourhood, serve as the first stop for in-person mental health support for anyone aged 12 and above.

## Eligibility
- Anyone aged 12 and above in need of mental health support
- No referral required

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the [AIC website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Community-Mental-Health-Services) to search for a service provider by postal code, address, or current location
- Contact the team directly for an appointment
- For further assistance, contact AIC via hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry (enquiries@aic.sg)

## Support / Benefits
- Every visit is strictly confidential
- Trained care professionals help identify needs, develop a personalised care plan, and provide counselling and therapy
- Can escalate and make referrals if needed

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Feeling anxious or depressed and not sure where to get help nearby
- Worried about a teenager's mental health and where to seek support
- Need confidential counselling without a doctor's referral
- Unsure how to find a mental health service provider in my neighbourhood
