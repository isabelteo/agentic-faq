# Severe Disability Assessment (SDA)

## Overview
The SDA is an assessment of disability status conducted by an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor, required for applying to or reviewing eligibility under various long-term care severe disability financial assistance schemes.

## Eligibility
Required if you are:
- Applying for AIC-administered schemes: CareShield Life, ElderShield, ElderFund, Interim Disability Assistance Programme for the Elderly (IDAPE), MediSave Care
- Applying for supplement schemes of CareShield Life and ElderShield administered by private insurers - Great Eastern, Income and SingLife
- Undergoing a disability review to ascertain continued scheme eligibility
- Undergoing underwriting to enroll into selected schemes

Assessed against 6 Activities of Daily Living: feeding, dressing, toileting, washing, walking or moving around, and transferring from bed to chair and vice versa.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Assessments are strictly by appointment only - call ahead to book with the clinic or assessor
- Bring relevant medical reports or discharge summaries to the appointment
- If a mental capacity assessment is also needed (for AIC scheme certification), explicitly request it when booking - it can only be completed by doctors
- Find an assessor via the [AIC website](https://aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment) by postal code, address, or current location
- If a listed provider does not conduct SDA, email apply@aic.sg
- For further assistance, contact AIC hotline 1800 650 6060, or visit the nearest AIC Link, or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg

## Support / Benefits
- A typical SDA lasts about 45 minutes or longer
- Fees: $100 if you visit an assessor's clinic; $250 if an assessor visits your house
- Fee is reimbursed if you are assessed to be severely disabled
- For CareShield Life claims, the fee for the first disability assessment is waived regardless of outcome
