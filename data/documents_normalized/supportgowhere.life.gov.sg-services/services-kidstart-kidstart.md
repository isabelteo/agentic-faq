# KidSTART

## Overview
KidSTART Singapore Limited is a non-profit organisation and Institution of a Public Character specialising in supporting families in early childhood development. It partners with the government and community to empower lower-income families in building strong foundations for their children.

## Eligibility
- Pregnant mother and/or has a child who is a Singapore Citizen aged 6 and below
- Gross monthly household income of $2,500 and below, or per capita income of $650 and below

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- [Fill up the application form](https://go.gov.sg/beakidstartfamily) to sign up as a KidSTART Family
- [Learn more about this programme and how it can help you](https://kidstart.sg/be-a-kidstart-family/?utm_source=supportgowhere&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=supportgowhere)

## Support / Benefits
- Supports children in achieving age-appropriate child development outcomes
- Improves parent-child interactions and family functioning
- Home-based service, free of charge

## Related Issues & Concerns
- pregnant and worried about my baby's development given financial difficulties
- looking for free support for my young child's early development as a lower-income family
- unsure if my household income qualifies for early childhood support programmes
- want help improving parenting skills and my child's development outcomes
