# Special Education (SPED) School (Autism Spectrum Disorder with intellectual impairment)

## Overview
Government and community-funded Special Education (SPED) schools provide education for children and youth of school-going age who have moderate-to-severe special educational needs, requiring more intensive and specialised support.

## Eligibility
- These SPED schools offer a customised curriculum for students with Autism Spectrum Disorder with intellectual impairment
- All schools serve students aged 7 to 18, except: APSN Chaoyang School (7 to 12 years old), APSN Katong School (7 to 16 years old), APSN Tanglin School (13 to 16 years old), Grace Orchard School (7 to 18 years old), Metta School (7 to 21 years old), APSN Delta Senior School (17 to 21 years old)
- The six schools listed above specifically cater to students who have Autism Spectrum Disorder with mild intellectual disability
- Metta School and APSN Delta Senior School offer Vocational Certification Programmes for students aged 17 to 21

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit MOE Special Education (SPED) schools website for details on the application process for SPED schools
- Referral not required; centre-based; fees payable

## Support / Benefits
- Customised curriculum and more intensive, specialised support for students with Autism Spectrum Disorder and intellectual impairment
- Vocational Certification Programmes available at Metta School and APSN Delta Senior School for ages 17 to 21
