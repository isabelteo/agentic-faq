# Special Education (SPED) School (Multiple Disabilities)

## Overview
Government and community-funded Special Education (SPED) schools provide education for children and youth of school-going age who have moderate-to-severe special educational needs, requiring more intensive and specialised support.

## Eligibility
- These SPED schools offer a customised curriculum for students aged 7 to 18 with Multiple Disabilities
- Schools include: Cerebral Palsy Alliance Singapore School (East), Cerebral Palsy Alliance Singapore School (West), Rainbow Centre Margaret Drive School
- Note: from 2026, AWWA School @ Napiri will not be enrolling students with Multiple Disabilities

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit MOE Special Education (SPED) schools website for details on the application process for SPED schools
- Referral not required; centre-based; fees payable

## Support / Benefits
- Customised curriculum and more intensive, specialised support for students aged 7 to 18 with multiple disabilities

## Related Issues & Concerns
- looking for a special education school for a child with multiple disabilities
- need a SPED school that supports children with Cerebral Palsy
- checking which schools still accept students with multiple disabilities after 2026
- unsure where to enrol a child aged 7 to 18 with complex disability needs
