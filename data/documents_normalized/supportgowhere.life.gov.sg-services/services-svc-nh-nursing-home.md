# Nursing Home

## Overview
A long-term residential care facility for seniors who have limited or no caregiver support and require assistance with daily nursing and living needs.

## Eligibility
- Senior who has a physical or mental disability because of illness
- Unable to be cared for at home by family or a paid domestic worker, and has tried all other care options such as day care, home medical, home nursing services, etc

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or medical social worker who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- View the list of providers on AIC's website
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Round-the-clock nursing and care services, including help with daily living activities (toileting, showering, eating, etc.)
- Nursing support such as management of nasogastric tube, urine catheter, wound dressing, etc.
- Physical and social activities such as maintenance exercises, outings, etc.
- For selected nursing homes with dementia and/or psychiatric facilities: programmes and activities for cognitive stimulation to slow deterioration of physical and mental functions
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents); additional fees such as deposits and other charges may apply

## Related Issues & Concerns
- family member can no longer be cared for at home and needs round-the-clock nursing care
- looking for a nursing home with dementia care facilities
- exhausted all home-based care options for an elderly parent
- checking eligibility and subsidies for long-term residential eldercare
