# Active Ageing Centres (AAC)

## Overview
Active Ageing Centres provide free opportunities for seniors to stay physically and mentally active, build social connections, and access community health services. Active Ageing Centre (Care), or AAC (Care), also provides additional care services such as day care and community rehabilitation. No referral required.

## Eligibility
- Seniors aged 60 and above who are Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Drop by an AAC in your neighbourhood to sign up
- Find out more at any [AIC Link](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- Visit [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Active-Ageing-Centres) to search for AACs by postal code, address, or current location
- For further assistance, contact AIC hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg

## Support / Benefits
- Active ageing programmes: karaoke, arts & craft, cooking, exercise
- Befriending and buddying through house visits and phone calls for vulnerable seniors
- Information and referral to care services, including schemes, grants and support, and emergency alert response call management
- Social connector support for adherence to health plan recommendations (e.g. registering for programmes)
- Community screening to help measure vital signs as required

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Elderly parent feeling lonely or isolated and needs social activities
- Vulnerable senior living alone who could use regular befriending visits
- Looking for a nearby centre for day care or community rehabilitation for a senior
- Want to know what schemes and grants are available for an elderly family member
- Need help with routine health screening for an ageing parent
