# Home Nursing

## Overview
Home Nursing provides home visits by nurses to help seniors who are unable to leave their homes, offering vital signs monitoring, caregiving training, care coordination, medication management, and nursing care.

## Eligibility
- Homebound seniors and their caregivers who need help with nursing care at home
- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the [AIC Links](https://aic.sg/Contact-Us)
- View the list of service providers on [AIC's website](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Nursing)
- For further assistance, contact AIC via hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry (airtime charges apply for mobile calls; calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID)

## Support / Benefits
- Vital signs monitoring (e.g. blood pressure and blood sugar levels)
- Caregiving training
- Care coordination
- Medication management
- Nursing care (e.g. injections, wound dressing and tube changes)
- Subsidies available depending on [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result

## Related Issues & Concerns
- need help with wound care or injections for an elderly parent at home
- worried about managing medication for a homebound senior
- unsure how to arrange nursing visits instead of hospital trips
- want to know if subsidies are available for home nursing care
