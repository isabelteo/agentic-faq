# Home Medical

## Overview
Home Medical provides home visits by doctors for house-bound seniors, including check-ups, prescriptions and consultations for those who are frail, homebound and require help with medical needs at home.

## Eligibility
- Seniors who are frail, homebound and require help with medical needs at home
- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- View the list of service providers on [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Medical)
- For further assistance, contact AIC via hotline 1800 650 6060 or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg) (airtime charges apply for mobile calls; calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID)

## Support / Benefits
- Physical examinations
- Review and prescription of medications
- Medical advice
- Management of stable medical condition(s)
- Minor medical procedures
- Caregiver education
- Subsidies available depending on [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result
