# Halogen SPARK!

## Overview
Halogen SPARK! is an upstream intervention programme for Secondary 3 students from the G1/G2 Subject Based Banding (SBB) stream, targeting pre/low/mid-risk students from the Financial Assistance Scheme (FAS) and/or those from challenging and disadvantaged backgrounds. It is a structured, evidence-based entrepreneurship curriculum developed by NFTE (Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship) that incorporates English and Math to build social-emotional and academic competencies, numeracy and literacy skills, self-confidence and self-efficacy.

## Eligibility
- Secondary 3 students who are from the G1/G2 Subject Based Banding (SBB) stream

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Schools refer students to the programme at the start of the calendar year
- Halogen SPARK! and teachers jointly decide on identified students' service eligibility
- The one-year programme is available in selected partner schools
- A pre-programme survey is conducted for all enrolled students to allow further adjustments based on student demographics
