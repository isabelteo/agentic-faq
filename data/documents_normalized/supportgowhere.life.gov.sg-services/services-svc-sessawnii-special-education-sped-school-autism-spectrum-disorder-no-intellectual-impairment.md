# Special Education (SPED) School (Autism Spectrum Disorder with no intellectual impairment)

## Overview
Government and community-funded Special Education (SPED) schools provide education for children and youth of school-going age who have moderate-to-severe special educational needs, requiring more intensive and specialised support.

## Eligibility
- These SPED schools offer the national curriculum for students aged 7 to 21 with Autism Spectrum Disorder who have the cognitive ability to access the same curriculum as mainstream schools
- Schools include: Pathlight School (Ang Mo Kio), Pathlight School (Tampines), St. Andrew's Mission School, Anglo-Chinese School (Academy)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit MOE Special Education (SPED) schools website for details on the application process for SPED schools
- Referral not required; centre-based; fees payable

## Support / Benefits
- Access to the national curriculum (same as mainstream schools) in a setting suited to students with Autism Spectrum Disorder and no intellectual impairment

## Related Issues & Concerns
- child with autism can follow the mainstream curriculum but needs a specialised school environment
- looking for Pathlight School or similar SPED schools for a child with autism
- want a school that offers the national curriculum for a student with Autism Spectrum Disorder
- unsure which SPED school suits a child with autism but no intellectual impairment
