# Meals on Wheels (MOW)

## Overview
Delivers meals to homebound clients who are unable to buy and prepare their own meals and do not have a caregiver to help them do so.

## Eligibility
- Seniors who are frail and homebound
- Unable to buy and prepare their own meals, and do not have a caregiver to help them

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Get a referral from a hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Contact the service provider for a discussion; visit AIC's website to search for service providers by postal code, address or current location
- For further assistance, contact the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) at 1800 650 6060, email enquiries@aic.sg or walk in to a nearby AIC Link
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Delivers lunch and dinner meals 7 days a week islandwide (including public holidays)
- Caters meals to dietary requirements (halal, non-halal, vegetarian, or blended/soft food)
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents)

## Related Issues & Concerns
- elderly parent living alone cannot cook or buy food themselves
- need meal delivery for a homebound senior without a caregiver
- looking for halal or soft-food meal options for a frail elderly relative
- checking if a housebound family member qualifies for subsidised meal delivery
