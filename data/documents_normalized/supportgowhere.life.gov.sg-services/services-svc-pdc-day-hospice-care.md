# Day Hospice Care

## Overview
Provides a safe and supportive environment during the day for patients with life-limiting illnesses and their caregivers, offering specialised care aimed at improving patients' quality of life.

## Eligibility
- Patients diagnosed by a doctor to have a life-limiting illness and a prognosis of less than 1 year
- Do not require 24-hour nursing assistance

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- View the list of service providers on AIC's website
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Palliative care with medical and nursing support
- Emotional and social support for patients and caregivers
- Multidisciplinary team of doctors, nurses, medical social workers, art therapist, spiritual counsellor, etc.
- Transportation for patients to and from their homes
- Engaging games and activities
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents)

## Related Issues & Concerns
- looking for daytime support for a family member with a terminal illness
- need transport and activities for a loved one receiving palliative care
- want emotional support for both patient and caregiver during a life-limiting illness
- checking if a family member with less than a year's prognosis qualifies for day hospice care
