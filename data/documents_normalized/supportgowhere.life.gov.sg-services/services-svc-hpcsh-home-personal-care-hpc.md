# Home Personal Care (HPC)

## Overview
Home Personal Care (HPC) is provided by trained care professionals to assist frail or homebound seniors and their caregivers with activities of daily living and/or housekeeping. From 1 April 2026, HPC will be enhanced to provide more tailored and frequent support, including for those with higher care needs, with new services such as technology-enabled monitoring (see [enhanced HPC](https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/services/o0cdiebU/enhanced-home-personal-care-hpc)).

## Eligibility
Senior who:
- Requires personal services to continue living well at home
- Is a Singapore Citizen (SC), Permanent Resident (PR) or foreigner

Caregiver who:
- Needs support in their caregiving duties
- Is a Singapore Citizen (SC), Permanent Resident (PR) or foreigner

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Get a referral from a hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Contact the service provider for a discussion; visit [AIC's website](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Personal-Care) to search for service providers by postal code, address or current location
- For further assistance, contact the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) at 1800 650 6060, email enquiries@aic.sg, or walk in to a nearby [AIC Link](https://aic.sg/Contact-Us)
- Applicants may need to fulfil the services' eligibility criteria and providers' service boundaries; enrolments are subject to availability of vacancies

## Support / Benefits
- Activities of Daily Living (e.g. showering, light housekeeping)
- Medication support
- Mind stimulating activities
- Elder-sitting and respite for caregivers
- Light household chores (e.g. sweeping floors, cleaning fans and windows, washing toilets)
- Subsidies available depending on [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-personal-care#faq) result (only applicable to SC and PR)

## Related Issues & Concerns
