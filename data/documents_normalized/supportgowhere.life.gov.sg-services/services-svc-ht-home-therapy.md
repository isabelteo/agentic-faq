# Home Therapy

## Overview
Home Therapy provides home-based therapy to help frail, homebound seniors regain and maintain mobility, speech and daily living skills, for those unable to go to hospitals or centres for therapy. Therapy provided includes physiotherapy (for mobility issues due to physical disability, trauma or illness such as stroke), occupational therapy (to learn, recover or maintain daily living and work skills), and speech therapy (for swallowing or speech disorders).

## Eligibility
- Senior who is homebound and unable to go to hospitals or centres for therapy
- Only applicable to Senior Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- View the list of service providers on [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Therapy)
- Contact AIC via hotline 1800 650 6060, visit the nearest AIC Link, or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg) (airtime charges apply for mobile calls; calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID)

## Support / Benefits
- Physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy at home
- Subsidies available depending on [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about helping a parent recover mobility after a stroke without leaving home
- need speech therapy for a homebound senior with swallowing difficulties
- unsure how to arrange physiotherapy or occupational therapy at home
- want to know if subsidies apply for home therapy sessions
