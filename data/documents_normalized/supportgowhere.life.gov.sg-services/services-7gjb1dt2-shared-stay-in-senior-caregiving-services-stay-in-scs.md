# Shared Stay-in Senior Caregiving Services (Stay-in SCS)

## Overview
Shared Stay-in Senior Caregiving Services (Stay-in SCS) provide trained caregiving staff who support seniors living together in a shared residence with basic care, help with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs), meal preparation, domestic tasks, and social activities.

## Eligibility
- Senior who is able to live mostly independently but needs help with some ADLs
- Is able to make decisions about their care, or has a next-of-kin who can do so on their behalf

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Contact the service provider directly to find out more about their service offerings
- Visit [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Shared-Stay-in-Senior-Caregiving-Services) to search for service providers by postal code, address or your device's current location
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg)
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Stay-in SCS does not provide medical or nursing care
- Subsidies are currently not available, as it is a private service

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about caring for an elderly parent who lives mostly independently but needs help with daily tasks
- looking for shared caregiving arrangements for seniors living together
- unsure if there is financial help for private senior caregiving services
- need help finding a caregiver who can assist with meals and domestic chores for an elderly relative
