# Protection Specialist Centre (PSC)

## Overview
Protection Specialist Centres (PSC) provide a holistic range of socio-emotional, supportive and practical services to survivors of family violence, intimate partner violence or sexual violence, including their families and other vulnerable members, to address the danger and likelihood of future harm and meet survivors' needs.

## Eligibility
- Survivors of family violence, intimate partner violence or sexual violence
- Persons who abuse
- Vulnerable family members who are witnesses of abuse or neglect/self-neglect

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Refer to the Service Providers section and call or email any of the centres for assistance
- Clients who approach or are referred to the centres will undergo an interview where caseworkers assess needs, then appropriate information, referral and/or services will be provided
- Care Corner Project StART: 6476 1482, projectstart@carecorner.org.sg, [Website](https://www.carecorner.org.sg/family-violence-protection)
- TRANS SAFE Centre: 6449 9088, transsafe@trans.org.sg, [Website](https://www.trans.org.sg/services/family-violence-protection-sexual-violence-recovery/)
- PAVE Integrated Services for Individual and Family Protection Specialist Centre (ISIFPSC), for both family violence and child protection: 6555 0390, intake@pave.org.sg, [Website](https://www.pave.org.sg/)

## Support / Benefits
- Casework
- Counselling
- Application of personal protection orders

## Related Issues & Concerns
- scared for my safety due to violence from my partner or family member and don't know where to get help
- need help applying for a personal protection order
- worried about a family member who may be a witness to abuse or neglect
- looking for counselling support after experiencing sexual violence
- concerned about someone who is a perpetrator of abuse needing intervention
