# Counselling (Seniors)

## Overview
Provides counselling to older persons aged 50 and above and/or their caregivers, to improve their mental well-being and resilience to cope with life's challenges. Gerontological counselling helps seniors and caregivers discuss issues and address personal, social or psychological challenges, through individual or group counselling.

## Eligibility
- Persons aged 50 years old and above with issues such as grief and loss, loneliness/isolation, depression, stress and anxiety, relationship problems, or difficulty adjusting to transitions (e.g. retirement, 'empty nest' syndrome, onset of illnesses)
- Family members/caregivers who experience stress caring for elderly loved ones with the above concerns can also be supported

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Call or email the respective service provider for enquiries or appointment booking
- Providers include Hua Mei Centre for Successful Ageing (298 Tiong Bahru Road, #15-01/06), SAGE Counselling Centre (1 Jurong West Central 2, Jurong Point Shopping Centre, #06-04), and O'Joy Limited (5 Upper Boon Keng Road, #02-10)

## Support / Benefits
- Individual and group counselling available, at the counselling centre or at home if necessary
- The counselling centre arranges an appointment with a professional counsellor who will assess needs and advise, or refer to other services if counselling is not suitable
- A session takes around 1 to 1.5 hours; most issues need more than 1 session
- Hua Mei Centre for Successful Ageing also provides individual and group coaching in emotion management, inter-generational relationship-building and communication skills
- Fees payable for Hua Mei Centre for Successful Ageing and O'Joy Limited

## Related Issues & Concerns
- struggling with loneliness or isolation after retirement
- caring for an elderly parent and feeling overwhelmed with stress
- coping with grief after losing a spouse in old age
- finding it hard to adjust to major life transitions in later years
- looking for affordable counselling support for an older family member
