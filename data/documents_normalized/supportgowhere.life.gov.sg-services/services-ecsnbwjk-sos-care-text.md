# SOS Care Text

## Overview
SOS Care Text is a 24/7 confidential text-messaging service run by Samaritans of Singapore (SOS) providing emotional support to individuals facing a crisis, thinking about suicide, or affected by suicide. It is targeted at youths aged 10 to 29 with suicide risk, but is open to anyone.

## Eligibility
- Targeted at youths aged 10 to 29 years old with suicide risk, but services are open to all users

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Text WhatsApp [9151 1767](tel:91511767) to access CareText, available 24 hours a day
- The service is anonymous, run by trained volunteers and professional staff

## Support / Benefits
- Provides emotional support, crisis intervention and suicide prevention via text messaging
- Depending on need, users may be encouraged to seek immediate support from hospitals (e.g. IMH), and/or receive community services or further counselling at SOS
- For texters with immediate suicide risk, SOS activates its mobile crisis response team ("Active Rescue") to coordinate emergency intervention, led by a Crisis Support Supervisor on duty
- Complements the existing SOS crisis hotline service

## Related Issues & Concerns
- feeling suicidal and not comfortable talking on the phone
- a teenager or young adult in crisis who prefers texting for support
- worried about a friend or family member who may be at risk of suicide
- need anonymous, round-the-clock emotional support during a mental health crisis
