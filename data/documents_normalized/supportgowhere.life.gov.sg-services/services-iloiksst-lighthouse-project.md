# Lighthouse Project

## Overview
The Lighthouse Project, run by Limitless, provides psychotherapy, psychological assessment, and case management for youths aged 12-25 with complex moderate to high mental health needs, directing them to accessible and appropriate help.

## Eligibility
- Youths aged 12-25 with moderate to high mental health needs
- Clients need to be referred by service providers or restructured hospitals; self-referrals are also accepted and assessed for eligibility

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Contact Limitless at *SCAPE, 2 Orchard Link, #04-06 grovve, Singapore 237978
- Operating hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm
- Clients need to be referred to the programme; Lighthouse project staff then make contact with the youth for assessment and basic emotional support

## Support / Benefits
- Psychotherapy (e.g. cognitive behavioural therapy) for youths with complex moderate to high mental health needs
- Psychological assessments (e.g. UCLA PTSD scale) to evaluate and monitor clients
- Case management including referral to hospitals, case conferencing, and handholding into hospital-based treatment
- Right-siting of youths requiring higher-tiered care into primary, secondary, or tertiary care
- The duration and frequency of sessions depends on clinician assessment

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about a teenager or young adult showing signs of serious mental health struggles
- looking for psychological assessment and therapy for a youth with complex needs
- need help navigating referral to hospital-based mental health treatment for a young person
- unsure how to access mental health case management support for youths
