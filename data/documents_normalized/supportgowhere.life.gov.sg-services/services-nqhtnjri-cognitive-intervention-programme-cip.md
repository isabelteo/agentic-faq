# Cognitive Intervention Programme (CIP)

## Overview
CIP is a weekly centre-based programme that helps seniors with mild to moderate dementia improve their cognitive functions and stay socially engaged, through brain/memory activities, physical exercises, and social group-based activities.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen (SC) or Permanent Resident (PR)
- Senior diagnosed by a doctor to have mild to moderate dementia and suitable for group-based activities

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Cognitive-Intervention-Programme) to search for service providers by postal code, address or current location
- Contact the service provider directly to find out more
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg)
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore

## Support / Benefits
- CIP runs for 7 weeks, with 3-hour sessions held twice a week
- Activities include word/memory games, physical exercises (e.g. resistance training, silent disco), and social activities (e.g. art and music)
- Subsidies available depending on [means-test result](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Cognitive-Intervention-Programme#faq), only applicable to SCs and PRs
- Transport to and from the centre is available at an additional cost

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about a parent recently diagnosed with mild to moderate dementia
- looking for a structured programme to keep a senior with dementia cognitively and socially engaged
- unsure if there is financial help for dementia care programmes
- need transport arrangements to bring an elderly relative to a day programme
