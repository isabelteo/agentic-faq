# Psychiatric Rehabilitation and Re-integration

## Overview
Provides long-term support for persons with stable, chronic mental health conditions who are in recovery, to improve their quality of life and help them live in the community.

## Eligibility
- Anyone diagnosed by a doctor to have a stable and long-term mental health condition
- Subsidies applicable only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the doctor currently providing care, as assessment is needed for the services required
- View a list of providers on [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Psychiatric-Rehabilitation-and-Re-Integration)
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg)
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore

## Support / Benefits
- Life skills training
- Social and emotional rehabilitation
- Recovery support (e.g. symptom management, follow-up appointments)
- Psychological support (e.g. counselling, family engagement)
- Assistance with job skills training and placements
- Finding housing services
- Services provided at either a stay-in facility or a day centre
- Subsidies available depending on [means-test](https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq) result

## Related Issues & Concerns
- looking for long-term support for a family member recovering from a mental health condition
- need help finding housing or job placement for someone recovering from mental illness
- unsure how to access subsidised psychiatric rehabilitation services
- caring for someone with a stable chronic mental health condition who needs community reintegration support
