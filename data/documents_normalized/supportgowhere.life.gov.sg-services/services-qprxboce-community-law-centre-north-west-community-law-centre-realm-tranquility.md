# Community Law Centre in North West (Community Law Centre @ Realm of Tranquility)

## Overview
Pro Bono SG's Community Law Centre @ Realm of Tranquility delivers integrated legal care in the North West District of Singapore, working with community partners to help and empower socially excluded individuals with legal and complex issues. Free, no referral required.

## Eligibility
- Socially excluded persons residing or working in the North West District (per CDC postal locator) who face legal and other complex issues
- Community partners such as Family Service Centres, Community Development Councils, Social Service Offices and Social Service Agencies in the North West District
- Socially excluded persons are those facing significant barriers to legal services due to socio-economic, mobility, psychological, psychiatric, language and technological factors, including transnational families and the "sandwich class" who do not qualify for Government legal aid schemes

## Support / Benefits
- Facilitate or provide legal guidance and representation to socially and virtually excluded persons
- Work with North West district community partners to provide integrated legal care
- Provide training and support to community partner staff/volunteers to identify unmet legal needs and make referrals
- Provide training and support to volunteer lawyers at Pro Bono SG's community legal clinics and pro bono representation schemes
- Conduct law awareness talks for socially/virtually excluded persons and caregivers
- Build a corpus of best practices as a centre of excellence in integrated, holistic socio-legal healing

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Facing a legal problem but cannot afford a lawyer and don't qualify for government legal aid
- Part of the "sandwich class" needing legal help but excluded from existing aid schemes
- Family service centre staff wanting to refer a client with unmet legal needs
- Elderly or vulnerable person in the North West district needing legal guidance
- Transnational family facing complex legal issues in Singapore
