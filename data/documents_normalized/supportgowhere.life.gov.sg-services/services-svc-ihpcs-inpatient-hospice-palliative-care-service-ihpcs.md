# Inpatient Hospice Palliative Care Service (IHPCS)

## Overview
Provides medical and emotional support for end-of-life patients who cannot be cared for at home and require inpatient care, along with support for their caregivers.

## Eligibility
- Patient with a prognosis of less than 3 months who needs specialised care especially in the last phase of life, when care at home, in a nursing home or outpatient setting is not possible
- Patient with a prognosis of less than 12 months who requires time-limited care up to 28 calendar days for severe symptoms that cannot be managed in outpatient settings

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- View the list of service providers on AIC's website
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Specialist palliative care focusing on management of symptoms such as pain, breathlessness, nausea and vomiting
- Emotional, spiritual and grief support and counselling
- Palliative rehabilitation, including physiotherapy and occupational therapy
- Music and art therapy
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents)
- MediShield Life and MediSave can be used to cover costs
- Further financial assistance information available from service providers

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about caring for a dying family member at home when it's no longer possible
- looking for inpatient care for a loved one with a terminal illness
- unsure how to pay for end-of-life hospital care
- need emotional or grief support while caring for a family member near end of life
- want to know if MediSave or MediShield Life covers hospice care
