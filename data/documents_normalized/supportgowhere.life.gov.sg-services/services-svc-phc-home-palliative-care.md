# Home Palliative Care

## Overview
Provides end-of-life care services to improve the quality of life for patients with life-limiting illness at home, supporting both patients and their caregivers.

## Eligibility
- Home-bound patient diagnosed by a doctor to have an advanced life-limiting illness with a prognosis of less than 1 year

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- View the list of service providers on AIC's website
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Medical and nursing support to manage pain and symptoms
- Emotional and social support
- Caregiver training
- Equipment loan
- Advance Care Planning
- Care coordination
- 24/7 clinical services
- Bereavement care
- Charges may apply; check with the service provider

## Related Issues & Concerns
- want to care for a dying family member at home instead of in hospital
- need equipment or training to manage a loved one's pain and symptoms at home
- looking for round-the-clock clinical support for home-based end-of-life care
- need bereavement support after a family member's death at home
