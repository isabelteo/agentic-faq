# Epworth Literacy and Behaviour Intervention

## Overview
Epworth offers literacy and behavioural intervention to support children aged 5 to 12 years old (in pre-schools and mainstream schools) with learning difficulties, including language/literacy skills and behavioural challenges. Parenting workshops are also offered.

## Eligibility
- Children aged 5-12 years old (in pre-schools and mainstream schools) with learning difficulties assessed to be behind their same-aged peers in literacy and/or language skills
- Epworth screens to assess if the child is eligible and suitable for the programme

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Contact the service provider directly for enquiries and to sign up
- Providers: Epworth (Bukit Batok), 106 Bukit Batok Central, #01-217; Epworth (Jurong West), 471 Jurong West Street 41, #01-505

## Support / Benefits
- Literacy and/or language intervention - skills and strategies to overcome learning challenges and develop literacy skills
- Behavioural intervention - supports essential executive functioning for learning, task readiness, on-seat behaviour, and basic social competency skills
- Parenting workshops to increase knowledge in effective parenting and understanding children's needs
- Fees payable; centre-based

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried my child is falling behind peers in reading and language skills
- looking for behavioural support for a child with difficulty sitting still or focusing in class
- want parenting workshops to better support a child with learning difficulties
- need help assessing whether my child needs literacy intervention
