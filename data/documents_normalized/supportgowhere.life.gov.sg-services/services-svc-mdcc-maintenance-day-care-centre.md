# Maintenance Day Care Centre

## Overview
Centre-based day care that supports seniors in ageing well in the community, providing a full day programme of activities, exercises, and personal care, along with respite for family and caregivers.

## Eligibility
- Seniors suitable for centre-based care setting during the day, and/or who require supervision while their caregivers are at work

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- View the list of service providers on AIC's website
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Meals (e.g. lunch and tea break)
- Basic nursing and health services
- Suitable maintenance exercises created for seniors based on their Individualised Care Plan
- Social and recreational activities (e.g. arts & crafts, games and karaoke sessions)
- Personal care (e.g. bathing and getting dressed)
- Mostly offered on weekdays, but some centres also provide weekend support
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents)

## Related Issues & Concerns
- need daytime care for an elderly parent while I am at work
- looking for a day care centre that provides meals and personal care for seniors
- want respite support as a caregiver for an ageing family member
- checking if my parent qualifies for subsidised day care
