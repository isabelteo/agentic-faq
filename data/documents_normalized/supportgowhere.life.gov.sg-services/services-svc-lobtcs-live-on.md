# Live On!

## Overview
Live On! is a year-long, high-frequency, family-centric psychological intervention for youths aged 12 to 18 with high suicide risk as they transition from hospitalisation into the community.

## Eligibility
- Youths aged 12 to 18 years old who have been discharged from IMH, KKH, NUH, and other public hospitals due to suicidality, and assessed by a clinician to be eligible for referral
- Referral required

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Eligible only for youths discharged by IMH, KKH, NUH, and other public hospitals due to suicidality, after clinician assessment for referral
- If you do not have a hospital referral but still require support, call the TOUCHline (1800 377 2252, 9am to 6pm Monday to Friday) or email mentalhealth@touch.org.sg
- If suicidal and requiring immediate support, call Samaritans of Singapore (SOS) at 1767

## Support / Benefits
- Individual sessions (for the youth): Acute phase - weekly meetings with social worker/counsellor/psychologist for 6 to 12 sessions to de-escalate emotions; Continuation phase - twice a month for another 5 to 12 sessions; Maintenance phase - contact via calls/text for 3 to 6 months for continuous support and monitoring, with case re-opened if relapse occurs
- Family sessions (for caregivers): meet with social worker/counsellor twice a month from programme start for 4 to 6 sessions, learning skills such as safety planning and crisis management; school counsellors also contacted with client and parental consent
- Free; centre-based

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about a teenager who was hospitalised for suicidal thoughts or a suicide attempt
- looking for ongoing family support after a child's discharge from a psychiatric hospital
- need help managing a family member's suicide risk after leaving hospital care
- want crisis support for a suicidal youth without a hospital referral
