# Enhanced Home Personal Care (HPC+)

## Overview
Enhanced Home Personal Care (HPC+) provides trained care professionals who help frail or homebound seniors with activities of daily living and general routine tasks at home, and also support their caregivers.

## Eligibility
- Senior who requires personal services to continue living well at home; is a Singapore Citizen (SC), Permanent Resident (PR) or foreigner
- Caregiver who needs support in their caregiving duties; is a SC, PR or foreigner
- Subsidies (means-tested) only applicable to SC and PR
- Applicants must fulfil the service's eligibility criteria and providers' service boundaries; enrolment subject to vacancy availability

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Get a referral from a hospital, polyclinic or GP familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Or contact a service provider directly - search via the [AIC website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Enhanced-Home-Personal-Care) by postal code, address, or current location
- For further assistance, contact AIC at 1800 650 6060, email enquiries@aic.sg, or visit a nearby AIC Link

## Support / Benefits
- Personal care (e.g. showering, feeding), mind-stimulating activities, prescribed maintenance exercises, elder-sitting/respite for caregivers
- General support with routine tasks (check-ins, medication reminders, errands)
- 24/7 fall monitoring and response via digital devices installed at home
- Household chores (sweeping, cleaning fans/windows, washing toilets)
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (SC/PR only)

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Worried about an elderly parent living alone and needing help with daily tasks
- Need respite because caregiving duties are overwhelming
- Concerned about falls at home and no one to respond
- Wondering if there is financial help for home care services
- Looking for help with wound-free household chores for an ageing parent
