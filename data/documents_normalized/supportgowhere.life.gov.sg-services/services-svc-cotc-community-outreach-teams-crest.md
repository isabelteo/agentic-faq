# Community Outreach Teams (CREST)

## Overview
CREST teams are free, government-funded community mental health teams (over 100 islandwide) that act as an in-person first stop for mental health concerns, offering outreach, education, and psychosocial support for individuals and caregivers. They are developed under the Community Mental Health Masterplan driven by the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) with the Ministry of Health (MOH).

## Eligibility
- Any individual at risk of mental health conditions and/or dementia, and their caregivers
- Aged 18 and above
- Applicants may need to fulfil the services' in-take criteria and providers' service boundaries, if any

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Call or email the providers to make an appointment
- Visit AIC's website (https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Community-Mental-Health-Services) to search for service providers by postal code, address or current location
- If already seeing a General Practitioner (GP) or Polyclinic doctor, you may approach them for support

## Support / Benefits
- Promotes early recognition of signs and symptoms of mental health conditions and/or dementia
- Improves mental health/dementia literacy through outreach events and education for residents and caregivers
- Provides psychosocial intervention to reduce risk of escalation of mental health needs
- Provides collaborative, coordinated care through case management
- Fully funded by the government with no out-of-pocket cost

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about a family member showing early signs of dementia or mental health issues
- don't know where to start looking for mental health support in my neighbourhood
- caring for someone with a mental health condition and need coordinated support
- looking for free mental health services near me
- need education or coping techniques as a caregiver for someone with dementia
