# MightyKids, Families & Community (MightyKFC)

## Overview
MightyKFC is a developmental mentoring programme that aims to nurture at-risk children and youth in the Telok Blangah region.

## Eligibility
- MightyKFC's client group is latchkey children and youth from the lower income group, aged 4 to 16 years old
- Targets children and youth who primarily reside or study in schools in the Telok Blangah region

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Walk into the centre
- Email mkfc@life-community.org
- Call the centre at 62707198
- Referral not required
- An intake interview will be arranged to register for the programme

## Support / Benefits
- Centre-based activities
- Life skills workshops
- Individual mentoring
- Community service
- Parent-child bonding sessions
- Free; upon successful registration, services provided for the duration of the programme at the activity centre

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about a latchkey child from a lower-income family in Telok Blangah needing supervision and guidance
- looking for a mentoring programme for an at-risk child or youth
- want parent-child bonding activities and life skills support for a struggling family
- need free community-based support for a child aged 4 to 16 in the Telok Blangah area
