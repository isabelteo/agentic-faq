# My Legacy

## Overview
My Legacy is a trusted digital companion that makes legacy planning easy and convenient, using technology to help document and share plans for legal, healthcare and estate matters securely with people you trust.

## Eligibility
- Individuals aged 30 and above who are interested to find out how they can start their legacy planning

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application is needed
- Find out more on how to start legacy planning via the My Legacy website

## Support / Benefits
- Consolidated information on legacy planning and services like applying for a Lasting Power of Attorney, Advance Care Planning
- Can download a copy of a deceased's digital death certificate from My Legacy if it is still within 30 days from the date of issuance
- Free

## Related Issues & Concerns
- want to start planning ahead for my family's legal and healthcare arrangements
- looking for one place to organise Lasting Power of Attorney and Advance Care Planning information
- need to download a deceased family member's death certificate
- unsure how to begin legacy or estate planning
