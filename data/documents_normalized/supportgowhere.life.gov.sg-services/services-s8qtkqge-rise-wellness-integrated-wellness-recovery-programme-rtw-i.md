# Rise to Wellness - Integrated Wellness Recovery Programme (RTW-I)

## Overview
RTW-I is a free, centre-based, integrated wellness recovery programme delivered by PSALT Care that aims to improve and sustain the mental health recovery of persons with a mental health condition (PMHCs) and/or addiction issues. No referral required.

## Eligibility
- Singaporean residents, 18 years old and above
- Current or new members of PSALT Care
- Formal diagnosis of a mental health condition (self-declaration); those without a formal diagnosis but interested will be assessed by PSALT Care staff, clinicians or a collaboration partner

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- WhatsApp: +65 6205 5538 or 9628 5608
- Email: support@psaltcare.com
- Website: www.psaltcare.com.sg

## Support / Benefits
- 9 to 12 month programme with a dedicated supporter for 1-to-1 recovery journey
- Peer support groups, anonymous groups, recovery talks/workshops/activities, befriending, 1-to-1 peer support, counselling/therapy (needs basis), coaching, short-term crisis respite
- Personal wellness recovery plan to chart progress and activities supporting social connection, inclusion, and wellbeing

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Struggling with a mental health condition and looking for ongoing peer support, not just one-off counselling
- Recovering from addiction and want a structured programme with a dedicated supporter
- Feeling isolated and want to build social connections while managing a mental health condition
- Need short-term crisis respite during a difficult period
