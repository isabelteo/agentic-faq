# SHINE Children and Youth Services - Learning and Social Support Programme

## Overview
Provides literacy and language intervention to children aged 7 to 12 years old in mainstream schools who have learning difficulties.

## Eligibility
- Children aged 7 to 12 years (in mainstream schools) with learning difficulties

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Contact the service provider directly for enquiries and to sign up for the programme
- No referral required

## Support / Benefits
- Actualise Learning Potential and Promote Skills (ALPS): language and literacy intervention programme for children 7 to 12 years old who typically lag 2 to 3 years behind their same-aged peers in language or literacy levels
- Parenting sessions to equip and empower parents and caregivers to understand their children's needs and better engage with them
- Reading Odyssey: enhances reading ability of children 7 to 12 years old and improves motivation to read
- Centre conducts an intake assessment and consultation session with the child to understand needs and discuss follow-up support and suitability for the programme
- Fees payable; centre-based

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried my child is falling behind in reading or language at school
- looking for help for a child with learning difficulties in mainstream school
- want parenting guidance on supporting a child who struggles academically
- looking for a centre-based literacy programme for a school-age child
