# Local Outreach to Suicide Survivors (LOSS)

## Overview
LOSS provides immediate and long-term emotional support to persons who have lost a loved one or someone they knew to suicide, primarily through support groups, texts, phone calls, and counselling sessions.

## Eligibility
- Persons grieving a suicide loss, including self-referrals, referrals from police, healthcare, and community partners, and concerned third parties
- Referral not required

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Reach out to SOS via the 24/7 Hotline: 1767
- Or via CareText: WhatsApp 91511767

## Support / Benefits
- Support groups for persons grieving a suicide loss, facilitated by SOS grief counselling specialists and trained volunteers with lived experience ("Healing Companions")
- Additional emotional support through phone calls, text, and counselling sessions (where appropriate)
- When you reach out, SOS staff or trained volunteers provide emotional support and, if needed, encourage grief counselling and support groups for longer-term support
- Free; centre-based

## Related Issues & Concerns
- grieving after losing a family member or friend to suicide
- looking for a support group for suicide loss survivors
- need someone to talk to after a loved one's suicide
- want ongoing counselling after a sudden bereavement by suicide
