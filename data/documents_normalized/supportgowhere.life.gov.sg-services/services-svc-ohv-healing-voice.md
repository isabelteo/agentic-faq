# Our HEALing Voice

## Overview
Our HEALing Voice (OHV) is a structured 12-session training platform that empowers clients to share their lived experience and provide peer mentoring/support to others.

## Eligibility
- Recovered Persons with Mental Health Conditions (PMHCs)
- Individuals with no formal diagnosis of mental health condition(s) but who exhibit signs and symptoms assessed to be mild, manageable, and stable

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- May be referred by healthcare professionals, social workers, or through self-referral
- For self-referral: contact number 9113 8052, or email ourhealingvoice@clubheal.org.sg
- Referral not required; centre-based; fees payable

## Support / Benefits
- Structured 12-session training to equip clients with skills to share their lived experience and provide peer mentoring/support
- Clients who complete the OHV programme can join the OHV network to share their lived experience at Club HEAL's outreach platforms and/or provide peer support to other PMHCs/individuals in distress

## Related Issues & Concerns
- recovered from a mental health condition and want to help others going through similar struggles
- looking for peer support training programmes in mental health recovery
- want to share my lived experience with mental illness in a structured way
- interested in becoming a peer mentor for people with mental health conditions
