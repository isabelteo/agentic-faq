# Remarriage Preparation Programme for Muslim Couples

## Overview
Provides pre- and post-marriage support for remarrying Muslim couples and their dependent children (below 21) from a previous marriage.

## Eligibility
- One or both parties are marrying for the second time or more
- At least one party is a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- One or both parties contemplating marriage or already in a stepfamily have at least one dependent child (below 21 years of age)
- Both parties contemplating marriage or already in a stepfamily must be Muslim

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Call, email, visit, or apply through referrals by other agencies
- Email or call the agency directly to request more information on the Marriage Preparation Programme (MPP) and marriage support services
- Agency worker will assess and register eligible couples for MPP
- Referral not required; centre-based; free

## Support / Benefits
- Upon completion of MPP, agency worker will refer higher-risk couples to Family Service Centres (FSCs) or Social Service Offices (SSOs)
- Agency also refers eligible couples to KidStart

- need guidance navigating a blended Muslim family
