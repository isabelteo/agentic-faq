# Babes Pregnancy Crisis Support

## Overview
Babes provides free, no-referral support for pregnant teenagers (aged 21 and below) and anyone needing information about teenage pregnancy, via casework, counselling, and a 24-hour helpline.

## Eligibility
- Pregnant teenager who needs help, or anyone who needs information about teenage pregnancy
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Call or text the 24-hour helpline: 6206 6641
- Or text via WhatsApp: 8111 3535 to speak to caseworkers
- The Helpliner assesses eligibility for casework; if suitable, Babes arranges a meeting with the teenager and/or family/partner within 3-5 working days

## Support / Benefits
- Casework and counselling
- 24-hour helpline (call/text)
- Practical support: befriender, baby essentials, employment and back-to-school assistance
- Information and resources
- Outreach and youth engagement events

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Teenager pregnant and unsure what options are available
- Need someone to talk to urgently about an unplanned teenage pregnancy
- Looking for baby essentials and practical help as a young parent
- Want to know how to support a friend or family member who is a pregnant teen
- Concerned about continuing school or work while pregnant as a teenager
