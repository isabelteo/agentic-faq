# Permanent Residency

## Overview
Facilitates the process for eligible foreigners to reside in Singapore on a permanent basis; Permanent Residents aged 15 and above are issued the Singapore blue Identity Card (IC).

## Eligibility
- Spouse of a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Unmarried child (aged below 21) born from a legal marriage to / legally adopted by a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Aged parent of a Singapore Citizen
- Employment Pass or S Pass holder
- Student studying in Singapore
- Foreign investor in Singapore
- Note: criteria are not exhaustive; individual eligibility subject to ICA's approval

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Refer to [ICA's explanatory notes](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/PR/apply) and prepare required supporting documents
- Submit all applications online via [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/epr) using Singpass, or Foreign Identification Number and immigration pass issuance date if a student
- Refer to the [FAQs](https://ask.gov.sg/ica) and [step-by-step guide](https://www.ica.gov.sg/docs/default-source/ica/eservices/epr/step-by-step-guide.pdf) for e-Service help
- For further assistance, contact ICA at 6391 6100 or [submit an enquiry](https://go.gov.sg/ica-pr-enquiry)

## Support / Benefits
- Processing time for each application is at least 6 months
- Check application status via [MyICA](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/icsbip)
- A $100 non-refundable processing fee is payable per applicant at submission
- If successful, applicants must complete PR formalities personally at the ICA office
