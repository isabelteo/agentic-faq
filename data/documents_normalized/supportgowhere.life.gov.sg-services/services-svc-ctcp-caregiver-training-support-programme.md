# Caregiver Training and Support Programme

## Overview
The Caregiver Training and Support Programme (CTSP) is a free, centre-based programme run by Mindfull Community Limited that provides training and support to caregivers of Persons with Mental Health Issues (PMHIs), helping them cope with caregiving duties. No referral is required.

## Eligibility
- Caregivers of Persons with Mental Health Issues (PMHIs)
- This can range from spouses, partners, parents, siblings, grandchildren, friends, neighbours, etc of someone experiencing a mental health condition

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Register online through the form (https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=mH6MQ8-ncE-ro_WXOpzXWwTicfzzzZ1Jqtl-rNfRemJUMEpJWUo4NFFQVjZMWDE4NEs3N1hPWTBHNiQlQCN0PWcu&route=shorturl)
- After registration, participants will receive a call and email from Mindfull Community Limited to confirm registration

## Support / Benefits
- CTSP consists of Caregivers-to-Caregivers (C2C) Programme and Engagement-to-Empowerment (E2E) Programme
- C2C provides training courses ranging from 8 to 12 sessions in 3 different languages, covering general or specific mental health conditions; caregiver leaders are identified and trained to support other caregivers and do outreach/advocacy work
- E2E includes Caregiver-for-Caregiver (C4C) Support Programme: 12 support group sessions over a year to increase support networks; first six sessions in-person providing coping skills, later sessions may be online or in-person
- E2E also includes Train-the-Trainer (TTT): interested caregivers coached and trained as Volunteer Trainers to support advocacy and outreach work

## Related Issues & Concerns
- struggling to cope as a caregiver for a family member with a mental health condition
- looking for a support group to connect with other caregivers
- want training on how to better support a spouse or parent with mental health issues
- interested in becoming a caregiver leader or volunteer trainer
- need coping skills and knowledge for caregiving duties
