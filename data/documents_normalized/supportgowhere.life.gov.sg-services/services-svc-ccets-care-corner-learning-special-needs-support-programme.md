# Care Corner Learning and Special Needs Support Programme

## Overview
This is a centre-based learning support programme run by Care Corner for children in mainstream schools who struggle with learning difficulties such as ADHD, Autism Spectrum Disorder, dyslexia, dyscalculia, dysgraphia, dyspraxia, and related conditions. Fees are payable and no referral is required.

## Eligibility
- Children aged 4 to 13 in mainstream schools struggling with learning difficulties
- Services include KidsReady (N2 to K2) for preschoolers needing support in English, Mathematics, handwriting and fine motor skills
- Services include KidsLearn (P1 to P6) for primary school children needing learning support in English & Mathematics

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Contact the service provider directly for enquiries
- Or complete the online referral form: https://form.jotform.com/CC_LSN/lsnreferral
- Care Corner will conduct a pre-intake assessment to screen if the child is suitable for the programme

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried my child with ADHD or dyslexia is falling behind in mainstream school
- looking for learning support for my preschooler before they start primary school
- need help with my child's handwriting and fine motor skills
- concerned about my primary school child struggling with English or Maths
- looking for a centre-based programme for a child with autism or developmental delay
