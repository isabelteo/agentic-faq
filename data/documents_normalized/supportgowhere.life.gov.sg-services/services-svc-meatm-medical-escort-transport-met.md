# Medical Escort and Transport (MET)

## Overview
Provides transportation services with trained care aides to help frail seniors travel to and from their homes for medical appointments, and to accompany them during the appointment if needed.

## Eligibility
- Senior who needs help to move around, and has no caregiver, or whose caregiver is unable to provide support because of their own health and physical condition

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- View the list of service providers on AIC's website
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents)

## Related Issues & Concerns
- elderly parent has no one to bring them to medical appointments
- need transport help for a frail senior who cannot travel alone
- caregiver unable to accompany elderly family member to the hospital due to own health issues
- looking for subsidised transport for seniors to and from clinics
