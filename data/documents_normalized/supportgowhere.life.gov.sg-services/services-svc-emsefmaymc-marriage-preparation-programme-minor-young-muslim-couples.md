# Marriage Preparation Programme for Minor and Young Muslim Couples

## Overview
Provides pre- and post-marriage support for minor and young Muslim couples through premarital consultation sessions, marriage preparation workshops, and a parents support group.

## Eligibility
**Minor Couples** (mandatory to complete Marriage Preparation Programme before applying for marriage):
- Either party is below 21 years old
- At least one party is Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Both bride and groom are Muslims

**Young Couples:**
- Groom is between 21 and 24 years old and bride is aged 21 years and above
- At least one party is Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Both bride and groom are Muslims

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Call, email, visit, or apply through referrals by other agencies
- **For minor couples**: sign up (email or call) directly with the agency for the mandatory, complimentary Marriage Preparation Programme (MPP) before proceeding with marriage application via the Registry of Muslim Marriages (ROMM) website
- Upon completion of MPP, the agency will provide casework and counselling if necessary for up to 10 years, and refer higher-risk couples to FSC or SSO
- Eligible couples will also be auto-referred to KidStart; those who prefer not to be auto-referred can complete an opt-out form instead
- **For young couples**: MPP is not mandatory; couples can apply e-file for marriage (OMJ) before attending MPP. Upon completion of MPP, the same post-marriage services offered to minor couples apply
