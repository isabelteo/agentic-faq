# Nursing Home Respite Care

## Overview
A short-term residential care option at select nursing homes that provides respite for caregivers of seniors with high care needs.

## Eligibility
- Seniors with stable medical conditions who need help with daily nursing care needs
- Caregivers who need a respite or are going away temporarily

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- View the list of service providers on AIC's website
- For further assistance, contact AIC via the hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry to enquiries@aic.sg
- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines; calls are free only from regular land lines in Singapore
- Calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Short-term subsidised residential care for caregivers who need a short, full-time break for personal or medical reasons
- Support when a domestic helper goes on home leave
- A break at night to rest when the senior has challenging night-time behaviour
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents)

## Related Issues & Concerns
- caregiver needs a short break from caring for an elderly family member
- domestic helper going on home leave and no one else can care for elderly parent
- exhausted from managing a senior's challenging night-time behaviour
- looking for temporary residential care while going away for personal or medical reasons
