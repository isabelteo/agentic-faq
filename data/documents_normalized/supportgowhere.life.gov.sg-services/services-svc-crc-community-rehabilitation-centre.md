# Community Rehabilitation Centre

## Overview
Community Rehabilitation Centres provide centre-based therapy for seniors to help them regain and maintain the ability to do daily tasks, including physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy. Fees are payable and a referral is required.

## Eligibility
- Senior diagnosed with Parkinson's disease, or who has had a stroke, fracture or other conditions requiring assistance with moving, speaking or swallowing
- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through the hospital, polyclinic or GP familiar with your loved one's condition and needs
- Apply at any of the AIC Links (https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- View the list of service providers at AIC's website (https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Community-Rehabilitation)
- For further assistance, contact AIC via hotline 1800 650 6060 or submit an enquiry (mailto:enquiries@aic.sg); airtime charges apply for mobile calls, calls free from regular land lines; calls from AIC Hotline may show as 6650 6060 on caller ID

## Support / Benefits
- Physiotherapy for those with mobility issues due to physical disability, trauma or illness such as stroke
- Occupational Therapy for those who need to learn, recover or maintain daily living and work skills
- Speech Therapy for those with swallowing or speech disorders
- Subsidies available depending on means-test result (https://aic.sg/care-services/home-medical#faq)
