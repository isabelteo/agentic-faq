# HCSA SPIN

## Overview
SPIN supports single parents and lone caregivers through casework and counselling, a social support network such as befriending, practical support, and an online portal of information and resources. It aims to empower single parents and lone caregivers to be resilient and self-reliant, and to improve caregiving/parenting capacity, social support, psychological well-being and access to practical resources.

## Eligibility
- Single parents or lone caregivers of children below 18 years old, and these children
- No income criteria and no geographical boundaries

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach any Family Service Centre (FSC) for referral
- Sign up online at https://crm.hcsa.org.sg/spin-member-application-form/
- Call or email the programme for assistance in signing up
- A social work associate will respond to the enquiry/referral within 3-5 working days and arrange an intake assessment; a social worker will then be assigned and trained befrienders matched to the parent
- The programme runs for a maximum period of 12 months, after which parents can join the Alumni Chapter to stay connected

## Support / Benefits
- Needs assessment and co-creation of individualised care plans
- Counselling for single parents and children (aged 7 to 17) assessed to need emotional/mental health support
- Befriending and peer support to build social support and trust
- Tuition and homework support for children of clients
- Workshops and events on life and career skills
- Practical support such as referrals, disbursement of grocery vouchers and food items

## Related Issues & Concerns
- struggling to cope as a single parent and need emotional and practical support
- worried about my child's wellbeing while raising them alone
- looking for a community of other single parents for peer support
- need help with groceries or basic necessities as a lone caregiver
- unsure how to get tuition or homework help for my child while managing as a single parent
