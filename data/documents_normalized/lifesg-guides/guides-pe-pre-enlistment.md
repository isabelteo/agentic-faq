# Pre Enlistment

## Overview
This guide walks through the pre-enlistment journey for National Service (NS)-liable males, from receiving early notices at age 13 through to Enlistment Day.

## Eligibility
- From age 13, NS-liable individuals will receive NS notices via mail registered with ICA.
- If aged 13 and above and overseas for 3 months or longer, an Exit Permit (EP) is required (not required for under 3 months); a bond may also be required.
- Deferment for pre-university studies is available at government-funded institutions (GCE 'O', 'N', or 'A' Level, polytechnic diploma, Higher NITEC, or NITEC) or selected privately-funded institution courses, as long as academic qualifications are being advanced. Deferment is not granted for university degree courses even if already started.
- Pre-enlistee IPPT: eligible for an 8-week reduction in full-time NS duration based on medical fitness and attaining 61 points or more; those enlisting before Oct 2027 can take the pre-enlistee IPPT if medically graded PES A/B1; those enlisting from Oct 2027 onwards will be informed via OneNS if eligible.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Failure to comply with NS notices is an offence under the Enlistment Act 1970 — fine up to $10,000, jail up to 3 years, or both.
- At 13 years old: apply for an Exit Permit if needed, per the Notice on Exit Permit requirements sent to the ICA-registered mailing address.
- At 16.5 years old: register for NS on the OneNS web portal (www.ns.gov.sg/web/pre-enlistee/), following the NS Registration Notice; deferment for full-time pre-university studies can be applied for at this time.
- Undergo pre-enlistment medical screening to determine medical fitness for NS; those enlisting before Oct 2027 receive a Physical Employment Standard (PES) status, those enlisting from Oct 2027 onwards are graded under the refreshed Medical Classification System.
- Qualify for direct basic training by attaining the required pre-enlistee IPPT result at least two weeks before the Physical Training Phase (PTP) enlistment.
- Visit the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) website for more information such as pre-enlistee IPPT and posting to uniformed services.

## Support / Benefits
- Medical fitness status is used to determine deployment during full-time NS so training is suitable and safe.
- Eligible pre-enlistees can receive an 8-week reduction in full-time NS duration.
