# Notice Periods

## Overview
This guide explains how notice periods work when an employment ends, including in retrenchment situations.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Check whether your employment contract specifies a notice period.
- If no notice period is stated in the contract, the Employment Act applies and the notice period depends on your length of service (read more about Termination with Notice).
- Employment can be ended without notice if "payment in lieu of notice" is included in the contract — the employer pays instead of providing a notice period.
- If facing an employment dispute about payment or notice periods, contact the Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management (TADM) for help — TADM resolves salary-related claims and employment disputes.
