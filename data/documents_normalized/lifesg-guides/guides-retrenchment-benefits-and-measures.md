# Retrenchment Benefits And Measures

## Overview
This is an overview guide explaining what retrenchment is and what employees facing retrenchment might be eligible for.

## Eligibility
- Retrenchment happens when companies need to reduce their workforce due to redundancy, reorganisation, or changes in the industry.
- If facing retrenchment, an employee might be eligible for: a notice period, retrenchment benefits, alternatives to retrenchment, and help in finding a new job.

## Support / Benefits
- Companies with ten or more employees must notify the Ministry of Manpower (MOM) of retrenchments within 5 working days after notifying their employees (read more about mandatory retrenchment notifications).
