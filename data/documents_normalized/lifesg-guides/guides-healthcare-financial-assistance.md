# Medical expenses

## Overview
This guide covers financial assistance options for medical expenses beyond MediSave and MediShield Life, including caregiving support, independent living support, and disability assistance.

## Eligibility
- Home Caregiving Grant (HCG): for caregivers of a loved one with permanent moderate disability.
- Foreign Domestic Worker (FDW) Levy Concession: for employers caring for loved ones with mild disability.
- Tax relief for caregivers: for those supporting parents, grandparents, parents-in-law or grandparents-in-law.
- ElderFund: for lower-income citizens aged 30 and above who do not benefit from other disability schemes.
- Pioneer Generation Disability Assistance Scheme (PioneerDAS): for Pioneers with disabilities.
- Interim Disability Assistance Programme for the Elderly (IDAPE): for seniors with severe disabilities not eligible for ElderShield.

## Support / Benefits
- Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS): subsidies at participating CHAS GP and dental clinics.
- Subsidies for government-funded intermediate and long-term care: higher subsidies for lower-income households.
- MediFund: financial assistance for needy citizens who struggle to pay bills after subsidies, insurance, and MediSave.
- Medical Fee Exemption Card: free treatment for eligible residents of government-funded nursing homes or sheltered and disability homes.
- Home Caregiving Grant (HCG): monthly cash payout to offset caregiving costs.
- Caregivers Training Grant: annual subsidy for caregivers to attend approved courses.
- Assistive Technology Fund (ATF): subsidies to purchase assistive technology devices for persons with disabilities.
- Seniors' Mobility and Enabling Fund (SMF): subsidies on assistive devices or home healthcare items.
- Enhancement for Active Seniors (EASE): elderly-friendly home improvement items for comfort and mobility.
- ElderFund, PioneerDAS, IDAPE: monthly cash payouts for eligible disabled seniors/citizens.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- To get help applying for financial assistance schemes, speak to a medical social worker from the hospital, or a Care Consultant from the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC).
- Related e-services (Singpass login may be required): check medical appointments, view outstanding bills at public healthcare institutions, refill prescribed medicine, apply for CHAS card, check CHAS subsidy balance.
