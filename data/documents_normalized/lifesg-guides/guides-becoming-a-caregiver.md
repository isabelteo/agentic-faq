# Understand your role

## Overview
This guide helps new caregivers understand their role, whether caring for active or bedbound seniors, or someone with a health condition such as dementia, stroke, mental health conditions, or cancer.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Learn how to care for seniors depending on their condition (active seniors, seniors using mobility aids, bedbound seniors).
- Learn how to care for someone with a mental health condition, dementia, stroke recovery, or cancer.
- Prepare for hospital discharge and make home modifications to remove hazards.
- Speak to a doctor about assistive devices.
- Use the "4 Ps" to plan a manageable care routine.
- Take caregiver training courses, such as the ABCs of Caregiving or the Basic Eldercare Course (for seniors aged 65 and above).
- Plan for emergencies, finances, and advance care planning (including legal tools such as a will).
- Use related e-services: view outstanding bills at public healthcare institutions, refill prescribed medicine, check Medisave withdrawal limits and MediShield Life claims, and check medical appointments (Singpass login may be required).
