# Safety During Ns

## Overview
This guide describes how the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF), and Singapore Police Force (SPF) ensure training safety during National Service, and how to report safety concerns.

## Support / Benefits
- Safety is anchored by 3 components: leadership emphasis on safety, safety management processes aligned to ISO 45001 (international occupational health and safety standard) with progressive training curricula, and a culture of safety where servicemen are empowered to call a safety pause at any time without fear of punishment.
- Safety hotlines are available to report incidents:
  - SAF Army: 9723 3891 / Army_Safety@defence.gov.sg
  - SAF Navy: 9172 3389
  - SAF Air Force: 9234 7233
  - SCDF: FormSG for SCDF Feedback
  - SPF: 9067 5063 / SPF_Training_Safety@spf.gov.sg
