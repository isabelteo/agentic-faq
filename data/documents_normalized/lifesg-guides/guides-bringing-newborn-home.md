# Care basics: a video series

## Overview
This guide helps new parents transition smoothly to parenthood, covering newborn care basics, adjusting to parenthood, building family bonds, and raising a healthy child.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Learn newborn care basics: bathing, umbilical cord care, breastfeeding, burping, changing diapers, swaddling, and safe cot positioning.
- Learn what to expect in the early days, including confinement practices and myths, and why babies cry.
- Access resources on bonding with your newborn and building strong family relationships.
- Track immunisations and developmental milestones via the Child's Digital Health Booklet, per the National Childhood Immunisation Programme.
- Introduce solid food to your baby's diet from around 4-6 months of age.
- Apply for birth registration and Baby Bonus, and view Baby Bonus application status/statement (Singpass login may be required).
- Search for Baby Bonus Approved Institutions to use Child Development Account funds.

## Related Issues & Concerns

- worried about confinement practices and what's medically necessary
- looking for guidance on introducing solid food to my baby
- want to track my child's immunisations and health records
