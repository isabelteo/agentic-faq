# Get Career Advice And Coaching

## Overview
This guide describes how job seekers can get career advice and coaching from Workforce Singapore (WSG) or the Employment and Employability Institute (e2i).

## Support / Benefits
- By working with a career coach, you can personalise your career plan to your goals and interests, build confidence and receive job-hunting support, get job search and interview skills, and learn about relevant programmes, events and workshops.
- Can also book a consultation with a Skills Ambassador for advice on skills upgrading and suitable training courses.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Meet a career coach from Workforce Singapore (WSG) or Employment and Employability Institute (e2i).
- Book a consultation with a Skills Ambassador.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- want personalised advice on planning my career
- nervous about job interviews and want coaching
- unsure which training courses would help me upgrade my skills
- looking for encouragement and confidence-building during my job search
