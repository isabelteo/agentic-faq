# Vocations In Ns

## Overview
This guide explains the 33 National Service (NS) vocations across the SAF, SCDF, and SPF, and how pre-enlistees can indicate their vocation interest.

## Eligibility
- Applies to NS-liable pre-enlistees who have registered for NS.
- Assignment to SAF vocations of Commandos and Naval Divers is based on stringent selection criteria; shortlisted individuals receive a notice to attend a Vocational Assessment.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Familiarise yourself with the 33 NS vocations across SAF, SCDF, and SPF, grouped into 7 categories (SAF Categories 1-3, SCDF Categories 1-2, SPF Categories 1-2).
- Consider the different vocations carefully — think about personality type, skills you wish to learn, and what makes you feel fulfilled — and discuss with family, friends, or NSF peers.
- Indicate vocation interest on the OneNS web portal before your medical screening appointment; select at least 2 vocations from each category (at least 14 vocations total in total), or indicate no specific interest.
- You are given only 1 chance to indicate interest and cannot change your choice after submitting.
- Visit the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) website to watch videos of each vocation or read the vocation handbook for more information.

## Support / Benefits
- NS deployment considers indicated interest, suitability, medical fitness, and the manpower/operational requirements of the SAF, SCDF, and SPF.
