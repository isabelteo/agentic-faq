# Give your family financial protection

## Overview
This guide covers ways to protect and grow your family's finances: life insurance for dependants (DPS), a scheme to help with your child's early expenses (CDA/Baby Bonus), and ways to top up loved ones' CPF Special/Retirement Account and MediSave Account.

## Eligibility
- **Dependants' Protection Scheme (DPS)**: automatically extended to all Singaporeans and Permanent Residents (PRs) between 21 and 65 years old upon their first CPF working contribution; those 16 years old and above who have not been offered automatic coverage can apply directly with Great Eastern Life.
- **Child Development Account (CDA) / Baby Bonus**: applies to Singapore Citizen children whose date of birth is on or after 18 Feb 2025 (rates below); eligibility can be checked via the parent-helper eligibility tool.
- **Retirement Sum Topping-Up Scheme (RSTU)**: top-ups can be made to a loved one's CPF Special Account if they are below 55, or Retirement Account if 55 and above; Matched Retirement Savings Scheme (MRSS) government matching applies to eligible seniors who have yet to reach the Basic Retirement Sum (BRS).
- **MediSave top-ups**: can be made for yourself or family members, including parents, grandparents, spouse, children, or siblings.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- **DPS**: check coverage status via your yearly CPF Statement of Account or online via the CPF website (Singpass login); if not automatically covered, apply directly with Great Eastern Life. Premiums are automatically deducted from CPF savings on annual policy renewal, or can be paid directly in cash via GIRO to Great Eastern Life.
- **CDA**: (1) check eligibility via the parent-helper eService; if your child was born in Singapore and is a Singapore citizen, Baby Bonus application is part of birth registration on LifeSG — otherwise apply directly via the Apply for Baby Bonus service; select a bank (DBS/POSB, UOB, or OCBC) during application; (2) go to the selected bank's website to open the CDA, which opens with an initial balance of $3,000; (3) a NETS card is issued under the child's name and sent to the registered address.
- **RSTU**: use the Retirement Payout Planner on the CPF website to explore top-up options; loved ones can log in to the CPF website with Singpass to check how much top-up they can receive.
- **MediSave top-ups**: make a cash top-up via the Top up MediSave Account form on the CPF website using PayNow QR; you'll need the recipient's NRIC or CPF account number for top-ups to loved ones.

## Support / Benefits
- **DPS**: maximum sum assured is $70,000 (up to 60 years old) or $55,000 (above 60 up to 65 years old); covers insured CPF members for death, terminal illness, or total permanent disability.
- **Baby Bonus (for children born on/after 18 Feb 2025)**: 1st child — $11,000 cash gift, $5,000 CDA First Step Grant, up to $4,000 government co-matching; 2nd child — $11,000 cash gift, $5,000 CDA First Step Grant, up to $7,000 co-matching; 3rd/4th child — $13,000 enhanced cash gift, $10,000 First Step Grant (increased from $5,000 under the Large Families Scheme), up to $9,000 co-matching; 5th and subsequent child — $13,000 enhanced cash gift, $10,000 First Step Grant, up to $15,000 co-matching.
- CDA funds can be used for a child's medical and education needs (e.g. childcare centre or outpatient fees) at Approved Institutions; unused CDA funds can transfer to the Post-Secondary Edusave Account (PSEA, interest 2.5% p.a.) and, when the child turns 30, remaining funds transfer to the parent's CPF Ordinary Account (interest up to 3.5% p.a.).
- **RSTU**: top-ups can earn interest of up to 5% or 6% per annum; tax relief of up to $8,000 per calendar year for cash top-ups to loved ones (parents, grandparents, spouse, siblings), plus up to $8,000 per calendar year for cash top-ups to yourself; under MRSS, the Government matches every dollar of cash top-ups to eligible seniors, up to $600 per year.
- **MediSave**: working members save 8% to 10.5% of monthly salary (depending on age) into MediSave; can be used to pay for selected outpatient treatments, hospitalisation, day surgery expenses (subject to withdrawal limits), and MediShield Life, ElderShield and CareShield Life premiums; MediSave top-ups can earn up to 6% interest per annum and qualify for tax relief.
