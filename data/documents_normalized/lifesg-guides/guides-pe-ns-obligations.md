# Ns Obligations

## Overview
This guide sets out the legal National Service (NS) obligations of male Singapore citizens and certain Permanent Residents, from age 13 through to becoming an operationally ready NSman.

## Eligibility
- Male Singapore citizens must serve NS.
- Male Permanent Residents (PR) must serve NS if they received PR status as a foreign student or under their parents' sponsorship.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Keep track of NS obligations via the OneNS web portal (www.ns.gov.sg).
- Follow instructions in NS notices and Further Reporting Orders (FROs).
- Complete the pre-enlistment process, upload relevant documents, and attend all scheduled appointments.
- From 13 years old: obtain a valid Exit Permit (EP) to remain overseas for 3 months or longer (not needed for under 3 months); a bond may also be required in some cases.
- From 16.5 years old: register for NS and submit documentation, attend medical examinations and any further medical reviews, follow FRO instructions, and have a valid Exit Permit with bond for overseas stays of 3 months or longer.
- On receiving an Enlistment Notice: report for NS enlistment and serve full-time NS.
- After full-time NS (as an NSman): report for in-camp training when instructed (generally once a year for 10 years), report for service if mobilised, update your unit promptly of changes in particulars (contact number, mailing address, next of kin), maintain a valid Exit Permit for overseas stays of 12 months or longer, and pass the annual Individual Physical Proficiency Test (IPPT) if applicable.
- Notices and FROs are sent to the mailing address registered with the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA); log in to the OneNS web portal for a personalised view of obligations.
- Visit the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) website for more information on offences.

## Support / Benefits
- Examples of notices: Exit Permit Information Notice, NS Registration Notice, Enlistment Notice.
- FROs can be issued for activities such as Vocational Assessment and medical appointments.
