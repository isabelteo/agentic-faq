# Financial support

## Overview
This guide covers financial support schemes for prenatal care and assisted conception, plus resources for those planning for or in early pregnancy.

## Support / Benefits
- Co-funding for Assisted Conception Procedures (ACP): up to 75% co-funding from the government for treatment at public hospitals.
- MediSave for Assisted Conception Procedures (ACP): CPF MediSave can be used to pay for ACP treatments.
- MediSave Maternity Package: CPF MediSave can be used to pay for pre-delivery and delivery expenses.
- A MediSave and MediShield Life claims calculator is available to estimate how much CPF MediSave can be used for ACP.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Use the MediSave and MediShield Life claims calculator on the CPF website to estimate usable funds.
- Related resources: About your pregnancy, Pre-pregnancy preparations, Post-natal preparations, and Conception problem (options including IUI and IVF) — via KKH, Families for Life, HealthHub, and NUH.
- Early pregnancy resources cover adjusting to changes (communicating with partner, tips for dads-to-be), caring for your body (morning sickness, pregnancy symptoms, nutrients), and complications (pregnancy complications, miscarriage, gestational diabetes).
- Related service (Singpass login may be required): check your medical appointments.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- struggling to conceive and want to know what financial help is available for IVF or IUI
- wondering how much of my MediSave I can use for assisted conception procedures
- planning for pregnancy and looking for guidance on what to expect
- experiencing symptoms in early pregnancy and unsure if they're normal
- worried about pregnancy complications like gestational diabetes or miscarriage
