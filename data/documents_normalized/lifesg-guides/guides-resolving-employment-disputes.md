# Salary and other entitlements

## Overview
This guide covers an employee's basic rights in Singapore, including salary payments, termination of employment, and protection from unsafe or harassing work environments, and directs to the relevant government channels for disputes.

## Eligibility
- Maternity leave protections: if you've worked for your employer for at least 3 months, they cannot dismiss you unfairly during your pregnancy.
- Re-employment: employer is obliged to re-employ you after retirement age if you are eligible.
- Retirement: if you joined your company before turning 55, you cannot be forced to retire before the retirement age.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Learn about salary payments, employment rights (overtime, rest day, paid leave under the Employment Act), and the Progressive Wage Model (for cleaning, security, and landscape sectors) via MOM resources.
- Learn about termination with notice, wrongful dismissal, maternity leave protections, re-employment, and retirement rules via MOM resources.
- Report workplace harassment via TAFEP (Tripartite Alliance for Fair and Progressive Employment Practices).
- Report unsafe workplace conditions confidentially via SnapSAFE (MOM eFeedback).
- Apply for work injury compensation if injured at work.
- File a wrongful dismissal or salary claim via TADM (Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management) eServices (Singpass login may be required).
- Appeal under the Retirement and Re-employment Act for dismissal or denial of re-employment, via MOM.
- Report workplace discrimination or harassment via TAFEP.
- Report a workplace injury via MOM eServices.
