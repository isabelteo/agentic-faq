# Preschool education

## Overview
This guide covers preschool options, subsidies, and early intervention programmes for children between 2 months and 7 years old. Enrolment in preschool is not compulsory, but most parents choose to enrol their child.

## Eligibility
- Preschools are available for children between 2 months and 7 years old.
- Anchor Operator (AOP) preschools have a fee cap of $720 for full-day child care and $1,275 for full-day infant care.
- Partner Operator (POP) preschools have a fee cap of $760 for full-day child care and $1,330 for full-day infant care.
- Child care and infant care subsidies: higher subsidies for working parents, with additional subsidies for lower-income families.
- Kindergarten Fee Assistance Scheme (KiFAS) is for lower-income families with children in MOE kindergartens or preschool anchor operators.
- Development Support (DS) and Learning Support (LS) Programme is for K1 or K2 students with mild developmental or learning needs.
- Early Intervention Programme for Infants and Children (EIPIC) is for children under 6 who are at risk of developmental, intellectual, sensory or physical disabilities.
- Enhanced Pilot for Private Intervention Providers offers subsidies for parents whose child has been referred to EIPIC but choose to enrol in selected private intervention centres instead.
- Child concession card is for children under 7 who are above 0.9m tall, to travel for free.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Learn how preschool supports child development and help your child prepare for starting preschool, especially if it's their first time away from family.
- Choose a type of preschool for your child (child care, kindergarten, or selected government-funded operators).
- Consider childminding services for infants aged 2 to 18 months, cared for by a professional childminder in a home or community space.
- Call 6735 9213 (Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 6pm, or Saturday, 8:30am to 1pm) to talk to someone about enrolling your child in preschool.
