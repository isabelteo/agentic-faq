# Retrenchment Benefits

## Overview
This guide explains eligibility for and typical amounts of retrenchment benefits for employees in Singapore.

## Eligibility
- Normally eligible for retrenchment benefits if you're an employee who has worked for 2 years or more at your current company.
- Those with less than 2 years of service could be granted a payment out of goodwill, depending on the company (read more about responsible retrenchment).

## Support / Benefits
- The amount of retrenchment benefits follows the employment contract or the collective agreement for unionised companies.
- Typically 2 weeks to 1 month's salary for each year of service, depending on the company's financial position and industry.
- For unionised companies, the norm is 1 month's salary for each year of service.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Understand the terms and conditions clearly before signing your retrenchment package — it is legally binding once signed and will affect your ability to negotiate or make claims later.
- If facing an employment dispute about retrenchment benefits, contact the Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management (TADM) for help — TADM resolves salary-related claims and employment disputes.
