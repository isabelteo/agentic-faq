# Why Serve Ns

## Overview
This guide explains to parents why their son or ward, as a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident (PR), is required to perform National Service (NS) once he turns 18 years old.

## Eligibility
- Applies to sons/wards who are Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents (PR).
- NS obligation begins once the son/ward turns 18 years old.

## Support / Benefits
- The NS journey consists of two years of full-time NS in the Singapore Armed Forces (SAF), Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF), or Singapore Police Force (SPF), followed by 10 years of Operationally Ready NS training.
- NS is described as the only way Singapore, as a small country, can maintain a strong and credible force to ensure its independence and sovereignty.
