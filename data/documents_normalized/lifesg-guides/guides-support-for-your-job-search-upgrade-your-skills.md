# Upgrade Your Skills

## Overview
This guide describes learning options available for job seekers who want to change or advance their careers.

## Support / Benefits
- Courses available from over 32 different sectors.
- Options to improve employability skills for job hunting.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- want to advance my career but don't know which courses to take
- looking to switch industries and need to build new skills
- want to make myself more employable before job hunting
