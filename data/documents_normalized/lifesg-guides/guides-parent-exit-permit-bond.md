# Exit Permit Bond

## Overview
This guide explains the Exit Permit (EP) and bond requirements for male Singaporeans and Permanent Residents above 13 years old who plan to stay outside Singapore for an extended period before completing National Service.

## Eligibility
- Every male Singaporean/PR above 13 years old must comply with EP and bond requirements.
- Between 13 and 16.5 years old: an EP is needed for 3 months to less than 2 years overseas; an EP and bond are needed for 2 years or longer overseas.
- Above 16.5 years old: an EP and bond are needed for 3 months or longer overseas.
- No EP is needed if travelling overseas for less than 3 months.
- A bond is not needed if overseas for work attachments that are part of the academic curriculum, or seafaring internships.
- Bond amount is either $75,000 or 50% of the combined annual gross income of both parents for the preceding year, whichever is higher; default is via Banker's Guarantee. CMPB will evaluate case-by-case if parents cannot provide the bond.
- It is an offence to remain outside Singapore for 3 months or longer without a valid EP, punishable by a fine of up to $10,000, jail of up to 3 years, or both.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for an EP via the OneNS web portal using Singpass, up to 3 months before the departure date. If below 15 years old without Singpass, contact the NS Call Centre at 1800 367 6767 or contact@ns.gov.sg.
- For deferment for overseas studies, submit the latest school letter (issued within the last 6 months) certifying level, course, and course dates, or other supporting documents.
- For overseas deferment for other purposes, submit supporting documents relating to the travel/stay purpose.
- If a bond is required, submit parents'/guardians' latest Income Tax Assessment Notice or income statements (or employment letter if unavailable), proof of overseas employment/business registration, and copies of identity documents.
- If the EP application is successful, notification is sent via email; validity is up to 2 years or the duration of travel, whichever is shorter.
- If staying overseas longer, renew the EP at least 2 months before expiry via the OneNS web portal.
- If the EP has expired while overseas, apply immediately for a new EP; it can only be issued after all outstanding issues are resolved.
- Apply for a new EP if there is a change in place of study, course of study, or institution.
- Cancel the Exit Permit within 3 months of its start date if not leaving Singapore.
- Visit the CMPB website for more information on Exit Permit and bond.
