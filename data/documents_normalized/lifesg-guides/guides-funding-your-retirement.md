# CPF savings

## Overview
This guide covers how CPF savings, housing monetisation, and health insurance schemes can help fund basic needs and protect retirement funds after retirement.

## Support / Benefits
- CPF Life: monthly payouts starting between age 65 and 70, for as long as you live.
- Retirement Sum Scheme: monthly payouts starting between age 65 and 70, until you turn 90.
- Topping up your CPF Retirement Account: attractive interest rates, higher monthly payouts, and tax relief.
- HDB Lease Buyback Scheme: sell part of your flat's lease for higher CPF payouts and a cash bonus.
- Silver Housing Bonus: downsize your flat and receive a cash bonus of up to S$30,000.
- MediShield Life: helps pay for large hospital bills and selected treatments.
- ElderShield: monthly payouts for severe disabilities, for up to 6 years.
- CareShield Life: monthly payouts for severe disabilities, for as long as you live.
- CareShield Life and ElderShield supplements: upgrade coverage with plans from private insurers.
