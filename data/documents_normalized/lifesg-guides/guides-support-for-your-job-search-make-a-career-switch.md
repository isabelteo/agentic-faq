# Make A Career Switch

## Overview
This guide describes support for job seekers who want to switch industries or careers.

## Support / Benefits
- Support in finding opportunities to move into new jobs or sectors.
- Support with skills training and work attachments.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Explore different industries to find the next step in your career.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- considering switching to a completely different industry
- want work attachment or on-the-job training in a new field
- unsure how to transition my skills into a new sector
