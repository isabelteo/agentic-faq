# What you can get (as a Singapore Citizen or PR)

## Overview
This guide explains Singapore's national healthcare financing schemes for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents — MediShield Life, Integrated Shield Plans, MediSave, CareShield Life, and CHAS — and how self-employed persons and caregivers can manage healthcare costs.

## Eligibility
- MediShield Life: all Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents are automatically covered from birth or when attaining SC/PR status; no application needed.
- CareShield Life: those born in 1980 or later are automatically covered on 1 October 2020 or when they turn 30, whichever is later, regardless of pre-existing conditions. Those born in 1979 or earlier have optional participation and may apply (Singpass login required), except those born between 1970-1979 who were insured under ElderShield 400 without severe disability, who are automatically enrolled. The opt-out deadline was 31 December 2023. Foreigners who become SC/PR from 1 October 2020 onwards have compulsory participation unless they have severe disability.
- CHAS: since 1 November 2019, all Singaporean citizens are eligible to apply.
- Self-Employed Persons (SEPs) who are SC/PR and earn annual Net Trade Income (NTI) of more than $6,000 must contribute to MediSave.
- Flexi-MediSave: available to those aged 60 or older (or using a spouse's MediSave if the spouse is also 60+), for treatment at polyclinics, Specialist Outpatient Clinics, and participating CHAS GPs.
- Life Insurance Relief: requires CPF contributions to Medisave Account (mandatory and voluntary) in the previous year of less than $5,000, and having paid life insurance premiums in the previous year; cannot be claimed on accident/health insurance, ElderShield, CareShield Life, or Integrated Shield Plan premiums.

## Support / Benefits
- MediShield Life helps pay for large hospitalisation bills and selected costly outpatient treatments (e.g. dialysis, chemotherapy), sized based on subsidised bills in public hospitals (B2/C wards); unsubsidised/private treatment bills are covered at a smaller proportion.
- Integrated Shield Plan (IP): optional private insurance offering additional benefits on top of MediShield Life, paid via MediSave and cash; premiums rise sharply with age. Private insurance premiums could be around 5 times MediShield Life premiums for someone in their 70s/80s.
- MediSave: personal healthcare savings account; workers save 8%-10.5% of monthly salary depending on age. Can be used for self or approved dependants (spouse, children, parents, grandparents, siblings — grandparents/siblings must be SC/PR) for hospitalisation, day surgery, certain outpatient expenses, and health insurance premiums, up to MediSave Withdrawal Limits.
- Flexi-MediSave: withdraw up to $300 per year per patient for outpatient treatment for those aged 60+; covers consultation fees, medical services, drugs, and diagnostic/treatment tests (excludes dental).
- CareShield Life: lifetime coverage once premiums are fully paid (at age 67 or 10 years after joining, whichever later); government subsidies available depending on financial circumstances (coverage not lost if unable to pay); fully payable with MediSave (own or family's); worldwide coverage.
- CHAS: medical subsidies for outpatient care at medical and/or dental care at select GP clinics.
- Self-employed contributing to MediSave: up to 6% interest per annum; tax relief up to 37% of annual NTI or the CPF Annual Limit of $37,740, whichever is lower; may also be eligible for Workfare Income Supplement (WIS) payouts of up to $2,667 annually.
- Caregivers can top up family members' MediSave (with tax relief) up to the Basic Healthcare Sum, and access grants including the Caregivers Training Grant (CTG), Medical Fee Exemption Card (for loved ones in nursing homes), and Home Caregiving Grant (for loved ones with at least permanent moderate disability).

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Check personalised MediShield Life premiums via the CPF healthcare dashboard.
- Use the Health Insurance Planner to assess whether your health insurance aligns with your needs.
- Apply to join CareShield Life (if born 1979 or earlier and wish to opt in) via Singpass login.
- Apply for a CHAS card online; visit any participating CHAS clinic and present a valid CHAS/MG/PG card and NRIC (or student ID/birth certificate for those under 15); check CHAS subsidy balance via MyCHAS (Singpass) or call 1800-275-2427.
- Self-employed persons: use the Self-Employed MediSave Contribution Calculator to determine contribution amount; contribute via GIRO, my cpf Digital Services, mail, PayNow QR, or NETS/CashCard at SingPost; check contributions via the Self-employment dashboard (Singpass login).
- Use the MediSave/MediShield Life Calculator to estimate withdrawal/claim amounts.
- Use the self-assessment tool (SupportGoWhere) to check for caregiver grants and subsidies you may be eligible for.
