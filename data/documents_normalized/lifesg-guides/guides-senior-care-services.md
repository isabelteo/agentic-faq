# Care services

## Overview
This guide lists government-supported care services for seniors or those who need help with daily activities due to a health condition, spanning home care, centre-based care, stay-in care, and caregiver support.

## Eligibility
- Singapore citizens and permanent residents (PRs) who enrol in government-funded intermediate and long-term care services may be eligible for subsidies of up to 75%.
- Senior Activity Centre: free for those 60 years old and older who need help with activities of daily living and have little or no family support.
- Befriending service: free for seniors who are lonely, isolated, or bored.

## Support / Benefits
- **Care at home** (estimated costs before subsidies): Meals on wheels ($4.80–$6.96 per meal, delivered 7 days a week); Medical escort and transport ($62–$90 per round trip); Home medical ($170 per visit); Home nursing ($80 per visit); Home therapy — physio, occupational, speech ($116 per visit); Home personal care — showering, laundry, light housekeeping ($20 per hour); Hospice home care for life-limiting illnesses e.g. advanced dementia or cancer ($100–$150 per visit); Befriending service (free for eligible seniors).
- **Care in a centre** (estimated costs before subsidies): Day care — general $400–$1,500/month, dementia $900–$1,700/month; Senior Activity Centre (free for eligible seniors); Community rehabilitation centre — active rehab $36–$85/session, maintenance rehab $12–$70/session; Centre-based nursing ($23–$35 per visit).
- **Stay-in care**: Community hospital (short-term care after discharge from acute hospital); Nursing home ($2,000–$3,600/month); Senior group homes for independent seniors without family to care for them ($400–$700/month).
- **Caregiver support**: caregiver training courses, respite care (short-term help from day care centres if caregivers need a break), and caregiver support groups.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- See more information on long-term care subsidies via MOH.
- Related services (Singpass login may be required): check medical appointments, view outstanding bills at public healthcare institutions, refill prescribed medicine, and check MediSave withdrawal limits and MediShield Life claims.
