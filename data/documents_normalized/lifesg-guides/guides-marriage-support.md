# Getting married

## Overview
This guide covers what to know before submitting a marriage application in Singapore, practical tips for a healthy marriage, and advice from couples and experts.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Follow the civil marriage process (if at least 1 person is non-Muslim) or the Muslim marriage process, to get legally married.
- Search marriage records to check if someone has or had a civil marriage registered in Singapore.
- Find a solemniser if you intend to solemnise your civil marriage outside the ROM building.
- File your civil or Muslim marriage application (Singpass login may be required).
- Withdraw owner-occupier tax rates if you and your spouse are claiming on separately owned properties post-marriage.
- Access practical tips on building intimacy, strengthening communication, and marriage counselling for couples who need professional help.
