# Focus on recovery

## Overview
This guide helps heart attack survivors recover physically and emotionally after hospital discharge, and reduce the risk of future heart attacks.

## Support / Benefits
- Cardiac rehabilitation programme to improve fitness and strength safely.
- Guidance videos on coping after discharge and living with heart disease.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Follow healthy eating guidelines for heart health.
- Get active with regular exercise to improve fitness and muscle strength.
- Quit smoking to reduce the risk of another heart attack by 50%.
- Learn ways to overcome and relieve stress.
- Manage health numbers: reverse pre-diabetes, lower blood pressure, and use Screen for Life (SFL) for affordable, subsidised screening of diabetes, blood cholesterol, blood pressure and other conditions.
- Related e-services (Singpass login may be required): check medical appointments, view outstanding bills at public healthcare institutions, refill prescribed medicine, apply for CHAS card, check CHAS subsidy balance, locate a family service centre.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- just had a heart attack and don't know what recovery looks like
- worried about having another heart attack and want to reduce my risk
- looking for a program to help rebuild fitness safely after a heart attack
- trying to quit smoking after a health scare
- feeling stressed or anxious after a serious health event
