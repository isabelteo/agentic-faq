# Alternatives To Retrenchment

## Overview
This guide describes alternatives companies are encouraged to consider before retrenching workers.

## Eligibility
- Applies to companies considering options instead of retrenching employees.

## Support / Benefits
- **Redeployment**: the company could redeploy and assign the employee to new job roles within the organisation instead of retrenchment.
- **Shorter work weeks**: the company could ask the employee to work only a few days a week; this reduced duration should not exceed 3 days a week or last more than 3 months.
- **Temporary layoffs**: the company could ask the employee to take up to 50% of their earned annual leave; the company must pay at least 50% of the employee's gross salary, and the layoff period should not exceed 1 month, subject to review.
