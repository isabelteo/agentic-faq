# Find Job Opportunities

## Overview
This guide lists ways to search for job opportunities online and in person in Singapore.

## Eligibility
- Persons with disabilities (PWDs) can apply for employment support with SG Enable.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Explore job postings online with MyCareersFuture.
- Attend career fairs and networking events.
- Find SGUnited traineeship opportunities.
- Apply for SGUnited Mid-career Pathways.
- PWDs can apply for employment support with SG Enable.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- looking for job listings or career fairs to attend
- mid-career and want to know about SGUnited pathways
- have a disability and need employment support to find a job
- looking for a traineeship to gain work experience
