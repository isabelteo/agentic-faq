# Support For Lower Income Workers

## Overview
This guide describes Workfare financial help for lower-income workers and self-employed persons while finding a job.

## Eligibility
- Targeted at lower-income workers and self-employed persons with a gross monthly income of $2,300 and below.

## Support / Benefits
- If eligible, Workfare payouts can come in the form of: skills training allowance, CPF top-ups for retirement, and cash supplement for income.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- earning a low income and wondering what financial support I can get while job hunting
- self-employed with low income and want to know if I qualify for Workfare
- want to grow my CPF retirement savings through a low-income work support scheme
