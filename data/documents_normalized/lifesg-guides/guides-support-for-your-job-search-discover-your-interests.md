# Discover Your Interests

## Overview
This guide encourages job seekers to reflect on their skills and interests to find a job that matches them, since this can take research and reflection.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- not sure what kind of job or career suits my skills and interests
- feeling stuck and want to explore new career directions
