# Planning your flat purchase

## Overview
This guide covers how to plan and go through the process of buying an HDB flat, whether a new flat from HDB or a resale flat, including eligibility, financing, and the application steps.

## Eligibility
- Eligibility to buy an HDB flat depends on citizenship, age, income ceiling, and who you are buying the flat with.
- You (and everyone listed in your application) must not own other property overseas or locally, and must not have disposed of any property within the last 30 months.
- From 9 May 2023, you must have a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter before you can apply for a flat in HDB's sales launches or open booking, or obtain an Option to Purchase (OTP) from a resale seller and submit a resale application.
- For resale flats, a valid HFE Letter is required before obtaining an OTP and submitting a resale application; it checks eligibility for the flat, HDB housing loan, and CPF housing grant.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Work out your flat budget using the budget or payment plan calculator, and check eligibility for an HDB housing loan when applying for the HFE letter.
- Consider a housing loan from a Financial Institution (FI) regulated by MAS; you may request an In-Principle Approval (IPA) and Letter of Offer (LO) via the HDB Flat Portal.
- You can use CPF Ordinary Account (OA) savings to pay for your flat; use the Home Purchase Planner to see the impact on your retirement goal.
- Existing HDB flat owners buying another flat can consider the Contra Payment Facility to reduce cash outlay and loan amount, and collect keys while selling their existing flat.
- New flat: apply for an HFE letter, check upcoming HDB sales launches, and consider applying through HDB Priority Schemes (up to 2 schemes).
- New flat types include 2-room Flexi, 3-room, 4-room, and 5-room flats.
- New flat application steps: apply via Build-To-Order, Sale of Balance, or Open Booking on the HDB Flat Portal; check application status; if shortlisted, attend a flat selection appointment within 2 weeks of ballot results and pay a booking fee (ranging from $500 to $2,000 depending on flat type) via NETS.
- Sign the Agreement for Lease, bringing identity cards, Letter of Offer (if using an FI loan), latest CPF statements (if employed), and Power of Attorney documents if applicable. Downpayment is usually 10% of the flat price; consider the Staggered Downpayment Scheme to split it into 2 instalments.
- Collect keys when the project is ready, bringing identity cards, certificate of fire insurance (from HDB's appointed insurer FWD if taking an HDB loan), latest CPF statements, Power of Attorney if applicable, and a signed GIRO form if paying loan instalments by cash.
- Resale flat: register a Preliminary HFE Check to find out if you can buy a resale flat, which CPF housing grants you can apply for, and whether you can get an HDB housing loan.
- Evaluate flat options using past resale transactions, Ethnic Integration Policy quotas, Singapore/PR quotas, and resale price index statistics.
- Get an Option to Purchase (OTP): the seller grants the OTP with an agreed Option Fee (between $1 to $1,000); review during the 21-day Option Period; exercise the OTP to proceed, or let it expire to withdraw.
- Choose your mode of financing (HDB loan or FI loan), then submit the resale application after exercising the OTP — either party can submit first, but the other must submit within 7 calendar days.
- Endorse resale documents and pay resale fees online when invited by HDB.
- Check resale application status; the process takes about 8 weeks from HDB's acknowledgement to completion.
- At the final resale appointment, sign the mortgage document (if taking an HDB loan), acknowledge receipt of keys, agree on outstanding property tax payment arrangements, and indicate how you'll pay the first month's service/conservancy charges.

## Support / Benefits
- First-time buyers may receive an Enhanced CPF Housing Grant of up to $120,000 when buying a new flat from HDB.
- For resale flats, first-time buyers can get up to $230,000 in housing grants, comprising the CPF Housing Grant of up to $80,000, Enhanced CPF Housing Grant of up to $120,000, and Proximity Housing Grant of up to $30,000.
- Note: grant amounts must be returned to your CPF Ordinary Account with accrued interest when you sell the flat later; it is not a price discount/subsidy.
