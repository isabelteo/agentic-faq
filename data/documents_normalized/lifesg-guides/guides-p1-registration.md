# Registration phases

## Overview
This guide explains Primary 1 (P1) registration phases, balloting, and support for children with special educational needs, for parents registering a child for primary school in the year the child turns 6 (or 5 if born on 1 January).

## Eligibility
- Phase 1: for children with a sibling currently studying in the preferred school.
- Phase 2A: for those who are, or whose child's sibling is, a former student, meet other criteria, or child is in an affiliated kindergarten.
- Phase 2B: for volunteers at the preferred school, or those endorsed by an affiliated church or clan.
- Phase 2C: for citizens or PRs who have not secured a place in a primary school.
- Phase 2C Supplementary: for citizens or PRs who have not secured a place after Phase 2C.
- Phase 3: for international students, after all citizens and PRs have been allocated a place; must indicate interest before the phase begins.
- If a school has more registrants than vacancies, computerised balloting is conducted, with priority affected by distance from the school.
- Children who have applied to a special education (SPED) school do not need to participate in the P1 registration exercise.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Check when your child is eligible for Primary 1 registration, find primary schools within 2km of home, and check your child's registration phase (via MOE online tools).
- If unsure whether a mainstream school can support your child, get a professional assessment.
- Prepare your child for primary school: choose the right school, prepare and support your child for day 1, and help them adjust to school life (pocket money, friendships, routines).
