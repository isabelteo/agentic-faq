# Endorsing Msq

## Overview
This guide explains the Medical Screening Questionnaire (MSQ) process for NS pre-enlistees, and the role of the parent or guardian nominated to endorse it.

## Eligibility
- The pre-enlistee must nominate one parent or guardian who knows his medical history well to endorse the MSQ.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- The pre-enlistee provides medical information (medical history, childhood illness) on the MSQ.
- The nominated endorser receives an SMS and email from MINDEF requesting endorsement.
- Log in to the OneNS web portal (Singpass required) to view the MSQ.
- Review the MSQ, verify family medical history, drug allergies, and other information is correct and complete, and provide comments or additional information if needed.
- Endorse the MSQ before the pre-enlistee's medical screening appointment at the Central Manpower Base (CMPB).
- If the MSQ is not endorsed before the medical appointment, the medical fitness assessment may be delayed, which could delay enlistment and subsequent university matriculation after NS.
- Visit the CMPB website for more information on medical screening and the MSQ.
