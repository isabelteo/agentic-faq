# Look after yourself

## Overview
This guide helps caregivers manage stress and burnout, emphasizing self-care and how to seek support.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Practice self-care and recognise signs of caregiver stress and burnout.
- Build a support team by involving family members and connecting with other caregivers in support groups.
- Contact Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) Link for personalised advice on care services and support schemes.
- Learn about MOH's Caregiver Support Action Plan framework.
- Consider the Eldercarer Foreign Domestic Worker Scheme to hire a domestic helper trained in eldercare.
- Find suitable senior community care services to support caregiving duties.
- Access community mental health resources if caring for a loved one with a mental health condition.
- Use related e-services: view outstanding bills at public healthcare institutions, refill prescribed medicine, check Medisave withdrawal limits and MediShield Life claims, and check medical appointments (Singpass login may be required).

## Related Issues & Concerns
- feeling burnt out and overwhelmed from caring for a family member
- looking for support groups or advice from other caregivers
- want to hire a domestic helper trained specifically in eldercare
- caring for a loved one with a mental health condition and need resources
- unsure who to talk to for personalised advice on caregiving support schemes
