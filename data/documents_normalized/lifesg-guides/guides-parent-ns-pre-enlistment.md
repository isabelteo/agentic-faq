# Ns Pre Enlistment

## Overview
This guide gives parents an overview of the National Service (NS) pre-enlistment journey, from age 13 notices through registration, deferment, medical screening, and enlistment day.

## Eligibility
- From age 13, sons/wards receive NS notices at the mailing address registered with ICA; failure to comply is an offence under the Enlistment Act 1970, punishable by a fine of up to $10,000, jail of up to 3 years, or both.
- At age 13, an Exit Permit (EP) notice is issued; an EP (and possibly a bond) is required if overseas for 3 months or longer.
- At age 16.5, registration for NS is required on the OneNS web portal; deferment for full-time pre-university studies may be applied for at this point.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Register for NS via the OneNS web portal upon turning 16.5 years old, following the NS Registration Notice.
- Apply for deferment for full-time pre-university studies at government-funded institutions (GCE 'O'/'N'/'A' Level, polytechnic diploma, Higher NITEC, NITEC) or selected private institution courses; not granted for university degree courses.
- Attend pre-enlistment medical screening to determine medical fitness (PES status if enlisting before Oct 2027, or the refreshed Medical Classification System if enlisting from Oct 2027 onwards).
- Consider the pre-enlistee IPPT: attaining 61 points or more may qualify for an 8-week reduction in full-time NS duration (details vary by enlistment date).
- Prepare for enlistment day, the start of full-time NS.
- Visit the CMPB website for further information on pre-enlistment, IPPT, and posting to uniformed services.
