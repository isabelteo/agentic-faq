# Enlistment Day

## Overview
This guide helps parents prepare for their son or ward's National Service (NS) enlistment day, including what to expect and how to support him through the transition.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Read the invitation sent to you for details on dress code, programme, and items to bring.
- The number of accompanying guests differs based on the service and unit; details are in the invitation.
- All guests must wear smart casual attire for the oath-taking ceremony; singlets, bermuda shorts, shorts, slippers, and sandals are not allowed.
- The enlistment day programme includes a guided tour of bunks and training grounds, a briefing from the Commanding Officer, witnessing the oath of allegiance, and tasting cook house food.
- Basic training centre depends on the uniformed service: SAF at Basic Military Training Centre (Pulau Tekong) or other SAF units; SCDF at the National Service Training Centre (Civil Defence Academy); SPF at the Home Team Academy.
- If your son or ward is unwell on enlistment day, he must call CMPB at 6373 3127 (only active on enlistment day), email his medical certificate to contact@ns.gov.sg by 12:00pm the same day, and provide the reason for medical leave; he may need to report to the Medical Classification Centre at CMPB (91 Hillview Link) for a medical review.
- Visit the CMPB website for more information on enlistment day.
