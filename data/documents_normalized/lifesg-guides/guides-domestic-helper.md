# Eligibility and guidelines

## Overview
This guide covers requirements and guidelines for employing a live-in migrant domestic worker (MDW), including eligibility, employer responsibilities, and levy concessions.

## Eligibility
- Employers must be at least 21 years old, not bankrupt, and mentally able.
- The MDW must meet age, nationality, and education requirements to obtain a Work Permit.
- Levy concession is available for employers living with a child below 16, a senior aged 67 or above, or a person with disabilities.
- Levy waiver is available if the helper is on overseas leave for at least 7 consecutive days, or on hospitalisation leave in Singapore.
- Tax relief on the foreign domestic worker levy is available for working married, divorced, or widowed mothers with school-going children.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- First-timers should attend the Employers' Orientation Programme (3 hours), and helpers new to working in Singapore should attend the Settling-in Programme (1 day).
- Apply for a Work Permit on your own or through an employment agency; low-income employers or senior citizens not staying with working adults may apply under the joint income or sponsorship scheme.
- Buy a $5,000 security bond for each non-Malaysian helper employed.
- Buy medical and personal accident insurance meeting minimum coverage requirements.
- Send the helper for a pre-employment medical examination before the Work Permit can be issued.
- Get the Work Permit issued within 2 weeks of the helper's arrival, or 7 days from Stay-Home Notice completion.
- The helper is entitled to 1 weekly rest day and proper living conditions; salary must be paid every month, within 7 days after the end of the salary period.
- Pay the monthly levy via GIRO during the employment period, and send the helper for a medical examination every 6 months.
- Cancel the Work Permit within 1 week after the helper stops working for you, or 1 day after the permit expires.
- Related e-services (Singpass login may be required): apply/manage Work Permit for migrant domestic workers, apply for levy concession (young child/elderly person, or persons with disabilities), and apply for levy waiver.
