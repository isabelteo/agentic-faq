# Learning Opportunities Ns

## Overview
This guide describes learning and career opportunities available to National Servicemen (NSFs) during their full-time NS, including SkillsFuture@NS initiatives and MINDEF-SAF Work-Learn Schemes.

## Eligibility
- SkillsFuture@NS (SF@NS) initiatives are available towards the last few months of full-time NS.
- MINDEF-SAF Work-Learn Schemes (WLS) require serving up to 4 years with SAF (combining full-time NS and Regular service, with a Regular service salary after the full-time NS component), across 8 available schemes in the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Digital and Intelligence Service.
- MINDEF/SAF Schemes with Early Enlistment (for final-year polytechnic diploma students) allow enlistment 1 year earlier than schoolmates, with military service recognised towards the polytechnic's final-year internship requirement; involves up to 4 years of combined full-time NS and regular service.
- SAF-Polytechnic Sponsorship for Integrated Curriculum (SPS-IC) requires 5 years of full regular service, across 3 programmes in Navy, Air Force, and Army.

## Support / Benefits
- SkillsFuture@NS Learning eXperience Platform (SF@NS LXP): unlimited access to over 75,000 online courses.
- SkillsFuture@NS Fairs (last 3-6 months of NS): information on career/education opportunities and access to prospective employers and education institutes.
- Employability Resilience Workshops: practical career skills such as resume writing, networking, and workplace communication.
- Work-Learn Schemes: obtain a MINDEF-sponsored diploma or partial university credits while undergoing specialised on-the-job training (options include Medic Specialists, Army Technicians, Supply Supervisors, NSF Trainers, Naval Warfare System Specialist, Air Force Technicians, Cyber Specialists, Digital Specialists).
- Early Enlistment Work-Learn Schemes and SPS-IC: diploma sponsorship combined with early enlistment or full regular service.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the OneNS web portal for more information on SkillsFuture@NS initiatives.
- For Army Work-Learn Schemes, complete the Army Recruitment Centre (ARC) Application Form, or contact ARC at 6040 4700 or arcwospe@defence.gov.sg.
- For Navy schemes, complete the Navy Recruitment Centre (NRC) Work Learn Scheme Interest Form, or contact navycareers@defence.gov.sg.
- For Air Force schemes, complete the Air Force Recruitment Centre (AFRC) Work Study Diploma Application Form.
- For Digital and Intelligence Service schemes, complete the DIRC Work Learn Scheme Application Form (only available within the application window), or contact disworklearn@defence.gov.sg.
- For SPS-IC programmes, contact the respective recruitment centre (ARC, NRC, or AFRC).
