# Get to know yourself

## Overview
This guide helps students and young adults explore their personality, interests, and options to plan their education or career path.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Use Know Yourself Profiling Tools (for secondary school and pre-university students) to discover personality traits relevant to education/career planning.
- Use RIASEC Profiling Tools (for adults or polytechnic/ITE students) to discover career interests, skills confidence, and work values.
- Explore the Singapore industry landscape for facts, figures, and available roles by industry.
- Explore the Singapore education landscape for paths after secondary school.
- Use SchoolFinder to explore schools by distance, location, CCAs, and subjects/programmes.
- Use CourseFinder to explore courses by aggregate type, score, institute type, and area of interest.
- Follow OMW! Network Connects (a Telegram channel) for bite-sized education and career resources.
- Search and apply for internships with public agencies via Careers@Gov.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- not sure what course of study or career suits my personality
- looking for tools to explore career interests as a polytechnic or ITE student
- want to find schools or courses that match my interests
- looking for internship opportunities with government agencies
