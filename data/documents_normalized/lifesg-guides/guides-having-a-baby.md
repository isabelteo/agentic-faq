# Financial support

## Overview
This guide covers financial support schemes that help lighten the costs of having and raising children, as well as leave entitlements for working parents.

## Eligibility
- Maternity leave: up to 16 weeks of paid leave for working mothers, including self-employed.
- Maternity benefit: for working mothers who do not qualify for paid maternity leave due to their employment arrangements.
- Adoption leave: up to 12 weeks of paid leave for working mothers who adopt a child.
- Adoption benefit: for working mothers who do not qualify for adoption leave due to their employment arrangements.
- Paternity leave: up to 4 weeks of paid leave for working fathers, including self-employed and adoptive fathers.
- Paternity benefit: for working fathers who do not qualify for paid paternity leave due to their employment arrangements.
- Shared Parental Leave (from 1 April 2025): 6 weeks of additional paid parental leave, shared between both parents.
- Shared Parental Benefit (from 1 April 2025): for working parents who do not qualify for paid shared parental leave.
- Childcare leave: up to 6 days of paid leave for those with children below 7 years old.
- Extended childcare leave: 2 days of paid leave for those with children between 7 and 12 years old.
- Unpaid infant care leave: up to 12 days of unpaid leave for those with children below 2 years old.
- Child LifeSG Credits (CLC): $500 credits for all families with Singapore Citizen children aged 0-12.
- Large Families Scheme: administered by MSF, to strengthen support for Singaporeans with three or more children.

## Support / Benefits
- Medisave Maternity Package: use CPF MediSave to pay for pre-delivery and delivery expenses.
- Baby Bonus Cash Gift: a cash gift given out every 6 months until the child turns 6.5 years old.
- Baby Bonus Child Development Account: special savings account with a one-off grant and government matching.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for birth registration and Baby Bonus (Singpass login may be required).
- View Baby Bonus application status and statement online.
- Call 1800 111 2222 (say "Baby Bonus") Monday to Friday 8:30am-6pm, Saturday 8:30am-1pm (closed public holidays) for Baby Bonus enquiries.
- Call 1800 111 2222 (say "GPLS") for enquiries on leave entitlements for working parents.
