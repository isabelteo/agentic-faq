# Ippt Ns Duration

## Overview
This guide explains the pre-enlistee Individual Physical Proficiency Test (IPPT) and how medical fitness and IPPT scores can affect the duration of full-time National Service (NS) and basic training.

## Eligibility
- A pre-enlistee may be eligible for an 8-week reduction in full-time NS duration based on medical fitness and attaining 61 points or more (minimum 1 point per station) in the pre-enlistee IPPT.
- Enlisting before Oct 2027: only those medically graded PES A/B1 can take the pre-enlistee IPPT.
- Enlisting from Oct 2027 onwards: those graded under the refreshed Medical Classification System (MCS) who are eligible will be informed via OneNS to book their IPPT.
- The 61-point result must be attained at least 14 calendar days before the Physical Training Phase (PTP) enlistment date to qualify for direct basic training and enlist 8 weeks later.
- The pre-enlistee IPPT can be attempted as many times as desired up to the last Wednesday at least 14 calendar days before the enlistment date; results are valid for 1 year.
- IPPT score is based on age, push-ups and sit-ups done in 1 minute, and 2.4km run timing.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Pre-enlistees in Junior College, Millennia Institute, Polytechnic, or ITE typically take the pre-enlistee IPPT in school.
- Others (or those wanting a re-test) can book an appointment at the CMPB Fitness Conditioning Centre (FCC), open Wednesdays except public holidays, subject to availability.
- Visit the CMPB website to calculate pre-enlistee IPPT score and BMI, and learn more about PES and the refreshed MCS.
