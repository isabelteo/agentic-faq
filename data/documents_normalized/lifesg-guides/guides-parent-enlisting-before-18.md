# Enlisting Before 18

## Overview
This guide explains the Voluntary Early Enlistment Scheme (VEES), which allows a son or ward to enlist for National Service before turning 18, with parental consent.

## Eligibility
- Must be at least 16.5 years old.
- Must be assessed as medically and physically fit for NS.
- Must submit the VEES application form, including a short write-up on why he wants to apply, plus parental or guardian consent.
- Each VEES application is assessed and approved on a case-by-case basis.
- If enlisted through VEES, he will not be able to disrupt from full-time NS to pursue studies up to GCE 'A' Level, polytechnic diploma, or equivalent pre-university qualifications; he must complete full-time NS before pursuing such studies.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Complete the online VEES application form.
- Email the signed and completed form to contact@ns.gov.sg.
- Receive a notice in the mail to begin registration, pre-enlistment documentation, and/or schedule a medical screening.
- Attend a medical screening and be assigned a medical fitness status.
- Successful applicants are scheduled for enlistment in the next available intake, subject to training capacity.
- Visit the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) website for more information on VEES.
