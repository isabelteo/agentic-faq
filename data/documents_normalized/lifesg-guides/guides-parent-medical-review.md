# Medical Review

## Overview
This guide explains the medical review process for pre-enlistees assigned PES D or "Pending Medical Review", where more medical information or specialist appointments are needed before medical fitness can be determined.

## Eligibility
- Applies to pre-enlistees assigned PES D or "Pending Medical Review" under the refreshed Medical Classification System.
- Attending scheduled medical appointments is compulsory; not complying with the Further Reporting Order (FRO) is an offence under the Enlistment Act 1970, punishable by a fine of up to $10,000, jail of up to 3 years, or both.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Review the FRO (sent by MINDEF, also on the OneNS web portal) with your son or ward to confirm deadlines and appointment information; check if you need to be present at specialist appointments.
- Ensure your son or ward brings required documents, such as a printed Letter of Identity (LOI), to further medical review appointments.
- Upload the Progress Report (PR) on Medical Status on the OneNS web portal after appointments at public hospitals, if instructed in the FRO.
- Depending on the medical condition, the review may involve appointments at NHCS, NUH/Raffles Hospital/SGH, other public hospitals, the Medical Classification Centre (MCC) at CMPB, or submission of additional medical documents.
- To reschedule an appointment at select hospitals/centres, fill in the reschedule form with 2 preferred dates and supporting documents; for other listed hospitals, call or email directly to reschedule, then update details on the OneNS web portal and bring the new FRO and LOI to the appointment.
- The Letter of Identity (LOI) waives consultation and investigative fees up to diagnosis at public hospitals (not treatment/medication costs); a new LOI may be needed for subsequent appointments or changed appointment dates.
- The Progress Report (PR) must be completed by the attending doctor after each appointment and submitted via the OneNS web portal if instructed; keep all forms and a hard copy of the PR for at least 6 months.
- To request a medical review for a changed medical condition, submit a doctor's memo to the MCC via the OneNS web portal.
- If PES BP or Medically Fit for Service (Obese) and substantial weight has been lost, visit MCC at CMPB on weekdays from 2pm to 4pm for reassessment.
- For other reasons affecting medical fitness (e.g. LASIK), upload supporting documents on the OneNS web portal or email contact@ns.gov.sg.
- After completing all reviews, check medical fitness results and exemptions on the OneNS web portal after about two months.
