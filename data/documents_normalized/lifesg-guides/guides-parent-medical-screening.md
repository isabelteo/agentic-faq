# Medical Screening

## Overview
This guide explains the pre-enlistment medical screening process, which determines a National Service (NS) pre-enlistee's medical fitness, exemptions, basic training, and vocation assignment.

## Eligibility
- Applies to pre-enlistees registering for NS via the OneNS web portal after receiving a letter from MINDEF.
- Attendance at the medical screening appointment at the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) is compulsory; failure to attend may result in a fine of up to $10,000, jail of up to 3 years, or both.
- Parents and guardians are not required to attend the medical screening appointment.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- From 23 June 2025, CMPB has permanently moved to 91 Hillview Link, S669723.
- Book the medical screening appointment via the OneNS web portal; appointments can only be rescheduled up to 3 days before the appointment.
- The screening takes about 5 hours and includes: photo studio (ID photo), e-fitting (uniform/gear measurements), medical screening (about 2.5 hours across 6 stations: Clinical Laboratory, Dental, Ear/Nose/Throat, Eye, X-ray, Clinical Examination), and a psychometric test (about 1.5 hours, ungraded, assessing reasoning, technical, and mental-spatial skills).
- Provide detailed medical information, including medical history and childhood illness, on the Medical Screening Questionnaire (MSQ); nominate one parent/guardian to endorse the MSQ.
- If further investigation is needed, the pre-enlistee will be assigned PES D / "Pending Medical Review" and undergo a medical review; MINDEF pays for consultation and investigative fees only up to diagnosis (not treatment/medication after diagnosis).
- If no further review is needed, medical fitness results and exemptions can be checked on the OneNS web portal about a month after screening.
- Additional medical screening may be required later, e.g. if deferment for full-time studies was granted after the fitness status was assigned.
