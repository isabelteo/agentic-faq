# Managing Ns Studies

## Overview
This guide explains how National Service (NS)-liable males can manage their studies around NS through deferment (before enlistment) and disruption (after enlistment).

## Eligibility
- **Deferment for full-time pre-university studies at local schools**: available for GCE 'O' Level, 'N' Level and 'A' Level, Polytechnic diploma, International Baccalaureate (IB), NITEC or Higher NITEC, or an equivalent pre-university qualification; you must not have already attained such qualifications.
- **Deferment for privately-funded or overseas schools**: available for pre-university qualifications (e.g. IB Diploma), subject to a cut-off age — on 1 January of the year the course starts, you must be younger than 19 years old if currently a Secondary 4 student, or younger than 20 years old if currently a Secondary 5, NITEC, or Higher NITEC student.
- Deferment will not be granted for any university course, even if already started.
- **Disruption for university studies**: available if you enlisted for NS later than your peers in the same academic cohort, to start university at the same time as your peers.
- Disruption will not be granted for pre-university qualifications.
- Each deferment or disruption application is assessed on a case-by-case basis.
- Further deferment may be granted, case-by-case, if you failed your pre-university studies and want to repeat them full-time.
- Disruption is also available (without application, informed by unit) if accepted into medical school at NUS or NTU, or awarded a Public Service Commission (PSC) scholarship.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for deferment when registering for NS (from 16.5 years old), or via the "Apply for deferment" eService on the OneNS web portal if already registered.
- May need to provide: official letter from the school (with school logo/stamp) certifying enrolment, course of study, and course commencement/completion dates.
- To cancel deferment (if no longer studying and starting the pre-enlistment process), contact the NS Call Centre at 1800 367 6767 or contact@ns.gov.sg.
- Apply for disruption via the "Manage Disruption" eService on the OneNS web portal (for those enlisted into the SAF), at least 3 months before the date of disruption.
- If enlisted into the Home Team, submit the disruption application to the Human Resource Services Centre of the Ministry of Home Affairs via MHA_HRSC-NSSB@mha.gov.sg.
- Local autonomous university applications (NUS, NTU, SMU, SUSS, SUTD, SIT) can be made before or during NS; the university may reserve a place until NS is completed.
- Overseas university applicants should check directly whether the university can reserve a place until NS is completed.
- Check your Operationally Ready Date (ORD) or disruption eligibility before confirming a matriculation date.
- Visit the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) website for more information on disruption for further studies.

## Support / Benefits
- Time is set aside in NSFs' training schedules to attend local university admission and scholarship interviews and tests during allocated periods or weekends; time-off/leave can be requested for interviews/tests outside these periods.
- Remaining full-time NS liability (after disruption) can be completed during school holidays or after graduating from university, but must be completed in 1 continuous period.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about how to continue studying while still owing NS
- wanting to delay enlistment to finish A Levels or a diploma
- trying to figure out if I can defer NS for a university course
- planning to start university at the same time as my peers despite enlisting later
- unsure how to apply for deferment or disruption through OneNS
- accepted into medical school or awarded a scholarship and need to know about disruption
