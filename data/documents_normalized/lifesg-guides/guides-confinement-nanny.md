# Eligibility and requirements

## Overview
This guide covers what employers must know and do before hiring a confinement nanny to help care for a newborn.

## Eligibility
- You can hire a confinement nanny from Malaysia if you have a newborn under 4 months old.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for the Work Permit from 4 weeks before your estimated date of delivery.
- Extend the nanny's visit pass if it expires before the end of her employment period.
- Pay the monthly levy; the amount depends on whether your newborn is a Singapore citizen.
- Cancel the Work Permit if the nanny is not working for you.
- Related e-services (Singpass login may be required): apply or manage Work Permit for migrant domestic workers, request extension of a confinement nanny's visit pass, and check and pay foreign worker levy.

## Related Issues & Concerns

- need to know the levy amount for a confinement nanny
- confinement nanny's visit pass is expiring and need an extension
