# Life In Ns

## Overview
This guide describes what to expect during a National Serviceman's (NSF) basic training, including stay-in training, booking out, training duration, living arrangements, and meals.

## Eligibility
- Basic training length and type depend on the enlisted service (SAF, SCDF, or SPF), medical fitness status, and pre-enlistee IPPT results (if applicable).
- During stay-in training (initial phase), NSFs must stay in camp for between 10 to 19 days depending on the service, then may book out on weekends/public holidays without scheduled training.

## Support / Benefits
- SAF: Basic Military Training (BMT) duration ranges from 9 weeks (PES A/B1 with 61+ IPPT points, PES B2/B3/B4/C/E) up to 17 weeks (PES A/B1 with under 61 points; includes 8-week Physical Training Phase) or 19 weeks (PES BP). Locations include BMTC Pulau Tekong or BMTC School V in Kranji Camp 2, depending on grading. Under the refreshed Medical Classification System (enlisting Oct 2027 onwards), BMT Programmes 1-3 range from 9 to 19 weeks depending on medical exemptions and IPPT score.
- SCDF: Basic Rescue Training (BRT) duration ranges from 5 days (PES E) to 4 weeks (PES A/B1 with 61+ points, B2, B3/B4, C) up to 12 weeks (PES A/B1 under 61 points, or PES BP; includes 8-week Physical Training Phase). Held at the National Service Training Centre, Civil Defence Academy.
- SPF: Police Officers Basic Course (POBC) duration ranges from 10 days (PES E) to 8 weeks (PES B3/B4, C) up to 14 weeks (PES A/B1 with 61+ points, B2) or 22 weeks (PES A/B1 under 61 points, or PES BP; includes 8-week Physical Training Phase). Held at the Home Team Academy.
- NSFs are given a full list of issued items (uniform, clothes, shoes, training necessities) specific to their service.
- Living arrangements: NSFs live in a common bunk with enlistees of different backgrounds and are jointly responsible for cleanliness.
- Meals: 3 meals a day are provided, plus a night snack if there is night training; dietary restrictions or allergies should be reported to officers upon enlistment.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- After basic training, NSFs are posted to their SAF, SCDF, or SPF unit to complete the rest of their training and full-time NS, and may undergo vocation training or be selected for leadership training.
- Visit the CMPB website for more details on life in NS in the SAF, SCDF, and SPF.
