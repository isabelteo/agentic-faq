# Living expenses

## Overview
This guide covers interim financial assistance for those experiencing sudden loss of income, and support for housing instalments, school fees, and digital access needs.

## Support / Benefits
- ComCare Interim Assistance: cash, vouchers, or food rations for low-income households needing urgent financial assistance. Call 1800 222 0000 (daily, 7am to midnight).
- ComCare Short to Medium Term Assistance: support for low-income households who temporarily need help with basic living expenses. Call 1800 222 0000 (daily, 7am to midnight).
- CapitaLand-U Care Resilience & Enablement (CAP+Ure) Fund: NTUC Fairprice vouchers for union members to help with children's needs during unforeseen circumstances.
- HDB financial assistance measures: for owners in financial hardship, to defer or reduce monthly loan instalments for 6 months.
- Renting out bedrooms in a flat (3-room or bigger) for additional income.
- Kindergarten Fee Assistance Scheme (KiFAS): grants for families with children in MOE kindergartens or preschool anchor operators.
- School Pocket Money Fund: monthly allowance for students at most levels of education, except universities.
- Financial assistance for government/government-aided schools, independent schools, and post-secondary education institutions (ITE, polytechnics, arts institutions, autonomous universities).
- DigitalAccess@Home: subsidised broadband and laptops/tablets for lower-income households.
- Mobile Access for Seniors: subsidised smartphone and mobile plan for lower-income seniors.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Learn healthy money habits, including budgeting, via MoneySense.
- Apply to rent out a spare bedroom in your HDB flat (Singpass login may be required).
- Check eligibility for subsidised internet access at home (Singpass login may be required).

- need financial help paying for my child's school fees
