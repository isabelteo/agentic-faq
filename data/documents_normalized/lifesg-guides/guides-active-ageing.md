# Health screening

## Overview
This guide helps seniors in Singapore stay active, healthy and socially connected, covering health screening, vaccination and community programmes for the elderly.

## Support / Benefits
- Screen for Life (SFL): regular health screening at subsidised rates.
- Project Silver Screen: check-up for vision, hearing and oral health.
- COVID-19 vaccination to reduce chances of infection and severe illness.
- Recommended vaccination based on age group and gender.
- AIC Wellness Programme: activity resources for well and frail seniors.
- Active ageing programmes: free programmes near home.
- National Silver Academy (NSA) Courses: short courses and online learning for seniors aged 50 and above.
- Seniors Go Digital: digital skills for messaging, e-services and payments.
- Be a Silver Generation Ambassador: volunteer opportunity.
