# Education Before After Ns

## Overview
This guide explains deferment (delaying NS enlistment for pre-university studies) and disruption (temporarily pausing NS to start university), for parents of sons or wards liable for National Service (NS).

## Eligibility
- Deferment: for a son/ward who needs to study for a pre-university qualification before enlistment; not granted for any university course, even if already started.
- Deferment for full-time pre-university studies at local schools is granted for GCE 'O' Level, 'N' Level, 'A' Level, Polytechnic diploma, International Baccalaureate (IB), NITEC or Higher NITEC, or an equivalent qualification, provided the qualification has not already been attained.
- Deferment for privately-funded or overseas schools has a cut-off age: on 1 January of the year the pre-university course starts, the son/ward should be younger than 19 (if currently Secondary 4) or 20 (if currently Secondary 5, NITEC, or Higher NITEC).
- Further deferment may be granted case-by-case if the son/ward failed pre-university studies and wants to repeat full-time.
- Disruption: for an NSF who enlisted later than his peers, to start university at the same time as his cohort; not granted for pre-university qualifications.
- NSFs accepted into NUS/NTU medical school or awarded a Public Service Commission (PSC) scholarship may be eligible for disruption without application (informed by their units).
- Each deferment or disruption application is assessed case-by-case.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for deferment when registering for NS (from 16.5 years old) or via the "Apply for deferment" eService on the OneNS web portal if already registered. May need to provide an official school enrolment letter, course of study, and course dates.
- To cancel deferment, contact the NS Call Centre at 1800 367 6767 or contact@ns.gov.sg.
- Can apply for university admission before enlisting; local autonomous universities (NUS, NTU, SMU, SUSS, SUTD, SIT) may reserve a place until NS completion; overseas universities should be checked individually.
- Time is set aside in NSFs' training schedules for local university admission/scholarship interviews and tests; requests outside these periods require time-off or leave.
- Apply for disruption via the "Manage Disruption" eService on the OneNS web portal (for SAF) or via email to MHA_HRSC-NSSB@mha.gov.sg (for Home Team), at least 3 months before the disruption date.
- Visit the Central Manpower Base (CMPB) website for more information on disruption for further studies.
