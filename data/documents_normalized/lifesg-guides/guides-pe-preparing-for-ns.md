# Things to note

## Overview
This is a hub page linking to guides that help male Singapore citizens or permanent residents prepare for National Service (NS), covering pre-enlistment, obligations, medical matters, and preparation resources.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Related guides linked from this hub: Pre-enlistment, Managing NS and your studies, Vocations in NS, Managing your NS Pre-enlistment Journey on LifeSG, Why serve NS?, Your NS obligations, Exit Permit (EP) and bond for pre-enlistees, Medical screening for pre-enlistees, Medical reviews, Pre-enlistee IPPT and NS duration, Enlisting before 18 years old (Voluntary Early Enlistment Scheme, VEES, with parent's consent), Learning opportunities during NS, Enlistment day, Psychological and physical preparation for NS, Life in NS.
