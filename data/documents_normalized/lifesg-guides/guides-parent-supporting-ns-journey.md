# Things to note

## Overview
This guide is an index for parents on how to support their son or ward through the National Service (NS) journey, covering pre-enlistment, education, health and well-being, and life after enlistment.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Learn about pre-enlistment, what to expect after receiving the Exit Permit letter.
- Endorse the Medical Screening Questionnaire (MSQ) as the nominated parent or guardian.
- Track the NS pre-enlistment journey via LifeSG.
- Review Exit Permit (EP) and bond requirements for pre-enlistees 13 years or older staying overseas 3 months or longer.
- Consider the Voluntary Early Enlistment Scheme (VEES) for enlisting before 18, with parental consent.
- Explore education avenues before and after NS, and learning opportunities during NS.
- Understand the medical screening and medical review process, and pre-enlistee IPPT for potential NS duration reduction.
- Prepare for enlistment day, life in NS (basic training), safety measures during NS, and well-being support during NS.
- Endorse the Medical Screening Questionnaire online (Singpass login may be required).
