# Help In Finding A New Job

## Overview
This guide points retrenched individuals to organisations that help with finding new jobs, training, resumes/interviews, and information on schemes and benefits.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Get help from unions and agencies to find new job opportunities, explore training courses, prepare resumes/CVs/interviews, and find information on schemes and benefits.
- If recently retrenched, get career advice from Career Grit at WSG and mental wellness support from mindline.sg (mindline.sg maintains anonymity and does not collect any Personally Identifiable Information).
- Contact Workforce Singapore (WSG): 6883 5885.
- Contact Employment and Employability Institute (e2i): 6474 0606.
- Contact U PME Centre (NTUC): 6213 8008.
- Read more about support for your job search.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- just got retrenched and don't know where to start looking for a new job
- need help updating my resume and preparing for interviews after retrenchment
- feeling stressed or anxious after losing my job and want mental wellness support
- want to know which organisations can help me find schemes and benefits after retrenchment
