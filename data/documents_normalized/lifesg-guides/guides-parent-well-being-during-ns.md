# Well Being During Ns

## Overview
This guide helps parents support their son or ward's mental health and well-being during National Service (NS), especially during the adjustment period of basic training.

## Support / Benefits
- Buddy system: peer support paired during basic training.
- Regular check-ins by commanders on coping, mentally and emotionally.
- Training in self-awareness and recognising physical/psychological signs of stress.
- On-site Care Hub: Orientation Officers, counsellors, psychologists, and Medical Officers available for confidential support.
- Financial assistance for NSFs facing family financial difficulties: SAF (via unit manpower officer/S1, interview session, or MINDEF Shared Services Personnel Services Centre at 6373 1140 or 6373 1155), SCDF (via Platoon Commander to HR Department), SPF (via course manager or Divisional Manpower Officer).
- Preventive drug education resources for parents to support a drug-free lifestyle; SAF, SCDF, SPF have zero tolerance for drug abuse, vaping, and drink-driving.
- 24-hour counselling helplines: SAF 1800 278 0022; SCDF 1800 286 6666; SPF 1800 255 1151. Singapore Anti-Narcotics Association (SANA): +65 6732 1122 / Sana@sana.org.sg (Monday to Friday, 6:00pm-9:30pm).

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Engage your son or ward, ask about his NS experience, encourage him to share feelings, and lend a listening ear.
- Be aware of common adjustment issues (homesickness, difficulty with communal living, adjusting to a regimental environment) and offer reassurance rather than dismissing his concerns.
- Encourage healthy stress management: physical activity, talking to others, avoiding compulsive/addictive behaviours, and focusing on solutions.
- Speak with your son's commanders if you have concerns, or contact professional counsellors and helplines directly.
- Visit the CMPB website for more information on common issues and where to seek help.

## Related Issues & Concerns
- son is homesick and struggling to adjust during basic training
- worried about my son's mental health during NS and don't know who to contact
- family is facing financial difficulty and want to know if my NSF son can get help

- concerned about drug or vaping risks during my son's NS
