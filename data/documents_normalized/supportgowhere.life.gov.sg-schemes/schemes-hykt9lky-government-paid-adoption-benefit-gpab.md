# Government-Paid Adoption Benefit (GPAB)

## Overview
Provides up to 12 weeks of Government-paid income for eligible adoptive working mothers who do not qualify for Adoption Leave for Mothers (AL) due to their employment arrangements.

## Eligibility
- Adopting a child in accordance with any Singapore law relating to adoption of children
- You are not the natural mother of the child
- Child is below 12 months old on Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date
- FIA date on or after 1 January 2021
- If child is not a Singapore Citizen on FIA date, a Dependent's Pass has been issued and either you or your husband has been a Singapore Citizen since the Dependent's Pass issue date
- Employed or self-employed for at least 90 days in the 12 months before FIA date
- You do not qualify for the Adoption Leave (AL) scheme except under specific conditions such as end of employment contract or retrenchment

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Record employment contract dates or trade/business periods from the 12 months before your FIA date
- Submit your claim [online](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/eservices/submit-a-claim) within 15 months of your child's birth
- An officer will contact you post-submission

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 8 weeks of Government-paid income for the 1st and 2nd child, capped at $20,000
- Up to 12 weeks of Government-paid income for the 3rd and subsequent child, capped at $30,000

## Related Issues & Concerns
- adopting a child and unsure if I qualify for paid leave benefits
- self-employed and wondering if I can still get adoption income support
- worried about income loss while caring for a newly adopted child
- checking how much financial support is available for adopting a third child
