# Interim Disability Assistance Scheme for the Elderly (IDAPE)

## Overview
Provides monthly cash payouts for elderly Singapore Citizens with severe disabilities who are not eligible for ElderShield due to age or pre-existing disabilities.

## Eligibility
- Born on or before 30 September 1932, or born between 1 October 1932 and 30 September 1962 (inclusive) with pre-existing disabilities as of 30 September 2002
- Singapore Citizen living in Singapore
- Monthly household income per person of $3,600 or less (annual value of property under $21,000 for households with no income)
- Not a CareShield Life or ElderShield policyholder/supplement plan policyholder, or have made any claims before

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online at the [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)
- Apply via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in
- Apply at any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- Contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the application
- Processing time takes between 6 to 8 weeks

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly household income per person $0–$2,600: $250 payout
- Monthly household income per person $2,601–$3,600: $150 payout
- Payouts for up to 72 months
