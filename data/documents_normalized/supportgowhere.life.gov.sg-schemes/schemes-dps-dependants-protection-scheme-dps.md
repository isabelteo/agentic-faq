# Dependant's Protection Scheme (DPS)

## Overview
Term life insurance scheme, solely administered by Great Eastern Life, that provides basic financial protection for you and your family in the event of death, terminal illness or total permanent disability.

## Eligibility
- Automatic inclusion of DPS coverage for Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents between age 21 and 65 upon a valid CPF working contribution
- DPS claim payout is made to:
  - Next-of-kin/DPS nominee of a policyholder who is deceased, or
  - DPS policyholder certified by a registered medical practitioner as suffering from terminal illness (likely to result in death within 12 months) or total permanent disability (inability to take part in any employment permanently, or total permanent loss of physical function of both eyes, two limbs, or one eye and one limb)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- CPF members between age 16 and 65 not automatically covered can [apply to join DPS with Great Eastern Life directly](https://www.greateasternlife.com/content/dam/corp-site/great-eastern/sg/gels-ftrp-imc-cm/life-insurance-/dependants-protection-scheme/proposal-for-dps.pdf)
- Claim on death grounds: next-of-kin/DPS nominee can contact [Great Eastern Life](https://greateasternlife.com/DPS) directly for claim application forms and required documents
- Claim on medical grounds (DPS and either Home Protection Scheme or CPF withdrawal due to reduced life expectancy): submit application to CPF Board via the [CPF website](https://cpf.gov.sg/applyrleandclaimhpsdps); the claim will be forwarded to Great Eastern Life for concurrent assessment
- Claim on medical grounds (DPS only): contact [Great Eastern Life](https://greateasternlife.com/DPS) directly

## Support / Benefits
- Insured CPF Members up to age 60: maximum sum assured of up to $70,000
- Insured CPF Members above age 60 and up to age 65: maximum sum assured of up to $55,000
- Processing of claim application generally takes 7 working days upon Great Eastern Life's receipt of requested documents
