# Assistance Scheme for Second Timers (Divorced/ Widowed Parents) (ASSIST)

## Overview
ASSIST allocates up to 5% of flats for eligible divorced or widowed parents applying for 2-room Flexi and 3-room Build-To-Order (BTO) flats.

## Eligibility
- Divorced or widowed with at least one child (natural offspring from a legal marriage or legally adopted) aged 18 or below
- Adoptive parents must have the Adoption Order of their children when applying for a flat
- Must not have any interest in an HDB flat or private residential property after divorce/separation or spouse's demise, except for the matrimonial flat/property

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the HDB Flat Portal
- If you meet the eligibility criteria during your HFE letter application, you may apply for this scheme in your flat application

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 5% of 2-room Flexi and 3-room BTO flats in non-mature estates are allocated to eligible applicants

## Related Issues & Concerns
- recently divorced with children and need help getting a flat
- widowed parent looking to apply for a BTO flat with priority
- unsure whether owning the matrimonial flat affects eligibility for a new flat
- need to know how to get an HFE letter before applying for a flat
