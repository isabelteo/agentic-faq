# PAssion Card

## Overview
The PAssion Card is a membership programme run by People's Association that lets members enjoy discounts on courses and activities at Community Clubs and other PA outlets, plus exclusive discounts at more than 2,000 participating PAssion Merchant outlets.

## Eligibility
- Membership available for all aged 15 and above with a valid Singpass
- PAssion POSB Debit Card for those aged 16 and above with a POSB/DBS Savings or Current account
- PAssion Silver Concession Card for Singapore Citizens aged 60 and above

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- To sign up for a PAssion Card: visit any Community Club or online; 5-year membership fees are $12.00 for a Principal Card (ages 18-59) or $10.00 (below 18, or 60 and above)
- To sign up for a free PAssion e-Membership: visit onePA | Membership, sign in with Singpass; free for members of the public aged 15 and above
- To sign up for PAssion POSB Debit Card, visit www.posb.com.sg/PAssion
- To apply for PAssion Silver Concession Card, apply via the linked application or submit via SimplyGo Ticket Offices or SimplyGo App

## Support / Benefits
- Exclusive privileges for courses, activities, and programmes; unique promotions with lifestyle merchants and partners
- PAssion POSB Debit Card functions as an ATM, NETS, Debit MasterCard, and EZ-Link card, offering savings, rewards and privileges at over 2,000 places
- PAssion Silver Concession Card provides seniors with concessionary fares on public transport and merchant benefits
- Access to exclusive privileges on eCapitaVoucher and deals on the CapitaStar App
- Earn yuu Points from a wide-ranging coalition of brands including BreadTalk, CS Fresh, Cold Storage, Giant, Guardian Health & Beauty, 7 Eleven, Mandai.com, Toast Box and more, and through PAssion CARES events
- Free PAssion e-Membership for all aged 15 and above with a valid Singpass, with auto-conversion of expiring PAssion Card to e-Membership
