# Parenthood Tax Rebate (PTR)

## Overview
The Parenthood Tax Rebate (PTR) provides a one-off tax rebate of up to $20,000 per child to eligible parents to encourage them to have more children.

## Eligibility
- Singapore tax residents who are married, divorced or widowed in the relevant year
- Different qualifying conditions apply based on whether the child was born/adopted before or on/after 1 Jan 2008 (PTR is not available for the first child, nor from the fifth child onwards, for children born before 1 Jan 2008)
- Child must be a Singapore Citizen at the time of birth/marriage/legal adoption or within 12 months thereafter
- Additional conditions apply for children given for adoption, if the marriage is dissolved under a court order, or if a spouse passes away
- See IRAS's PTR page for full qualifying conditions; a PTR Eligibility Tool is available to check eligibility

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- e-Filing (first-time claim): log in with Singpass or Singpass Foreign user Account (SFA) at myTax Portal, go to "Individuals" > "File Income Tax Return" > "File" > "4. Deductions, Tax Reliefs and Rebates" > "Add New" > "Child/Parenthood Tax Rebate", fill in details and select "Yes" to indicate share of PTR agreed with spouse, then "Update"
- If claimed before, unutilised balance is carried forward automatically
- Paper filing (first-time claim): complete particulars of your child in Appendix 2 of Form B1 & B
- If claimed before via paper filing, unutilised balance carries forward without further action

## Support / Benefits
- Rebate amount increases per child: $5,000 for the first, $10,000 for the second, and $20,000 for all subsequent children
- PTR may be shared between parents based on an agreed apportionment
- Unutilised PTR carried forward to offset future income tax payable, and may be transferred online via myTax Portal between spouses
