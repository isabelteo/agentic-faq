# Change in HDB Flat Ownership After Flat Owner's Demise

## Overview
This process allows remaining family or single occupiers to retain an HDB flat following the demise of a joint owner or a sole owner/tenant-in-common, by taking over interest in the flat or registering the right to administer the deceased's estate.

## Eligibility
As the remaining family or single occupier, you can retain the existing flat provided you:
- Are a Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident who is at least 21 years old
- Satisfy the eligibility rules and conditions to own a flat

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- For demise of a joint owner: lodge a Notice of Death with the Singapore Land Authority (SLA) personally, via your own solicitor, or via HDB's services (bringing identity cards of remaining joint-owners, original death certificate/e-death certificate, title document if any, and paying registration/conveyancing fees)
- For demise of a sole owner/tenant-in-common with a will: obtain a Grant of Probate via a private solicitor to give the Executor legal authority to manage the estate
- For demise of a sole owner/tenant-in-common without a will: obtain a Grant of Letters of Administration via a private solicitor to give the Administrator legal authority to manage the estate
- Once the Grant is obtained, engage your own solicitors or HDB's solicitors to register your legal right as Administrator/Executor; approach the HDB Branch managing the flat with required documents (original Grant/Statement, original Syariah Court Inheritance Certificate for Muslim estates, death certificate copy, title document, identity cards)
- Sign relevant documents, pay fees (stamp fees, conveyancing fees, etc.), and apply to HDB to effect the ownership change (to eligible beneficiaries or via resale of part-share, subject to beneficiaries meeting eligibility conditions)
