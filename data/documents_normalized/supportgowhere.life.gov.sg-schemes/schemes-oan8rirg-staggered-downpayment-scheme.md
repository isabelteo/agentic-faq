# Staggered Downpayment Scheme

## Overview
The Staggered Downpayment Scheme eases home purchase by allowing eligible first-timer couples and right-sizing flat owners to pay their flat downpayment in 2 instalments instead of one lump sum.

## Eligibility
- First-timer couples, including those with 1 second-timer applicant
- Valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter obtained on or before the younger applicant's 30th birthday
- Booked an uncompleted 5-room or smaller flat in any HDB sales exercise
- Flat owners right-sizing to a 3-room or smaller flat in non-mature estates who have not completed the sale of their existing flat at the time of HFE letter application, and booked an uncompleted 3-room or smaller flat in a non-mature estate

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Buyers will be informed during the flat booking appointment if they are eligible

## Support / Benefits
- Downpayment split into 2 instalments for easier financial management
- First instalment paid at signing of the Agreement for Lease: 5% or 10% of purchase price (depending on housing loan choice)
- Second instalment paid at key collection: 20% or 35% of purchase price (depending on housing loan choice and Loan-To-Value limit)
- Can be paid in cash and/or CPF Ordinary Account savings (including CPF housing grant, if eligible), depending on loan type
- For flat applications eligible for deferred income assessment from the Jun 2024 sales exercise (young couples): first instalment capped at 2.5%, second instalment 22.5% or 42.5% depending on loan type and LTV limit; those with 75% or 55% LTV loans from a Financial Institution must pay at least 5% or 10% of the purchase price/value (whichever is lower) in cash before disbursement
