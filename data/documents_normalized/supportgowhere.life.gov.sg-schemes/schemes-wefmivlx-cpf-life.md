# CPF LIFE

## Overview
CPF LIFE (CPF Lifelong Income For the Elderly) is a national longevity insurance annuity scheme that provides Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents with monthly payouts for as long as they live.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident born in 1958 and after, with at least $60,000 in the Retirement Account when starting monthly payouts, will be automatically included in CPF LIFE
- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents not automatically included can still choose to join, anytime from age 65 to one month before turning 80
- Can choose to be exempted from CPF LIFE if you have a pension or private annuity plan that pays the same or higher monthly payouts than CPF LIFE

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- CPF Board will send a notification three months before your 65th birthday with options for your CPF savings, including the "Plan my monthly payouts" service
- If you have not instructed the CPF Board by then, payouts will automatically start when you turn 70 under the LIFE Standard Plan

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly payouts for life, guaranteed by the government (not subject to investment market returns), earning risk-free interest of up to 6% per annum
- Option to start payouts at a later age (up to age 70), with monthly payouts increasing by up to 7% for each year of deferral
- Three plans to choose from:
  - Escalating Plan: increasing monthly payouts to help maintain lifestyle as prices increase
  - Standard Plan: steady monthly payouts within a fixed budget
  - Basic Plan: payouts get progressively lower when balances fall below $60,000
