# Public Rental Scheme - Family Scheme

## Overview
Provides heavily subsidised rental flats from HDB for Singaporean households who have no other housing options and no family support.

## Eligibility
- 21 years old and above
- Singapore Citizen
- Form a family nucleus with at least another SC or SPR: you and your spouse; if single, you and your parents; if widowed/divorced, you and your children under your legal care and control; or if orphaned, you and your siblings
- No family support and no other housing options — HDB will assess this based on individual circumstances such as household income, household size and housing budget
- Not existing HDB flat owners, essential occupiers, tenants under HDB's rental schemes or private property owners
- For families with children below 21 years old, the application comes under the ComLink+ Rental Scheme, which offers customised support from government agencies and community partners

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online at the [HDB website](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/renting-a-flat/public-rental-scheme/application-process/application-and-selection-of-rental-flat)
- Provide the required supporting documents as listed on the same page
