# Senior Priority Scheme (SPS) (near existing home)

## Overview
The Senior Priority Scheme (near existing home) supports seniors who wish to age-in-place by giving them priority access when applying for a 2-room Flexi flat within 4km of their existing home.

## Eligibility
- Seniors who wish to buy a 2-room Flexi flat that is within 4km of their owner-occupied property (either an HDB flat or private residential property)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the HDB Flat Portal
- If eligible during your flat application, apply for this scheme in your flat application

## Support / Benefits
- Priority access when applying for a 2-room Flexi flat close to an existing home
