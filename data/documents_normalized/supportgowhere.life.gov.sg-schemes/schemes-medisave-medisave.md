# MediSave

## Overview
MediSave is a personal healthcare savings account that helps you pay for you and loved ones' healthcare expenses over your lifetime, especially when you retire and no longer have regular income.

## Eligibility
- Must be a Singapore Citizen (SC) or Permanent Resident (PR)
- Spouse, children and parents can be of any nationality, except grandparents and siblings who must be SC or PR

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Inform the Public Healthcare Institution (PHI) or Medical Institution (MI) where you are receiving treatment that you would like to use MediSave to pay for your medical expenses
- If this is your first time, sign the Medical Claims Authorisation Form (MCAF)
- If you previously gave standing authorisation, the MI will submit a claim on your behalf
- The PHI/MI submits the medical claim on your behalf and payment is made directly to them
- Upon approval, the Board and/or insurer will notify you

## Support / Benefits
- Covers selected outpatient treatments, day surgery, and hospitalisation expenses
- Covers rehabilitation and palliative care costs
- Can be used to pay health insurance premiums for yourself and your loved ones
