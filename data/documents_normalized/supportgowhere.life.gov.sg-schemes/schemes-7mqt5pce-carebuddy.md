# CAREbuddy

## Overview
CAREbuddy is a supportive service for caregivers of persons with disabilities, provided by trained volunteers who are also caregivers themselves. The 6-month programme provides emotional support, shares resources, and helps caregivers progress in their caregiving journey.

## Eligibility
CAREbuddy is a highly subsidised programme made affordable to all caregivers of persons with special needs. Interested caregivers can contact CaringSG to find out the eligible subsidies available to them.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Interested caregivers or an external agency can click the CaringSG link to request for CAREbuddy

## Support / Benefits
- Caregivers are matched with a CAREbuddy of a similar profile, according to availability
- Trained volunteer CAREbuddies befriend caregivers one-to-one, lend a listening ear, and journey with them over a six-month period with at least 4 touchpoints
- Meet-up dates and timing are arranged according to both the CAREbuddy's and caregiver's convenience
- 97% of caregivers felt connected to their CAREbuddy and enjoyed good rapport/communication
- Note: CAREbuddy does not provide respite and emergency support, is not a mental health or special needs professional service, and does not provide 24-hour hotline, SOS service, child-minding or child-buddy service

## Related Issues & Concerns
- feeling isolated or overwhelmed as a caregiver of a person with disabilities
- looking for peer support from someone who understands caregiving challenges
- want emotional support but don't need professional counselling
- unsure where caregivers of special needs dependants can turn to for a listening ear
