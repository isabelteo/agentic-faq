# Infant and Childcare Subsidies

## Overview
Provides tiered subsidies to help cover infant care and childcare fees for Singapore Citizen children from different income backgrounds, enrolled in an ECDA-licensed centre.

## Eligibility
- For all subsidies: child must be a Singapore Citizen and enrolled in an infant care or childcare centre licensed by ECDA
- Additional Subsidy: working applicants (mother or single father who works at least 56 hours per month); monthly household income of up to $12,000, or monthly household income per capita of up to $3,000 for larger families with 5 or more members (from 1 January 2027, thresholds rise to $15,000, or $3,400 per capita)
- Special Approval: for main subsidy applicants not working due to reasons such as looking for a job, studying/training for at least 56 hours a month, pregnancy, medically unfit for work, caring for a special needs child or sick family member, caring for a younger child aged 24 months and below not enrolled in preschool, incarceration, or being a non-parent caregiver (e.g. grandparent, legal guardian, foster parent, head of a children's home)
- Families who are tenants of a rental flat under HDB's Public Rental Scheme, or beneficiaries of ComCare Short-To-Medium-Term Assistance (SMTA) or Long-Term Assistance (LTA), automatically qualify for maximum preschool subsidies upon Special Approval application

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Refer to the [ECDA website](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/preschool-subsidies/how-to-apply-or-make-changes-to-subsidies) to apply or make changes to subsidies

## Support / Benefits
- Working applicants: Basic Subsidy of $600 for infant care and $300 for childcare; Additional Subsidy of up to $710 for infant care and up to $467 for childcare
- Non-working applicants: Basic Subsidy of $150 for infant care and childcare; can apply for Special Approval to receive the higher Basic Subsidy and Additional Subsidy that working applicants receive
