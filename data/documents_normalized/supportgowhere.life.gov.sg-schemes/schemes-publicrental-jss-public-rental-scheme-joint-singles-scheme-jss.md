# Public Rental Scheme - Joint Singles Scheme (JSS)

## Overview
Provides heavily subsidised rental flats from HDB for Singaporeans who are single and have no other housing options and no family support.

## Eligibility
- You and your proposed applicant(s) must be singles: unmarried, divorced, or legally separated from spouse and at least 35 years old; or widowed or orphaned and at least 21 years old
- Singapore Citizens
- No family support and no other housing options — HDB will assess this based on individual circumstances such as income and housing budget
- Not existing HDB flat owners, essential occupiers, tenants under HDB's rental schemes or private property owners

## How to Apply / Next Steps
