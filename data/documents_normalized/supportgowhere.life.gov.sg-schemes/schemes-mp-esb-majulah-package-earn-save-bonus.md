# Majulah Package – Earn and Save Bonus

## Overview
The Majulah Package Earn and Save Bonus supplements eligible working seniors' retirement savings through an annual bonus of up to $1,000 credited to their CPF account, tiered by income.

## Eligibility
- Born in 1973 or earlier
- Singapore Citizen residing in Singapore
- Employed, self-employed, and/or platform worker
- Average monthly income in preceding Year of Assessment of $500 to $6,000
- Annual Value of the place of residence (as indicated on NRIC) must not exceed $31,000, as at 30 November of the preceding year
- Does not own more than 1 property

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Employees and Platform Workers: no application required; eligibility is automatically assessed
- Self-employed persons: declare income to IRAS to be assessed for the Earn and Save Bonus

## Support / Benefits
- $400 to $1,000 annual Earn and Save Bonus in CPF account, tiered by income (lower income receives a higher bonus)
- Bonus credited in March each year
