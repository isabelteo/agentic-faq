# Persons with Disabilities Concession Card (PWDCC)

## Overview
Provides persons with disabilities with concessionary rates for travel on public transport.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen and Permanent Resident
- Aged 7 to below 60 years old
- Have a permanent disability based on any one of: physical disability requiring assistance with at least 1 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living; moderate visual impairment or worse in the better eye; moderate hearing loss or worse in the better ear; intellectual disability; or autism
- Proof of disability must be provided using the [Disability Verification Form](https://www.enablingguide.sg/disability-verification) completed by a relevant registered healthcare professional

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Provide proof of disability using the Disability Verification Form completed by a relevant registered healthcare professional
- For more information on the application process, visit [Enabling Guide](https://www.enablingguide.sg/im-looking-for-disability-support/transport/persons-with-disabilities-concession-card)
- The card will be mailed out within 14 working days after SimplyGo receives the application

## Support / Benefits
- Discount of up to 55% off adult public transport fares
- Option to purchase a Monthly Public Transport Concession Pass at $55 per month for unlimited travel on train and basic bus services
- No additional fares for distance beyond 7.2km
