# Special Needs Savings Scheme (SNSS)

## Overview
Enables parents to set aside CPF savings for the long-term care of children with special needs, so that the child can receive payouts from the parent's CPF savings after the parent passes on.

## Eligibility
The nominating applicant should be:
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- The parent or an appointed legal guardian of the child with special needs

The child with special needs should:
- Have a permanent disability based on any one of the following, with proof via the Disability Verification Form completed by a relevant registered healthcare professional:
  - Physical Disability: Requires some assistance with at least 1 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living due to physical impairment
  - A visual impairment: Moderate visual impairment or worse in the better eye
  - A hearing impairment: Moderate hearing loss or worse in the better ear
  - Intellectual disability
  - Autism
- Or, is attending or had attended a Special Education School

The 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) are: Eating, Bathing, Dressing, Transferring, Toileting, Walking or moving around.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Before proceeding, check if your child's disability status has already been verified: [enablingguide.sg/disability-verification](https://www.enablingguide.sg/disability-verification)
- Apply by submitting the completed [application form](https://www.sgenable.sg/snss) and supporting documents:
  - via mail to Special Needs Savings Scheme (SNSS), Future Care Planning Resource Centre by SG Enable, 22 Lengkok Bahru, Vista, Enabling Village #03-03 Singapore 159824
  - via email to [futurecareplanning@sgenable.sg](mailto:futurecareplanning@sgenable.sg)
- You'll need to provide copies of: Nominating applicant's and child's NRIC; Child's Birth Certificate
- If your child's disability status has not been verified, also provide: a supporting document from the SPED school on official letterhead stating current or past attendance, or a completed Disability Verification Form (DVF) by a Registered Healthcare Professional (note: a submitted DVF does not by itself mean the disability status has been verified)
- After mailing the application and upon approval by MSF, parents will receive an eligibility letter, which they should bring to the CPF Board to make the SNSS nomination

## Support / Benefits
- Parents may nominate a child with special needs to receive payouts from their CPF savings after death, with a minimum monthly payout of $250 (parents may decide on a higher amount)
- If the nominating parent's CPF savings upon death is insufficient to support a year's worth of payout (e.g. minimum monthly payout of S$250 x 12 months = S$3,000), the CPF savings will be disbursed as a lump sum to the nominee with special needs
