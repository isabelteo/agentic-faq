# Parenthood Provisional Housing Scheme (PPHS)

## Overview
PPHS provides temporary rental housing from HDB for families waiting for the completion of their new flats, at subsidised rental rates.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen who is with another Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident in the PPHS household
- Booked an uncompleted flat under HDB's sales exercises as a married couple, under the Fiance/Fiancee scheme, or as a divorced or widowed parent with children
- All members of the PPHS household must not own an existing HDB flat
- Monthly household income of less than or equal to $8,000

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online at the HDB website; PPHS application window is from the 1st to the 14th of even months (Feb, Apr, Jun, Aug, Oct, Dec)

## Support / Benefits
- Subsidised rental rates ranging from $400 to $1,500 per month for 2-, 3- or 4-room flats, depending on location and flat type
