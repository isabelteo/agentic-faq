# SkillsFuture Mid-Career Enhanced Subsidy

## Overview
Provides an enhanced course fee subsidy of up to 90% for Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above, applied automatically on top of existing base course fee subsidies.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- Aged 40 and above
- Qualifies for the Ministry of Education (MOE)/Skills & Workforce Development Agency (SWDA) base course fee subsidy

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Enrol in a course supported by SWDA, or offered by an Autonomous University (NUS, NTU, SMU, SUTD, SIT, SUSS), Polytechnic, Institute of Technical Education (ITE), LASALLE College of the Arts, NAFA, or the National Institute of Early Childhood Development (NIEC)
- Ensure the course is eligible for the MOE/SSG base course fee subsidy — the enhanced subsidy is applied automatically at enrolment if eligible

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 90% subsidy on course fees for courses offered by SWDA-appointed Continuing Education and Training (CET) Centres
- At least 90% subsidy on programme costs for MOE-subsidised full-time and part-time courses at Autonomous Universities, Polytechnics, and ITE
- For certifiable-skills training courses, up to 70% subsidy on course fees
