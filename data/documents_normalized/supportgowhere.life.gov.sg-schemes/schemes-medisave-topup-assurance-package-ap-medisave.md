# Assurance Package (AP) MediSave

## Overview
The Assurance Package (AP) MediSave provides a $150 MediSave top-up each year from 2023 to 2025 for Singapore Citizens aged 20 and below, or 55 and above, residing in Singapore.

## Eligibility
- For AP MediSave Payout in 2025: Singapore Citizen residing in Singapore
- Aged 20 and below, or 55 and above in 2025

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required

## Support / Benefits
- $150 MediSave top-up each year, for Singapore Citizens aged 20 and below or 55 and above
- Top-ups credited in February from 2023 to 2025
