# Stamp Duty Remissions for Married and Divorced Couples

## Overview
Provides various stamp duty remissions for eligible couples buying new residential property or transferring their current one(s) as part of matrimonial proceedings.

## Eligibility
- Joint purchase of first residential property by married couple: married couple with at least 1 Singapore Citizen spouse; property jointly purchased under couple's names only; both spouses do not already own any residential properties at the time of purchase
- Joint purchase of second residential property by married couple: ABSD already paid on the second property; married couple with at least 1 Singapore Citizen spouse; property jointly purchased under couple's names only; couple must not own or have an interest in more than 1 residential property each when purchasing second property; couple's first property must be sold within 6 months after the date of purchase of the second property (or TOP/CSC issue date, whichever earlier, if uncompleted)
- Couples undergoing matrimonial proceedings: transfer of property due to court-ordered division of matrimonial assets or result of divorce proceedings; property must be transferred from 1 party to another (both involved in the matrimonial proceedings); property may also be transferred by any child/children of the marriage/former marriage

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- For partial ABSD remission (second property): navigate the [e-Stamping Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/estamping/) to "Request" > "Apply for Refund"; for forms submitted on or after 2 Jul 2023, ABSD is automatically refunded within 6 weeks from stamping of the first property's sale, without needing a refund application; for stampings before 2 Jul 2023, manually submit the Stamp Duties (Spouses) (Remission of ABSD) refund application via the same portal
- For full ABSD remission (first property): e-Stamp via the [e-Stamping Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/estamping/) to obtain the remission certificate, or have a legal representative assist with the stamping

## Support / Benefits
- Full Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) remission for a couple's joint purchase of their first residential property
- Partial ABSD remission for a couple's jointly purchased second property
- Potential remission of BSD, ABSD and SSD on property transfers resulting from matrimonial proceedings
