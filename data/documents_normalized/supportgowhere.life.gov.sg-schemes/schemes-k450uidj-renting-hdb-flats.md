# Renting Out of HDB Flats

## Overview
Enables eligible Singapore Citizen flat owners to rent out their HDB flat, in whole or part, after meeting the Minimum Occupation Period (MOP), subject to HDB approval.

## Eligibility
- Owner must be a Singapore Citizen; Singapore Permanent Residents are not allowed to rent out their flat
- Must fulfil a Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) of 5 years, or 3 years for non-subsidised flats purchased before 30 August 2010 (non-subsidised = purchased from the open market without a housing grant); MOP calculated from date of purchase or effective date of ownership transfer
- Owners of Prime and Plus flats cannot rent out the flat even after the 10-year MOP
- Owners of 2-room Flexi flats, Studio Apartments and Lease Buyback Scheme (LBS) flats are not eligible to rent out their flat
- Non-Citizen Quota applies when renting to non-Malaysian non-citizens (SPRs or foreigners); check via [HDB's e-Service](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWNCQuota/)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Ensure compliance with regulations on [HDB's website](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/home-ownership/renting-out-a-flat-or-bedrooms/renting-out-a-flat/eligibility), including number of tenants and their legal residency status
- Apply via the 'Apply/Manage Application to Rent Out Flat' [e-Service](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR12AWFlatRentOutApplication) or the [MyHDB mobile app](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/myhdb-mobile-app); an administrative fee of $18 applies per application
- Notify HDB of any changes in rental details within 7 days, and obtain approval for new tenants before they move in

## Support / Benefits
- Receive rental income from your flat
- Provides seniors an option to monetise their flat and move to live with family members
