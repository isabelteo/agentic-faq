# MediSave Grant for Newborns (MGN)

## Overview
All Singapore Citizen newborns are eligible for the MediSave Grant for Newborns (MGN) of up to $5,000, credited to their CPF MediSave account.

## Eligibility
- All Singapore Citizen newborns born on or after 26 August 2012 are eligible for MGN
- Children who are Singapore Citizens at birth and born before 1 January 2015 are entitled to up to $3,000; born on or after 1 January 2015, up to $4,000; born on or after 1 April 2025, up to $5,000
- Children born overseas can qualify for MGN upon registering their birth with ICA or through a Singapore Overseas Mission; the amount depends on the date the child becomes a Singapore Citizen
- Children who obtain Singapore Citizenship after the age of 2 are not eligible for MGN

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required
- The grant is automatically credited into your newborn's MediSave account within 1 week from birth registration / acquiring Singapore citizenship
- You will receive a notification once the MGN has been credited
- You may check your child's CPF statement online at [cpf.gov.sg/childdashboard](https://cpf.gov.sg/childdashboard) with Singpass

## Support / Benefits
- Up to $5,000 credited to each Singapore Citizen newborn's CPF MediSave account
- 4% annual interest
- Accounts are opened automatically for newborns
- Covers MediShield Life premiums, recommended childhood vaccinations, hospitalisation, and approved outpatient treatments
