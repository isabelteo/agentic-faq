# Silver Support Scheme

## Overview
Provides a quarterly cash supplement of $215 to $1,080, depending on HDB flat type and monthly per capita household income, to give eligible seniors greater financial assurance in retirement.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- 65 years old and above
- Total CPF contributions made by age 55 not more than $140,000
- Lives in a 1-room to 5-room HDB flat
- Recipient and spouse do not own a 5-room or larger HDB flat, private property, or multiple properties
- Monthly household income per person of up to $2,300

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required — eligible seniors will be notified by the CPF Board

## Support / Benefits
- Quarterly cash payments of $215 to $1,080, depending on HDB flat type and monthly per capita household income
- Seniors living in smaller HDB flats or with lower monthly per capita household income receive higher payments
