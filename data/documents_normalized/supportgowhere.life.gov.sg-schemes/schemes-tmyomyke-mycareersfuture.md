# MyCareersFuture

## Overview
MyCareersFuture is the one-stop portal for job search and application needs, using AI and Machine Learning to help jobseekers discover career opportunities, make career transitions, or search for jobs based on skills matches. It also provides career-related resources such as career insights and guidance.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents who are looking to plan their careers, make career transitions, or search for jobs

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit [MyCareersFuture](https://www.mycareersfuture.gov.sg/) to begin
- Hotline: 6669 7932
- [Submit an enquiry](https://service-portal.swda.gov.sg/s/feedback)

## Support / Benefits
- Job-to-Skills matching algorithm connecting users with opportunities tailored to their skills and experience
- CareersFinder: personalised upskilling and career recommendations based on user profile
- Ability to filter jobs eligible for Government support such as Career Conversion Programmes (CCP), Graduate Industry Traineeships (GRIT) Programme, and Mid-Career Pathways Programmes (MCPP)
- CareersCompass: curated articles, career guides and expert insights for career planning, skills development and job search strategies
- CareersHorizon: platform for career-related event registrations, online articles, virtual career events, seminars, workshops, and networking with employers

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Looking for a job or planning a career change
- Wondering which government-supported programmes (like CCP or GRIT) their job search is eligible for
- Wanting personalised advice on upskilling for their career
- A mature worker looking for mid-career attachment opportunities
- A fresh graduate seeking traineeship opportunities
- Wanting to attend career events, webinars, or networking sessions
