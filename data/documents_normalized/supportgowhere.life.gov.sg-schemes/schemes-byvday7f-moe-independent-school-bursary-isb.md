# MOE Independent School Bursary (ISB)

## Overview
The MOE Independent School Bursary (ISB) provides financial assistance to lower- and middle-income families with children in Independent Schools and some Specialised Independent Schools.

## Eligibility
- Singaporean students studying in Independent Schools and some Specialised Independent Schools
- Average Gross Household Income of up to $12,000, or Average Per Capita Income of up to $3,000

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online through the MOE eFASIS link

## Support / Benefits
- Up to full waiver of school and miscellaneous fees, depending on household income
- Students with Average Gross Household Income of up to $4,000, or Average Per Capita Income of up to $1,000, also receive: for secondary students, free textbooks and school attire, $21 transport credit per month for those who take public transport, and 10 meals per school week; for pre-university students, a bursary of $1,600 per annum

## Related Issues & Concerns
- child attends an Independent School and school fees are a financial burden
- want to know if my household income qualifies for a full fee waiver
- looking for help with textbooks, school attire, or transport costs for a secondary school child
- pre-university student needing financial assistance
