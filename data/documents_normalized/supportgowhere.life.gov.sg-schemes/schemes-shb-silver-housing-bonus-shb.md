# Silver Housing Bonus (SHB)

## Overview
Seniors aged 55 or above may receive a cash bonus when they right-size from their current home to a 3-room or smaller HDB flat, and top up the sale proceeds into their CPF Retirement Account (RA) to join CPF LIFE for monthly retirement payouts.

## Eligibility
- At least 1 owner must be a Singapore Citizen (SC), aged 55 or above
- Monthly household income not more than $16,000
- Existing property is an HDB flat that has met the minimum occupation period for resale, or a private residential property with an Annual Value (AV) of up to $31,000
- No concurrent ownership or interest in a private residential property, or in more than 1 non-residential property

**Other conditions**
- The next property purchased must be a 3-room (excluding 3-room terrace) or smaller flat from HDB or the resale market
- Must have positive net sale proceeds
- Booking of new HDB flat or application to buy resale flat must be either before the sale of the existing property, or within 12 months of completing that sale
- SHB application must be submitted within 1 year from the legal completion date of the second housing transaction (purchase or sale)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Check eligibility and estimated monthly payouts via [HDB's 'Check Housing Monetisation Option' e-Service](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BR05AWEnquireHMOptions)
- Enquire with a Customer Relations Executive during the appointment for the second housing transaction (resale completion or new flat keys collection)
- HDB will then schedule a financial counselling session to share details such as the CPF top-up amount, bonus, and monthly payout amount

## Support / Benefits
- Since 1 December 2025, seniors who commit to a net increase of up to $60,000 in their CPF RA after right-sizing (which can be done using CPF housing refunds if sufficient, without needing a cash top-up) can qualify for an SHB amount of $30,000
- An additional $10,000 cash bonus is available for right-sizing to a 2-room or smaller flat, including a Community Care Apartment (CCA), regardless of the CPF RA top-up amount
- Maximum SHB amount eligible seniors may receive is $40,000
- The net increase to commit to the CPF RA is capped at $60,000 per household and further capped by the prevailing Full Retirement Sum (FRS)
- CPF LIFE provides monthly payouts for life
