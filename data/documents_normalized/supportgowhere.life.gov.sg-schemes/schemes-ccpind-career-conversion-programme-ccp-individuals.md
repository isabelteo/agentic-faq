# Career Conversion Programme (CCP) for Individuals

## Overview
The Career Conversion Programme (CCP) for Individuals supports mid-career new hires or existing employees seeking to transition into growth job roles with good longer-term prospects, through on-the-job training.

## Eligibility
Mid-career individuals should meet the following criteria:
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Aged 21 or above
- Graduated or completed National Service, for at least 2 years
- Career conversion required: for new hires, the new job role trained for under the CCP should be substantially different from the previous job role before joining CCP; for existing employees, the new job role should be a growth job role identified under the Jobs Transformation Maps (JTMs)
- Able to commit to full-time employment or attachment in a participating company
- Should generally not be shareholders, immediate ex-staff, or related to owners/decision-makers of the CCP participating company or its related entities
- Different CCPs may have additional course-specific entry requirements, and employers may apply their own shortlisting process

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for CCPs through CCP-supportable jobs on MyCareersFuture

## Support / Benefits
- Access on-the-job training to switch careers into growth sectors with long-term prospects
- Salary support of up to 90% for mid-career workers
- Embark on a new career with CCPs across around 30 sectors

## Related Issues & Concerns
- want to switch careers into a growth sector but worried about lost income during training
- mid-career and unsure how to qualify for a career conversion programme
- existing employee whose role is being transformed and needs reskilling
- looking for jobs on MyCareersFuture that offer career conversion support
