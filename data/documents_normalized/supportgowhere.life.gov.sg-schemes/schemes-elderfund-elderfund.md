# ElderFund

## Overview
A discretionary government assistance scheme that supports the care needs of lower-income Singapore Citizens with severe disability, through a monthly cash payout.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen aged 30 and above
- Lives in Singapore
- Has a monthly household income per capita of $1,500 or less
- Has a MediSave balance of less than $10,000
- Assessed by an MOH-accredited assessor to require full assistance with at least 3 out of 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs): washing, dressing, feeding, toileting, walking or moving around, transferring
- Not a CareShield Life or ElderShield policyholder
- Not a CareShield Life or ElderShield supplement plan policyholder
- No longer an active recipient of ElderShield claims (including supplement claims)
- Not eligible for IDAPE, or no longer an active recipient of IDAPE
- Applications from Singaporeans who do not meet the guiding financial criteria but have difficulty meeting long-term care needs will be considered on a case-by-case basis

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online at [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)
- Via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in
- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- Contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the application; a list of assessors is on [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment)

## Support / Benefits
- Up to $250 cash per month for as long as eligible
- Payout can be used for long-term care needs such as hiring a helper and daily expenses
- Processing time takes between 6 to 8 weeks
