# ActiveSG Membership

## Overview
ActiveSG Membership is part of a national movement for individuals, families and communities to experience and share the joy of living better through sports.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
- Those below 15 years old are eligible for supplementary membership, tagged to the accounts of their parents/guardians

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply via the ActiveSG website
- For supplementary account: parents/legal guardians can apply for supplementary membership for children under 15 years old

## Support / Benefits
- Free entry to ActiveSG swimming pools and gyms for Singaporeans aged 65 and above
- Access to swimming complexes and gym admissions, and payment for ActiveSG programmes and activities (up to 30% of the programme price)
- Booking of ActiveSG sport facilities
- Purchase of MyActiveGYM™ and MyActiveSWIM™ schemes
- Invitation to exclusive members' events and Active Health Lab access

## Related Issues & Concerns
- senior citizen wanting to know if gym or swimming pool entry is free
- want a membership that lets my child use sports facilities under my account
- looking for discounts on sports programmes and activities
- unsure how to book community sport facilities
