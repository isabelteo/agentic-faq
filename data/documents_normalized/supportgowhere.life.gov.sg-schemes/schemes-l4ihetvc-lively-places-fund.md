# Lively Places Fund

## Overview
Provides funding for community-driven projects that enhance public spaces and foster community bonds in HDB neighbourhoods, ranging from small short-term activities to larger physical transformations of public spaces.

## Eligibility
- Open to all Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
- Individual or team applicants, with at least 1 applicant above 17 years old
- Not for projects already funded by other agencies (co-funding may be considered for new projects meeting the fund's objectives)
- Previous awardees must complete existing projects before reapplying
- Cannot support projects that duplicate existing programmes, or projects/studies started before funding approval
- Proposals reviewed based on: fund objective, level of neighbourhood involvement, level of community match, and outcomes

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit [https://go.gov.sg/hdb-osglpf-application](https://go.gov.sg/hdb-osglpf-application) to apply

## Support / Benefits
- Project Fund: up to $5,000 for smaller, short-term community bonding activities (e.g. block parties, anti-littering campaigns, community recycling days)
- Building Fund: up to $5,000 for physical transformation projects of 6 months or less (e.g. community arts projects, short-term pop-up installations); up to $20,000 for projects lasting more than 6 months (e.g. converting void decks into community living rooms or cafes)
- Street Closure: support limited to road closure essentials — safety barriers, signage and security personnel — for short-term street events
