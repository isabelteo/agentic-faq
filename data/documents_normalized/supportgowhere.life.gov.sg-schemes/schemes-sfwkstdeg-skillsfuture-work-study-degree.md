# SkillsFuture Work-Study Degree

## Overview
Provides work-relevant skills through a blend of classroom learning and structured on-the-job training, for prospective students and working adults pursuing a degree with a sponsoring company.

## Eligibility
- Prospective Polytechnic or Junior College students
- Working adults interested in pursuing a degree
- Keen on enrolling in a local university offering the SkillsFuture Work-Study Degree programmes
- Interested in integrating work experience with academic learning

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the [SkillsFuture Work-Study Degree page](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/education-career-personal-development/wsdeg.html) for a list of programmes offered by universities and their contact information

## Support / Benefits
- Structured on-the-job training with classroom learning
- Financial support from sponsoring companies, which may include tuition fee sponsorship, stipends, and sign-on bonuses
- Opportunities for career advancement with the sponsoring company after graduation
- Flexible study modes with Term-in/Term-out or Work-day/Study-day options
