# ITE Monthly Financial Assistance Scheme (MFAS)

## Overview
MFAS provides a monthly allowance to full-time Singaporean ITE students from lower-income households who require regular financial assistance for daily expenses.

## Eligibility
- Full-time Singaporean ITE students who require regular financial assistance

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online via the ITE portal (myITE): click on the iStudent icon > Financial Assistance icon
- Refer to the step-by-step guide if you need more help

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly allowance of $200 per month with effect from Academic Year 2026
- Number of months of award depends on the needs of each individual student

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording daily expenses while studying at ITE
- wondering how to apply for financial help as an ITE student
- need ongoing monthly support, not just a one-time bursary
