# Government-Paid Paternity Leave (GPPL)

## Overview
Provides up to 4 weeks of Government-Paid Paternity Leave (GPPL) for eligible working fathers, with the government reimbursing employers (or paying self-employed fathers directly) for the leave taken.

## Eligibility
**Natural fathers with live births**
- Child born or estimated delivery date on or after 1 January 2017
- Child is a Singapore Citizen or becomes one within 12 months from birth
- Lawfully married to the child's mother between conception and birth or within 12 months from birth
- Served employer for at least 3 months before child's birth (or, for self-employed, engaged in trade/business/profession/vocation for at least 3 months before birth)

**Natural fathers with stillborn children**
- Stillbirth occurred on or after 1 November 2021
- Lawfully married to the child's mother between conception and stillbirth or within 12 months from stillbirth, and either parent is a Singapore Citizen
- Served employer for at least 3 months before the date of stillbirth (or equivalent self-employment period)

**Adoptive fathers**
- Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date on or after 1 January 2017
- Not the natural father of the child
- If child is not a Singapore Citizen, either the father or his wife must be a Singapore Citizen since the child's Dependant's Pass issuance
- Served employer for at least 3 months before FIA date (or equivalent self-employment period)

**Leave entitlement by child's birth/FIA/estimated delivery date**
- Before 1 Jan 2024: up to 2 weeks GPPL, reimbursement capped at $2,500/week up to $5,000 total
- Between 1 Jan 2024 and 31 Mar 2025: up to 4 weeks GPPL, reimbursement capped at $2,500/week up to $10,000 total (weeks 3-4 additional, employers can grant voluntarily)
- On or after 1 Apr 2025: up to 4 weeks GPPL, reimbursement capped at $2,500/week up to $10,000 total
- Leave must be taken within 12 months from the child's date of birth

## How to Apply / Next Steps
**For employees**: Notify your employer early; submit the [declaration form (GPPL1)](https://file.go.gov.sg/gppl1form.pdf) to your employer with supporting documents; apply for leave per your employer's procedures
**For self-employed**: Record your leave dates; submit your claim online via the [Government-Paid Leave (GPL) Portal](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/login?redirect=eservices/submit-a-claim) within 3 months after your leave; check status on the GPL Portal
**For employers**: Obtain and verify the declaration form (GPPL1) from your employee; keep records for 5 years from the last leave date; submit your claim for reimbursement online via the GPL Portal within 3 months after your employee's leave

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 4 weeks of Government-Paid Paternity Leave for eligible fathers
- Government reimbursement/payment for GPPL capped at $2,500 per week, up to a total of $10,000
