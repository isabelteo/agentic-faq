# MUIS Zakat Financial Assistance

## Overview
MUIS Zakat Financial Assistance provides monthly financial assistance and support in other forms to eligible low-income Muslim households.

## Eligibility
- Available to Muslim Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
- Per Capita Income (PCI) must be $500 or lower to qualify
- Applicants will undergo an assessment interview with a Social Development Officer (SDO) at the nearest Social Development (SD) mosque
- Required documents: NRIC, birth certificate, income and bank documents, and other relevant supporting documents
- Outcome notified by mail within up to 6 weeks

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Call a Social Development (SD) mosque closest to your home for an appointment
- Schedule an assessment interview with an SDO
- Bring personal identification documents (NRIC, birth certificate), income and bank documents (payslips, CPF statements, bank books), and other relevant supporting documents (bills, medical memos, referral letters) to the interview

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly financial support dependent on Per Capita Income (PCI) criteria
- One-time settlement of arrears for basic necessities (utility and conservancy charges)
- Emergency assistance in times of crisis such as natural disasters, fire, or accidents
- Coverage of burial expenses upon death of a Zakat beneficiary and their immediate family members
- Study grants: subsidies for national examinations, mosque-based part-time Islamic education, and low-income students with special needs
- Fidyah food vouchers to purchase food supplies at approved Fidyah retailers islandwide

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording daily expenses as a low-income Muslim household
- need help with utility bill arrears
- looking for emergency assistance after a fire or accident
- worried about affording funeral/burial costs for a family member
- need study grant support for a low-income Muslim student
