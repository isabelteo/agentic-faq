# Kindergarten Fee Assistance Scheme (KiFAS) and KiFAS Startup Grant (SUG)

## Overview
Provides subsidies for kindergarten programmes to ensure affordability for low and middle-income Singapore Citizen families, plus an annual KiFAS Start-Up Grant (SUG) to help low-income families with initial costs.

## Eligibility
- Child must be a Singapore Citizen
- Enrolled in an Anchor Operator or Ministry of Education Kindergarten
- Monthly household income of up to $12,000, or monthly household income per capita of up to $3,000 for larger families with 5 or more members (from 1 January 2027, thresholds rise to $15,000, or $3,400 per capita)
- Yearly SUG: applicants with monthly household income up to $1,900, or per capita income up to $650 for larger families with 5 or more members, can further apply for SUG; each application assessed case-by-case

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Refer to the [ECDA website](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/preschool-subsidies/how-to-apply-or-make-changes-to-subsidies/subsidy-application-for-new-preschool-enrolment) for steps on how to apply for subsidies

## Support / Benefits
- Subsidies range between $17 and $161 per month, depending on monthly household income, disbursed directly to the child's kindergarten
- Yearly KiFAS SUG capped at $240 per year to help cover initial costs such as registration fee, deposit, school uniform, insurance, and educational material fees

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording kindergarten fees on a low or middle income
- need help covering the initial registration and uniform costs for kindergarten
- checking how much monthly subsidy my household income qualifies me for
- wondering if I qualify for both the monthly subsidy and the Start-Up Grant
