# Integrated Shield Plan (IP)

## Overview
A private medical insurance plan, offered by private insurers, that provides optional additional coverage on top of what you are already covered for under MediShield Life.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- There are a variety of IPs with different levels of coverage
- When deciding whether to buy an IP, consider what you would like to be covered for and whether you can afford the plan, especially in the long term
- Refer to [MOH's Comparison of Integrated Shield Plans](https://www.moh.gov.sg/healthcare-schemes-subsidies/medishield-life/comparison-of-integrated-shield-plans) or the [Health Insurance Planner](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/planners/cpf-planner-health-insurance) for a comparison of IP benefits and long-term premiums

## Support / Benefits
- MediShield Life component: fully payable by MediSave, sized for subsidised care (e.g. B2/C wards) in public hospital
- Additional private insurance component: payable by MediSave, but only up to the Additional Withdrawal Limits; covers unsubsidised care (e.g. A, B1 wards) in public hospitals or private hospital bills

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording private hospital bills or unsubsidised ward care
- wondering how much of my Integrated Shield Plan premium MediSave can cover
- comparing different Integrated Shield Plans before buying one
- unsure what MediShield Life alone actually covers versus an add-on IP
