# Enhanced Climate Friendly Households Programme (Climate Vouchers)

## Overview
The Enhanced Climate Friendly Households Programme provides Climate Vouchers to encourage households to be more efficient in energy and water consumption, helping to defray the upfront cost of adopting energy- and water-efficient appliances.

## Eligibility
- Singaporean and Permanent Resident (PR) HDB households
- Private property households with at least 1 Singapore Citizen

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application is required
- Claim your Climate Vouchers via the signup.redeem.gov.sg website
- Find out how to claim your Climate Vouchers at climate-friendly-households.gov.sg

## Support / Benefits
- Each Singaporean and PR HDB household is entitled to a total of $400 Climate Vouchers, comprising $300 from April 2024 and an additional $100 from 15 April 2025
- Each Singaporean household living in a private residential property is entitled to a total of $400 Climate Vouchers from 15 April 2025
- The list of eligible products will be expanded to include 5-tick clothes dryers and induction stoves from 15 April 2026
- Multiple families or tenants living at the same registered address are collectively entitled to one set of Climate Vouchers for that address
