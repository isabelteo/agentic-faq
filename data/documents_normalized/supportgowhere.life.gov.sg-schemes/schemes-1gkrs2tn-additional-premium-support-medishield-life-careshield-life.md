# Additional Premium Support for MediShield Life and CareShield Life

## Overview
Additional Premium Support (APS) provides financial support for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents who are unable to afford their MediShield Life and/or CareShield Life premiums (where applicable) even after Government subsidies and MediSave use, and who have limited family support.

## Eligibility
- Eligibility is based on the financial means of the applicant and his/her immediate family members
- You will not be eligible for APS if you have sufficient MediSave to pay for your MediShield Life and/or CareShield Life premiums, or have purchased an Integrated Shield Plan and/or ElderShield/CareShield Life Supplements
- If you are granted APS and purchase an Integrated Shield Plan and/or ElderShield/CareShield Life Supplement afterwards, you will stop receiving APS
- Open to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents living overseas

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- You will be invited to apply for APS if you have insufficient MediSave and have limited family support to help pay for your MediShield Life premiums
- The invitation will include an application form with details on the application process, which must be submitted along with the relevant supporting documents
- There is no need to apply for APS ahead of time
- Applications may take up to 20 working days to be assessed, and applicants will receive a notification on the application outcome

## Support / Benefits
- Covers MediShield Life and/or CareShield Life premiums (where applicable) for the next 2 policy years upon approval, as well as any outstanding premiums

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about not being able to afford MediShield Life or CareShield Life premiums
- concerned that MediSave savings are insufficient to cover healthcare insurance premiums
- have limited family support and can't pay for compulsory health insurance
- living overseas and unsure if still eligible for premium help
- already have outstanding MediShield Life premiums and need help
