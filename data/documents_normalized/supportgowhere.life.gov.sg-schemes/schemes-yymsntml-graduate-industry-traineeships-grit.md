# GRaduate Industry Traineeships (GRIT)

## Overview
Equips fresh graduates with experience and skills, easing their transition into full-time employment through structured traineeships. Jointly introduced by the Ministry of Manpower (MOM) and Skills & Workforce Development Agency (SWDA), providing meaningful traineeships in both private companies and public agencies.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Graduated or graduating in 2024, 2025 or 2026 from Universities, Polytechnics, Institute of Technical Education (ITE) and other Educational Institutions (e.g. private universities and overseas institutions); or graduated earlier from above institutions and completed National Service in 2024, 2025 or 2026
- Applicants must have no prior working experience and must be prepared to commit to a full-time traineeship

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Traineeship opportunities will be progressively made available on MyCareersFuture

## Support / Benefits
- Offers up to 800 structured traineeships across various sectors for 3 to 6 months
- Monthly allowance ranging from S$1,800 to S$2,400, co-funded by the government and host organisations

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Fresh graduate worried about lacking work experience needed to land a full-time job
- Not sure how to transition from National Service into a first job
- Looking for paid work experience opportunities in a specific sector before committing to a permanent job
- Wondering if their diploma or degree qualifies them for a structured traineeship programme
- Confused about where to find traineeship listings and how to apply
