# Proximity Housing Grant (Families)

## Overview
The Proximity Housing Grant (Families) provides up to $30,000 for families buying a resale flat to live with or near their parents or children.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen (SC), or include at least 1 other SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR)
- At least 21 years old
- Buying a resale flat to live with, or within 4 km of, parents or child
- Have not received the Proximity Housing Grant (PHG) before

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the HDB Flat Portal to check eligibility for grants and loans
- If eligible, apply for the grant when submitting your resale application

## Support / Benefits
- $30,000 grant to live with your parents or child
- $20,000 grant to live near your parents or child (if resale flat is within 4km); if living near them, parents/child may reside in either an HDB flat or private residential property to be eligible
