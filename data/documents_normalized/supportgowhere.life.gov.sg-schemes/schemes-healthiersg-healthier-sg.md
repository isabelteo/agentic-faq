# Healthier SG

## Overview
A national strategy focused on preventive care that empowers individuals to take steps toward better health, with the support of their enrolled Healthier SG clinic and community partners. Available to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 40 and above.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 40 years old and above, to enrol with a Healthier SG clinic or polyclinic

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Self-enrol via HealthHub: if eligible, you will be invited via SMS to enrol
  1. Download the HealthHub app (Google Play or Apple App Store)
  2. Choose a preferred Healthier SG clinic from the [list of more than 1,000 clinics](https://gowhere.gov.sg/healthiersg)
  3. Book an appointment for your fully subsidised first Health Plan consultation
- If you have not received an SMS invitation, request one at [https://go.gov.sg/healthiersg-sms-invite](https://go.gov.sg/healthiersg-sms-invite) or contact the MOH hotline at 6325 9220
- Enrol with caregiver's assistance: grant a caregiver/family member access to your HealthHub so they can help you enrol
- Visit any ServiceSG Centre or AIC Link for enrolment assistance

## Support / Benefits
- Fully subsidised first Health Plan consultation and subsequent annual Health Plan check-ins with your enrolled clinic
- Healthpoints (worth $20) via Healthy 365 app after the first Health Plan consultation
- One-off Healthpoints (worth $10) via Healthy 365 app upon completing an in-person annual Health Plan check-in and viewing the Health Plan on HealthHub, within 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2029
- Special subsidies for Healthier SG screening tests and vaccinations (for eligible Singapore Citizens)
- Healthier SG Chronic Tier subsidy at Healthier SG clinics for CHAS cardholders with high medication requirements, at prices comparable to polyclinic prices
- Full MediSave coverage for treatment of chronic conditions listed under the Chronic Disease Management Programme (CDMP), up to the relevant MediSave withdrawal limit
