# GST Voucher (GSTV) – Cash

## Overview
Enhanced cash payouts of up to $850 from 2024 to help lower-income Singaporeans residing in Singapore with their daily living expenses.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen residing in Singapore
- Aged 21 and above in 2026
- Assessable Income for Year of Assessment 2025 must not exceed $39,000
- Annual Value (AV) of home (as indicated on NRIC) not exceeding $31,000 as at 31 December 2025
- Does not own more than 1 property

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application needed if you have previously signed-up for past GSTV payouts
- If you have never signed-up for past GSTV payouts, apply by logging in to the e-services on the [govbenefits portal](https://www.govbenefits.gov.sg/)

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible Singapore Citizens can receive up to $850 in cash payout
- Payouts are disbursed in August each year
