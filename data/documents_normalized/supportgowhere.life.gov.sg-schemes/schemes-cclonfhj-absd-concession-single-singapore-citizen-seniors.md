# ABSD Concession for Single Singapore Citizen Seniors

## Overview
This scheme fully refunds the Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) for eligible single Singapore Citizen seniors (aged 55 and above) on their second residential property purchase.

## Eligibility
- Must be a single Singapore Citizen senior (aged 55 and above) who purchased a second residential property on or after 16 February 2024
- Must have paid the ABSD on the second property
- First residential property must be solely owned by the applicant, or co-owned with immediate family members (all single Singapore Citizens aged 55 and above)
- The owners of the first residential property must also be owners of the second
- Any additional owners purchasing the second property must also be single Singapore Citizens aged 55 and above who are immediate family members
- Buyer(s) can only own up to 1 residential property each when purchasing the second property
- Buyers must not have purchased or acquired any other residential property since the purchase of the second property
- The first residential property must be sold or disposed of within 6 months of the purchase date of the second property (completed property) or the date of issue of the TOP/CSC, whichever is earlier (incomplete property)
- No change in ownership of the second property at the time of the first property's sale
- The value of the second property must be less than each of the first properties sold
- The application for ABSD refund must be made within 6 months after the date of sale of the first property
- Immediate family members refer to one's parent, child, or sibling

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for the ABSD refund online through the e-Stamping Portal: select "Request" then "Apply for Assessment / Appeal", select "Assessment (including remission and penalty)", indicate whether the document is stamped, and if not, stamp it and pay the ABSD before applying for the concession
- At the "Supporting Documents" page, under "Additional Information", key in "ABSD Concession for Single SC Seniors"
- Submit both: a copy of the exercised Option to Purchase / Sale and Purchase Agreement for the purchase of the second residential property, and a copy for the sale of the first residential property
- For successful applicants, the refund is made to the party liable for payment of stamp duty

## Support / Benefits
Fully refunds the ABSD for eligible seniors on their second residential property purchase.
