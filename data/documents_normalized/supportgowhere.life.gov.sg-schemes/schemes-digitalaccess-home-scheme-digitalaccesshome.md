# DigitalAccess@Home

## Overview
Provides subsidised broadband and laptops/tablets to lower-income households to support their lifestyle needs, including work and social activities.

## Eligibility
- Staying in a HDB flat
- At least one member of your household is a Singapore Citizen
- Monthly household income (MHI) or per capita income (PCI) meeting either of the following:
  - No primary school student or person with disabilities in household: MHI ≤ $1,900 or PCI ≤ $650
  - If there is a primary school student or person with disabilities in household: MHI ≤ $3,400 or PCI ≤ $900
- Check eligibility with the [online self-assessment tool](https://go.gov.sg/digitalaccesschecker) before applying

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Only one application form per household is needed
- Online submission: visit [IMDA's Digital Access Programme site](https://eservice.imda.gov.sg/das/homepage), click "For Applicant", log in with Singpass, complete the prefilled form and submit; household members give consent for data collection via Singpass
- Hardcopy submission: [download the form](https://go.gov.sg/digitalaccess-application-form) or obtain from your nearest [SG Digital community hub](https://go.gov.sg/supportgowhere-sdohubs), complete it, and post to Info-communications Media Development Authority, 10 Pasir Panjang Road #03-01, Mapletree Business City, Singapore 117438
- You will receive an update via SMS/email in 10 working days, or check status by logging into www.digitalaccess.gov.sg with Singpass

## Support / Benefits
- Subsidised fibre broadband, with monthly payment over 36 months
- Subsidised laptop or tablet, with one-time payment
