# Grandparent Caregiver Relief (GCR)

## Overview
Provides tax relief of up to $3,000 to working mothers who engage their parents, grandparents, parents-in-law or grandparents-in-law as caregivers for their children.

## Eligibility
- Working mother who is married, divorced or widowed
- Caregiver resides and lives in Singapore (dependant staying in Singapore on a permanent basis; foreign dependants must have stayed in Singapore for at least 8 months in the previous year)
- Caregiver is a parent / grandparent / parent-in-law / grandparent-in-law (in-laws include those of ex-spouses)
- As of last year, caregiver has been looking after a Singapore Citizen child aged 12 and below / an unmarried child with disabilities
- Caregiver's annual income did not exceed $8,000 from any trade, business, profession, vocation and/or employment in the previous year
- No one else has claimed GCR on the same caregiver; relief is limited to 1 claim for 1 parent/grandparent (a single claim of $3,000)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- For e-Filing (first-time claim): log in with Singpass or SFA at [myTax Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/login/selection) > "Individuals" > "File Income Tax Return" > "File" > "4. Deductions, Tax Reliefs and Rebates" > "Add New" > "Grandparent Caregiver" > fill in details > "Update"
- If previously claimed, the amount will be pre-filled and granted automatically
- For paper filing: fill in the "Grandparent Caregiver Relief" section on Form B1 & B, and include dependant's details in Appendix 2 if it's your first time claiming

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible working mother can claim relief of $3,000
- Caregiver may be the subject of other relief claims e.g. Parent Relief, Spouse Relief
