# Shared Parental Leave Benefit (SPB)

## Overview
Supports eligible working natural or adoptive parents who do not qualify for the Shared Parental Leave (SPL) scheme due to their employment arrangements.

## Eligibility
**For natural parents with live births:**
- Child's birth or estimated delivery date between 1 Apr 2025 and 31 Mar 2026 (entitled to 6 weeks), or on/after 1 Apr 2026 (entitled to 10 weeks)
- Child is a Singapore Citizen or becomes one within 12 months from birth

**For adoptive parents:**
- Not the natural father or mother of the child
- Child's Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date between 1 Apr 2025 and 31 Mar 2026 (6 weeks), or on/after 1 Apr 2026 (10 weeks)
- Child is below 12 months old on FIA date
- If child is not a Singapore Citizen (SC), either adoptive parent has been SC since the child's dependant's pass issuance

**Applicable to all:**
- Employed or self-employed for at least 90 days in the 12 months before the child's birth
- For fathers: lawfully married to the child's mother between conception and birth or within 12 months of birth
- Not eligible for SPL, except under specific conditions such as end of employment contract or retrenchment

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Refer to details on the [GPL Portal](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/shared-parental-leave-benefit)

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 6 weeks of income support (for children/FIA dates between 1 Apr 2025 to 31 Mar 2026), or up to 10 weeks (on or after 1 Apr 2026)
- Government payment capped at $2,500 per week
