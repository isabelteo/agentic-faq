# Fresh Start Housing Scheme

## Overview
Helps families with children who are currently living in public rental flats to own a 2-room Flexi flat or 3-room flat on a shorter lease.

## Eligibility
- Have at least 1 parent and 1 child who are Singapore Citizens
- Applicant and spouse (if applicable) must be aged at least 35 and below 55 years old
- Child must be aged below 21
- Household monthly income of $7,000 and below
- Stayed in public rental flat for at least 1 year, without accumulating 3 or more months of rental arrears in the preceding 12 months
- Have not received any public rental tenancy discount under Relocation, Sale of Flat to Sitting Tenants, or Rent & Purchase Schemes
- Do not own other properties overseas or locally, and have not disposed of any within the last 30 months

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online at the [HDB website](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/AA11AWFreshStartAppWeb/AA11PFreshStartNote)

## Support / Benefits
- Both first-timer and second-timer families can purchase a 2-room Flexi or 3-room Standard flat on a shorter lease in BTO, SBF, or open booking of flats; HDB Concessionary Loan is subject to credit assessment
- First-timer families: Enhanced CPF Housing Grant of up to $120,000
- Second-timer families: Fresh Start Housing Grant of $75,000 ($60,000 disbursed into CPF Ordinary Accounts before key collection; $15,000 may be disbursed after key collection over 5 years); resale levy cap of $30,000
