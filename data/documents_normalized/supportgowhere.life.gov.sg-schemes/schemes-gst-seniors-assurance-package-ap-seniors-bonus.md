# Assurance Package (AP) Seniors' Bonus

## Overview
Cash payouts of $600 to $900 for eligible Singapore Citizens aged 55 and above, disbursed in February over 3 years, from 2023 to 2025.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen residing in Singapore
- Aged 55 and above in 2025
- Assessable Income for Year of Assessment 2023 must not exceed $34,000
- Annual Value of home (as indicated on NRIC) not exceeding $31,000 as at 31 July 2024
- Does not own more than one property

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required — payouts are disbursed automatically

## Support / Benefits
