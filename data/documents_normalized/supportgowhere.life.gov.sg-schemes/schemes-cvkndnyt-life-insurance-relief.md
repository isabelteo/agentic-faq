# Life Insurance Relief

## Overview
Provides tax relief of up to $5,000 for individuals who paid annual insurance premiums on their own life insurance policies.

## Eligibility
- You have paid, and are claiming, for your own life insurance policy
- Married men may also claim for premiums paid on their wife's life insurance policy
- The insurance company has an office or branch in Singapore, and the Singapore office should perform similar activities as the overseas company where the policy was purchased
- Your / your wife's total CPF contributions (compulsory employee's CPF contribution, and compulsory MediSave or voluntary CPF contribution for self-employed individuals) for the previous year were less than $5,000

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- If previously approved and circumstances have not changed, the relief amount is pre-filled and automatically granted
- For e-Filing (first-time claim): log in with Singpass or Singpass Foreign user Account (SFA) at [myTax Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/login/selection) > "Individuals" > "File Income Tax Return" > "File" > "4. Deductions, Tax Reliefs and Rebates" > "Add New" > "Life Insurance" > fill in details > "Update"
- For paper filing: fill in the "Life Insurance Relief" section on Form B1 & B; if claiming for the first time, provide details of premiums paid in Appendix 2

## Support / Benefits
- The amount of relief is capped at the lower of:
  - Difference between $5,000 and your CPF contribution; or
  - Up to 7% of the insured value of your own / your wife's life, or the amount of insurance premiums paid
