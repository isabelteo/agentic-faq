# SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career)

## Overview
For Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above, to pay for or offset out-of-pocket course fees for selected courses oriented towards meeting industry and employment needs.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- Aged 40 and above

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Use the credit to offset nett course fees for [selected courses](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?q=Tags%3A%22SkillsFuture%20Credit%20(Mid-Career)%22&search=true&jumpstart=false) with better employability outcomes
- Refer to the [SkillsFuture Level-Up Programme](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/education-career-personal-development/SkillsFuture_Level-Up_Programme.html) and [SkillsFuture Credit](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/education-career-personal-development/SkillsFuture_Credit.html) pages for more details

## Support / Benefits
- Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above as of 1 Jan 2024 received a $4,000 top-up in May 2024, which does not expire (the outstanding balance from the 2020 Additional SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career Support) top-up is now merged into this credit)
- Singapore Citizens turning 40 after 2024 receive their $4,000 top-up in end-January of the year they turn 40
- Can be used on top of existing SWDA course fee subsidies
