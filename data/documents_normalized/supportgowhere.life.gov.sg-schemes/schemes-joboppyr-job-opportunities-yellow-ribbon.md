# Job Opportunities (Yellow Ribbon)

## Overview
Provides career support and job access to help ex-offenders reintegrate into the workforce, administered by Yellow Ribbon Singapore (YRSG).

## Eligibility
- Ex-offenders who are Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Contact Yellow Ribbon Singapore at [6513 1537](tel:65131537) or [Career_Services@yellowribbon.gov.sg](mailto:Career_Services@yellowribbon.gov.sg) for more information

## Support / Benefits
- Career advisory services to guide ex-offenders in finding suitable employment and improve access to opportunities
- Preparation for unemployed ex-offenders to re-enter the workforce
- Career retention support to ease re-integration and stay in employment

## Related Issues & Concerns
- recently released and struggling to find a job as an ex-offender
- worried about staying employed after re-entering the workforce
- looking for career guidance to reintegrate into society after incarceration
