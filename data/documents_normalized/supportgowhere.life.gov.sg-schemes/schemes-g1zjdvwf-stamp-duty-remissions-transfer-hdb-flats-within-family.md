# Stamp Duty Remissions for Transfer of HDB Flats within Family

## Overview
Provides remission of Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD) and Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) on transfer of an HDB flat between immediate family members.

## Eligibility
- The incoming lessee must be immediate family of any remaining lessee of the flat and a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Repayment goes to the outgoing lessee's Central Provident Fund (CPF) account
- At least 1 of the flat's current lessees must remain after the transfer, and must have either: acquired the flat and paid stamp duty on its initial acquisition; acquired the flat via mutual exchange and paid stamp duty then; acquired the flat by assent, distribution or pursuant to a will; or acquired the flat via the right of survivorship as a joint tenant

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the [e-Stamping Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/estamping/) to e-Stamp and obtain the remission certificate
- To e-Stamp, prepare written confirmation from HDB that the incoming lessee will form a family nucleus with the remaining lessee and is authorised by HDB to stay in the flat
- If there is no incoming lessee, HDB confirmation is not required

## Support / Benefits
- Remission on Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD) and Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) for HDB flat transfers among immediate family
