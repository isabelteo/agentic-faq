# Majulah Package – Retirement Savings Bonus

## Overview
The Majulah Package Retirement Savings Bonus is a one-off bonus of $1,000 or $1,500 for seniors with CPF retirement savings below the 2023 Basic Retirement Sum, given in December 2024.

## Eligibility
- Born in 1973 or earlier
- Singapore Citizen residing in Singapore
- CPF retirement savings below the 2023 Basic Retirement Sum of $99,400 (retirement savings refers to the sum of Retirement Account (RA) and CPF LIFE account balances, or the sum of Ordinary Account and Special Account balances if RA has not yet been created)
- Lives in a property with an Annual Value of up to $25,000
- Does not own more than 1 property

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required

## Support / Benefits
- $1,000 or $1,500 one-off Retirement Savings Bonus, tiered by CPF retirement savings
