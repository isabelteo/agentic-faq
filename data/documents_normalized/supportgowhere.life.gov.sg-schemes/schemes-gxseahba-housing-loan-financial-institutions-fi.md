# Housing Loan from Financial Institutions (FI)

## Overview
Supports flat purchases through housing loans from Financial Institutions (FI) regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore, offering an alternative to an HDB housing loan.

## Eligibility
- Applicants must pay at least 5% of the flat price or flat value (whichever is lower) in cash; the minimum cash payment depends on age, loan period, and any outstanding housing loans
- Applicants purchasing a unit from a property developer under the Executive Condominium Housing Scheme must take a housing loan from an FI
- Loan-to-Value (LTV) limit is up to 75% of the flat purchase price or flat value, whichever is lower; FIs may restrict loans depending on the flat's remaining lease and/or if the repayment period exceeds 25 years or there are additional housing loans

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- [Compare housing loan packages from participating FIs](https://homes.hdb.gov.sg/home/calculator/budget)
- Apply early for an In-Principle Approval (IPA) from participating FIs through HDB's Integrated Loan Application service on the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp28/TimeLine/my-flat-dashboard) for an indicative assessment of loan eligibility
- Obtain a valid Letter of Offer (LO) to confirm the loan offer from an FI before signing the Agreement for Lease for a new flat or exercising the Option to Purchase for a resale flat
- Use the integrated loan application service on the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp28/TimeLine/my-flat-dashboard) to apply for IPA and LO

## Support / Benefits
- LTV limit of up to 75% for both new and resale flats
- Repayment period of up to 30 years
- Option to use any amount in CPF Ordinary Account (OA) for flat purchase, subject to certain applicable limits
- Can refinance the housing loan with another loan from a different FI or a different interest rate package
