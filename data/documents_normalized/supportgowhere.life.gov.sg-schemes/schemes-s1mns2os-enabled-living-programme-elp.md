# Enabled Living Programme (ELP)

## Overview
A community living initiative that empowers adults with disabilities with low to moderate support needs to live independently in public rental flats for as long as possible, through an ecosystem of care and social support including daily living and social skills coaching and community engagement.

## Eligibility
Applicants should:
- Be a Singapore Citizen
- Be aged 35 to 64
- Have a permanent disability based on one or more of: physical disability requiring assistance with at least one of the six Activities of Daily Living (washing, dressing, feeding, toileting, mobility, transferring); moderate visual impairment or worse in the better eye; moderate hearing loss or worse in the better ear; intellectual disability; or autism (refer to the disability profile each ELP site serves)
- Proof of disability provided using the [Disability Verification Form](https://www.enablingguide.sg/disability-verification) completed by a relevant registered healthcare professional
- Not be enrolled in any government-funded adult disability hostel, adult disability residential home, or day activity centre
- Not have any caregivers who are available or capable of caring for them
- Not have significant physical health, mental health, or behavioural needs beyond what ELP can reasonably support
- Fulfil HDB's public rental flat criteria under the Joint Singles Scheme
- Applicants who do not meet all criteria may still be considered on a case-by-case basis

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Submit the online [Interest Form](https://form.gov.sg/68ef4f88c0ad227e08a66dcb) to SG Enable
- The relevant Service Provider will reach out for assessment within 10 working days (submitting the form does not guarantee enrolment)
- ELP sites and appointed service providers are listed on the [Enabling Guide](https://www.enablingguide.sg/elp-and-hsp), including sites at Ang Mo Kio, Bedok, Bukit Merah, Jurong West, and Woodlands, each serving different disability profiles with different service providers

## Support / Benefits
- Basic support such as home maintenance, community engagement, daily living and social skills coaching, and referral to other agencies as needed, to enable independent living and social engagement in the community
