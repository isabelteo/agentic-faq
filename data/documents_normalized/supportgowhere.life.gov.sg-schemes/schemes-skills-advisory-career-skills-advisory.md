# Career and Skills Advisory

## Overview
Offers personalised advice, delivered through SWDA and partners including NTUC's e2i and Career Matching Providers Ingeus and AngusKnight Singapore, to help navigate job search challenges and identify skills and training needs.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens, Permanent Residents, and foreign spouses who are Long-Term Visit Pass/Long-Term Visit Pass Plus Holders with Pre-Approved Letter of Consent or Letter of Consent

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Make an appointment at the [Lifelong Learning Institute](https://careershorizon.mycareersfuture.gov.sg/appointment) or [other service touchpoints](https://www.lifelonglearningsg.org/advice/skills-and-training-advisory)

## Support / Benefits
- Skills and training recommendations to support career transition or progression
- Career exploration and planning
- Resume and interview guidance
- Job search strategies and resources

## Related Issues & Concerns
- struggling with job search and not sure what skills to build
- want personalised career advice before switching jobs
- need help improving my resume and interview skills

- where do I book an appointment for career advisory
