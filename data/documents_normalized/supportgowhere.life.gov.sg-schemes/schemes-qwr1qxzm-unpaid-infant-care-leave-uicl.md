# Unpaid Infant Care Leave (UICL)

## Overview
Provides unpaid leave for eligible parents with a Singapore Citizen child below 2 years old, to help with infant care needs.

## Eligibility
- Child is a Singapore Citizen below 2 years old
- Parent has served current employer for at least 3 months
- For adoptive parents, eligibility starts after the Adoption Order is passed

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Inform your employer in advance of your intention to take UICL and get their approval

## Support / Benefits
- 12 days of unpaid leave per relevant period for each parent, starting from 1 January 2024
- 6 days of unpaid leave per relevant period for each parent, before 1 January 2024
- The relevant period is the calendar year, or any 12-month period mutually agreed between employer and employee; if self-employed or no mutual agreement exists, the relevant period defaults to the calendar year (1 Jan to 31 Dec)
