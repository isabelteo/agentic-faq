# National Silver Academy

## Overview
National Silver Academy offers learning opportunities, short-term courses and programmes for seniors aged 50 and above, administered by the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC).

## Eligibility
- For short courses offered by post-secondary education institutions (PSEIs): Singaporeans aged 50 and above receive a subsidy of up to 50% off course fees
- For courses run by community-based organisations: Singaporeans and PRs aged 50 and above receive a subsidy of up to 80% off course fees

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Refer to each course listing on the AIC website for the application process

## Support / Benefits
- Subsidised learning opportunities through a network of post-secondary education institutions and community-based organisations across Singapore
