# Post-Secondary Education Account (PSEA)

## Overview
The Post-Secondary Education Account (PSEA) provides educational financial support for eligible Singapore Citizens' post-secondary education.

## Eligibility
- You must be a Singapore Citizen with a balance in either the Child Development Account (CDA) when it is closed, or the Edusave Account when turning 17 years old or no longer studying in an MOE-funded school, whichever is later
- The PSEA will also be opened for Singapore Citizens who are eligible for PSEA top-ups by the Government, recipients of the first payment of the National Service Housing, Medical and Education (NS HOME) Awards, or recipients of the annual education bursary under the Home Ownership Plus Education (HOPE) Scheme

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required

## Support / Benefits
- PSEA Funds can be used to pay for one's own or siblings' approved education programmes at approved institutions, and government education loans and financial schemes
- PSEA balances earn interest at the same rate as the CPF Ordinary Account (CPF-OA), currently 2.5% per annum
- Unused PSEA funds will be transferred to the CPF-OA when the account holder turns 31, subject to CPF-OA usage rules
