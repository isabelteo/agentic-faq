# Community Care Apartments (CCAs)

## Overview
Community Care Apartments (CCAs) are senior-friendly housing integrated with care and social services that can be scaled according to care needs, supporting seniors aged 55 and above to age independently within the community.

## Eligibility
- Applicant must be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
- If applying with a spouse, or parent(s)/child(ren), at least one must be an SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR); a spouse who is not an SC or SPR must be included as a core occupier
- All applicant(s) and their spouse(s) must be at least 55 years old at the time of HFE letter application
- Monthly household income should not exceed $16,000
- If any person listed in the application owns or has an interest in any HDB flat or private residential property, it must be disposed of within 6 months from completion of the flat purchase
- Applicant(s) and their spouse(s) are not eligible to buy a CCA if they had previously enjoyed 2 or more housing subsidies, one of which was a Studio Apartment, or a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat or CCA
- Some applicants may need to make an additional payment on top of the flat price (e.g. resale levy)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter early to be informed of your eligibility to buy a CCA and CPF housing grant amounts
- Visit the HDB Flat Portal for details on flats offered in the upcoming Build-To-Order (BTO) exercise; Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercise details are available during the sales exercise
- With a valid HFE letter, apply online via the HDB Flat Portal during the application period of a sales exercise
- From 4 weeks after the release of ballot results, you will be invited to book a flat
- Within 9 months after booking a flat, you will be invited to sign the Agreement for Lease
- When your booked flat is ready, you will be invited to sign the Basic Service Package (BSP) Agreement and collect the keys

## Support / Benefits
- Senior-friendly design features such as easy-to-slide partitions, built-in wardrobe and cabinets, and a wheelchair-accessible bathroom with slip-resistant flooring and grab bars
- Flexibility to choose a lease ranging from 15 to 45 years (in 5-year increments), lasting the applicant(s) and spouse(s) until at least age 95
- Eligible first-timers may apply for the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (for Families or Singles) for the flat purchase
- All residents must subscribe to the mandatory Basic Service Package (BSP), which includes help from CCA staff with simple home fixes, arranging add-on care and support services, and 24-hour emergency response
- Optional services available for Activities of Daily Living (ADL) assistance, fall monitoring, housekeeping and meal services
- Eligible CCA applicants requiring permanent assistance with at least one ADL may qualify for means-tested subsidies for relevant BSP components
