# Deferred Downpayment Scheme (DDS)

## Overview
Provides seniors the option to defer downpayment until key collection when rightsizing to a new flat, paying only stamp duty and legal fees at the Agreement for Lease signing.

## Eligibility
- Flat buyers aged 55 and above at the time of HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter application
- Have not sold (or completed the sale of) their existing flat at the time of HFE letter application
- Booked an uncompleted 3-room or smaller flat in any HDB sales exercise

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Buyers will be informed during the flat booking appointment if they are eligible

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible seniors can defer downpayment until key collection when rightsizing to a new flat
- Pay only stamp duty and legal fees when signing the Agreement for Lease, within 9 months after booking a flat
- Full payment of the flat's purchase price is deferred until the flat is ready for key collection

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Senior wanting to rightsize to a smaller flat but worried about affording the downpayment before selling their current home
- Unsure if they qualify for deferred downpayment since they haven't sold their existing flat yet
- Confused about what fees are due at Agreement for Lease signing versus at key collection
- Wondering if a larger flat purchase qualifies for this scheme
- Not sure how age is determined for eligibility (at HFE letter application vs. booking)
