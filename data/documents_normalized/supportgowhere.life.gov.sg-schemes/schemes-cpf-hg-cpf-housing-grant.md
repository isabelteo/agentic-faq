# CPF Housing Grant

## Overview
Provides more support for first-time homebuyers to purchase resale HDB flats, through cash grants that can be up to $80,000 for eligible First-Timer families and additional top-ups depending on flat size.

## Eligibility
- Applicant must be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
- At least 1 applicant must be a first-timer i.e. have not received any housing subsidy before
- Average gross monthly household income must not exceed:
  - $16,000 for families
  - $24,000 for extended families
  - $8,000 for singles purchasing a flat on their own
- All applicants and occupiers cannot own or have an interest in a private property in the last 30 months prior to flat application

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- More details on the application can be found on the [HDB website](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/flat-grant-and-loan-eligibility/couples-and-families/cpf-housing-grants)

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible First-Timer families: $80,000 grant for 2-room to 4-room resale flats; $50,000 grant for 5-room or larger resale flats
- Eligible First-Timer singles: $40,000 grant for 2-room to 4-room resale flats; $25,000 grant for 5-room resale flats
- Up to $80,000 for eligible first-time homebuyers purchasing resale HDB flats
- Increased by $30,000 for eligible First-Timer families purchasing 2-room to 4-room resale HDB flats
- Increased by $10,000 for eligible First-Timer families purchasing 5-room or larger resale HDB flats
