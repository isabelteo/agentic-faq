# GRaduate Industry Traineeships @Gov (GRIT@Gov)

## Overview
GRIT@Gov equips fresh graduates with experience and skills, easing their transition into full-time employment, through 3 to 6-month traineeships within public sector agencies.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident; and
  - Graduated or graduating in 2024, 2025 or 2026 from Universities, Polytechnics, Institute of Technical Education (ITE) and other Educational Institutions (e.g. private universities and overseas institutions); or
  - Graduated earlier from above institutions and completed National Service in 2024, 2025 or 2026
- Applicants must have no prior working experience and must be prepared to commit to a full-time traineeship
- Trainees who complete their studies within 2026 but who will only receive their qualification/certification award in 2027 are also eligible

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- [Apply now](https://jobs.careers.gov.sg/?s=grit%40gov&utm_source=c%40gshopfront&utm_medium=click&utm_id=grit-gov) via Careers@Gov
- Fresh graduates considering a role in the Public Service can check the [Early Careers](https://www.careers.gov.sg/join-us/early-careers/?utm_source=gritgovpage&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=gritgovprog) page
- View all vacancies (including for mid-careerists) at the [Careers@Gov job portal](https://jobs.careers.gov.sg/?utm_source=gritgovpage&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=gritgovprog)
- [Submit an enquiry](https://form.gov.sg/6603c569d4c3dae8e1eaf228)

## Support / Benefits
- 3 to 6-month traineeships in Public Service for fresh graduates
- Monthly training allowance ranging from S$1,800 to $2,400, which may vary depending on the scope of each traineeship

## Related Issues & Concerns
- A fresh graduate unsure how to gain work experience without prior employment
- Wondering if traineeships in the Public Service pay an allowance
- Graduated but qualification will only be awarded next year, unsure of eligibility
- Completed National Service and now looking for entry into the workforce
- Considering whether a traineeship or full-time job search is the better first step
