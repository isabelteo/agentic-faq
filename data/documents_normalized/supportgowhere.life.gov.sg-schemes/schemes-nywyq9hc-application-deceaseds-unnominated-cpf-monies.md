# Application for Deceased's Unnominated CPF Monies

## Overview
This scheme handles the distribution of a deceased CPF member's un-nominated CPF monies, in accordance with applicable intestacy laws, for the benefit of next-of-kin.

## Eligibility
- Next-of-kin of a deceased CPF member who wish to make a claim

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online via the "Deceased's un-nominated CPF / Baby Bonus / Edusave / PSEA" option at Public Trustee's Office (PTO) E-Services using Singpass
- If ineligible for Singpass, apply for a login ID and password via PTO E-Services ("Login" > "Register as Individual")
- Refer to Annex A (for Muslims) and Annex B (for non-Muslims) for documents required
- If the deceased was a Muslim, apply for a Certificate of Inheritance via the Syariah Court
- Attach required documents in the online submission, or send via e-services or post to Public Trustee's Office, The URA Centre (East Wing), 45 Maxwell Road, #07-11, Singapore 069118, indicating the case reference number and NRIC of the deceased
- A case officer will be assigned after filing the online application

## Support / Benefits
- Un-nominated CPF monies are distributed according to applicable intestacy laws
- If beneficiaries include minors, the Public Trustee's Office can hold the money in trust until they reach 21 years old
