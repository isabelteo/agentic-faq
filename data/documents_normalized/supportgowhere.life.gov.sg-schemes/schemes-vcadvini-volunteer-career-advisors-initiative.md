# Volunteer Career Advisors Initiative

## Overview
The Volunteer Career Advisors Initiative is a community-driven effort connecting individuals with industry experts from diverse sectors who volunteer their time and expertise, to support career resilience and growth.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the [VCA App](https://go.gov.sg/vcai-app) for more information and to view the list of Volunteer Career Advisors
- Hotline: 6669 7932
- [Submit an enquiry](mailto:capo@wsg.gov.sg)

## Support / Benefits
- Sector- and occupation-specific career advice from industry veterans
- Connection to relevant government programmes and services
- Insights into industry trends and skills to inform career decisions

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Feeling unsure how to navigate a career change without professional guidance
- Wanting advice from someone experienced in a specific industry or sector
- Unsure which government career programmes to pursue next
- Looking for free mentorship rather than paid career coaching
