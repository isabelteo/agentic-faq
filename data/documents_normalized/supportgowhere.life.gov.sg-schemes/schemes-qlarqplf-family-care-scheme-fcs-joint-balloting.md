# Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Joint Balloting)

## Overview
Helps parents and their child(ren), married or single, to jointly apply for 2 units in the same Build-To-Order (BTO) project, supporting families who want to live closer together.

## Eligibility
- Parents
- Married couples (applicable to fiancé/fiancée applicants)
- Singles above 35

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://safe.menlosecurity.com/https:/services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/)
- If you meet the eligibility criteria during your flat application, apply for this scheme as part of your flat application

## Support / Benefits
- Parents and their child(ren) can make a joint application for 2 units in a BTO project offering 2-room Flexi or 3-room flats
- Successful parent-child pairs each receive a distinct queue position within the respective flat supplies
- HDB will schedule flat booking appointments based on the respective queue positions, which may take place separately
