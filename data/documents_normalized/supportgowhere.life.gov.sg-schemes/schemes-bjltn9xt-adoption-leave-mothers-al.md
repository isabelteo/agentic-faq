# Adoption Leave for Mothers (AL)

## Overview
Adoption Leave (AL) provides up to 12 weeks of leave for eligible adoptive mothers to care for their adopted child.

## Eligibility
- You are not the natural mother of the child
- Adopting a child in accordance with any Singapore law relating to the adoption of children
- Child is below 12 months old on your Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date
- FIA date on or after 1 July 2017
- You are employed or self-employed for at least 3 months before the FIA date
- If the child is not a Singapore citizen on the adoption application date, they must have a Dependent's Pass (DP) and either parent must have been a Singapore citizen since the issue date

## How to Apply / Next Steps
For employees:
- Notify your employer about your leave arrangement early
- Submit the declaration form (AL1) to your employer with necessary documents at least one week before starting AL, or within one week after starting
- Apply for AL as per your employer's leave application procedures

For self-employed:
- Record your AL dates
- Submit your claim online via the Government-Paid Leave (GPL) Portal after starting the government-paid portion of AL
- Claims should be submitted within 3 months after the last day of AL

For employers:
- Obtain and verify the declaration form (AL1) and supporting documents from your employee
- Keep the declaration form or records for 5 years from the last AL date
- Submit your claim for reimbursement online via the GPL Portal after your employee has taken the government-paid portion of AL, within 3 months after the last date of the employee's AL

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 12 weeks of Adoption Leave for mothers to care for their adopted infant
- For the first and second child, the employer pays for the first 4 weeks; the government reimburses the 5th to 12th week, capped at $10,000 per 4 weeks, up to $20,000 per child order
- For the third and subsequent child, the government reimburses all 12 weeks, capped at $10,000 per 4 weeks, up to $30,000 per child order
- Option to share up to 4 weeks of Adoption Leave with the adoptive father under the Government-Paid Shared Parental Leave scheme
