# Mid-Career Pathways Programme (MCPP)

## Overview
A full-time attachment programme for mature, mid-career individuals seeking a career switch, offering 4 to 6 months attached to a host organisation to gain industry-relevant skills and experience.

## Eligibility
- All Singaporeans and Permanent Residents aged 40 and above

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for attachment opportunities through [MyCareersFuture](https://mycareersfuture.gov.sg/)

## Support / Benefits
- Full-time attachment opportunities of 4 to 6 months to widen professional networks and gain industry-relevant skills and experience
- Training allowance of $1,800 to $3,800 per month for the duration of the attachment, with the government co-funding 70% of the training allowance (amount varies depending on the scope of the attachment proposed by the host organisation)
- Trainees may be offered a full-time role with the host organisation if performance during the attachment is assessed satisfactory
