# Earned Income Relief

## Overview
Earned Income Relief provides tax relief of up to $8,000 (up to $12,000 for Persons with Disabilities) for individuals who are gainfully employed or carrying on a trade, business, profession or vocation.

## Eligibility
- Employed or self-employed, and received taxable earned income from employment, pension, or trade/business/profession/vocation in the previous year
- For higher relief, Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) must have a permanent disability or mental impairment affecting work ability: physical disability requiring assistance with any of 6 Activities of Daily Living (washing/bathing, dressing, feeding, toileting, transferring, mobility), or mental disability impaired in self-care/ADLs, compliance to psychiatric treatment, or education/work

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Relief is granted automatically if you have taxable earned income
- If claiming Earned Income Relief (Disability) for the first time: email a completed Tax Relief Form ("Application for Claim of Disability-Related Tax Reliefs") and supporting documents from the disability association via myTax Mail
- Complete your Income Tax Return: for e-Filing, enter disability details under "4. Deductions, Reliefs and Parenthood Tax Rebate" > "Earned Income Relief"; for paper filing, include handicap details in Appendix 2
- Once claimed, relief is granted automatically in subsequent years if eligible

## Support / Benefits
- Tax relief of up to $8,000, depending on age
- Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) granted increased relief of up to $12,000
