# Service and Conservancy Charges (S&CC) Rebate

## Overview
Provides quarterly rebates to offset Service and Conservancy Charges (S&CC) fees for HDB households, given as 1.5 to 3.5 months of the GST Voucher (GSTV) – S&CC Rebate.

## Eligibility
A HDB household is eligible if all of the following are met:
- At least one Singapore Citizen flat owner or occupier in the flat
- The flat owner(s) and essential occupier(s) do not own or have any interest in a private property
- The flat owner(s) have not rented out the whole flat

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required — rebates are credited directly to the household's S&CC account managed by the respective Town Council
- Rebates are given out in January, April, July and October
- Residents can check or enquire about eligibility using the e-Services for [purchased flats](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/AG10AWScRebate/AG10SRebatesMain) and [rental flats](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/AG10AWScRebate/AG10SRentalRebatesMain)

## Support / Benefits
- Offsets between 1.5 and 3.5 months of S&CC in each Financial Year
