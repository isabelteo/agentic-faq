# Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS)

## Overview
Reserves up to 60% of Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) and up to 40% of Build-To-Order (BTO) flats for eligible first-timer buyers who are families or expecting a child.

## Eligibility
- You and your spouse must be first-timer buyers
- Have a Singapore Citizen child (natural or legally adopted) aged 18 and below
- Expecting a child and have a doctor's certification of pregnancy

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain a HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/)
- If eligible during your HFE letter application, apply for this scheme in your flat application

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 40% of BTO flats and up to 60% of SBF flats are set aside for first-timer buyers
- You can apply for the FPPS priority scheme in addition to 1 other priority scheme
- A computerised ballot is conducted for each priority scheme (e.g. if unsuccessful under the Third Child Priority Scheme, your application is balloted again under FPPS)
