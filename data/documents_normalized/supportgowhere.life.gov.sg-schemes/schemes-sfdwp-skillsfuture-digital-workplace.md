# SkillsFuture for Digital Workplace

## Overview
Equips Singapore Citizen and Permanent Resident adults with essential digital skills for the evolving digital economy, and can also be used by employers to upskill their employees.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Employers can also tap on SFDW2.0 to equip their employees with necessary aptitudes and skills

- Learn to safely navigate technology-rich environments with cybersecurity awareness
- Master various digital tools, including nationally recognized platforms, for strategic data utilization
