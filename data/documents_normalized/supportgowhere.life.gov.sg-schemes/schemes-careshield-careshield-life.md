# CareShield Life

## Overview
CareShield Life provides basic financial protection for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents with severe disability, especially during old age and long-term care needs, through monthly cash payouts.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents who are CareShield Life policyholders
- Assessed by an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor as requiring full assistance with at least 3 out of the following 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs): Washing, Dressing, Feeding, Toileting, Walking or moving around, Transferring

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online at the AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)
- Apply via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- Contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the application; a list of assessors is available on AIC's website

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly cash payouts if you remain severely disabled and meet the eligibility criteria for claims
- Monthly cash payout for 2026 is $689 and increases annually until age 67 or whenever a successful claim is made, whichever is earlier
