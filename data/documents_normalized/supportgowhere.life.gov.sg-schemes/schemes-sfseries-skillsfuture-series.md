# SkillsFuture Series

## Overview
Offers short, industry-relevant training programmes for in-demand skills in the Care, Digital, Green, and Industry 4.0 sectors, across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced proficiency levels.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents; criteria may vary depending on the course

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the [MySkillsFuture portal](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/index.html) and log in with Singpass to access the course directory
- Search for SkillsFuture Series courses under [Care Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1%3A%5B2022-12-15T00%3A00%3A00Z%20TO%20*%5D&fq=IsValid%3Atrue&q=care%20economy), [Digital Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1%3A%5B2022-12-15T00%3A00%3A00Z%20TO%20*%5D&fq=IsValid%3Atrue&q=digital%20economy), [Green Economy](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1%3A%5B2022-12-15T00%3A00%3A00Z%20TO%20*%5D&fq=IsValid%3Atrue&q=green%20economyI), or [Industry 4.0](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-directory.html?fq=Course_Supp_Period_To_1%3A%5B2022-12-15T00%3A00%3A00Z%20TO%20*%5D&fq=IsValid%3Atrue&q=industry%204.0)
- Select a course at the Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced level according to your proficiency, and follow the sign-up instructions

## Support / Benefits
- Training across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels to match different skill proficiencies
- Acquire foundational or advanced skills to boost career prospects and enhance employability
