# CPF Housing Grant (Families) - Top-Up Grant

## Overview
Provides up to $40,000 in housing grants to eligible families who previously received the CPF Housing Grant for Singles and now qualify for the CPF Housing Grant for Families, for example after marriage.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen (SC) aged 21 and above, with spouse or child who is a SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR)
- Previously received the CPF Housing Grant for Resale Flats (Singles) or bought a 2-room Flexi flat from HDB as a single, with other single citizens, or with a non-resident spouse; must not have taken any housing subsidy other than the Singles Grant or purchase of a 2-room Flexi flat
- Average gross monthly household income must not exceed: $16,000 if a Singles Grant recipient who bought an HDB resale flat or a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat; $8,000 if bought a 2-room Flexi flat on a 99-year lease
- You, your spouse, and other persons listed in the application must not have ownership or interest in any private residential property, local or overseas, with no disposal of such property within the last 30 months prior to application
- Must not have ownership or interest in more than one non-residential private property, with no disposal within the last 30 months prior to application

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- [Submit your application for the Top-Up Grant](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/AB22AWTopUpGrantWeb/AB22PMain) within 6 months of your marriage registration or spouse/child becoming a SC/SPR

## Support / Benefits
- Up to $40,000 in Top-Up Grant, directly credited into all eligible SC and SPR members' CPF Ordinary Accounts
- May also apply for other grants (e.g. Enhanced Housing Grant and Proximity Housing Grant), if eligible
