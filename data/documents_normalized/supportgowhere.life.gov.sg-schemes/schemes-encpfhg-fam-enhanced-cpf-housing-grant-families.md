# Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (Families)

## Overview
Provides up to $120,000 for eligible first-time applicants applying as a family to assist with new or resale flat purchases, with lower-income households receiving larger grants.

## Eligibility
- Applicants forming a core family nucleus, consisting of the core applicant(s) and occupier(s)
- Average gross monthly household income of $9,000 or below for first-timer households (including all applicants and occupiers)
- For mixed households (first-timer and second-timer), half of the average gross monthly household income must not exceed $4,500
- If buying a flat with parents, you and at least 1 parent must be a Singapore Citizen, and both parents must be first-timers meeting all eligibility conditions
- Have worked continuously for at least 12 months, 2 months before the HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter application, and were employed at the time of the HFE letter application
- Flat has a remaining lease of more than 20 years
- To qualify for the full grant, flat must have sufficient remaining lease to cover the youngest core applicant and/or core occupier to age 95
- Must not own, have an interest in or disposed of any private residential property (local or overseas) within the last 30 months
- All persons listed in the HFE application can, as a household, own up to 1 non-residential property at HFE letter application and up to 30 months before

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) to determine eligibility for this Grant and others
- If eligible, apply for the grant when submitting your resale application or after you have booked a flat from HDB
- The Grant will be credited to the CPF Ordinary Accounts of eligible household members; only core applicants can use their share of the housing grants to pay for the flat purchase

## Support / Benefits
- Up to $120,000 for applicants purchasing a new or resale flat as a family
- Up to $60,000 for couples comprising a first-time and second-time applicant
- Grant amount is based on average gross monthly household income, with lower-income households receiving more support
- Can be used to offset the purchase price of the flat, or reduce the housing loan required
