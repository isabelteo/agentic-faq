# MOE Higher Education Student Loan (HESL) Scheme

## Overview
Helps students in MOE-subsidised diploma, undergraduate or postgraduate programmes finance tuition fees and borrow a living allowance loan for other education-related expenses, including institution-approved overseas student programmes for undergraduates.

## Eligibility
- Diploma students: SC, PR and international students (IS) studying full-time MOE-subsidised diploma courses in local polytechnics; SC and PR in ITE diploma courses; SC and PR in diploma/undergraduate programmes at LASALLE, NAFA and UAS
- Undergraduate/postgraduate: SC, PR and IS studying full-time MOE-subsidised undergraduate, full-time postgraduate by coursework, and full-time/part-time postgraduate by research programmes in autonomous universities; SC studying part-time MOE-subsidised undergraduate programmes
- Means-tested loan provisions: SC and PR with gross monthly household per capita income (PCI) of $3,500 or less; IS with gross monthly household PCI of $1,875 or less

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Newly matriculated students in AY2026 intake, and existing students (before AY2026) without a Tuition Fee Loan (TFL)/Study Loan (SL), can apply via the [StudentLoanSG (SLSG)](https://go.gov.sg/slsg) portal from July 2026
- Refer to the respective institution website for application window and procedures
- Existing students who already have TFL/SL continue receiving their current loan provision with no action needed; for new or additional loan provision, first approach the institution for advice and needs assessment

## Support / Benefits
- Diploma students: base provision covers up to 90% of subsidised tuition fees (depending on citizenship); means-tested loan covers remaining 10% (SC only); living allowance loan of up to $3,200 per academic year
- Undergraduate/postgraduate students: base provision covers up to 90% of subsidised tuition fees; means-tested loan covers remaining 10% (SC undergraduates only); living allowance loan of up to $4,100 per academic year (full-time undergraduates only); loan of up to $12,000 for one institution-approved overseas student programme (full-time SC undergraduates only)
- Loan is interest-free during the course of study; interest and repayment commence upon graduation or leaving the institution

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording tuition fees for polytechnic or university
- need a loan to cover daily living expenses while studying
- planning an overseas student exchange programme and need financing
- international student wondering if I qualify for a study loan
- checking how the student loan repayment works after graduation
