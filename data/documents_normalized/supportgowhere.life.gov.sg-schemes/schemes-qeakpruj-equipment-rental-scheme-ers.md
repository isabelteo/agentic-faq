# Equipment Rental Scheme (ERS)

## Overview
Subsidises the rental of essential care equipment for patients with serious illnesses who have a prognosis of less than a year.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Currently receiving care from a palliative care provider, whether home-based or in a public healthcare institution (PHI)
- Diagnosed to have a serious illness with a prognosis of less than a year
- Assessed by a qualified assessor on the type(s) of equipment required
- Must not be receiving subsidies for the same or similar equipment through other Government assistance schemes (prior assistance is assessed case-by-case)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach the [home palliative care provider](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Home-Palliative-Care) or PHI you are receiving care from to apply for ERS
- If not currently under the care of any home palliative care provider or PHI, reach out to the [Singapore Hospice Council](https://www.singaporehospice.org.sg/#contact-pop)

## Support / Benefits
- Weekly rental fees for approved equipment such as wheelchair/pushchair, hospital bed, oxygen concentrator, pressure relief mattress and commode
- Cost of two-way transport for delivery and collection of rented equipment
