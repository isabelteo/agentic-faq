# Enabling Transport Subsidy (ETS)

## Overview
Supports eligible Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents with disabilities who need to use dedicated transport provided by Social Service Agencies (SSAs) to access programmes such as EIPIC, SPED schools, DACs, SWs, SSCCs, ESLP, EBHs and ESHs.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen (SC) and Permanent Resident (PR)
- Monthly household income per capita of $4,800 and below for SCs and PRs
- Annual Value (AV) of residence reflected on NRIC of $21,000 and below for households with no income
- Persons with disabilities on transport services provided by SSAs and attending EIPIC, SPED schools, DACs, SWs, SSCCs, ESLP, EBHs and ESHs
- From July 2026, SCs and PRs with monthly household income per capita of up to $4,800 will qualify for ETS

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply through SPED schools or SSAs that are arranging your transportation services; they will assist in conducting a means test and submit the subsidy request to SG Enable on your behalf

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 80% means-tested subsidy for transport fees
- From July 2026, additional 15% subsidies for Singapore Citizens born in 1969 or earlier
