# Home Caregiving Grant (HCG)

## Overview
Provides financial support to help with caregiving expenses for households caring for persons with moderate to severe disabilities, via a monthly cash payout of $200, $400 or $600.

## Eligibility
- Care recipient is a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident with a parent, child or spouse who is a Singapore Citizen
- Lives in Singapore
- Always requires some assistance on a permanent basis with at least 3 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs): eating, bathing, dressing, transferring, toileting, walking or moving around
- Monthly per capita household income of $4,800 or less (or property annual value of $21,000 or below for households with no income)
- Households with multiple properties will receive the lowest HCG payout of $200 per month
- Care recipient must not be in a residential long-term care institution

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online at the [AIC eService Portal](https://efinance.aic.sg/)
- Apply at any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us)
- You'll need: a completed Functional Assessment Report from your General Practitioner, a registered Nurse, or a registered Physio/Occupational Therapist; NRIC details; bank details
- Processing time takes up to 4 weeks

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly cash payout of $200, $400 or $600
