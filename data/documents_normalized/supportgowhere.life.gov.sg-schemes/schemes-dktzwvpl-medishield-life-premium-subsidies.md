# MediShield Life Premium Subsidies

## Overview
To keep MediShield Life premiums affordable, the Government provides several premium support measures, such as Premium Subsidies, Pioneer Generation Subsidies and Merdeka Generation Subsidies.

## Eligibility
- Household monthly income per person of $3,600 or below; and
- Living in property with an Annual Value (AV) of $31,000 and below; and
- Not owning more than 1 property
- Pioneer Generation Subsidies: applicable for Pioneers, regardless of household monthly income or AV of residence
- Merdeka Generation Subsidies: applicable for Merdeka Generation seniors, regardless of household monthly income or AV of residence, on top of Premium Subsidies

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- The subsidies that you are eligible for will be automatically applied to your MediShield Life premiums

## Support / Benefits
- Premium Subsidies of up to 60% for eligible lower- to middle-income households
- Pioneer Generation Subsidies of between 40-60%, depending on age
- Merdeka Generation Subsidies of between 5%-10%, depending on age
- As of 1 Apr 2025, subsidy tiers by monthly income per person: $0-$1,500 → 25%-60%; >$1,501-$2,600 → 20%-55%; >$2,601-$3,600 → 15%-40%
- Properties with AV of $21,000 or less get the full listed rates; AV between $21,001 and $31,000 receive 10% less subsidies; Permanent Residents receive half the subsidy rate of Singapore Citizens; residences with AV above $31,000 or multiple properties owned do not receive these subsidies
