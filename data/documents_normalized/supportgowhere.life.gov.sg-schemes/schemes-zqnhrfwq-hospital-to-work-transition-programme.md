# Hospital-to-Work Transition Programme

## Overview
Provides case management, rehabilitation and employment support for persons with acquired disability from accidents or medical conditions who are looking to return to work. Jobseekers get personalised career re-assessment, rehabilitation and job-matching services, and a case manager supports them throughout the transition.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Above 16 years of age
- Medically certified as fit for employment
- Persons with a permanent acquired disability based on any one of the following:
  - Physical Disability: requires some assistance with at least one of the six Activities of Daily Living due to physical impairment
  - Moderate Visual Impairment or worse in the better eye
  - Moderate Hearing Loss or worse in the better ear
- If your condition does not fall under the categories listed above, you may still approach SG Enable for a discussion
- You can check if your disability status is verified on SupportGoWhere by logging in with your Singpass or contacting H2W_SWW@sgenable.sg

## How to Apply / Next Steps
**Preparation of documents required before applying:**
- Disability Verification Form (DVF) (completed by a relevant registered healthcare professional)
- Fitness for Employment and Rehabilitation Form (completed by a relevant registered healthcare professional)
- Hospital-to-Work Application Form (for email/physical submissions only)
- OneMSF Omnibus Consent Form
- Copy of NRIC Front and Back

**For persons with disabilities:**
- Apply online via SupportGoWhere, or
- Email H2W_SWW@sgenable.sg with the completed supporting documents
- Approach SG Enable's Information and Career Centre, located within the Enabling Village, for assistance

**For Healthcare Professionals (HCP) making referrals:**
- Apply online via Partner Portal, or
- Email H2W_SWW@sgenable.sg with the supporting documents filled and endorsed

## Support / Benefits
- Rehabilitation: for clients requiring rehabilitation support, SG Enable collaborates with partner agencies such as SPD and ABLE to provide integrated rehabilitation and employment support services
- Case management: assessing the jobseeker's strengths, job interests and skills, identifying job options; facilitating psychosocial and community support through referrals if needed; recommending relevant training courses, resume building and interview skills; assessing job suitability and workplace accessibility; working with employers on job redesign and workplace modification if needed
- Employment support: vocational re-assessment, training coordination, job matching and placement, and on-site job coaching to help integrate into the workplace
- Personalised rehabilitation and training facilitation, and 1-1 case management services

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Recovering from an accident or medical condition and worried about how to return to work
- Newly disabled and unsure what employment support or rehabilitation services are available
- Already employed but struggling to cope in the workplace due to a disability
- Not sure if their condition qualifies as an "acquired disability" for support
- Healthcare professional wanting to refer a patient for return-to-work support
- Needing help with workplace modifications or job redesign due to a disability
