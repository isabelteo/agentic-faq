# Step-Up CPF Housing Grant (Families)

## Overview
Provides a $15,000 Step-Up CPF Housing Grant to eligible second-timer married couples and families buying a new or resale flat, to offset the flat's purchase price or reduce the housing loan needed.

## Eligibility
- Must be a married couple or family who has taken one housing subsidy before and meets the following conditions:
  - Currently live in a 2-room Standard flat bought from HDB after October 2024, or bought on the open market with CPF housing grant(s); or a 2-room unclassified flat in a non-mature estate bought from HDB after October 1995, or bought on the open market with CPF housing grant(s); and are buying a 3-room Standard/unclassified flat in a non-mature estate, from HDB or on the open market
  - OR currently live in a flat under the Public Rental Scheme and are buying (from HDB) a 2-room Flexi or 3-room Standard flat, or a 2-room Flexi or 3-room unclassified flat in a non-mature estate; or (on the open market) a 2-room or 3-room Standard flat, or a 2-room or 3-room unclassified flat in a non-mature estate
  - Non-mature estates include Bukit Batok, Bukit Panjang, Choa Chu Kang, Hougang, Jurong East, Jurong West, Punggol, Sembawang, Sengkang, Tengah, Woodlands, and Yishun
- At least one core member must have worked continuously for at least 12 months during the assessment period ending 2 months before the HFE letter application, and be working at the time of application
- Average gross monthly household income must not exceed $8,000
- Must not own, have an interest in, or dispose of any private residential property (local or overseas) in the last 30 months
- For resale flats, the remaining lease must be more than 20 years and be sufficient to cover the youngest core applicant and/or core occupier to the age of 95

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for the HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) to check your eligibility for this Grant and others
- If you meet the eligibility criteria, you may apply for the grant when submitting your resale application or after you have booked a flat from HDB

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible applicants can receive a grant of $15,000 to offset the flat's purchase price or reduce the housing loan needed
- The grant is directly credited into all eligible Singapore Citizen (SC) and Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR) member's CPF Ordinary Accounts. Only core applicants may use their shares to offset the flat purchase price or reduce the housing loan required
- Eligible resale flat buyers may also apply for the Proximity Housing Grant (Families)
