# New/Resale Flats (Singles)

## Overview
Helps eligible singles decide if a new or resale flat would be suitable for them, and gives a holistic understanding of their housing and financing options, including access to housing grants.

## Eligibility
**Singles / Two or more singles (all applicants)**
- Be a Singapore Citizen (SC), or include at least 1 other co-applicant who is an SC if applying jointly

**If buying a new flat**
- At least 35 years old
- Monthly household income ceiling not exceeding $8,000
- Have not taken any housing subsidy
- Must not own, have an interest in, or dispose of any private residential property (local or overseas) in the last 30 months
- An additional amount is payable if buying a new flat alone
- Flat type available: 2-room Flexi flat (99-year lease)

**If buying a resale flat**
- At least 21 years old (widowed or orphan) or at least 35 years old (unmarried or divorced)
- For Unclassified/Standard flat: no income ceiling (income ceiling applies only to qualify for HDB housing loan and CPF housing grants, excluding Proximity Housing Grant)
- For Plus flat: monthly household income ceiling not exceeding $16,000
- For Prime flat: monthly household income ceiling not exceeding $8,000
- Must not own, have an interest in, or dispose of any private residential property (local or overseas) in the last 30 months if buying with CPF housing grants
- Flat types: all types (excluding 3Gen flats) in any location; up to 5-room if applying for CPF housing grants

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for a HFE (HDB Flat Eligibility) letter by visiting the [HDB Flat Portal](https://homes.hdb.gov.sg/home/landing)
- The letter gives a holistic understanding of housing and financing options, confirms eligibility to purchase new or resale flats, and determines the amount of CPF housing grants and HDB housing loan you are eligible for

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible first-timer singles may receive housing grants of up to $60,000 for new flats, and up to $115,000 for resale flats
- Eligible singles may apply for a 2-room Flexi flat from HDB or a resale flat on the open market
