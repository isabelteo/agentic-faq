# Settling Outstanding Tax Matters and Bills for a Deceased Person

## Overview
This process enables executors or administrators to settle a deceased person's taxes and outstanding debts using the estate's funds before distributing the remainder to beneficiaries.

## Eligibility
- Executors or administrators of a deceased's estate who are responsible for settling outstanding taxes, debts and liabilities
- The executor or administrator must ensure all outstanding taxes, loans, funeral expenses, and medical bills are paid from the deceased's estate before distributing remaining assets to beneficiaries

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- To file personal income tax for the deceased: provide IRAS via myTaxMail with the legal personal representative's details, a copy of the death certificate, a copy of the Grant of Probate/Letter of Administration (if available), income details from all sources up to the date of death, and applicable tax relief details
- To file an Income Tax Return for Estate or Trust Income: download and complete the Request Form for Form T from IRAS's website, email it to trust@iras.gov.sg to receive a Trust Reference Number and copy of Form T, then mail the completed Form T to The Comptroller of Income Tax, 55 Newton Road, Singapore 307987
- For guidance, consult a lawyer or contact IRAS directly; appointments can be made at least 2 working days in advance

## Support / Benefits
Executors or administrators may act on the deceased's matters including:
- Filing personal income tax for the deceased on income earned up to the date of death
- Filing estate or trust income tax on income generated by the deceased's assets after death during estate administration, or income from assets held in trust
- Settling taxes and other bills such as loans, funeral expenses and medical bills from estate funds before distribution to beneficiaries
