# Government-Paid Maternity Benefit (GPMB)

## Overview
Provides up to 16 weeks of income support for eligible working mothers who do not qualify for Government-Paid Maternity Leave (GPML) due to their employment arrangements.

## Eligibility
**For live births:**
- Child's birth or estimated delivery date is on or after 1 Jan 2017
- Child is a Singapore Citizen or becomes one within 12 months from birth
- Employed or self-employed for at least 90 days in the 12 months before the child's birth
- Not eligible for GPML or maternity leave under the Employment Act, except under specific conditions such as end of employment contract or retrenchment

**For stillbirths:** similar conditions, plus:
- Date of stillbirth is on or after 1 November 2021
- You are a Singapore Citizen at the time of stillbirth, or the child's father is a Singapore Citizen and was lawfully married to the mother at some point between conception and stillbirth

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Record employment contract dates or trade/business periods from the 12 months before the birth
- Submit your claim [online](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/eservices/submit-a-claim) no later than 15 months after the child's birth
- An officer will contact you post-submission

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 8 weeks of income support for the 1st and 2nd child, capped at $20,000
- Up to 16 weeks of income support for the 3rd and subsequent child, capped at $40,000

## Related Issues & Concerns
- freelancer or self-employed and worried about income during maternity
- not eligible for standard maternity leave due to my employment arrangement
- experienced a stillbirth and unsure what support is available
- checking how much maternity income support I can get for my third child
