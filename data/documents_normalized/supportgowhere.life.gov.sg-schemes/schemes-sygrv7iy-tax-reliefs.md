# Tax Reliefs

## Overview
Provides tax reliefs of up to $14,000 to taxpayers who support family members in need, including dependants with disabilities or impairment. This is an umbrella scheme covering several related reliefs: Grandparent Caregiver Relief, Parent Relief/Parent Relief (Disability), Sibling Relief (Disability), and Spouse/Spouse Relief (Disability).

## Eligibility
- Working mothers (married, divorced, or widowed) with a Singaporean child under 12 or an unmarried child with disabilities, and a caregiver who resides in Singapore (for Grandparent Caregiver Relief)
- Taxpayers supporting dependant(s) aged 55 and above, including those with disabilities, living in their household (for Parent Relief/Parent Relief (Disability))
- Taxpayers supporting sibling(s) with a disability/mental impairment in the same household (for Sibling Relief (Disability))
- Taxpayers supporting a spouse with an annual income under $4,000, including those with disabilities (for Spouse/Spouse Relief (Disability))

- Apply via [myTax Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/login/selection)

## Support / Benefits
- Grandparent Caregiver Relief (GCR): tax relief of up to $3,000 for working mothers who engage their parents, grandparents, or in-laws as caregivers for their children
- Parent Relief/Parent Relief (Disability): tax relief of up to $14,000 per dependant for individuals supporting their parents, grandparents, or in-laws, with additional relief for dependants with disabilities
- Sibling Relief (Disability): tax relief of up to $5,500 per dependant for individuals supporting siblings or siblings-in-law with disabilities
- Spouse/Spouse Relief (Disability): tax relief of up to $5,500 for individuals supporting a spouse, including those with disabilities
