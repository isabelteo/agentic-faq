# Sibling Relief (Disability)

## Overview
Sibling Relief (Disability) provides tax relief to individuals supporting their siblings and siblings-in-law with disability.

## Eligibility
- Dependant must have disability or mental impairment (certified by a doctor: disability = requiring assistance in any of the 6 Activities of Daily Living — Washing or Bathing, Dressing, Feeding, Toileting, Transferring, Mobility; mental impairment = impaired in Self Care and Activities of Daily Living, Compliance to Psychiatric Treatment, or Education or Work)
- Dependant lived / has been living with the claimant (on a permanent basis) in the same household in Singapore
- If they live in a separate household, the claimant must have incurred $2,000 or more in supporting them
- No other type of relief has been claimed on the same dependant by someone else (e.g. another family member)
- If multiple claimants are supporting the same sibling or sibling-in-law and claim the relief, it will be shared among the claimants

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- [Apply now](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/ESVWeb/default.aspx)
- If previously approved, amount is pre-filled and granted automatically the following year
- **For e-Filing (first-time claimants):** Log in with Singpass or Singpass Foreign user Account (SFA) at myTax Portal; navigate to "Individuals" > "File Income Tax Return"; select "File"; go to "4. Deductions, Tax Reliefs and Rebates"; select "Add New" > "Sibling"; fill in information and select "Update"; email IRAS via myTax Mail with the completed Application for Claim of Disability Related Tax Reliefs and supporting documents from a disability association or Special Education school
- **For paper filing:** Complete the "Sibling Relief (Disability)" item in Form B1 & B; if first-time claiming, fill in details in Appendix 2 and email IRAS via myTax Mail with the completed Application for Claim of Disability Related Tax Reliefs and supporting documents
- If no longer eligible, withdraw the claim from the Income Tax Return if auto-included
- IRAS hotline: 1800-356-8300
- [Submit an enquiry](https://www.iras.gov.sg/contact-us/individual-income-tax)
- [Visit your nearest branch](https://www.iras.gov.sg/contact-us/locate-us)

## Support / Benefits
- Claim up to $5,500 per dependant (relief of $5,500 granted for each sibling or sibling-in-law supported)
