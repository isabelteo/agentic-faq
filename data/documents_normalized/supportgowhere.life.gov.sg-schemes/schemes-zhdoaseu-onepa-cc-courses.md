# OnePA CC Courses

## Overview
Provides opportunities for residents to pick up new skills and interests through courses offered at Community Centres/Clubs (CCs) and Residents' Networks (RNs), run by the People's Association (PA).

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens, Permanent Residents and Non-Singapore Citizens / Foreigners

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit OnePA to browse and register for courses by logging in with your SingPass account
- Visit your nearest CC to register at the counter

## Support / Benefits
- Diverse range of courses at Community Centres/Clubs (CCs) and Residents' Networks (RNs) covering enrichment, health, hobbies, and fitness
- Industry Guru Series: tailored networking and deep dive learning opportunities to prepare for the evolving job market
- SkillsFuture@PA: use your SkillsFuture Credit (SFC) for courses at CCs
- PA SHIOK Series: customised courses and programmes by PA's Malay Activity Executive Committees Council (MESRA) and Malay Muslim Organizations (MMOs)
- Health and wellness courses including grooming, makeup, skincare, and body care
- Lifestyle and leisure courses in arts, crafts, cooking, and creativity
- Sports and dance fitness courses

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Looking for affordable ways to pick up new skills or hobbies in their neighbourhood
- Wondering if they can use their SkillsFuture Credit for community centre courses
- Interested in wellness or grooming classes but unsure where to find them
- Foreigner or PR unsure if they can join community centre courses
- Looking for networking or upskilling opportunities relevant to the job market
