# CPF Housing Scheme

## Overview
The CPF Housing Scheme enables CPF members to use their Ordinary Account (OA) savings to buy properties and pay for various housing purchase-related costs, if they are owners of the property.

## Eligibility
- The amount of OA savings members can use depends on the remaining lease of the property, the type of housing loan taken, the age of the youngest owner using CPF, and the number of properties you are using CPF for
- Refer to the terms and conditions for use of CPF savings under the CPF Housing Scheme for detailed information

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- HDB or the lawyer acting for you in the purchase must submit an application to the CPF Board with your CPF withdrawal details, along with required documentation
- Check how much OA you can use on the CPF website
- Before your OA savings can be used, you may need to pay certain amounts in cash, depending on the type of property and housing loan
- Savings withdrawn are paid to the property developer, HDB, sellers, banks, lawyers or other relevant entities on your behalf
- To adjust or stop your OA savings for loan payments, log in to HDB e-Services (for an HDB loan) or myCPF (for a bank loan)

## Support / Benefits
- OA savings can be used to cover downpayments, housing loans, stamp and legal fees, and Home Protection Scheme premiums (for HDB flats)
- Upon sale, transfer or disposal of the property during your lifetime, you are required to refund the CPF principal amount withdrawn together with accrued interest to your CPF account(s)
- If you are aged 55 or above and have pledged the property to make up your retirement sum, you will also be required to refund the pledged amount
