# Tech Immersion and Placement Programme (TIPP)

## Overview
Tech Immersion and Placement Programme (TIPP) is an immersive training bootcamp to prepare non-ICT professionals for ICT roles like web developers, user experience designers, and data analysts.

## Eligibility
- **Individuals:** Singapore Citizens who are non-ICT professionals and are interested in switching to ICT job roles
- **Companies:** Singapore registered Institute of Higher Learning (IHL); or Private Education Institution (PEI) with a valid Enhanced Registration Framework (ERF) registered with SkillsFuture Singapore (SSG) for the duration of the project qualifying period
- Other eligibility criteria varies by industry partner and course

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the list of eligible partners and courses and apply directly via the partners' website
- For more details, see the [IMDA TIPP page](https://www.imda.gov.sg/How-We-Can-Help/TechSkills-Accelerator-TeSA/Tech-Immersion-and-Placement-Programme-TIPP)
- IMDA Hotline: 6377 3800
- [Submit an enquiry](https://go.gov.sg/imda-feedback)

## Support / Benefits
- Immersive ICT training courses or mentorship & coaching programmes leading to a portfolio
- Interaction with leading experts in the ICT field
- Subsidised course fees upon completion of the programme

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Wondering how to switch careers into tech without an ICT background
- Worried about the cost of retraining for a new industry
- Looking for mentorship while transitioning into a tech role
- Unsure which training partners or courses qualify for subsidies
- Mid-career and considering becoming a web developer, UX designer, or data analyst
