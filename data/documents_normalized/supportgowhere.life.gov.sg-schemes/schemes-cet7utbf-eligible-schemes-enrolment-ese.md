# Eligible Schemes Enrolment (ESE)

## Overview
Eligible Schemes Enrolment (ESE) is a streamlined process to apply for several long-term care financial support schemes administered by the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) at the same time.

## Eligibility
- Anyone who would like to apply to multiple long-term care financial assistance schemes under ESE
- Aged 21 years and above
- Can make decisions about their long-term care needs

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- If you are currently undergoing a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) for any of the eligible schemes under ESE, inform your assessor that you would like to apply for ESE

## Support / Benefits
Simplifies the application process for those applying for the following long-term care financial assistance schemes under the ESE process:
- CareShield Life
- ElderFund
- ElderShield
- Home Caregiving Grant (HCG)
- Interim Disability Assistance Programme for the Elderly (IDAPE)

## Related Issues & Concerns
- want to apply for multiple long-term care schemes without submitting separate applications
- undergoing a disability assessment and unsure how it connects to other financial assistance schemes
- caring for an elderly or disabled family member and confused by the number of overlapping schemes
