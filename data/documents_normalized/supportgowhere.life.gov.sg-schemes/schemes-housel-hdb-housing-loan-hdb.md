# Housing Loan from HDB

## Overview
Provides eligible buyers with financing via a housing loan to purchase or take over ownership of a HDB flat, with a concessionary interest rate and use of CPF savings for repayment.

## Eligibility
- At least 1 applicant must be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
- Core member(s) in the HFE letter application must not have previously taken 2 or more housing loans from HDB
- Singles aged 35 and above buying a 2-room Flexi flat on a 99-year lease from HDB, or a 5-room or smaller resale flat
- Senior citizens aged 55 and above who are not applying for a 2-room Flexi flat on a short lease or a Community Care Apartment (CCA)
- Average gross monthly household income must not exceed: $16,000 for families; $24,000 for extended families; $8,000 for singles buying under the Single Singapore Citizen (SSC) Scheme
- All persons listed in the application cannot own or have an interest in any private residential property, local or overseas, and must not have disposed of any private residential property in the last 30 months before the HFE letter application
- Second HDB housing loan: additional requirements apply, including CPF refund and cash proceeds usage rules (up to 50% of cash proceeds from disposal of last-owned property, may retain up to $20,000 in CPF OA); commercial interest rate applies if buying before disposing of the existing flat, converting to concessionary rate after disposal

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain a HFE letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/)
- The HFE letter informs you of your eligibility for purchasing a flat and the amount of housing loan and grants you can receive
- Use the [payment plan calculator](https://homes.hdb.gov.sg/home/calculator/payment-plan) to estimate your budget and payments required

## Support / Benefits
- LTV limit of up to 75% for new and resale flats
- Monthly instalments capped at 30% of applicants' monthly income
- Concessionary interest rate for HDB housing loans
- Option to use CPF savings to pay for the loan
- Applicants may retain savings or use them to pay for CPF Home Protection Insurance
