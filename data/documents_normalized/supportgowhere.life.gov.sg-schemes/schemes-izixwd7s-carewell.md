# CAREwell

## Overview
CAREwell is a highly subsidised programme that supports caregivers of persons with special needs in their caregiving role, emotional and mental wellness, decision-making regarding services, and care management.

## Eligibility
- Made affordable to all caregivers of persons with special needs
- Applicants are screened for suitability before being enrolled into the programme

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Interested caregivers or an external agency can click the request link for CAREwell
- Caregivers will receive an email with a link to the 'CSG Caregiver Needs Form'; fill it in and the team will follow up

## Support / Benefits
- Trauma-informed emotional support and mental health advisory for caregivers
- Professional support on service navigation, care coordination, and service integration for meeting dependent and family's social and health needs
- Each family is paired with a Keyworker (a trained professional in social work, healthcare, special needs, or related fields) who assesses needs, establishes a family support plan, and implements strategies to support both caregiver and dependent

## Related Issues & Concerns
- feeling emotionally overwhelmed caring for a family member with special needs
- not sure how to coordinate the different services my dependent needs
- looking for a dedicated professional to help plan caregiving support
- wondering what subsidies are available for caregiver support programmes
