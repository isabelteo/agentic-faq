# Pioneer Generation Disability Assistance Scheme (PioneerDAS)

## Overview
PioneerDAS provides Pioneers with moderate or severe functional disabilities cash payouts of $100 per month for everyday expenses.

## Eligibility
- Must be a Pioneer (status checkable via the LifeSG app's Pioneer Generation e-card or the Singpass mobile app)
- Lives in Singapore
- Must be assessed by an approved healthcare professional to permanently require assistance with at least 3 of the following 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs): feeding, washing, dressing, transferring, toileting, walking or moving around

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online at the AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS): https://efinance.aic.sg/
- Apply via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in
- Apply at any of the AIC Links
- Provide a Functional Assessment Report from an approved healthcare professional, NRIC details, and bank details
- Processing time takes between 6 to 8 weeks

## Support / Benefits
- Cash payouts of $100 per month, which can be used for long-term care needs such as hiring a helper, paying for home care services, or daily expenses
