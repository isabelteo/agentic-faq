# Working Mother's Child Relief (WMCR)

## Overview
Working Mother's Child Relief (WMCR) provides tax relief for eligible working mothers, up to 25% of earned income per child, encouraging married women to stay in the workforce after having children.

## Eligibility
To be eligible for WMCR, you must fulfil all the following conditions in the previous year:
- You are a working mother who is married, divorced or widowed
- You have taxable earned income from employment, through pensions, trade or business, or via profession or vocation
- You have cared for a child who was a Singapore Citizen as at 31 December of the previous year and qualifies for Qualifying Child Relief (QCR) / Child Relief (Disability)
- You may still claim WMCR even for children who have passed away in the previous year
- You may still claim WMCR even if you and/or your husband or ex-husband has already claimed the QCR / Child Relief (Disability) on the same child, subject to conditions (QCR / Child Relief (Disability) claims must be utilised first; the total relief cap of QCR / Child Relief (Disability) plus WMCR is $50,000 per child)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- For e-Filing: if you previously claimed WMCR last year and your circumstances remain unchanged, the relief amount will be pre-filled and automatically granted. If claiming for the first time, log in to myTax Portal using Singpass or Singpass Foreign user Account (SFA), navigate to "Individuals" > "File Income Tax Return", select "File", go to "4. Deductions, Tax Reliefs and Rebates", select "Add New" > "Child", fill in details and select "Yes" under "Claim Working Mother's Child Relief", then select "Update"
- For paper filing: complete the "Child Relief" section in Form B1 & B; if claiming for the first time, indicate 'Y' under "Claim for WMCR" and fill in child's details in Appendix 2
- The WMCR amount will be computed and applied automatically based on your eligibility when your Income Tax Return is processed

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 25% of the mother's earned income can be claimed as tax relief for each child
- Relief amount is cumulative for multiple children, capped at 100% of the mother's earned income
- Relief may be claimed alongside QCR or Child Relief (Disability) on the same child, with total relief cap of $50,000 per child for all 3 combined
- WMCR amount by child order (from YA 2025): 1st child - 15% of mother's earned income (or $8,000 for children born/adopted on or after 1 Jan 2024); 2nd child - 20% (or $10,000); 3rd and beyond - 25% (or $12,000)
