# Long-Term Care Subsidies

## Overview
Provides means-tested subsidies for long-term care services delivered by MOH-funded long-term care providers, covering both home/community care and institutional care.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents
- Monthly per capita household income (PCHI) of less than or equal to $4,800 (or property annual value (AV) of less than or equal to $21,000 for households with no income)
- Receiving long-term care services from MOH-funded providers

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Persons who require long-term care services can approach their doctor at the hospital, polyclinic, or Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) for assistance in applying for admission to a subsidised long-term care service

## Support / Benefits
- For home and community long-term care services (e.g. Day Care, Community Rehabilitation services, Home Nursing, Home Medical): means-tested subsidies of up to 80%, with an additional 15% subsidy for Singapore Citizens born in or before 1969
- For institutional long-term care services (e.g. Nursing Home): means-tested subsidies of up to 75%, with an additional 5% subsidy for Singapore Citizens born in or before 1969

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording nursing home fees for an elderly parent
- need help paying for home nursing or home medical care for a family member
- unsure if household income qualifies for long-term care subsidies
- looking for day care or community rehabilitation services for an aging relative
- older Singaporean wondering if they get extra subsidy for being born before 1969
- don't know who to approach to start applying for subsidised long-term care
