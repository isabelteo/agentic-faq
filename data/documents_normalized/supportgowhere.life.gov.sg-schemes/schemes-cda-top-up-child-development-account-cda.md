# Top-up to Child Development Account (CDA)

## Overview
This scheme provided a one-off $400 top-up to the Child Development Account (CDA) for Singapore Citizens aged 0 to 6, credited from September 2023. Note: the SG Child Support Package announced at the 2026 National Day Rally will replace the current Baby Bonus Scheme and Large Families Scheme; those on the Baby Bonus Scheme will be automatically transitioned, with existing payouts continuing as usual in the meantime.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen child born between 1 Jan 2017 and 31 Dec 2023 (both dates inclusive)
- Children born on 1 Jan 2017 who started primary school in 2023 will receive the Edusave top-up instead
- Eligible child must open a CDA by 30 June 2024

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Eligible child must open a CDA by 30 June 2024, with DBS Bank, OCBC or UOB
