# Withdrawal of CPF Savings for Immediate Needs from Age 55

## Overview
CPF members can make withdrawals from their CPF savings starting from age 55 to meet immediate needs.

## Eligibility
Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents aged 55 and above.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online via the CPF website

## Support / Benefits
- Generally, upon turning 55, members can withdraw at least $5,000 or any amount in excess after setting aside their Full Retirement Sum (FRS)
- Members born in 1958 and after can withdraw an additional amount of up to 20% of their retirement savings when they turn 65
- Property owners with a remaining lease that lasts them until age 95 or older have the flexibility to set aside their FRS with a mixture of property (up to half the FRS, which is half the Basic Retirement Sum (BRS)) and cash, and withdraw their Retirement Account savings above their BRS
