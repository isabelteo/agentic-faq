# NSman Relief

## Overview
NSman Relief provides tax relief to eligible operationally ready National Servicemen (NSmen), as well as to the wives and parents of NSmen, to recognise their contributions and support.

## Eligibility
**NSman Self Relief**:
- Completed full-time National Service under the Enlistment Act, or deemed to have completed such service
- Not a regular in MINDEF, Singapore Police Force, Singapore Civil Defence Force, Central Narcotics Bureau, or Singapore Prison Service
- Have not committed disciplinary or criminal offences in the preceding work year
- If both you and your son are NSmen, eligible for either NSman Self Relief or NSman Parent Relief, whichever is higher

**NSman Wife Relief**:
- Singapore Citizen in the previous year
- Husband is eligible for NSman Self Relief
- Widows of deceased NSmen may also be eligible unless re-married

**NSman Parent Relief**:
- Singapore Citizen in the previous year
- Child is eligible for NSman Self Relief; child was born to you/spouse/ex-spouse, is a step-child, or was legally adopted
- Parents of deceased NSmen are also eligible
- If both you and your son are NSmen, eligible for either NSman Self Relief or NSman Parent Relief, whichever is higher
- Relief amount per parent capped at $750, regardless of how many children are NSmen

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application is required; relief is automatically granted based on eligibility

## Support / Benefits
- NSman Self Relief of $1,500 to $5,000 for eligible NSmen
- NSman Wife Relief of $750 for the wives of eligible NSmen
- NSman Parent Relief of $750 for each parent of the eligible NSman
