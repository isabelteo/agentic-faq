# 2025 Public Transport Voucher (PTV) Exercise

## Overview
Provides lower-income households with Public Transport Vouchers (PTVs) worth $60 each to help cope with the public transport fare adjustment from 27 December 2025. The vouchers can be used to top up fare cards or buy monthly passes.

## Eligibility
- Resident households with monthly household income per person of not more than S$1,800
- At least one member of the household must be a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Households that received a PTV under the 2024 PTV Exercise and continue to meet the income eligibility criterion would have received a digital or physical PTV notification letter (depending on contact preference) by mid-January 2026
- Households that meet the income eligibility criterion but did not receive a PTV may apply [online](https://go.gov.sg/ptv) or in person at their local Community Centres/Clubs (CCs) from 20 January 2026
- Households that missed the income eligibility criterion slightly can request a review of their eligibility through [this link](https://ptvonline.simplygo.com.sg/) or approach their local CCs for assistance
- Applications and appeals will be processed within four to six weeks from the date of application

## Support / Benefits
- PTVs worth $60 each, which can be used to top up fare cards or buy monthly passes

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording public transport fares after the fare increase

- missed the income cutoff by a small margin, can I still get help

- looking for help with daily commuting costs on a low income
