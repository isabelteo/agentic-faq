# Gift Of A Lifetime Plus (GOAL+)

## Overview
GOAL+ provides eligible caregivers of persons with disability (PWDs) with an initial capital of $5,000 to set up trust accounts with the Special Needs Trust Company (SNTC). The Government also sponsors matching donations of up to $10,000 for low- and middle-income families who contribute to the trust account within a five-year window.

## Eligibility
To qualify for GOAL+, the PWD must meet the following criteria:
- A Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- The family has a per capita household income (PCHI) of ≤ $3,600, or annual value (AV) of property ≤ $21,000 for households with a PCHI of $0
- Has a verified disability

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Check if the PWD's disability status has already been verified by logging into SupportGoWhere with Singpass. If the PWD is above 21 years old and does not have Singpass, check disability status by calling the Operator (Bizlink) at 64366635 or emailing msfdisability@bizlink.org.sg
- If not verified, the PWD must get a Disability Verification Form (DVF) completed by a relevant registered Healthcare Professional (a submitted DVF does not itself mean the disability status has been verified)
- Contact SG Enable for an appointment with a Case Manager to find out how the Special Needs Trust works and assess eligibility for GOAL+ sponsorship

## Support / Benefits
- GOAL+ runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031. Eligible caregivers whose PWDs do not have a Special Needs Trust (SNT) account can receive $5,000 from Community Chest to set one up
- Eligible caregivers of PWDs with verified disability can qualify for matching top-ups of up to $10,000 from the Government
- A five-year top-up window applies for eligible SNT accounts; contributions made beyond the five years will not be entitled to matching top-ups from the Government
