# National Service LifeSG Credits

## Overview
National Service LifeSG Credits provide $200 in LifeSG credits for all eligible past and present national servicemen, recognising their contributions to Singapore's defence and security.

## Eligibility
- Full-Time National Servicemen (NSFs) who have enlisted by 31 Dec 2024
- Past and present Operationally Ready National Servicemen (NSmen)
- Disbursements are subject to further eligibility checks
- Eligible national servicemen were notified by SMS and letter in 2024

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required
- Credits have been automatically disbursed via the LifeSG mobile application to eligible national servicemen in 2024
- SMS notification and notification letter were sent after credits were disbursed
- Those unable to access credits digitally may request hardcopy vouchers by contacting the NS Call Centre

## Support / Benefits
- $200 in LifeSG credits, accessible via the 'Your Benefits' section of the LifeSG mobile app
- Valid for 1 year from the date of disbursement (validity period will not be extended)
- Can be spent at online or physical merchants accepting PayNow UEN QR or NETS QR; some merchants offer additional discounts
