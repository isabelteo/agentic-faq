# Government-Paid Childcare Leave (GPCL) and Extended Childcare Leave (ECL)

## Overview
Provides up to 6 days of leave for eligible working parents and legal guardians with a Singapore citizen child below 7 years old (GPCL), and 2 days for those with a child aged 7 to 12 (ECL).

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen child below 7 years old for GPCL, or the child obtains Singapore citizenship within the calendar year
- Singapore Citizen child aged 7 to 12 years old for ECL, or the child obtains Singapore citizenship within the calendar year
- Parent has served their employer for at least 3 months or been self-employed for at least 3 months, and have lost income from ceasing to be actively engaged during the GPCL/ECL period
- Adoptive parents are eligible only after the Adoption Order is passed
- Natural fathers not eligible if not lawfully married to the child's mother at conception and not married subsequently
- Foster parents eligible until they cease providing care, protection or supervision
- Legal guardians with court-appointed guardianship are eligible

## How to Apply / Next Steps
**For employees:**
- Inform your employer of your leave plans
- Foster parents: show your Letter of Identity to your employer as proof that a foster child has been placed under your care under the Children and Young Persons Act; employer can write to the MSF Fostering Service at fostering@msf.gov.sg to verify eligibility
- Legal guardians: provide a copy of your Legal Guardianship document (i.e. Order of Court) to your employer for verification
- Submit a declaration form (GPCL1) with necessary documents
- Apply for leave as per your employer's procedures

**For self-employed:**
- Record your leave dates
- Submit your claim on the Government-Paid Leave (GPL) Portal within 3 months after the calendar year ends

**For employers:**
- Collect and verify the declaration form (GPCL1) from your employee
- Submit your reimbursement claim on the GPL Portal within 3 months after the 12-month period ends

## Support / Benefits
**For parents with a Singapore citizen child below 7 years old:**
- Up to 6 days of GPCL; employer pays for the first 3 days
- Government reimburses the 4th to 6th day, capped at $500 per day, up to $1,500 per year

**For parents with a Singapore citizen child between 7 and 12 years old:**
- 2 days of ECL, fully reimbursed by the Government, capped at $500 per day, up to $1,000 per year
