# Baby Bonus Cash Gift

## Overview
The Baby Bonus Cash Gift helps parents defray child-raising costs through regular cash payouts in their child's early years. Note: the SG Child Support Package announced at the 2026 National Day Rally will replace the current Baby Bonus Scheme and Large Families Scheme; those on the Baby Bonus Scheme will be automatically transitioned, and existing payouts continue as usual in the meantime.

## Eligibility
- Child is a Singapore Citizen
- Children born on or after 14 Feb 2023 qualify for the enhanced Cash Gift announced at Budget 2023
- Parents are lawfully married

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- All parents can apply for the Baby Bonus Scheme at LifeSG, either as a bundled service together with the child's birth registration, or as a direct application for a child who has already registered for birth

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible Singaporean children in the first and second birth order receive $11,000; children in the third and subsequent birth orders receive $13,000
- Cash Gift is disbursed regularly, every six months until the child is six-and-a-half years old
- Paid into the new Child Savings Account (CSA), a joint savings account that opens automatically alongside the Child Development Account (CDA) when a child is enrolled for the Baby Bonus Scheme
