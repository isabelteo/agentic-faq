# Platform Workers CPF Transition Support (PCTS)

## Overview
From 2025 to 2028, PCTS provides monthly cash payments to support eligible lower-income platform workers who see an increase in their CPF Ordinary and Special/Retirement Account contributions.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen only
- Born in 1995 or later, or born before 1995 and opted in to increase CPF contributions
- Net income from platform work (gross earnings minus Fixed Expense Deduction Amount) and other employment sources does not exceed $3,000 per month
- Must have an increase in the platform worker's own share of CPF contributions to the Ordinary and Special/Retirement Account compared to the previous year

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Assessment for PCTS is automatic, no application is required

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly cash payments from 2025 to 2028 to offset part of the year-on-year increase in CPF contributions
- An online PCTS calculator is available to estimate payment amounts
- Payments credited directly to PayNow NRIC-linked bank accounts, registered bank accounts, or via GovCash
