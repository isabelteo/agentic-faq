# Pioneer Generation Package

## Overview
The Pioneer Generation Package provides special recognition to Singapore's Pioneer Generation through benefits that assure them of affordable healthcare, including lifelong monthly cash assistance and healthcare subsidies.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- Born on or before 31 December 1949 and acquired citizenship on or before 31 December 1986
- Eligible applicants were notified in 2014
- Eligibility can be checked via the LifeSG app (Pioneer Generation e-card) or the Singpass mobile app

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required; eligible individuals receive the benefits automatically
- To apply for the Pioneer Generation Disability Assistance Scheme (PioneerDAS), visit the AIC website for more information

## Support / Benefits
- Annual MediSave top-ups for life, ranging from $300 to $1,200 depending on birth cohort, to offset premiums for MediShield Life, ElderShield and CareShield Life
- Additional MediSave top-ups of $50 to $200 disbursed from 2021 to 2025 for older Pioneers (aged 82 and above in 2021) with serious pre-existing conditions paying additional premiums
- Additional subsidies for outpatient care at polyclinics, public Specialist Outpatient Clinics and Community Health Assist Scheme clinics
- Special MediShield Life premium subsidies of up to 60%; combined with MediSave top-ups, Pioneers born in 1934 or earlier have premiums fully covered, and those born 1935-1949 have about two-thirds covered
- Pioneer Generation Disability Assistance Scheme: lifelong cash assistance of $100 each month for those needing permanent assistance with at least 3 of 6 Activities of Daily Living (eating, bathing, dressing, transferring, toileting, walking or moving around)
- CareShield Life Additional Participation Incentives (totalling $3,000) for Pioneers who joined CareShield Life by 31 Dec 2024 — this incentive has now ceased
