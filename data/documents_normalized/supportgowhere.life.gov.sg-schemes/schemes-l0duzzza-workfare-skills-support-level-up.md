# Workfare Skills Support (Level-Up)

## Overview
An enhanced tier of Workfare Skills Support (WSS) that supports lower-wage Singaporean workers who go for long-form training, covering courses such as Nitec, Higher Nitec, Diplomas, Post-Diplomas, and Undergraduate courses.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- At least 30 years old as of 31 December of the preceding year, or at least 13 years old for persons with disabilities
- Earn an average gross monthly income of not more than $3,000 for the past 12 months
- Self-employed individuals must have declared net trade income and made MediSave contributions in full
- Live in a property with an annual value of $21,000 and below as at 31 December of the preceding year
- Own not more than one property
- If married: you and your spouse together not own more than one property, and your spouse's assessable income does not exceed $70,000 for the preceding Year of Assessment
- Criteria are similar to Workfare Income Supplement (WIS) criteria

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online via the [Training Allowance System (TAS)](https://go.gov.sg/wss-level-up-tas) using your Singpass

## Support / Benefits
- Training Allowance (TA): full-time training pays 50% of average income over the latest 12-month period, with a minimum of $300 per month; part-time training pays $300 per month
- Coverage for multiple rounds of long-form training: up to 24 months of monthly TA between ages 30–39, and up to another 24 months from age 40 (shared cap with the SkillsFuture Level-Up Programme); for each age period, the cap is shared between full-time and part-time training
- Courses supported include SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme courses; Nitec, Higher Nitec, Diplomas, Post-Diplomas, and Undergraduate courses; from 2026 Q4, long-form Workforce Skills Qualifications (WSQ) full qualifications
