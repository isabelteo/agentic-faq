# SkillsFuture Work-Study Certificate

## Overview
Provides industry-relevant skills and knowledge related to a discipline of study through a work-study arrangement, combining full-time employment with structured training.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents
- Within 3 years of graduation from the Institute of Technical Education (ITE) and Polytechnics, or within 3 years of the Operationally Ready Date for full-time National Servicemen

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- View the [programme summary and listing](https://programmes.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/WorkStudyIndividualProgrammes/Programme_Summary.aspx)
- Select the programme of interest
- Click "Click here to Apply"

## Support / Benefits
- Full-time employment with a competitive starting salary
- Industry-recognised certificate upon completion
- Sign-on incentive of S$5,000
- Potential for skills-based wage increments and career advancement based on performance
