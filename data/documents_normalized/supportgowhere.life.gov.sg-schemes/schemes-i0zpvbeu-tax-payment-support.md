# Tax Payment Support

## Overview
Supports eligible individuals and businesses facing difficulties in paying their taxes through longer payment arrangements, such as extended GIRO-based instalment plans.

## Eligibility
- Individuals experiencing unexpected or extenuating circumstances such as financial issues, high medical expenses, loss of employment or income, or lawsuits (documentary evidence may be required)
- Retirees applying for the Extended Giro Scheme for Residential Property (Retirees) must meet all of: residing in the property (taxed at owner-occupier tax rates); assessable income for Year of Assessment 2023 does not exceed $34,000; all property owners aged 65 or above in 2024; outstanding property tax payable for the property
- If applicants cease to be eligible, their extended instalment plan reverts to the standard instalment plan

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- For individuals: apply for an extended instalment plan via the 'Apply for Payment Plan' digital service through the IRAS [myTax Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/) (instalment plans only available with GIRO); existing GIRO users must cancel their current plan and set up a new one; those without a GIRO arrangement may apply for GIRO (DBS/POSB or OCBC account) via myTax Portal
- For businesses without an existing GIRO arrangement: apply for GIRO with a DBS/POSB, OCBC, UOB, Bank of China or Maybank account via the myTax Portal and arrange a new plan
- Without an account at participating banks, fill up [this form](https://form.gov.sg/673c038018eb14d441d54153) to request a GIRO application form

## Support / Benefits
- Longer instalment plans for individuals or businesses facing financial challenges
- Extended Giro Scheme for Residential Property (Retirees) offers an instalment plan of up to 24 months for property tax payment

## Related Issues & Concerns
- struggling to pay my income tax or property tax bill on time
- lost my job and worried about outstanding tax payments
- retiree with high medical expenses needing more time to pay property tax
- wondering how to set up or change a GIRO tax payment plan
