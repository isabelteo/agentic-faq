# ElderShield

## Overview
Provides basic financial support to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents with severe disability, especially during old age when they need long-term care. Enrolment as a new policyholder has closed since 1 January 2020; existing policyholders can still claim.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents who are existing ElderShield policyholders
- Assessed by an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor to be unable to perform at least 3 out of 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs): washing, dressing, feeding, toileting, walking or moving around, transferring

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- As of 1 January 2020, applications for enrolment into ElderShield as a new policyholder have closed
- Existing policyholders can apply for claims online at [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/), via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in, or at any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)
- Contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the claim application; list of assessors on [AIC's website](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment)

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly cash payouts of $300 or $400 for up to 5 or 6 years upon severe disability, depending on the ElderShield plan
- Can be used for long-term care needs, such as hiring a helper, paying for care services, or daily expenses
- Processing time takes between 6 to 8 weeks
