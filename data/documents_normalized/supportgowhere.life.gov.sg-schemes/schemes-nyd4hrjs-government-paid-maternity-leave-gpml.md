# Government-Paid Maternity Leave (GPML)

## Overview
GPML provides up to 16 weeks of paid leave for eligible working mothers to care for their newborns, with government reimbursement for employers and support for self-employed mothers.

## Eligibility
**For live births**:
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Child is a Singapore Citizen at birth or within 12 months after birth
- For employees: served employer for at least 3 months before child's birth (GPML granted despite not meeting this criterion cannot be shared with husband under the Shared Parental Leave scheme)
- For self-employed: engaged in work for at least 3 months before child's birth and lost income during maternity leave

**For stillbirths** (similar conditions, plus):
- The date of stillbirth is on or after 1 November 2021
- You are a Singapore Citizen at the time of stillbirth, or the child's father is a Singapore Citizen lawfully married to the mother at some point between conception and stillbirth

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Employees: notify employer at least one week before starting leave; submit the declaration form (GPML1) with necessary documents at least one week before starting leave or within one month from the child's birth; follow employer's leave application process
- Self-employed: keep a record of GPML dates; submit claim online via the Government-Paid Leave (GPL) Portal after starting the government-paid portion of GPML; claims must be made within three months after the last day of GPML
- Employers: obtain and verify the GPML1 declaration form from the employee; keep records for five years; submit claim for reimbursement online via the GPL Portal within three months after the last day of the employee's GPML

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 16 weeks of Government-Paid Maternity Leave
- For the first and second child: employer pays the first 8 weeks, government reimburses the 9th to 16th week, capped at $10,000 per 4 weeks, up to $20,000 per child order
- For the third and subsequent child: government reimburses all 16 weeks, capped at $10,000 per 4 weeks, up to $40,000 per child order
- Option to share up to 4 weeks of GPML with the spouse under the Government-Paid Shared Parental Leave scheme
