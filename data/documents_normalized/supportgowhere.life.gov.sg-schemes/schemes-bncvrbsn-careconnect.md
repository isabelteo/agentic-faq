# CAREconnect

## Overview
CAREconnect offers inclusive social events for family bonding and connection to fellow caregivers and volunteers, through support groups, inclusive social and/or respite events, and webinars, for caregivers of persons with special needs.

## Eligibility
Caregivers of persons with special needs.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Click the CaringSG event link to view available events; registration information can be found at each individual event page

## Support / Benefits
- Informative webinars provide valuable caregiving resources and information, accessible from home
- Engaging physical events organised in collaboration with volunteers and grassroots organisations offer caregivers the chance to connect, share experiences, and receive emotional support
- 92% of caregivers agreed the events helped them feel more included in their neighbourhood
- 82% of caregivers at CAREconnect Webinars agreed the sessions helped them learn to care for their child better
- Builds an inclusive and accessible support network for caregivers within local neighbourhoods, in partnership with grassroots organisations

## Related Issues & Concerns
- want to connect with other caregivers of children with special needs
- looking for webinars on how to care for a dependant better
- feeling disconnected from the neighbourhood community as a caregiver
- want respite or social events for caregiving families
