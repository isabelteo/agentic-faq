# Short-lease 2-room Flexi Flats

## Overview
An affordable housing option for senior citizens (and their spouses) aged 55 and above who wish to right-size and continue living independently, with a flexible lease of 15 to 45 years (in 5-year increments) lasting until at least age 95.

## Eligibility
- Applicant must be a Singapore Citizen (SC)
- If applying with spouse, or parent(s)/child(ren), at least one of them must be a SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR)
- If your spouse is not an SC or SPR, they must be included as a core occupier
- All applicant(s) and their spouse(s) (if any) must be at least 55 years old at the time of HFE letter application
- Monthly household income should not exceed $16,000
- If any person listed in the application owns or has an interest in any HDB flat or private residential property, the property must be disposed of within 6 months from the completion of the flat purchase
- Applicant(s) and their spouse(s) (if any) are not eligible if they had previously enjoyed 2 or more housing subsidies, one of which was a Studio Apartment, or a short-lease 2-room Flexi flat or Community Care Apartment (CCA)
- Some applicants will have to make an additional payment on top of the flat price (e.g. resale levy)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply for an [HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) early to check eligibility and CPF housing grant amounts
- Visit the [HDB Flat Portal](http://homes.hdb.gov.sg/) for details on flats offered in upcoming Build-To-Order (BTO) exercises; Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) details are released during the sales exercise
- With a valid HFE letter, apply online via the [HDB Flat Portal](https://homes.hdb.gov.sg/home/landing) during the application period of a sales exercise
- From 4 weeks after ballot results, you will be invited to book a flat
- Within 9 months after booking a flat, you will be invited to sign the Agreement for Lease
- When your booked flat is ready, you will be invited to collect the keys

## Support / Benefits
- Monetisation option to supplement retirement needs
- Flexible lease ranging from 15 to 45 years (in 5-year increments) as long as it lasts until at least age 95
- Pre-installed senior-friendly fittings such as grab bars and easy-to-slide partitions
- Applicant(s) may apply for 1 priority scheme to enhance ballot chances: Senior Priority Scheme (SPS), Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Proximity), or Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Joint Balloting)
- Eligible first-timers may apply for the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (Families or Singles)
- Eligible buyers may apply for the Silver Housing Bonus (SHB) if selling their current flat or private housing with an Annual Value not exceeding $21,000 and buying a 3-room or smaller flat
