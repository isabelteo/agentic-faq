# ITE Special Student Assistance Scheme (SSAS)

## Overview
One-off financial assistance to help full-time ITE students tide over short-term financial difficulties.

## Eligibility
- Full-time Singaporean ITE students who require financial assistance to tide over short-term financial difficulties

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online via the [ITE portal](https://myite.ite.edu.sg/) — click on the iStudent icon > Financial Assistance icon
- If you need more help, refer to the [step by step guide](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/145/c7a7f637-986d-483a-9766-a19bccd726c0/Step%20By%20Step%20Guide%20on%20Applying%20Financial%20Assistance_Bursary_15%20Sep%2025.pdf)

## Support / Benefits
- One-off financial assistance from $100 to $400
- The amount will depend on the needs of each individual student

## Related Issues & Concerns
- ITE student facing a sudden short-term financial difficulty
- need one-off cash help while studying at ITE
- not sure how to apply for financial assistance through the ITE portal
- unsure how much one-off assistance an ITE student can get
