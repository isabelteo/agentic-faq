# Withdrawal of CPF Savings on Reduced Life Expectancy

## Overview
Allows CPF members with reduced life expectancy or certain severe medical conditions, as certified by an accredited doctor, to apply to withdraw some of their CPF savings.

## Eligibility
Singapore Citizen (SC) and Permanent Resident (PR) with any of the following conditions as certified by an accredited doctor:
- Severely reduced life expectancy due to medical condition
- Permanently unfit for work
- Permanently lack mental capacity to make decisions, as defined in the Mental Capacity Act

## How to Apply / Next Steps
**Applying for self**
- Submit [an online application](https://cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/forms-e-applications/withdraw-cpf-due-to-reduced-life-expectancy-and-claim-insurance) after logging in with Singpass
- Download the "Medical Assessment Report" form provided upon submission and have it completed by an [accredited doctor](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/who-is-considered-an-accredited-doctor); the medical institution submits the completed report to CPF Board directly
- If mailing documents from overseas, all documents must be witnessed/certified true by an official of a Singapore Overseas Mission with official seal/stamp

**Applying as a donee or deputy for your next-of-kin**
- Submit [this form](http://cpf.gov.sg/applyrlehpsdps) with a copy of your NRIC attached, plus original documents: a medical report from an accredited doctor stating the member permanently lacks mental capacity; Lasting Power of Attorney or Court Order; and a statement for the bank account held on trust for the member
- Make [an appointment](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/contact-us/visit-us) to submit the original documents at a CPF Service Centre, or mail to: CPF Board, Retirement Withdrawals Department (RWD), Privy Box No. 920651, Singapore 929292

## Support / Benefits
- May withdraw a lump sum of $5,000 or more, depending on the extent of reduced life expectancy and CPF balances (excludes monies received under the Retirement Sum Topping-Up Scheme and Matched Retirement Savings Scheme, government grants, and MediSave Account savings)
- May also need to set aside a reduced retirement sum in the Retirement Account (RA), from which monthly payouts are received
