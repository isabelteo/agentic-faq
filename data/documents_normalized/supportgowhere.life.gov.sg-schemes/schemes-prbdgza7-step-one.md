# Step One

## Overview
Step One is a free programme that equips caregivers of young children up to 7 years old diagnosed with developmental delay or disability with knowledge and skills to support their loved ones and themselves.

## Eligibility
- Caregivers of young children up to 7 years old with developmental needs or disability (defined as autism, intellectual disability, physical disability, hearing loss, visual impairment; excludes learning disorders)
- Caregivers or child(ren) should be Singapore Citizens or Singapore Permanent Residents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Sign up with CaringSG at https://caring.sg/stepone

## Support / Benefits
- Free programme
- Access to bite-sized, self-paced e-learning modules
- Regular 'Ask-Me-Anything' online support group sessions to learn practical strategies and tips from experienced caregivers on topics such as handling tantrums and meltdowns, supporting siblings, and money matters

## Related Issues & Concerns
- my young child was just diagnosed with a developmental delay, where can I learn how to support them
- looking for a caregiver support group for parents of children with disabilities
- need practical tips for handling meltdowns or tantrums in a child with special needs
- worried about how to support siblings of a child with a disability
