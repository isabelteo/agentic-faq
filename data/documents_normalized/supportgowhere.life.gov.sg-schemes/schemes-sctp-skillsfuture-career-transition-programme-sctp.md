# SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme (SCTP)

## Overview
Industry-oriented training programmes, from 3 to 12 months, to support mid-career individuals in gaining new, in-demand skills to enhance employability and transition to new career opportunities, complemented with career advisory and employment assistance.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 21 years old and above

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Browse the [course directory](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/training-exchange/course-landing.html) and apply directly to the training providers from 1 April 2022

## Support / Benefits
- 3 to 12 months long industry-oriented courses for skills upgrading, with flexibility of part-time or full-time courses
- Complemented with career advisory and employment assistance
