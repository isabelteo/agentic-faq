# Workfare Transport Concession Scheme

## Overview
The Workfare Transport Concession Scheme provides recipients of Workfare Income Supplement with concessionary rates for travel on public transport.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- Aged 30 to 60 years old
- Receiving Workfare Income Supplement payments

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply via the SimplyGo app, online at the SimplyGo website (https://concportal.simplygo.com.sg/NewApplication.aspx), or at any SimplyGo Ticket Office
- You will need to provide a recent passport-sized colour photograph
- Applications take 14 working days to process

## Support / Benefits
- 25% off adult public transport fares
- Option to purchase a Monthly Public Transport Concession Pass at $92 per month for unlimited travel on train and basic bus services
