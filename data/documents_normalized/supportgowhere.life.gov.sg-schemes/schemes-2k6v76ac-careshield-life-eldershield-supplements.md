# CareShield Life / ElderShield Supplements

## Overview
CareShield Life / ElderShield Supplements are private insurance add-ons that provide additional benefits, like higher payouts, for CareShield Life or ElderShield policyholders.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Existing CareShield Life or ElderShield policyholder

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Application for plans is done through the individual insurers
- To review and compare Supplement plans, you may visit insurer websites or contact them directly for plan details
- Read more about Supplements and see the comparison charts available online for CareShield Life and ElderShield supplements

## Support / Benefits
- Complements your CareShield Life or ElderShield plan with enhanced coverage, providing extra benefits such as increased monthly payouts
- Flexibility to buy multiple supplement policies for enhanced coverage
- Premiums for Supplements can be paid through cash or by using your or your family member's MediSave, limited to $600 per calendar year per insured person
- If you have existing Supplements under ElderShield and choose to join CareShield Life, you may continue to maintain your previous policy and can claim from both

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried that CareShield Life payouts alone won't be enough for long-term care costs
- want to know how to get higher long-term care insurance payouts
- have an existing ElderShield Supplement and joined CareShield Life, unsure what happens to it
- looking to use MediSave to pay for additional long-term care insurance
