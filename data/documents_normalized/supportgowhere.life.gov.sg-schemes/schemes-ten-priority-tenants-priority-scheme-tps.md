# Tenants' Priority Scheme (TPS)

## Overview
Allocates up to 10% of 2-room Flexi, Build-To-Order (BTO), or Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) flats for eligible HDB public rental flat tenants to help them purchase their own home.

## Eligibility
- You and your family have been living in a HDB public rental flat for at least 2 years

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain a HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/)
- If you meet the eligibility criteria during your HFE letter application, you may apply for this scheme in your flat application

## Support / Benefits
- Up to 10% of 2-room Flexi and 3-room BTO or SBF flats are allocated to eligible applicants
