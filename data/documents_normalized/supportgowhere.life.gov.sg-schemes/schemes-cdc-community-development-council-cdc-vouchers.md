# Community Development Council (CDC) Vouchers

## Overview
CDC Vouchers can be used at participating heartland merchants, hawkers, and supermarkets to help Singaporean households with everyday expenses.

## Eligibility
Households with at least 1 Singapore Citizen.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application needed
- Singaporean households can claim their vouchers via the RedeemSG website
- Find out how to claim your CDC vouchers at vouchers.cdc.gov.sg

## Support / Benefits
- Each Singaporean household can claim $500 in CDC Vouchers from June 2026, and an additional $300 from January 2027, for a total of $800 in FY2026
- Vouchers are valid until 31 December 2027
- Vouchers are allocated equally for spending at participating heartland merchants, hawkers, and supermarkets
