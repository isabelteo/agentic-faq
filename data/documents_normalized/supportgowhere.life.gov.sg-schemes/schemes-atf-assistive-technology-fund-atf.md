# Assistive Technology Fund (ATF)

## Overview
The Assistive Technology Fund (ATF) provides subsidies for persons with disabilities (PWDs) to purchase assistive technology devices that aid early intervention, education, training, employment, therapy, rehabilitation and/or independent daily living.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents
- Have a permanent disability based on: Physical Disability (requires assistance with at least 1 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living due to physical impairment), a visual impairment (moderate visual impairment or worse in the better eye), a hearing impairment (moderate hearing loss or worse in the better ear), Intellectual disability, or Autism
- Proof of disability must be provided using the Disability Verification Form completed by a relevant registered healthcare professional
- Must have undergone a qualified assessor's assessment to determine the need and type of device(s)
- Monthly Per Capita Household Income of $4,800 and below
- Annual Value (AV) of residence reflected on NRIC of $21,000 and below for households with no income
- Singaporean and PR seniors who have not tapped on ATF before age 60 are instead supported by the Seniors' Mobility and Enabling Fund (SMF); those who tapped on ATF before age 60 will be supported by ATF throughout their lifetime

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach a therapist or a social worker from public hospitals or social service agencies to assess and assist you to submit the application
- If not currently under the care of any public hospital or social service agency, approach a polyclinic to obtain a referral letter to a public hospital for assessment
- Check if your disability status is already verified by logging into SupportGoWhere with Singpass; if not verified, the touchpoint's registered healthcare professional will guide you
- Means testing is required and will be assisted by the social worker at the public hospital or social service agency

## Support / Benefits
- Subsidy of up to 90% of the cost of the required assistive technology device, with a lifetime cap of $40,000
- Subsidy amount is determined according to the applicant's monthly household income
- Funds can be used to buy, replace, upgrade, or repair assistive technology devices
- The Fund does not support consumables, but covers medical devices (e.g. oxygen concentrators and suction pumps) and spectacles for Singapore Citizens and PRs at or above age 60 who remain served by ATF

## Related Issues & Concerns
- need help paying for a wheelchair or hearing aid for a family member
- caring for someone with a disability and unsure how to get subsidised devices
- senior citizen wondering whether to apply to ATF or SMF for mobility aids
- unsure how to verify disability status before applying for equipment funding
