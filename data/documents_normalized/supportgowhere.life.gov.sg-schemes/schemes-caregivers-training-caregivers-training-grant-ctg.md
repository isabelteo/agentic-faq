# Caregivers Training Grant (CTG)

## Overview
The Caregivers Training Grant (CTG) is a subsidy for caregivers to offset the training fees of approved courses that equip them with the skills needed to care for their loved ones.

## Eligibility
Care recipient must be:
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident
- Aged 65 and older, OR have a disability assessed by an approved healthcare practitioner

Caregiver must be:
- The main person caring for the care recipient (e.g. family members and foreign domestic workers)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Choose an approved course
- Contact the training provider to confirm course details and register for the course
- Inform the trainer about using CTG at least two weeks before the course starts
- The trainer will give you the CTG application form to fill out and will submit it on your behalf (or you can download the form from AIC's website)

## Support / Benefits
- $400 grant for eligible care recipients in their first year of tapping on CTG
- CTG balance can be used to subsidise caregivers attending approved caregiver training courses, with a co-payment of at least $10 for every course attended
- $200 annual top-up in subsequent years, with total CTG balance capped at $400 at any given time
- If multiple caregivers of the same care recipient wish to attend training, they can tap on the same CTG balance to offset course fees

## Related Issues & Concerns
- caring for an elderly parent and want training on caregiving skills
- foreign domestic worker caregiver needs training subsidised
- unsure how much government support is available for caregiver courses
- caring for a family member with a disability and want to learn proper care techniques
