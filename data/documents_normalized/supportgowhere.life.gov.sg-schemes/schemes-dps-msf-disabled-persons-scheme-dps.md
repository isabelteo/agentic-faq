# Disabled Persons Scheme (DPS)

## Overview
Eligible drivers are exempted from paying the premium for the Certificate of Entitlement (COE) and the Additional Registration Fee (ARF) when they purchase a vehicle, for persons with permanent physical disabilities who need a vehicle to earn a living.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- Persons with permanent physical disabilities who are medically certified by doctors from the Tan Tock Seng Hospital, Clinic for Advanced Rehabilitation Therapeutics (CART) as permanently disabled and incapable of taking public transport (bus and/or MRT), but fit to drive
- Gainfully employed and need a vehicle to earn a living
- Possesses a valid Class 3 Driving Licence
- In the 30th income percentile or below, where household income does not exceed $6,500 for a 4-member household
- Vehicle must meet all of the following specifications: engine capacity not more than 1,600cc; maximum power output not more than 97kW (130bhp); Open Market Value (OMV) not more than S$20,000

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- For more information on the application process, visit the [Enabling Guide](https://www.enablingguide.sg/im-looking-for-disability-support/transport/disabled-persons-scheme)

## Support / Benefits
- Exemption from Certificate of Entitlement (COE) premium and Additional Registration Fee (ARF) for eligible drivers with disabilities purchasing a vehicle
