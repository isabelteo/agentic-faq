# U-Save

## Overview
U-Save provides quarterly rebates to offset HDB households' utilities expenses. In FY2026, eligible households will receive up to $760 in U-Save rebates.

## Eligibility
- Singaporean households living in HDB flats
- If the HDB flat is partially rented out or not rented out, there must be at least 1 Singapore Citizen owner or occupier in the household
- If the entire HDB flat is rented out, there must be at least 1 Singapore Citizen tenant
- Household members living in the same flat must not own or have any interest in more than 1 property

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application needed
- U-Save will be credited directly into eligible households' utilities accounts with SP Services
- [GSTV – U-Save](https://www.govbenefits.gov.sg/)
- Budget 2026 U-Save: more details will be provided when available
- Singapore Power: 6671 7117
- [Submit an enquiry](https://www.spgroup.com.sg/contact-us)

## Support / Benefits
- Total of $440 to $760 in U-Save rebates for eligible HDB households in FY2026
- 2026 Additional U-Save: $110 to $190 for eligible HDB households on top of GSTV – U-Save, credited in October 2026 and January 2027
- GST Voucher (GSTV) – U-Save: $220 to $380 for eligible HDB households, credited in April, July, October 2026, and January 2027
- Budget 2026 U-Save: $110 to $190 on top of GSTV – U-Save to help with utilities expenses amid higher utility bills from increased carbon tax from 2026, credited in April and July 2026
