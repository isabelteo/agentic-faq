# Job Placement and Job Support

## Overview
Provides persons with disabilities employment assistance through job matching and job coaching for up to 12 months, delivered by SG Enable and partner agencies such as APSN, Autism Resource Centre (Singapore), MINDS and SPD.

## Eligibility
Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents who are verified to have a permanent disability of any of the following:
- Physical Disability: requires some assistance with at least one of the Activities of Daily Living due to physical impairment
- Moderate Visual Impairment or worse in the better eye
- Moderate Hearing Loss or worse in the better ear
- Intellectual Disability
- Autism

You can check if your disability status is verified on [SupportGoWhere](https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/status/pwdr) or contact employment@sgenable.sg.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Prepare documents: [Disability Verification Form (DVF)](https://www.enablingguide.sg/docs/default-source/default-document-library/disability-verification-form.pdf) completed and endorsed by a registered healthcare professional; copy of NRIC front and back; One Referral Form (for email/physical submissions only)
- Apply online via SupportGoWhere, or download the [One Referral Form](https://www.enablingguide.sg/docs/default-source/default-document-library/one-referral-form-with-omnibus_2025.pdf?sfvrsn=ab89bee_1) and email employment@sgenable.sg, or approach SG Enable's Information and Career Centre within the Enabling Village
- Jobseekers with disabilities can also apply for vacancies via the [Disability Employment Portal](https://eservice1.enable.gov.sg/MSFPortal/EDS/Employment/Pages/Common/Index/Index.aspx)

## Support / Benefits
- Vocational assessment to assess work readiness
- Assessing the jobseeker's strengths, job interests and skills, and identifying training opportunities
- Helping identify job opportunities and link up with prospective inclusive employers
- Assessing job tasks and built environment for suitability and accessibility
- Working with the employer on job redesign, workplace modification, and assistive devices if needed
- On-site job coaching for up to 12 months to help the new employee integrate into the workplace
