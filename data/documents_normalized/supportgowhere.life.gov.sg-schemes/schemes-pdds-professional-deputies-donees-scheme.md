# Professional Deputies and Donees Scheme

## Overview
This scheme serves individuals who lack mental capacity and do not have suitable family members or friends to appoint as proxy decision makers, by allowing registered professionals (lawyers, doctors, accountants, allied health professionals, nurses, social workers) to act as their deputy or donee for remuneration.

## Eligibility
- Persons who lack mental capacity (known as 'P') who do not have suitable family members or friends to be their proxy decision makers
- Individuals with mental capacity can also make a Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) and appoint a Professional Deputy to act as a Donee for remuneration

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach any individual on the list of Registered Professional Deputies/Donees to enquire on professional fees and make an appointment

## Support / Benefits
- Professional deputies apply to Court to act for individuals lacking mental capacity on property, affairs, and personal welfare matters
- Professional deputies provide a P-focused plan and a Schedule of Assets as part of the application
- Professional deputies must pass a certification course and meet stringent criteria (e.g. no history of bankruptcy or criminal proceedings related to financial dishonesty), and re-register every 2 years with the Office of the Public Guardian
- Fees vary depending on the professional's field and individual case requirements, and are mutually agreed
