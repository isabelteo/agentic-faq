# Parent Relief/Parent Relief (Disability)

## Overview
Provides tax relief of up to $14,000 per dependant to recognise individuals supporting their parents, grandparents, parents-in-law, or grandparents-in-law in Singapore, with higher relief where the dependant has a disability.

## Eligibility
- The dependant was 55 years of age or above
- The dependant is living in your household in Singapore; if the dependant lived in a separate household in Singapore, you must have incurred $2,000 or more in supporting them
- The dependant has a disability or mental impairment (for the claim of Parent Relief (Disability))
- Relief can be claimed for up to 2 dependants
- May be shared with other claimants provided no one else is claiming any other relief (except Grandparent Caregiver Relief) on the same dependant

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- For e-Filing: log in with your Singpass or [Singpass Foreign user Account (SFA)](https://www.iras.gov.sg/digital-services/others/singpass-foreign-user-account-(sfa)-for-foreign-individuals) at [myTax Portal](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/login/selection); go to "Individuals" > "File Income Tax Return"; select "File" to File Income Tax Return; go to "4. Deductions, Tax Reliefs and Rebates"; select "Add New" > "Parent"; click "Update" and enter your claim
- For paper filing: complete the item on "Parent/Parent Relief (Disability)" on Form B1 & B, and complete the particulars of your dependant(s) in Appendix 2

## Support / Benefits
- For taxpayer who stays with dependant: $9,000 Parent Relief per dependant, or $14,000 Parent Relief (Disability) per dependant
- For taxpayer who does not stay with dependant: $5,500 Parent Relief per dependant, or $10,000 Parent Relief (Disability) per dependant
