# ComLink+

## Overview
ComLink+ empowers lower-income families to achieve stability, self-reliance, and social mobility, through family coaches and case workers who journey with families toward their long-term goals.

## Eligibility
Families are eligible for ComLink+ if they have children under the age of 21 and are:
- Living in public rental housing (PRH); or
- Not living in PRH, but are eligible for KidSTART or have school-going children at risk of long-term absenteeism

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- If you are eligible for ComLink+ but have not been approached to participate, contact MSF or visit your nearest Social Service Office

## Support / Benefits
Under ComLink+ Progress Packages, families that take active steps to improve their circumstances can receive:
- Partnership Payout of $500 per quarter (from Q3 2026) for continued engagement with family coaches or case workers at Family Service Centres
- Up to $250 per quarter (from Q3 2026) for regular preschool attendance
- Up to $1,250 per quarter (from Q3 2026) for stable employment (based on CPF records)
- 1:1 matched debt repayment up to a limit of $5,000 in total debt repaid
- 2:1 matched CPF contribution to help families save up for purchase of a flat
- Total payouts across all ComLink+ Progress Packages are subject to a lifetime cap of $50,000 (from Q3 2026)
- Family coaches from MSF's Social Service Offices and case workers from Family Service Centres journey with families and support them toward their long-term goals

## Related Issues & Concerns
- lower-income family living in rental flat looking for structured long-term support
- want help getting out of debt while raising children
- trying to save up for a flat but income is low
- worried about child's school attendance and preschool costs
- unsure if family qualifies for coaching and financial top-ups from MSF
