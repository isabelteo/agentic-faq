# CPF Investment Scheme (CPFIS)

## Overview
The CPF Investment Scheme (CPFIS) gives CPF members the option to invest their Ordinary Account (OA) and Special Account (SA) savings in a wide range of products to enhance their retirement savings.

## Eligibility
- At least 18 years old
- Not an undischarged bankrupt
- Have more than $20,000 in OA and/or more than $40,000 in SA
- Completed the CPFIS Self-Awareness Questionnaire (SAQ)
- If investing in CPFIS-OA, may only invest up to 35% of investible savings in stocks and 10% in gold (investible savings = OA balance plus CPF withdrawn for investment and education)
- Can check investable amounts via my cpf digital services, the CPF Mobile app, or at a CPF Service Centre
- Upon reaching age 55, can withdraw CPFIS-OA and CPFIS-SA investments and the cash balance in the Investment Account, after setting aside the Full Retirement Sum (FRS) in the Retirement Account (FRS can be set aside fully in cash, or with cash of at least the Basic Retirement Sum plus property)
- Withdrawal does not result in liquidation of investment holdings; agent bank and/or product providers will contact you about transferring investments

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- New CPFIS investors must first take the Self-Awareness Questionnaire (SAQ) to assess basic financial knowledge and suitability
- To start investing under CPFIS-OA, open a CPF Investment Account with one of the CPFIS agent banks: OCBC, UOB, or DBS
- After opening the account, approach product providers to buy or sell investments
- For CPFIS-SA, approach product providers directly to buy or sell investments
