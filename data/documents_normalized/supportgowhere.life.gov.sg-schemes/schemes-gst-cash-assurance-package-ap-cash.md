# Assurance Package (AP) Cash

## Overview
Provides $700 to $2,250 cash payouts over 5 years from 2022 to 2026 for adult Singapore Citizens aged 21 and above, residing in Singapore, based on Assessable Income and number of properties owned.

## Eligibility
For AP Cash Payout in 2026:
- Singapore Citizen residing in Singapore
- 21 years old and above in 2027

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required

## Support / Benefits
- Cash payouts of $700 to $2,250 in December over 5 years from 2022 to 2026
