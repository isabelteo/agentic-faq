# GST Voucher (GSTV) – MediSave

## Overview
MediSave top-up of up to $450 for Singapore Citizens aged 65 and above, residing in Singapore, credited each August.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen residing in Singapore
- Aged 65 and above in 2026
- Annual Value of home (as indicated on NRIC) not exceeding $31,000 as at 31 December 2025
- Does not own more than 1 property

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application needed if you have previously signed-up for past GSTV payouts
- If you have never signed-up for past GSTV payouts, apply by logging in to the e-services on the [govbenefits portal](https://www.govbenefits.gov.sg/)

## Support / Benefits
- Up to $450 top-up to MediSave account for eligible Singapore Citizens aged 65 and above
- Credited in August each year
