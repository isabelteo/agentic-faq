# CareShield Life Premium Subsidies

## Overview
This scheme ensures CareShield Life premiums are affordable through means-tested Premium Subsidies (up to 30%), Participation Incentives, Additional Participation Incentives for Merdeka and Pioneer Generation, and transitional support for premium increases.

## Eligibility
**Means-tested Premium Subsidies**:
- Subsidies of up to 30% for eligible Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents (PRs receive half the SC subsidy rate)
- Household monthly income per person of $3,600 or below
- Living in residences with an Annual Value (AV) of $31,000 and below
- Not owning more than 1 property
- As of 1 Jan 2025: Lower-Income ($0-$1,500/person) gets 30%; Lower-Middle-Income (>$1,501-$2,600) gets 25%; Upper-Middle-Income (>$2,601-$3,600) gets 20%. Rates apply to AV of $21,000 or less; AV $21,001-$31,000 gets 10% less subsidy; AV above $31,000 or multiple properties receive no subsidy.

**Participation Incentives**: Singapore Citizens born in 1979 or earlier who joined and were covered under CareShield Life by 31 December 2024

**Additional Participation Incentives**: Merdeka and Pioneer Generation seniors who joined and were covered under CareShield Life by 31 December 2024, provided on top of Participation Incentives

**Transitional Support**: All policyholders who see an increase in gross premiums (no application needed)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Eligible subsidies are automatically applied to CareShield Life premiums

## Support / Benefits
- Means-tested Premium Subsidies of up to 30%
- Participation Incentives up to $1,875 ($187.50/year) for Singapore Citizens born in 1979 or earlier who joined by 31 Dec 2024, spread equally over 10 years to offset premiums
- Additional Participation Incentives of $1,125 ($112.50/year) for Pioneer and Merdeka Generation citizens, also spread over 10 years
- Transitional support to help manage premium increases from 2026 to 2029 following the CareShield Life 2025 Review
