# Child LifeSG Credits

## Overview
Provides broad-based support for Singaporean families with children, through $500 in credits per Singapore Citizen child aged 0 to 12, disbursed via the LifeSG app.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- 2025 Child LifeSG Credits: aged 0 to 12 in 2025 (born from 1 January 2013 to 31 December 2025)
- 2026 Child LifeSG Credits: aged 0 to 12 in 2026 (born from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2026)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application needed
- Credits are disbursed to the eligible child's Child Development Account (CDA) trustee in MSF's records as of the eligibility assessment date; check CDA trustee information via [Family View service](https://www.babybonus.msf.gov.sg/) on Parent Portal Services
- Disbursement timeline: for 2025 credits, children born 2013-2024 assessed 1 June 2025 (disbursed week of 7 July 2025), children born 2025 assessed 1 March 2026 (disbursed from 8 April 2026); for 2026 credits, children born 2014-2025 assessed 1 June 2026 (disbursed from 14 July 2026), children born 2026 assessed 1 March 2027 (disbursed April 2027)

## Support / Benefits
- $500 Child LifeSG Credits disbursed to the Child Development Account (CDA) trustee via the LifeSG mobile app
- Can be used at merchants that accept payments using PayNow UEN QR or NETS QR
- Credits are valid for 12 months from disbursement, with expiry date shown in the LifeSG app
