# SG60 Vouchers

## Overview
SG60 Vouchers were given to all Singaporean adults in recognition of their contributions to the nation, to share the gains of Singapore's progress during SG60.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- Aged 21 and above in 2025

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application is required

## Support / Benefits
- $600 in SG60 Vouchers for Singapore Citizens aged 21 to 59 in 2025; $800 for Singapore Citizens aged 60 and above in 2025
- Vouchers were disbursed across July 2025, with seniors receiving vouchers first, followed by all other Singaporean adults
- Can be used at all participating businesses of the CDC Voucher scheme: half at participating heartland merchants and hawkers, and half at participating supermarkets
