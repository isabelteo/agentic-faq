# SPED Financial Assistance Scheme (FAS)

## Overview
Provides financial assistance to lower-income families with children who are in MOE-funded Special Education Schools.

## Eligibility
- Singaporean students studying in MOE-funded Special Education Schools
- Average Gross Household Income of up to $4,000, or Average Per Capita Income of up to $1,000

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online through [this link](https://go.gov.sg/moe-efassped)

## Support / Benefits
- Full waiver of school fees
- Free textbooks and school attire
- $21 transport credit per month for students who take public transport
- Meal subsidy of school meals: 7 meals per school week for students 7 to 12 years old; 10 meals per school week for students 13 to 21 years old

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording school fees for a child with special needs
- unsure how to pay for textbooks and school attire for a child in a Special Education School
- struggling with the cost of daily meals for a child at a SPED school
- need help with transport costs for a child commuting to a special education school
- want to know if household income qualifies for SPED financial assistance
