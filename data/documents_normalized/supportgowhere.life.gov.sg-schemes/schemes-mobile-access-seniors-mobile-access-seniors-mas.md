# Mobile Access for Seniors (MAS)

## Overview
Mobile Access for Seniors (MAS) provides subsidised smartphone and mobile plans to lower-income seniors who want to go digital but cannot afford them.

## Eligibility
- 60 years old and above (based on birth year)
- Singapore Citizen
- Current beneficiary of any of these government assistance schemes: MSF's ComCare Long Term Assistance (LTA), MSF's ComCare Short-to-Medium Term Assistance (SMTA), or HDB's Public Rental Scheme
- Not an existing IMDA Home Access beneficiary who has received a smartphone

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Step 1: Attend the Seniors Go Digital programme at any SG Digital community hub and pick up at least 1 basic skill (e.g. digital communication, navigating government digital services, transacting with e-payments)
- Step 2: Collect the MAS redemption voucher from Digital Ambassadors
- Step 3: Redeem the voucher at participating telcos
- Visit the Digital for Life website or call 6377 3800 to find your nearest hub
- Only 1 registration per senior is required

## Support / Benefits
- 2-year mobile plan at $5.10 per month
- Smartphone from $20.00 (one-time cost)
- Free SIM card registration
- Free caller ID
- Unlimited incoming local calls
- Continued mobile data connectivity with no excess data charges
- Other value-added services
