# Retirement Sum Topping-Up Scheme

## Overview
The Retirement Sum Topping-Up Scheme lets Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents boost their own or their loved ones' retirement savings and payouts through voluntary cash top-ups or CPF transfers to Special Account (SA) or Retirement Account (RA).

## Eligibility
- Must be a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident to receive cash top-ups and CPF transfers
- Members aged 55 and above can top up to the Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS), which increases each year
- Members below 55 can top up to the Full Retirement Sum (FRS)
- CPF transfers can be made to yourself: below 55, transfer Ordinary Account (OA) savings to SA; aged 55 or above, transfer OA savings to RA (amount depends on age and CPF balances)
- CPF transfers can be made to loved ones (parents, parents-in-law, grandparents, grandparents-in-law, spouse and siblings), but you must first set aside enough for your own retirement
- You can check your top-up limits by logging in to the Retirement Dashboard

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Make your cash top-up or CPF transfer online (CPF transfers and cash top-ups via PayNow or GIRO) or via the CPF Mobile app (CPF transfers and cash top-ups via PayNow): https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/forms-e-applications/cash-top-ups-and-cpf-transfers-for-retirement
- To complete the top-up form you will need the recipient's NRIC if making a cash top-up or CPF transfer to a loved one
- Additional supporting documents are needed if making a CPF transfer to a recipient for the first time (not required for cash top-ups)

## Support / Benefits
- Higher monthly payouts in retirement
- Earn CPF interest rates of up to 6% per annum on retirement savings
- Grow retirement savings through compound interest
- Tax relief of up to $16,000 for cash top-ups made to your own and loved ones' retirement savings each calendar year (cash top-ups that attract the MRSS matching grant do not qualify for tax relief)
- Note: cash top-ups and CPF transfers are irreversible and cannot be withdrawn for other purposes (e.g. housing, investment, or immediate needs after age 55)
