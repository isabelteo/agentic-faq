# PA Community Programmes and Events

## Overview
One-stop access to a diverse range of community programmes and events organised by the People's Association, open to Singapore Citizens, Permanent Residents and Non-Singapore Citizens / Foreigners.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens, Permanent Residents and Non-Singapore Citizens / Foreigners

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit [onePA](https://www.onepa.gov.sg/events) to browse and register
- Visit your [nearest Community Centre/Club (CC)](https://www.onepa.gov.sg/cc) to enquire about available programmes and register at the counter

## Support / Benefits
- Discover community programmes and events available in your neighbourhood
- Fitness activities, outings, tours and workshops to stay healthy and broaden experiences
- Neighbourhood celebrations, coffee talks and dialogues to get to know neighbours
- Volunteering opportunities with like-minded individuals

## Related Issues & Concerns
- feeling isolated and looking for ways to connect with my neighbours
- wanting to find free or low-cost community activities near me
- looking for volunteering opportunities in my area
- new resident wanting to know what my Community Centre offers
