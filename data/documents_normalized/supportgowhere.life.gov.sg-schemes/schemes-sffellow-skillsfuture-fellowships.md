# SkillsFuture Fellowships

## Overview
Awards a $10,000 monetary award to support skills mastery and lifelong learning for eligible Singapore Citizens with significant working experience.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens
- At least 10 years of working experience in the same or related industry or job function

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Applications are now closed
- For enquiries, contact the [Programme Manager for the SkillsFuture Fellowships](mailto:sfa@snef.org.sg)

## Support / Benefits
- Monetary award of $10,000 for skills mastery and lifelong learning
- National recognition by the President of the Republic of Singapore
- Opportunities to inspire and mentor future talent
