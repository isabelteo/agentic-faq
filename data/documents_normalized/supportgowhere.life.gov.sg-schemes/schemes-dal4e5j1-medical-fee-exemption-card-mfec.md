# Medical Fee Exemption Card (MFEC)

## Overview
Provides subsidised standard medical treatment for needy residents at approved stay-in care institutions, for eligible stay-in care recipients at government or restructured hospitals, polyclinics and approved Community Care Services.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen (SC), Permanent Resident (PR) or stateless person with valid ICA documentation
- Subsidised resident living in an approved MOH-funded institution
- Must meet the means-test criteria for MFEC

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach the medical social worker in the institution your loved one is staying in; they will help your loved one apply for the card

## Support / Benefits
- Subsidised standard medical treatment for eligible stay-in care recipients at government or restructured hospitals, polyclinics and approved Community Care Services
