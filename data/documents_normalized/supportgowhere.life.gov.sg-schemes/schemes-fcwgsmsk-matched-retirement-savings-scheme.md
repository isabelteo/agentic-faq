# Matched Retirement Savings Scheme

## Overview
Helps eligible Singaporeans with lower retirement savings to save more, by matching cash top-ups made to their Retirement or Special Accounts (RA/SA), boosting their monthly payouts in retirement.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens only
- Aged 55 and above as of 31 December of the assessment year, or below 55 years old with disability status verified with MSF
- Retirement Account (RA) savings or Ordinary and Special Account (OSA) savings are less than $110,200 (Current Basic Retirement Sum for 2026)
- Average monthly income not more than $4,000
- Annual Value of Residence not more than $21,000
- Owns not more than 1 property
- Eligibility is automatically assessed annually at the beginning of the year; no application needed

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- You do not need to apply, as eligibility is automatically assessed annually at the beginning of the year
- If eligible, CPF Board will notify you via email or post; check via your [Retirement Dashboard](http://cpf.gov.sg/retirementdashboard)
- To receive the matching grant, you need to receive cash top-ups in your RA/SA within the year you are eligible for MRSS; you can make a cash top-up [here](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/forms-e-applications/cash-top-ups-and-cpf-transfers-for-retirement)

## Support / Benefits
- The Government matches every dollar of cash top-ups made to the RA/SA of eligible Singaporeans, up to $2,000 per year, with a $20,000 cap over a member's lifetime
- The matching grant is automatically credited to the RA/SA at the beginning of the following year
- Eligible Singaporeans enjoy interest rates of up to 6% per annum on the cash top-up and matching grant
- Cash top-ups that attract the MRSS matching grant will not qualify for tax relief
