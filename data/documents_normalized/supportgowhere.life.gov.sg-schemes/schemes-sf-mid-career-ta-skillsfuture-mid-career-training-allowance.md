# SkillsFuture Mid-Career Training Allowance

## Overview
Supports both full-time and part-time long-form training for Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above to upskill and reskill, giving individuals the option of which type of training best suits their needs.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen aged 40 and above pursuing selected full-time and part-time long-form training programmes

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the [MySkillsFuture website](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/) to find and enrol in eligible programmes

## Support / Benefits
- Full-time training: allowance of 50% of average earned monthly income in the latest 12 months before course start, with a minimum of $300 and maximum of $3,000 per month; requires income in the latest 12 months before course start
- Part-time training: flat rate of $300 per month for employed individuals
- Up to 24 months of Training Allowance for each individual throughout their lifetime
