# Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (Singles)

## Overview
Provides up to $60,000 in Enhanced CPF Housing Grants (EHG) (Singles) for eligible first-timer applicants buying a new or resale flat on their own, or with their non-resident spouses.

## Eligibility
- First-timer applicants aged 35 or above, buying a HDB flat alone or with other first-timer single citizens, or buying a resale flat alone, with other first-timer single citizens or with their parents
- First-timer applicants aged 21 or above buying a resale flat with their non-resident spouse
- If buying a flat with parents, you and at least 1 parent must be a Singapore Citizen, and both parents must be first-timers meeting all eligibility conditions
- Applicants have worked continuously for at least 12 months, 2 months before the HFE letter application, and were employed at the time of the HFE letter application
- Monthly household income not exceeding $4,500 for singles and $9,000 for joint singles, non-resident spouses or parents (resale flats only)
- Flat has a remaining lease of more than 20 years
- To qualify for the full EHG grant, flat must have sufficient lease to cover the youngest core applicant and occupier to age 95
- Must not own, have an interest in or have disposed of any private residential property (local or overseas) within the last 30 months
- All persons listed in the HFE letter application can, as a household, own up to 1 non-residential property at HFE letter application and up to 30 months before

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) to determine eligibility for this Grant and others
- If eligible, apply for the grant when submitting your resale application or after you have booked a flat from HDB
- The Grant will be credited to the CPF Ordinary Accounts of eligible household members; only core applicants can use their share of the housing grants to pay for the flat purchase

## Support / Benefits
- Up to $60,000 to offset the purchase price of the flat / reduce the required housing loan (new or resale)
- Up to $120,000 total when jointly purchasing flats with other eligible first-timer single applicants (limited to 2 applicants, up to $60,000 each)
