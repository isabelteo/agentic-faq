# Large Families Scheme

## Overview
Provides additional support of up to $16,000 for each third and subsequent Singapore Citizen child, on top of existing Baby Bonus Scheme benefits, for Singaporean families with three or more children. Note: the SG Child Support Package announced at the 2026 National Day Rally will replace the Baby Bonus Scheme and Large Families Scheme — the increased CDA First Step Grant for large families will cease from April 2027, and Large Family LifeSG Credits will also cease with no disbursements in 2027.

## Eligibility
- Families with a third or subsequent Singapore Citizen child born on or after 18 February 2025 will receive the increased CDA First Step Grant, the Large Family MediSave Grant, and $1,000 annually in Large Family LifeSG Credits during the years the child turns 1 to 6 years old
- Large Family LifeSG Credits also apply to existing large families with at least one child aged six or below

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application is required

## Support / Benefits
- $10,000 CDA First Step Grant (increased from $5,000), for each third and subsequent Singapore Citizen child born on or after 18 February 2025, deposited into the eligible child's CDA within two weeks of CDA opening; can be used for approved preschool and healthcare expenses of the child and/or siblings
- $5,000 Large Family MediSave Grant, deposited into the mother's MediSave account following the child's Baby Bonus enrolment; can be used to offset the mother's pregnancy and delivery costs, or approved dependant's healthcare expenses
- $1,000 annually in Large Family LifeSG Credits, disbursed to the CDA trustee and accessible via the LifeSG mobile app; usable at any merchant accepting PayNow UEN QR or NETS QR; valid for 12 months from disbursement
