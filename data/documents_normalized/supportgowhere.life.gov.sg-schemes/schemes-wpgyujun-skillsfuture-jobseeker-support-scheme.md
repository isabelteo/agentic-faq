# SkillsFuture Jobseeker Support Scheme

## Overview
The SkillsFuture Jobseeker Support Scheme provides temporary financial support of up to $6,000 over 6 months to involuntarily unemployed individuals, to help them find a job that makes better use of their skills and experience.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident, aged 21 and above
- Earned an average gross monthly income of $5,000 or less in the past 12 months (excluding employer CPF contributions; includes basic salary and extra wages such as overtime pay, commissions and bonuses, counted only for periods employed and earning income)
- Previously employed in Singapore for at least six months in the past 12 months
- Unemployed due to involuntary reasons such as retrenchment, cessation of business, dismissals or termination due to illness, injury or accident
- Lives in a property in Singapore with annual value of $31,000 or less
- Has not received a payout from this scheme in the past three years

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Learn more and apply for the scheme at https://go.gov.sg/jobseekersupport
- To qualify for payouts, individuals must apply online to participate and take part in supported job search activities

## Support / Benefits
- Payouts of up to $6,000 over 6 months
