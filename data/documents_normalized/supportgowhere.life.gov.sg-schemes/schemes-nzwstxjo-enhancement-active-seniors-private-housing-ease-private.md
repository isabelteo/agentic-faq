# Enhancement for Active Seniors (Private Housing): EASE (Private)

## Overview
EASE (Private) provides subsidies to Singapore Citizen seniors living in private properties for the installation of senior-friendly fittings that improve mobility and safety within their homes, rolling out in phases from 1 April 2026.

## Eligibility
Available to private property households with at least one Singaporean household member who is:
- 65 years old and above; or
- Between 60 and 64 years old requiring assistance with at least one Activity of Daily Living (ADL: washing/bathing, dressing, feeding, toileting, mobility, transferring), with a Functional Assessment Report endorsed by a qualified assessor (doctor, registered nurse, or registered physiotherapist/occupational therapist)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Applications roll out in phases based on age of seniors in the household in 2026: from 1 Apr 2026 for households with seniors aged 80+ or seniors 60+ needing ADL assistance; from 1 Jul 2026 for seniors aged 70+; from 1 Oct 2026 for seniors aged 65+
- Letters and SMSes will be progressively sent to eligible households to apply
- Apply via BCA's EASE (Private) Portal: https://go.gov.sg/ease-private
- Seniors aged 60-64 needing ADL assistance must submit a Functional Assessment Report
- For assistance, visit any Community Centre (CC) or ServiceSG Centre, or call BCA's hotline 1800-342-5222
- Hard copy vouchers can be obtained and printed at any Community Centre

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible households receive $1,200 in vouchers to subsidise 75% of the cost of selected senior-friendly fittings from appointed contractors (household pays the remaining 25%)
- Fittings and prices after 75% subsidy: Single-Step Ramp $70.85 (from $283.40), Handrail $158.05 (from $632.20), Grab Bars (first set) $130.80 (from $523.20), Grab Bars (second set onwards) $76.30 (from $305.20), Home Fire Alarm Device $29.98 (from $119.90), Bidet Spray $16.35 (from $65.40), Slip-Resistant Toilet Flooring Treatment $59.95 (from $239.80), Shower Seat $76.30 (from $305.20)
