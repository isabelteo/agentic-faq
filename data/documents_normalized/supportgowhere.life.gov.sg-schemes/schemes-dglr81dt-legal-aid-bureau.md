# Legal Aid Bureau

## Overview
Provides legal help for eligible individuals in various civil proceedings, including legal advice, representation, and assistance drafting documents like Wills and Deeds of Separation.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents residing in Singapore
- Eligibility Tests: Means Test for Legal Advice and Assistance; Means and Merits Test for Legal Aid
- Duty Lawyer available for consultation and assessment of eligibility for legal aid

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Register case [online](https://eservices.mlaw.gov.sg/labesvc/) with the [step by step guide](https://lab.mlaw.gov.sg/files/AP_Portal_Guide_09032026.pdf) on the [Applicant Portal](https://eservices.mlaw.gov.sg/labesvc/?tabIndex=3)
- Attend the Legal Aid Bureau (LAB) within three working days after online registration
- Schedule an appointment via the [OneMinLaw Portal](https://go.gov.sg/omp) before visiting the Minlaw Services Centre at 45 Maxwell Road #07-11, the URA Centre (East Wing)
- If you're a minor or representing someone without mental capacity, a parent/guardian, or Court-appointed Deputy must apply on your behalf

## Support / Benefits
- Legal advice on Singapore law and practical steps for your case
- Assistance in drafting simple legal documents like Wills and Deeds of Separation
- Legal representation in various civil proceedings, subject to eligibility

## Related Issues & Concerns
- cannot afford a lawyer for a civil court case
- need help drafting a simple will or deed of separation
- wondering if I qualify for free legal advice based on my income
- applying for legal aid on behalf of a family member who lacks mental capacity
