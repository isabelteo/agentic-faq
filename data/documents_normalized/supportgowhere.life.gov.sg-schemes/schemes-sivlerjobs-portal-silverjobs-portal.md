# Silverjobs Portal

## Overview
A job platform created by the Centre For Seniors (CFS), a non-profit Social Service Agency, to help seniors explore work in various sectors for a meaningful retirement.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 50 and above
- Refer to each individual job listing for specific requirements

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Refer to each job position on the [Silverjobs website](https://silverjobs.sg/) for the application process
