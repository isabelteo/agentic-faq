# Workfare Income Supplement (WIS) Scheme

## Overview
Supplements eligible workers' income and CPF savings through cash and CPF payments, for employees, platform workers, and self-employed persons who earn low wages.

## Eligibility
- 30 years old and above as at 31 December of the Work Year
- Singapore Citizen
- Employee, self-employed person and/or platform worker
- Gross monthly income of $500* to $3,000 (if married, spouse's assessable income must not exceed $70,000), for the month worked
- Average gross monthly income of not more than $3,000 in the past 12 months as an employee or platform worker
- Annual value of place of residence (as indicated on NRIC) must not exceed $21,000, as at 31 December of the preceding year
- Does not own more than 1 property (if married, recipient and spouse must not own more than 1 property)
- *Persons with disabilities, workers who qualify for ComCare Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance, and caregivers of care recipients (generally residing with care recipients medically certified to have permanent moderate to severe disabilities) qualify for concessionary WIS even if earning less than $500

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Employees: no application required; eligibility automatically assessed based on income declared by employer(s)
- Self-employed persons: declare net trade income to IRAS and make required MediSave contributions in full
- Platform workers: no application required; eligibility automatically assessed based on net earnings declared by platform operator(s)

## Support / Benefits
- Employees: from Work Year 2025, up to $4,900 per year, comprising 40% cash and 60% credited to CPF Accounts
- Self-employed persons and Platform Workers: from Work Year 2025, up to $3,267 per year, comprising 10% cash and 90% credited to MediSave Account (self-employed paid annually, platform workers paid monthly)
- From Work Year 2029, eligible Platform Workers whose CPF contribution rates fully align with employees will receive higher WIS payments at the same level as employees
