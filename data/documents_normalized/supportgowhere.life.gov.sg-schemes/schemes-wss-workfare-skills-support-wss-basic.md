# Workfare Skills Support (WSS) (Basic)

## Overview
Workfare Skills Support (WSS) (Basic) provides training allowance and cash incentives to encourage lower-wage Singaporean workers to upskill through training, with support for self-sponsored trainees, trainees completing sustained training, and employers who send eligible employees for training.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- At least 30 years old or older as of 31 December of the preceding year, or at least 13 years old for persons with disabilities
- Earn an average gross monthly income of not more than $3,000 for the past 12 months
- Have declared net trade income and made MediSave contributions in full if self-employed
- Live in a property with annual value of $21,000 and below as at 31 December of the preceding year
- Own not more than one property
- If married: you and your spouse together do not own more than one property, and spouse's assessable income does not exceed $70,000 of the preceding Year of Assessment
- (Criteria are similar to the Workfare Income Supplement (WIS) criteria)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Employees: no application needed
- Self-employed persons: submit the TA claim form (https://go.gov.sg/wss-sep-ta) within 120 days of course end date, with proof of self-employment (e.g. contract for service, or ACRA business registration and business transaction documents) and course details
- Employers: apply online at the Enterprise Portal for Jobs and Skills (https://sfec.enterprisejobskills.gov.sg/Callbackhandler/Prelogin.aspx)

## Support / Benefits
- Training Allowance (TA) of $10.50 per training hour for self-sponsored trainees
- Training commitment award of $800 for one Workforce Skills Qualification (WSQ) or Academic Continuing Education and Training Qualification
- Absentee payroll for employers sending eligible employees for training: 95% of trainee's basic hourly wage, capped at $13 per training hour
- Supports short training such as eligible courses on the MySkillsFuture Portal and courses meeting Progressive Wage Model (PWM) training requirements
