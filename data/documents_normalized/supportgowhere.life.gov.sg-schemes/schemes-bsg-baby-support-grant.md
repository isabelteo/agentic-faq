# Baby Support Grant

## Overview
The Baby Support Grant helps parents ease financial concerns about raising a child during extraordinary times, through a one-off cash payout. Note: the SG Child Support Package announced at the 2026 National Day Rally will replace the current Baby Bonus Scheme and Large Families Scheme; those on the Baby Bonus Scheme will be automatically transitioned, with existing payouts continuing as usual in the meantime.

## Eligibility
- Child is a Singapore Citizen
- Children born from 1 Oct 2022 to 13 Feb 2023
- Parents are lawfully married
- Children already receiving the Baby Bonus Cash Gift are also eligible for the Baby Support Grant

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application needed if you have applied for the Baby Bonus Scheme previously
- From 1 July 2024, all parents can apply for the Baby Bonus Scheme at LifeSG, either bundled with the child's birth registration, or as a direct application for a child already registered for birth

## Support / Benefits
- Eligible Singaporean children born from 1 Oct 2022 to 13 Feb 2023 qualify for a one-off cash payout of $3,000, disbursed from the second half of 2023
