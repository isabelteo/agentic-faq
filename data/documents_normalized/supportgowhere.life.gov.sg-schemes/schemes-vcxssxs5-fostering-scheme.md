# Fostering Scheme

## Overview
The Fostering Scheme provides a safe and nurturing family environment for children needing care and protection, offering love, stability, and essentials like food and shelter, with the aim to reunite children with their birth families once safe to do so.

## Eligibility
- At least 25 years old
- Married and applying as a couple
- Medically fit to care for children
- Minimum secondary school education
- Minimum monthly household income of $2,000 and per capita income of at least $700 (retirees can apply to be assessed based on non-work sources of income and savings)
- Provide a child-safe home environment, including commitment not to use physical punishment, and installation of window grilles for foster children below 13 years old

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- [Apply now](https://volunteer.gov.sg/scheme-detail?code=fostering) online through the MSF Fostering Scheme portal: click "Apply", register or sign in, fill in the application form, upload supporting documents
- MSF or an appointed agency will contact applicants within three working days
- Assessment includes: interview session(s), home visit, medical screenings
- After approval: attend mandatory training sessions, await foster child placement
- MSF Consolidated Hotline: 1800-111-2222 (say "Foster" when prompted by the virtual assistant)
- [Submit an enquiry](mailto:fostering@msf.gov.sg)
- Address: 512 Thomson Rd, #11-00 MSF Building, Singapore 298136

## Support / Benefits
- Monthly fostering allowance of $1,100 for each foster child, or $1,500 for a child with special needs
- Childcare subsidies with monthly co-payment of $200 for full-day care and $150 for half-day care paid by foster parents
- Student care subsidies with monthly co-payment of $80 paid by foster parents for MSF-registered centres
- Medical subsidies covering treatment at public healthcare institutions (polyclinics, public hospitals, public specialist centres); foster children also receive subsidies at participating CHAS General Practitioners and dental clinics
- Support and guidance from a Foster Care Worker attached to every foster parent
- Training courses conducted by Social Service Institute for new foster parents
- 24/7 emergency support hotline
- Support groups and respite care available
- Foster parents enjoy childcare leave benefits, just as birth parents do under the Child Development Co-Savings Act and Employment Act
