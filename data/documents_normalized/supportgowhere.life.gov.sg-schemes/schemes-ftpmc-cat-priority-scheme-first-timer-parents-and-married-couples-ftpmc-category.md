# Priority Scheme - First-Timer (Parents & Married Couples), FT(PMC) Category

## Overview
To help young families settle down more quickly, additional support is provided for applicants who qualify for the FT(PMC) category when applying for BTO or SBF flats.

## Eligibility
- Families with at least 1 Singapore Citizen child aged 18 and below, or married couples aged 40 and below (includes married couples who are expecting and have a doctor's certification of pregnancy)
- Never owned or sold a residential property before, including a HDB flat
- Did not have a chance to book a BTO or SBF flat in the past 5 years prior to their flat application

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain a HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the [HDB Flat Portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp27/PrelimHFE/) and apply for a flat during BTO or SBF exercises
- You need not request to be considered for FT(PMC); HDB will assess and inform you about your eligibility after you have submitted your flat application

## Support / Benefits
- 1 additional ballot chance, on top of the 2 provided to First-Timer Families, when applying for BTO or SBF flats (totalling 3 ballot chances for FT(PMC) applicants)
- First priority for flats set aside under the Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS) when applying for a 4-room or smaller BTO flat in Standard projects
