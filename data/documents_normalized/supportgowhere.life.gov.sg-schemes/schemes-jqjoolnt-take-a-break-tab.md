# Take-a-Break (TAB)

## Overview
A respite care programme that provides caregivers temporary relief from their caregiving responsibilities through home-based respite care services, with subsidies available for eligible caregivers.

## Eligibility
- Caregiver or care recipient is a Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident
- Caregiver of persons with any one or more of: Autism, Intellectual Disability, Physical Disability (requires some assistance with at least 1 of the 6 Activities of Daily Living due to physical impairment), Sensory Disability, or Children and young persons with Developmental Needs and/or in EIPIC

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Approach a social service professional from a touchpoint (e.g. Social Service Agency (SSA), hospitals) to apply
- Contact SPD directly by email if assistance is needed: [Tab@spd.org.sg](mailto:Tab@spd.org.sg)

## Support / Benefits
- Temporary relief for caregivers from caregiving responsibilities, allowing them to rest, attend to personal needs, or manage other commitments
- Flexibility in respite options, choosing from a range of home-based respite care services and frequency
- Subsidies available for eligible caregivers

## Related Issues & Concerns
- feeling burnt out from caring for a family member with a disability
- need temporary relief from caregiving duties but not sure where to start
- caring for a child with developmental needs and looking for respite support
- wondering if subsidies are available for home-based respite care
