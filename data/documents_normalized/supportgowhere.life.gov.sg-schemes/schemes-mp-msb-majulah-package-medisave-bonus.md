# Majulah Package – MediSave Bonus

## Overview
The Majulah Package MediSave Bonus supplements the medical savings of seniors through a one-off CPF bonus of up to $2,000.

## Eligibility
- Born in 1973 or earlier
- Singapore Citizen

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application required

## Support / Benefits
- One-off CPF bonus of up to $2,000, tiered by age, Annual Value of residence, and number of properties owned

- worried about medical savings running low in old age
