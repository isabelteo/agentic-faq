# Cash Relief for Platform Workers and Taxi Drivers

## Overview
This scheme provides a $200 cash payout for eligible platform workers and taxi drivers, with payouts disbursed from end-April 2026.

## Eligibility
- Platform workers with net earnings from platform work of more than $500/month (excluding employment income), across all platform operators, for each month in the three-month period from Dec 2025 to Feb 2026
- Taxi drivers who were in an agreement with a Taxi Operator to hire a taxi throughout the three-month period from Dec 2025 to Feb 2026

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application needed

## Support / Benefits
- $200 cash payout for eligible platform workers and taxi drivers
