# Budget 2026 CPF Top-Up

## Overview
The Budget 2026 CPF Top-Up provides additional support to boost the retirement adequacy of eligible Singaporean seniors whose CPF retirement savings are below the prevailing Basic Retirement Sum (BRS), through an automatic CPF top-up in 2026.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen
- 50 years old and above in 2026
- CPF LIFE/Retirement Account or Ordinary/Special Account balances below the prevailing BRS in 2026 of $110,200 as at 31 December 2025
- Tiered by annual value of place of residence (as indicated on NRIC), as at 31 December 2025
- Does not own more than 1 property

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- No application needed – eligibility will be automatically assessed

## Support / Benefits
- $500 to $1,500 in CPF top-up for eligible Singaporean seniors, tiered by CPF retirement savings and Annual Value of the place of residence
