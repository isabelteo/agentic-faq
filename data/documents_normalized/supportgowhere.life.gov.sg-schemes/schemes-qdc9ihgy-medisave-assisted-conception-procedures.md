# MediSave for Assisted Conception Procedures

## Overview
Allows eligible couples to withdraw up to $15,000 per patient from MediSave to pay for Assisted Conception Procedures (ACP) such as in-vitro fertilisation and intra-uterine insemination, at both private and public Assisted Reproduction centres.

## Eligibility
- Only the MediSave of the patient and her spouse can be used
- If an ACP is not completed due to non-medical reasons, MediSave cannot be used to cover the costs of the incomplete cycle

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Inform the Assisted Reproduction centre where you are receiving ACP treatment that you would like to use your MediSave to pay for expenses
- The centre will submit the MediSave claims to CPF Board on your behalf for the treatment expenses

## Support / Benefits
- MediSave withdrawal limits vary per ACP treatment cycle: up to $6,000 for the first cycle, up to $5,000 for the second cycle, and up to $4,000 for the third and subsequent cycles
- Total lifetime MediSave withdrawal limit is $15,000 per patient
