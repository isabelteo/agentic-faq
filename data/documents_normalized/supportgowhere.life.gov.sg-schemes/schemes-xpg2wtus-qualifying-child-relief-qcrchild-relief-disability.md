# Qualifying Child Relief (QCR)/Child Relief (Disability)

## Overview
Provides tax relief to parents to recognise their efforts in supporting their children. QCR gives $4,000 per qualifying child, while Child Relief (Disability) gives $7,500 per child with disabilities.

## Eligibility
**For QCR, during the previous year:**
- Your child is unmarried and is either born to you and your spouse/ex-spouse, a stepchild, or legally adopted
- Your child was either below 16 years old, or above 16 years old and studying full-time at any university, college or other educational institution
- Your child did not have an annual income exceeding $8,000

**For Child Relief (Disability):**
- Your child must be unmarried / have been unmarried in the previous year and is either born to you and your spouse/ex-spouse, a stepchild, or legally adopted
- Your child meets any of the following criteria:
  - Certified by a doctor to require assistance in any of the 6 Activities of Daily Living: Washing or Bathing, Dressing, Feeding, Toileting, Transferring, Mobility
  - Certified by a doctor to be impaired in any of these 3 areas: Self-Care and Activities of Daily Living, Compliance to Psychiatric Treatment, Education or Work
  - Attending (or recommended by a doctor to attend) a Special Education (SPED) school
  - Not attending a SPED school but has significant special educational needs, resulting in severe functional impairments

**Conditions related to claiming WMCR together with QCR / Child Relief (Disability):**
- The total claimable relief for QCR, Child Relief (Disability) and WMCR combined is capped at $50,000 per child
- When claiming both, QCR and Child Relief (Disability) claims take precedence; WMCR claims are applied after

## How to Apply / Next Steps
**For QCR - e-Filing:**
- If claiming for the first time, log in to myTax Portal using Singpass or Singpass Foreign user Account (SFA)
- Navigate to "Individuals" > "File Income Tax Return"
- Select "File" to File Income Tax Return
- Go to "4. Deductions, Tax Reliefs and Rebates"
- Select "Add New" > "Child"
- Fill in the relevant information and select "Update"
- If previously approved, the amount will be pre-filled and granted automatically the following year

**For QCR - paper filing:**
- Complete the "Child Relief" section in Form B1 & B
- Include your child's details in Appendix 2 if it's your first time claiming

**For Child Relief (Disability), first-time claims:**
- Email a completed Tax Relief Form ("Application for Claim of Disability Related Tax Reliefs") or letter/supporting documents from the disability association / SPED school via myTax Mail

## Support / Benefits
- Claim up to $7,500 per dependant
- QCR of $4,000 per qualifying child
- Child Relief (Disability) of $7,500 per child with disabilities
- Can be applied alongside Working Mother's Child Relief (WMCR) for additional support
- Parents may share the QCR / Child Relief (Disability) based on an agreed apportionment
