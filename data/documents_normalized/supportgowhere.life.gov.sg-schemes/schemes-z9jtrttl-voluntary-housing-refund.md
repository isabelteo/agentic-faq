# Voluntary Housing Refund

## Overview
Allows you to return any CPF savings used for your home purchase to your CPF account while still owning your property, reducing the future refund amount owed, increasing cash proceeds upon sale/transfer, and boosting retirement savings through CPF interest rates.

## Eligibility
- You have used your Ordinary Account (OA) savings to purchase your property
- You can refund up to the CPF principal amount withdrawn towards the property plus its accrued interest to your CPF account
- If you are an undischarged bankrupt, you have to obtain the Official Assignee's approval to make this housing refund
- While housing grants will generally be refunded to your OA if you have an HDB flat, the grant(s) may be partially credited to your Special Account / Retirement Account and MediSave Account if you have received more than $30,000 in grant(s)
- If you are 55 and above, the refunded amount will be used to meet your Full Retirement Sum (FRS), which supports your retirement needs through monthly payouts
- Any refunds beyond your cohort's FRS will remain in your OA

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Check the amount of voluntary refund you can make under the "What happens if" section in your Home ownership dashboard
- Log in to my cpf digital services using your Singpass, choose your property and enter a refund amount; or
- Use the CPF Mobile app: download the app, log in with your Singpass, tap on 'Services', choose 'Voluntary Housing Refund'

## Support / Benefits
- Receive more cash proceeds and refund less to your CPF account when selling or transferring your property
- Take advantage of CPF's interest rates and retire with more
- Re-use CPF savings for the various CPF approved schemes if needed

## Related Issues & Concerns
- Worried about how much they'll need to refund to CPF when they sell their flat

- Turning 55 soon and unsure how a housing refund affects their Full Retirement Sum
- Received a housing grant and confused about which CPF account it gets credited to
