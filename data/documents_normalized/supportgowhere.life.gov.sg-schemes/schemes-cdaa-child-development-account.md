# Child Development Account

## Overview
The Child Development Account (CDA) is a special savings account for the child that can be used to defray child-raising costs including healthcare and preschool fees. The First Step Grant is automatically deposited after the CDA is opened, and subsequent savings are co-matched by the Government up to a cap. Note: the SG Child Support Package announced at the 2026 National Day Rally will replace the current Baby Bonus Scheme and Large Families Scheme; those on the Baby Bonus Scheme will be automatically transitioned, with existing payouts continuing as usual in the meantime.

## Eligibility
- Child is a Singapore Citizen
- Children born on or after 14 Feb 2023 qualify for the enhanced CDA benefits announced at Budget 2023
- Parents are lawfully married
- Children of unwed parents may be eligible for CDA benefits if born on or after 1 September 2016

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- All parents can apply for the Baby Bonus Scheme at LifeSG, either bundled with the child's birth registration, or as a direct application for a child already registered for birth

## Support / Benefits
- CDA First Step Grant: $5,000 automatically credited into the CDA upon account opening
- Government co-matching of CDA savings: up to a cap of $4,000 for the first child, $7,000 for the second child, $9,000 for the third and fourth child, and $15,000 for the fifth child and beyond
