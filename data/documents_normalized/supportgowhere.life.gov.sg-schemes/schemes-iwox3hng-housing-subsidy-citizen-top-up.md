# Housing Subsidy - Citizen Top-Up

## Overview
Provides a $10,000 housing subsidy for eligible Singapore Citizen/Permanent Resident households when a family member becomes a Singapore Citizen, credited into CPF Ordinary Accounts.

## Eligibility
- Households that paid a $10,000 premium when buying a flat from HDB
- Households that received a reduced Family Grant for a resale flat, a Design Build and Sell Scheme flat, or an Executive Condominium unit
- Granted when a Permanent Resident spouse, parent, child or sibling (for siblings, only for flats purchased as orphaned siblings or families with a non-resident spouse) originally listed in the flat application obtains Singapore Citizenship, or when a Singapore Citizen child is born to the SC applicant and spouse originally listed in the flat application

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Submit your application online using the [Application for Citizen Top-Up e-Service](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg//webapp/AB22AWTopUpGrantWeb/AB22PMain#) within 6 months of meeting the citizenship criteria

## Support / Benefits
- $10,000 housing subsidy, converted into equal shares and credited fully into the CPF Ordinary Accounts of eligible family members
- Only core flat owners can use their share of the CPF housing grants to pay for the flat (e.g. loan repayment)
- Can be used for a lump sum repayment or full redemption of an HDB housing loan; for FI loans, may also be used for monthly instalments (outstanding housing loan arrears must be cleared first)
- Remaining balance with no housing loan can be used to buy another HDB flat in the future, or form part of CPF funds available for withdrawal at age 55
