# Spouse Relief/Spouse Relief (Disability)

## Overview
This scheme provides tax relief of $2,000 (Spouse Relief) or up to $5,500 (Spouse Relief for Disability) to taxpayers who have supported their spouses.

## Eligibility
**For Spouse Relief**, all of the following must be true in the previous year:
- Your spouse has been living with / supported by you
- Your spouse did not have an annual income of more than $8,000
- You have made maintenance payments under a Court Order or Deed of Separation (if legally separated)

**For Spouse Relief (Disability)**:
- Dependant must be certified by a doctor to require assistance with any of the 6 Activities of Daily Living (washing/bathing, dressing, feeding, toileting, transferring, mobility)
- Dependant must be certified by a doctor to be impaired in any of: self-care and ADLs, compliance to psychiatric treatment, or education/work
- No other claimants may claim Parent Relief/Parent Relief (Disability) or Sibling Relief (Disability) on the same dependant (your spouse)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- e-Filing, first-time claim: log in with Singpass or SFA at myTax Portal, go to "Individuals" > "File Income Tax Return" > "File" > "4. Deductions, Tax Reliefs and Rebates" > "Add New" > "Spouse", fill in details and "Update"
- e-Filing, subsequent years: amount is pre-filled and granted automatically; select "Not Claiming" to withdraw
- Paper filing, first-time: complete "Spouse/Spouse Relief (Disability)" section on Form B1 & B and Appendix 2
- Paper filing, subsequent years: complete "Spouse/Spouse Relief (Disability)" section on Form B1 & B
- For first-time Handicapped Spouse Relief claims: email a completed Tax Relief Form and supporting documents from the disability association via myTax Mail

## Support / Benefits
- Spouse Relief of $2,000 for taxpayers who have supported their spouses
- Spouse Relief (Disability) of $5,500 for taxpayers who have supported spouses with disabilities
