# Executive Condominiums (ECs)

## Overview
Provides a more affordable option to eligible Singaporean households who aspire to own private housing. ECs are strata-titled and developed and sold by private developers, with design features and facilities similar to private condominiums.

## Eligibility
**For fiancé/fiancée, married couples and/or parent(s) with child(ren), orphaned siblings**
- Applicant must be a Singapore Citizen (SC), include at least 1 other SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR), and be at least 21 years old

**For 2 or more singles applying jointly**
- All applicants must be SCs and at least 35 years old

**Monthly household income ceiling**
- Total income of all persons listed in the EC application must not exceed $18,000 for new units in EC projects with tenders closing on or after 24 August 2026 (the ceiling was $16,000 for tenders awarded before 24 August 2026)

**Private residential property**
- Must not own, have an interest in, or dispose of any private residential property (local or overseas) in the last 30 months

**Housing subsidies and Resale Levy**
- Core applicants/occupiers who have taken a housing subsidy are considered second-timers and will need to pay the applicable resale levy when buying an EC unit from a developer
- Core applicants/occupiers who have taken 2 housing subsidies are not eligible to apply for or be listed as a core occupier in an EC application

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Submit an [application](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/executive-condominiums/process-for-buying-an-ec) when the EC project is launched
- Contact the property developer directly for details on the application process and required documents
- The property developer will inform you of the outcome of the application
