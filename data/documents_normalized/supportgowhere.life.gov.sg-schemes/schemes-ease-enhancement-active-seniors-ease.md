# Enhancement for Active Seniors (EASE)

## Overview
Provides a subsidy for Singapore Citizen households with at least one senior who meets the age criterion and needs help to install senior-friendly fittings in their flat.

## Eligibility
Singapore Citizen household with a senior who is aged:
- 65 and above; or
- Between 60 and 64, and requires assistance for one or more Activities of Daily Living (ADLs): eating, bathing, dressing, transferring, toileting, walking or moving around

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online via [EASE e-Service](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BN37AWEASE/BN37PMain.jsp) or Mobile@HDB
- Use the ['Calculate Cost of HIP/EASE Improvement Items' e-Service](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/DJ02AWFNCALV7/Main), choosing "EASE (Direct Application)" and indicating flat type and desired improvements
- Singapore Citizen flat owners with a household member aged 60-64 who requires ADL assistance must submit a [Functional Assessment Report](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/-/media/managing-my-home/upgrading-and-redevelopment/enhancement-for-active-seniors-ease/FAR-Sep-2025.pdf), endorsed by a qualified assessor (e.g. a Singapore Medical Council fully registered doctor), together with the application form
- HDB responds within 14 working days upon receipt of application

## Support / Benefits
- Depending on flat type, the subsidy can cover up to 95% of the total improvement cost
- Seniors living in a rental flat: preferred EASE improvement items are fully funded by the Government
