# Home Protection Scheme (HPS)

## Overview
Mortgage-reducing insurance that protects HDB flat owners and their loved ones from losing their flat in the event of death, terminal illness, or total permanent disability.

## Eligibility
- CPF members who own a HDB flat and are HPS policyholders paying monthly housing instalments using CPF savings or cash
- HPS claim payout is made to: next-of-kin of a deceased HPS policyholder, or a policyholder certified by a registered medical practitioner as suffering from terminal illness (likely to result in death within 12 months) or total permanent disability (inability to take part in any employment permanently, or total permanent loss of both eyes, two limbs, or one eye and one limb)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- [Apply online for HPS coverage](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/forms-e-applications/apply-adjust-home-protection-scheme-cover) via the CPF website with Singpass
- Claim on death grounds: if there is an outstanding housing loan, CPF Board automatically assesses the deceased's claim eligibility upon ICA's notification, and informs co-owner(s) or next-of-kin of the outcome
- Claim on medical grounds: submit the application to CPF Board directly via the [CPF website](http://cpf.gov.sg/applyrleandclaimhpsdps)
- Claim processing generally takes 5 working days upon CPF Board's receipt of requested documents

## Support / Benefits
- For claims under death grounds: HPS benefits paid in a lump sum (based on share of HPS cover) to HDB or the mortgagee to settle the outstanding housing loan; any excess is paid to the insured member's CPF Ordinary Account
- For claims admitted under medical grounds: housing instalments (based on share of HPS cover) may be paid by HPS for up to two years, before a review of eligibility to claim the remaining insured sum in full

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about losing my HDB flat if I become critically ill or pass away
- family member has passed away and there's still an outstanding housing loan
- diagnosed with a terminal illness and worried about mortgage payments
- checking how long HPS covers instalments if I become disabled
