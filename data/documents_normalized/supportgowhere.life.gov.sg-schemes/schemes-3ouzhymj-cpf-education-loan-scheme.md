# CPF Education Loan Scheme

## Overview
The CPF Education Loan Scheme allows a CPF member to use their Ordinary Account (OA) savings to pay for their own, children's, spouse's, siblings' or relatives' government subsidised tuition fees for full-time diploma/degree courses at Approved Educational Institutions (AEIs).

## Eligibility
- CPF members/lenders with OA savings are eligible to pay for their own, children's, spouse's, or relative's government subsidised tuition fees using OA savings, up to the Available Withdrawal Limit
- The Available Withdrawal Limit is up to 40% of accumulated OA savings, or the remaining OA balance, whichever is lower
- The CPF member can check the Available Withdrawal Limit through the CPF website using Singpass
- Only full-time diploma / degree courses at Approved Educational Institutions (AEIs) are covered under this loan scheme
- If you are 55 or older, you must set aside the Full Retirement Sum (FRS) in your Retirement Account before using the remaining OA savings, up to the Available Withdrawal Limit
- The amount that can be used is also subject to the tuition fees payable

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Apply online through "Forms and e-applications" at the CPF website with your Singpass
- Read the accompanying Terms & Conditions, and Procedures & Guidelines before completing the online form
- Before submitting, check the application period for the approved educational institution, ensure you are accepted by the institution and confirm it is an approved educational institution, and establish whether you are a new or existing student under the institution
- Submit 1 application form for each CPF lender, for the entire course of study, unless you enrol into a different AEI
- Applications will only be processed after the application period for the approved educational institution has closed
- Processing generally takes 3 working days upon receipt of the student's enrolment confirmation from the approved educational institution
- Note the application period differs for each AEI; refer to their schedules on the CPF website

## Support / Benefits
- The student (borrower) starts repaying the principal amount withdrawn plus accrued interest in cash, to the lender's CPF OA, one year after graduating from or leaving the course of study, whichever occurs earlier
- Alternative education financing options include the MOE Higher Education Student Loan, other financing options offered by institutions, and Post-Secondary Education Account (PSEA) funds
