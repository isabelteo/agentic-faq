# Proximity Housing Grant (Singles)

## Overview
The Proximity Housing Grant (Singles) provides up to $15,000 for eligible singles buying a resale flat to live with, or near, their parents or children.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizen aged 35 or above, buying alone, with other single citizens, or with their parents
- Singapore Citizen aged 21 or above, buying with a non-resident spouse
- The parents/child helping to qualify for the PHG must be Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident
- Buying a resale flat to live with, or within 4km of, parents or child
- If parents/child are living with the applicant, they must be included in the resale flat as co-applicants or essential occupiers
- If parents/child live in a private residential property they do not own, it must be owner-occupied by immediate family members (child, adopted child, parent(s) or parent(s)-in-law, or siblings)
- The resale flat has a remaining lease of more than 20 years
- Applicants must not have received the PHG before

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Obtain an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter through the HDB Flat Portal
- If eligible, apply for the grant when submitting your resale application

## Support / Benefits
- $15,000 grant for singles living with parents or child
- $10,000 grant for singles living within 4km of parents or child
