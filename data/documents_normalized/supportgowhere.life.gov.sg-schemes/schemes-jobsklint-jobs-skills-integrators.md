# Jobs-Skills Integrators

## Overview
Targeted at companies building industry-relevant skills and individuals seeking relevant jobs and skills matches, piloted in the Precision Engineering, Retail, and Wholesale Trade sectors.

## Eligibility
- Available to employers and individuals
- Contact Nanyang Polytechnic (NYP), Singapore National Employers Federation (SNEF), National Trade Union Congress (NTUC), or Singapore Business Federation (SBF) directly for details

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Retail Sector: contact [Singapore National Employers Federation](https://snef.org.sg/grants/jsit-r/) at jsit-r@snef.org.sg
- Wholesale Trade Sector: contact [Singapore Business Federation](https://www.sbf.org.sg/what-we-do/skills-empowered/jobs-skills-integrator-for-wholesale-trade-sector-(jsit-wst)) at jobs.skills@sbf.org.sg

## Support / Benefits
For Individuals:
- Training courses with integrated career services to equip in-demand skills for transitioning into or within the sector
- Sector-specific career advisory services and job matching support
- Careers advisory for employees to explore development opportunities
- A central point of contact for referrals to training and job matching services

For Employers:
- Tailored training courses to meet specific workforce skills needs
- Access to job seekers with the necessary skills for the targeted sector

## Related Issues & Concerns
- looking to switch careers into retail, wholesale trade, or precision engineering
- employer looking for skilled workers in a specific sector
- wanting career advisory and job matching support for a sector transition
- not sure who to contact for sector-specific job and skills matching
