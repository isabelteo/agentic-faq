# Shared Parental Leave (SPL)

## Overview
Shared Parental Leave (SPL) supports eligible working parents in Singapore in sharing parental responsibility and caring for their infants, by letting fathers (and, under the newer scheme, both parents) share part of the mother's maternity or adoption leave.

## Eligibility
**SPL (2013), for fathers, live births:**
- Child is a Singapore citizen or becomes one within 12 months from birth
- Child's birth or estimated delivery date is between 1 Jul 2017 and 31 Mar 2025 (inclusive)
- Wife is eligible for Government-Paid Maternity Leave or Adoption Leave and agrees to share the leave
- Married to child's mother between conception and birth or within 12 months from birth

**SPL (2013), stillbirths:** date of stillbirth between 1 Nov 2021 and 31 Mar 2025 (inclusive); either parent is a Singapore citizen

**SPL (2013), adoptive child:** Formal Intent to Adopt (FIA) date between 1 Jul 2017 and 31 Mar 2025 (inclusive); married to the adoptive mother when the 2013-SPL election was made; if child is not a Singapore citizen, either parent has been a citizen since the child's dependant's pass issuance

**SPL (from 1 Apr 2025), for both father and mother, live births:**
- Child is a Singapore citizen or becomes one within 12 months from birth
- Entitled to 6 weeks of SPL if birth/EDD is between 1 Apr 2025 and 31 Mar 2026, or 10 weeks if on or after 1 Apr 2026
- For fathers: lawfully married to the child's mother between conception and birth or within 12 months from birth
- For employees: served employer at least 3 months before child's birth
- For self-employed: engaged in trade, business, profession, or vocation at least 3 months before child's birth
- No SPL for stillbirths under the from-2025 scheme

**SPL (from 1 Apr 2025), adoptive child:**
- Entitled to 6 weeks of SPL if FIA date is between 1 Apr 2025 and 31 Mar 2026, or 10 weeks if on or after 1 Apr 2026
- Not the natural father or mother of the child
- If child is not a Singapore citizen, either adoptive parent has been a citizen since the child's dependant's pass issuance
- For employees: served employer at least 3 months before FIA date
- For self-employed: engaged in trade, business, profession, or vocation at least 3 months before FIA date

Parents whose child is born, has an EDD, or has an FIA date on or after 1 Apr 2025 are entitled to the new SPL scheme and should not elect 2013-SPL.

## How to Apply / Next Steps
**For SPL (2013):**
- Discuss and agree on the leave arrangement with your wife
- Wife must declare her intention to share leave through the Share My Leave eService in the Government-Paid Leave (GPL) Portal no earlier than 16 weeks before the estimated delivery date
- Employees: notify employer and submit the declaration form (SPL1) with necessary documents (https://go.gov.sg/spl1form)
- Self-employed: record SPL dates and submit claim online via the GPL Portal within 3 months after the last date of SPL
- Employers: obtain and verify the SPL1 form and supporting documents, keep records for 5 years, verify the employee's wife has declared to share leave, and submit reimbursement claim online no later than 3 months after the employee's last SPL date

**For SPL (from 1 Apr 2025):** refer to details on the GPL Portal (https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/shared-parental-leave/)

## Support / Benefits
- SPL (2013): eligible working fathers can take up to 4 weeks of leave from their wife's Government-Paid Maternity Leave/Adoption Leave; government reimbursement/payment capped at $2,500 per week, up to a total of $10,000 for 4 weeks
- SPL (from 1 Apr 2025): up to 6 weeks (births/FIA between 1 Apr 2025-31 Mar 2026) or up to 10 weeks (on or after 1 Apr 2026) to be shared between parents; government reimbursement/payment capped at $2,500 per week
- Leave may be taken in one continuous block or non-continuously, with mutual agreement between employer and employee
- Must be taken within 12 months from the child's date of birth; for the from-2025 scheme, only after GPML/GPPL/Adoption Leave is fully consumed
