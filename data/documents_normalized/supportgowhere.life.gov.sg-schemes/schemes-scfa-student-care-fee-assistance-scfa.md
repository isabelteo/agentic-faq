# Student Care Fee Assistance (SCFA)

## Overview
Provides fee assistance for children of lower-income working families enrolled in Student Care Centres (SCCs) registered with MSF, including Special SCCs (SSCCs) for children with disabilities.

## Eligibility
- Child is a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident (at least one immediate family member in the household must be a Singapore Citizen)
- Both applicant and spouse are each working at least 56 hours a month
- Child must attend a Student Care Centre registered with MSF
- For SCCs, children aged 7 to 14 (Primary 1 to Secondary 2): monthly gross household income must be $4,500 or less for families with 4 or fewer members, or per capita income $1,125 or less for families with 5 or more members (from January 2027: $6,500 or less, or per capita $1,625 or less)
- For SSCCs, children aged 7 to 18: monthly gross household income must be $9,200 or less for families with 4 or fewer members, or per capita income $2,300 or less for families with 5 or more members
- Income is calculated based on average gross monthly income over 12 months from CPF records or the latest IRAS assessment (within the last 2 years) for salaried employees, or average trade income over 12 months from the latest IRAS assessment or CPF Board declared income for self-employed individuals
- Family members (for the income test) refer to the child's grandparents or siblings below 21 living at the same address who are not working/have no income and are SC/PR

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Submit SCFA applications online via [SupportGoWhere](https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/grants/scfa) at least six months before the intended start date (e.g. apply from July 2026 to start January 2027)
- Approval commences from the month the application is received, or from the month after any existing SCFA expires, whichever is later
- See the [application process infographic](https://www.msf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/student-care/scfa-infographic---application-process.pdf) for more detail
- If unemployed or job-seeking, supporting documents (e.g. proof of job search, medical letter/certificate) are required
- If facing difficulty with the monthly co-payment after SCFA, submit an appeal through the SCC with supporting documents; approved appeals commence from the month the appeal was submitted

## Support / Benefits
- Subsidies of up to $290 per month for children enrolled in SCCs (percentage/amount depends on household income, from 98%/$1,500-and-below income tier down to 20%/$4,001-$4,500 tier)
- Co-payments from $11 per month for children enrolled in Special SCCs (SSCCs), depending on household income
- One-time Start-Up Grant (SUG) of up to $400 per child
