# Singapore Workforce Skills Qualifications

## Overview
A national credential system (WSQ) that trains, develops, assesses and certifies skills and competencies for the workforce, open to all regardless of academic background.

## Eligibility
- Open to all individuals, regardless of academic background
- Recognises prior learning and work experience

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Visit the [MySkillsFuture portal](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/index.html) to browse available WSQ courses
- Register for the course through the training provider
- Complete the course to receive a Statement of Attainment or WSQ qualification

## Support / Benefits
- Acquire job role-specific skills and competencies through competency-based training
- Gain portable credentials to improve employability and mobility
- Access bite-sized modules leading to Statements of Attainment and full WSQ qualifications
- Quality-assured qualifications awarded by SWDA or in partnership with established awarding bodies
