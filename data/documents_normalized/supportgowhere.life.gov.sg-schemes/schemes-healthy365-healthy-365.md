# Healthy 365

## Overview
Healthy 365 is a mobile application that helps residents discover and access healthy lifestyle offerings to achieve their health goals, for Singapore Citizens, Permanent Residents or FIN holders aged 15 and above with a Singpass account.

## Eligibility
- Singapore Citizens, Permanent Residents or Foreign Identification Number (FIN) holders aged 15 and above with a Singpass account

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- Download the Healthy 365 app on Apple Store / Google Play
- Follow the on-screen steps to create a new account or log in to your existing Healthy 365 app
- Use your Singpass account to sign up for a Healthy 365 account

## Support / Benefits
- Track daily physical activity and sleep duration with a compatible wearable
- Use the meal log tool to monitor daily calorie intake
- Participate in challenges such as the National Steps Challenge™ and Eat, Drink, Shop Healthy Challenge
- Earn Healthpoints and redeem them from a catalogue of lifestyle rewards
- Participate in recommended programmes and book activities such as free exercise classes and health workshops
- Access informative resources and partner programmes

## Related Issues & Concerns
- looking for free ways to stay active and healthy
- wanting rewards for exercising or eating healthily
- trying to track fitness progress with an app
- not sure how to sign up for the National Steps Challenge
