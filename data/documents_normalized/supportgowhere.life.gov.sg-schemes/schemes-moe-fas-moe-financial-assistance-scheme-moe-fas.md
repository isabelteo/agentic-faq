# MOE Financial Assistance Scheme (MOE FAS)

## Overview
MOE FAS provides financial assistance to lower-income families with children in Government and Government-Aided Schools and Specialised Schools.

## Eligibility
- Singaporean students studying in Government and Government-Aided Schools and Specialised Schools
- Average Gross Household Income of up to $4,000, or Average Per Capita Income of up to $1,000

## How to Apply / Next Steps
- For students from Government and Government-Aided Schools: apply online via https://go.gov.sg/moe-efasggas
- For students from Specialised Schools: apply online via https://go.gov.sg/moe-efasss

## Support / Benefits
- Full waiver of school and miscellaneous fees
- Free textbooks and school attire for primary and secondary school students
- Transport subsidy: 70% of school bus fares per year for primary students who take school bus, or $21 transport credit per month for primary and secondary students who take public transport
- Meal subsidy: 7 meals per school week for primary school students; 10 meals per school week for secondary school students
- Full waiver of school fees and bursary of $1,600 per annum for pre-university students

## Related Issues & Concerns
- worried about affording school fees, textbooks, or uniforms for my children
- wondering if my household income qualifies for school financial aid
- need help with transport or meal costs for my child's schooling
- looking for bursary support for a pre-university student
