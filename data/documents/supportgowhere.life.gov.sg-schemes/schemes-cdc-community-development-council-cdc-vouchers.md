About this support

- Each Singaporean household can claim $500 in CDC Vouchers from June 2026, and an additional $300 from January 2027. The vouchers will be valid until 31 December 2027

- The vouchers are allocated equally for spending at participating heartland merchants, hawkers, and supermarkets


Who is this for?

Households with at least 1 Singapore Citizen

How to apply?

- No application needed

- Singaporean households can claim their vouchers via the RedeemSG website

- Find out [how to claim your CDC vouchers](https://vouchers.cdc.gov.sg/residents/how-to-claim-and-spend/)
