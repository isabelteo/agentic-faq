About this support

Executors or administrators of a deceased can act on the deceased’s following matters:

- Filing personal income tax for the deceased on the income earned up to the date of death.

- Filing estate or trust income tax for the deceased on the income. Estate income refers to the income generated by the deceased’s assets after date of death, during the administration of the estate. Trust income refers to the income from assets held in trust.

- Settling the taxes and other bills such as loans, funeral expenses and medical bills from the estate funds of the deceased before distribution to deceased’s beneficiaries.


Who is this for?

- Executors or administrators of a deceased’s estate who are responsible for settling outstanding taxes, debts and liabilities.

- Remember, the executor or administrator must ensure all outstanding taxes, loans, funeral expenses, and medical bills are paid from the deceased's estate before distributing the remaining assets to the beneficiaries.


How to apply?

| File personal income tax for the deceased | Provide IRAS with the following details via [myTaxMail](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/portal/correspondence/mytax-mail):<br>- Name, address and identification number of the legal personal representative who is handling the affairs of the deceased;<br>  <br>- Copy of the death certificate;<br>  <br>- Copy of the Grant of Probate/Letter of Administration, if available;<br>  <br>- Income details from all sources, up to the date of death (please specify the breakdown for each income source); and<br>  <br>- Type and amount of tax relief available/applicable to reduce the tax burden. |
| File an Income Tax Return for Estate or Trust Income | - Download and complete the Request Form for Form T from [IRAS's website](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/other-taxes/trusts/filing-estate-trust-income-tax-(form-t)/form-t) _._<br>  <br>- Email the completed form to [trust@iras.gov.sg](mailto:trust@iras.gov.sg) and IRAS will provide you with:<br>  <br>  - a Trust Reference Number<br>    <br>  - a copy of Form T to complete<br>- Mail the completed Form T to this address: The Comptroller of Income Tax, 55 Newton Road, Singapore 307987. |
