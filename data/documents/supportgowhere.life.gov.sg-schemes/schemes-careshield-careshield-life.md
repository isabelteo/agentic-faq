About this support

- Monthly cash payouts if you remain severely disabled and meet the eligibility criteria for claims

- Monthly cash payout for 2026 is $689 and increases annually until age 67 or whenever a successful claim is made, whichever is earlier


Who is this for?

- Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents who are CareShield Life policyholders

- Assessed by an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor require full assistance with at least 3 out of the following 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs):

  - Washing

  - Dressing

  - Feeding

  - Toileting

  - Walking or moving around

  - Transferring

How to apply?

You can apply:

- Online at the [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)

- Via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in

- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)


You will need to contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the application. Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment) for a list of assessors.
