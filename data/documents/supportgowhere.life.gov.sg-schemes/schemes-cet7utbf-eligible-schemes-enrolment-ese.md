Eligible Schemes Enrolment (ESE)

Share link

A streamlined process to apply for several long-term care financial support schemes administered by the Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) at the same time.

SCHEME HIGHLIGHTS

- Apply for eligible schemes at one go

Expand all sections

About this support

Simplifies the application process for those applying for the following long-term care financial assistance schemes under the ESE process:

- CareShield Life

- ElderFund

- ElderShield

- Home Caregiving Grant (HCG)

- Interim Disability Assistance Programme for the Elderly (IDAPE)


Who is this for?

- Anyone who would like to apply to multiple long-term care financial assistance schemes under ESE

- Aged 21 years and above

- Can make decisions about their long-term care needs


How to apply?

If you are currently undergoing [Severe Disability Assessment (SDA)](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment) for any of the eligible schemes under ESE, inform your assessor that you would like to apply for ESE.
