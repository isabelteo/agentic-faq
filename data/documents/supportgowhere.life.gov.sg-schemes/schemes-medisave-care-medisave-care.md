# MediSave Care - SupportGoWhere

MediSave Care

Share link

Allows Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents with severe disability to make monthly cash withdrawals from their and/or their spouse’s MediSave Account for long-term care.

SCHEME HIGHLIGHTS

- Withdraw up to $200 cash per month
- Processing time will take between 6 to 8 weeks
- Apply through AIC eServices for Financing Services (eFASS)

About this support

- Supports severely disabled Singapore Citizens or Permanent residents with monthly cash withdrawals to support long-term care needs

- Withdraw up to $200 per month from care recipient’s and/or their spouse's MediSave account

- [Withdrawal amounts are dependent on the care recipient and/or their spouse’s MediSave account balance](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/medisave-care-for-long-term-care-needs)


Who is this for?

- Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident

- Aged 30 and above

- MediSave account balance of at least $5,000 for both the care recipient and their spouse

- Assessed by an MOH-accredited assessor to be unable to perform at least 3 out of the following 6 Activities of Daily Living (ADLs):

  - Washing

  - Dressing

  - Feeding

  - Toileting

  - Walking or moving around

  - Transferring

How to apply?

You can apply:

- Online at the [AIC eServices for Financing Schemes (eFASS)](https://efinance.aic.sg/)

- Via assistance from the nursing home your loved one is staying in

- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)


You will need to contact an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor for a Severe Disability Assessment (SDA) as part of the application. Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment) for a list of assessors.
