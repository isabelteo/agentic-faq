# Infant and Childcare Subsidies - SupportGoWhere

Infant and Childcare Subsidies

Share link

Provides tiered subsidies to help cover infant care and childcare fees for Singapore Citizen children from different income backgrounds.

SCHEME HIGHLIGHTS

- Working applicants: Basic Subsidy of $600 for infant care and $300 for childcare
- Non-working applicants: Basic Subsidy of $150 for infant care and childcare
- Additional Subsidy for working applicants

Expand all sections

About this support

**Working applicants (mother or single father who works at least 56 hours per month)**

- Basic Subsidy of $600 for infant care and $300 for childcare

- Additional Subsidy of up to $710 for infant care and up to $467 for childcare


**Non-working applicants**

- Basic Subsidy of $150 for infant care and childcare

- Eligible applicants who are not working or who are non-parent caregivers can apply for Special Approval to receive the higher Basic Subsidy and Additional Subsidy that working applicants receive


Who is this for?

| Types of subsidies | Eligibility criteria |
| For all subsidies | - Child must be a Singapore Citizen<br>  <br>- Enrolled in an infant care or childcare centre licensed by ECDA |
| Additional Subsidy | - Working applicants (mother or single father who works at least 56 hours per month)<br>  <br>- Monthly household income of up to $12,000, or monthly household income per capita of up to $3,000 for larger families with 5 or more family members in the same household<br>  <br>  - From 1 January 2027, monthly household income thresholds will be raised to $15,000, or $3,400 per capita |
| Special Approval | - Main subsidy applicants who are not working due to the following reasons:<br>  <br>  - Looking for a job<br>    <br>  - Studying, training, or on course for at least 56 hours a month<br>    <br>  - Pregnancy<br>    <br>  - Medically unfit for work due to hospitalisation, long-term illness and/or permanent disability<br>    <br>  - Taking care of a special needs child<br>    <br>  - Taking care of a sick family member who requires a full-time caregiver<br>    <br>  - Caring full-time for a younger child aged 24 months and below who is not enrolled in a preschool<br>    <br>  - Incarcerated<br>    <br>  - Non-parent caregivers who are caring for the child such as grandparents, legal guardian, foster parents, and head of a children’s home<br>- Families who are tenants of a rental flat under the HDB’s Public Rental Scheme or are beneficiaries of ComCare Short-To-Medium-Term Assistance (SMTA) or Long-Term-Assistance (LTA) will automatically qualify for maximum preschool subsidies upon application for Special Approval |

How to apply?

Refer to Early Childhood Development Agency (ECDA) [website](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/preschool-subsidies/how-to-apply-or-make-changes-to-subsidies) to apply or make changes to subsidies
