About this support

Career and Skills Advisory offers personalised advice to help you navigate job search challenges and identify skills and training needs to bring you closer to your career goals. This service is offered through Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA) and our partners including NTUC's e2i and our Career Matching Providers, Ingeus and AngusKnight Singapore.

Who is this for?

Singapore Citizens, Permanent Residents and foreign spouses who are Long-Term Visit Pass/Long-Term Visit Pass Plus Holders with Pre-Approved Letter of Consent or Letter of Consent

How to apply?

Make an appointment for Career and Skills Advisory at:

- [Lifelong Learning Institute](https://careershorizon.mycareersfuture.gov.sg/appointment)


- [Other service touchpoints](https://www.lifelonglearningsg.org/advice/skills-and-training-advisory)
