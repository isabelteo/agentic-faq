# Retirement Sum Topping-Up Scheme - SupportGoWhere

Retirement Sum Topping-Up Scheme

Share link

Boost your own or loved ones’ retirement savings and payouts through voluntary cash top-ups or CPF transfer to your own or loved ones' Special Account (SA) or Retirement Account (RA).

SCHEME HIGHLIGHTS

- Boost CPF retirement savings and monthly payouts
- Earn interest rates of up to 6% per annum on retirement savings
- Tax relief of up to $16,000 for cash top-ups

[Apply now](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/forms-e-applications/cash-top-ups-and-cpf-transfers-for-retirement)

About this support

- Receive higher monthly payouts in retirement

- Earn CPF interest rates of up to 6% per annum on retirement savings

- Grow your retirement savings through the power of compound interest for higher monthly payouts

- Enjoy tax relief of up to $16,000 for cash top-ups made to your own and loved ones' retirement savings each calendar year

- Find out more on the [conditions to qualify for tax relief](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-are-the-conditions-for-me-to-enjoy-tax-relief-to-special-account-or-retirement-account)


Note:

- In keeping with the objective of the scheme, cash top-ups and CPF transfers are irreversible, and cannot be withdrawn for other purposes (e.g. for housing, investment or withdrawn for immediate needs after age 55).

- Cash top-ups that attract the MRSS matching grant do not qualify for tax relief.


Who is this for?

- You must be a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident to receive cash top-ups and CPF transfers

- You can check your top-up limits by logging in to your [Retirement Dashboard](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/ds/dashboards/retirement)

- Members aged 55 and above can top up to the Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS)

  - The ERS increases each year so you can top up more each year
- Members below 55 can top up to the Full Retirement Sum (FRS)

- CPF transfers can be made to yourself or your loved ones:


| CPF transfer to | Remarks |
| Yourself | You can transfer your Ordinary Account (OA) savings to your SA if you are below 55. <br>If you are aged 55 or above, you can transfer your OA savings to your RA. <br>How much you can transfer depends on your age and CPF balances. |
| Loved ones <br>(parents, parents-in-law, grandparents, grandparents-in-law, spouse and siblings) | Before transferring your CPF savings to your loved ones, you must first set aside enough for your own retirement. <br>The amount of CPF transfers you can make to them depends on how much your loved one can receive, and the amount you can transfer. |

- For more information on your [eligibility for cash transfers, CPF top-ups etc., please refer here](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/growing-your-savings/saving-more-with-cpf/top-up-to-enjoy-higher-retirement-payouts)


How to apply?

- You can make your cash top-up or CPF transfer [online](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/forms-e-applications/cash-top-ups-and-cpf-transfers-for-retirement) (CPF transfers and cash top-ups via PayNow or GIRO) or via the CPF Mobile app (CPF transfers and cash tops-ups via PayNow)

- To complete your top-up form, you will need:

  - your recipient’s NRIC, if making a cash top-up or CPF transfer to a loved one

  - additional supporting documents if making a CPF transfer to a recipient for the first time (not required for cash top-ups)
- For more information on the application process, please refer [here.](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/how-do-i-apply-to-make-a-top-up)
