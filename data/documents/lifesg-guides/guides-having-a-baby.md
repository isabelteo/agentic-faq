# Financial support

Financial support

Schemes that help lighten the financial costs of having and raising children.

[Medisave Maternity PackageUse your CPF MediSave to pay for pre-delivery and delivery expenses.](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/marriage-and-parenthood-schemes)[Baby Bonus Cash GiftA cash gift that's given out every 6 months until the child turns 6.5 years old.](https://www.life.gov.sg/family-parenting/benefits-support/baby-bonus-scheme)[Baby Bonus Child Development AccountSpecial savings account with a one-off grant and government matching.](https://www.life.gov.sg/family-parenting/benefits-support/baby-bonus-scheme)[Large Families SchemeThe Large Families Scheme is a Government Scheme administered by the Ministry of Social and Family Development, to strengthen support for Singaporeans who have three or more children.](https://www.life.gov.sg/family-parenting/family-planning/large-families-scheme)[Child LifeSG Credits (CLC)$500 credits given to all families with Singapore Citizen children aged 0-12.](https://supportgowhere.life.gov.sg/schemes/GNAgZJDj/child-lifesg-credits)

Leave entitlements for working parents

Get time off to prepare for delivery, recover from childbirth, and bond with your baby.

For mothers[Maternity leaveUp to 16 weeks of paid leave for working mothers, including self-employed.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/maternity-leave)[Maternity benefitFor working mothers who do not qualify for paid maternity leave due to their employment arrangements.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/maternity-benefit)[Adoption leaveUp to 12 weeks of paid leave for working mothers who adopt a child.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/adoption-leave)[Adoption benefitFor working mothers who do not qualify for adoption leave due to their employment arrangements.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/adoption-benefit)

View more

For fathers[Paternity leaveUp to 4 weeks of paid leave for working fathers, including self-employed and adoptive fathers.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/paternity-leave)[Paternity benefitFor working fathers who do not qualify for paid paternity leave due to their employment arrangements.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/paternity-benefit)

For both parents[Shared Parental Leave (SPL)(From 1 April 2025) 6 weeks of additional paid parental leave, to be shared between both parents.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/shared-parental-leave)[Shared Parental Benefit (SPB)(From 1 April 2025) For working parents who do not qualify for paid shared parental leave due to their employment arrangements.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/shared-parental-leave-benefit)[Childcare leaveUp to 6 days of paid leave for those with children below 7 years old.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/childcare-leave)[Extended childcare leave2 days of paid leave for those with children between 7 and 12 years old.](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/leave/childcare-leave/extended-childcare-leave)[Unpaid infant care leaveUp to 12 days of unpaid leave for those with children below 2 years old.](https://www.profamilyleave.msf.gov.sg/schemes/unpaid-Infant-Care-Leave)
