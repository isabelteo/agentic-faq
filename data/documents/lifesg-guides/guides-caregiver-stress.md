# Look after yourself

Look after yourself

To care for others, you must first take care of yourself.

[Importance of self-careYou should not neglect your own physical, mental, and spiritual needs.](https://aic.sg/Caregiving-Support/What-is-Self-Care)[Support your needs as a caregiverMake time to care for yourself to avoid burnout.](https://aic.sg/Caregiving-Support/Knowing-Available-Care-Options)[Managing caregiver stressRecognise signs of stress and learn how to manage better.](https://aic.sg/Caregiving-Support/Managing-Stress-and-Avoiding-Burnout)

Reach out for support

Whether it’s a helping hand from family, or emotional support from other caregivers, it helps when you know you’re not alone.

[Family support and discussionBuild a strong support team, and learn to ask your family members for help.](https://aic.sg/Caregiving-Support/Involving-Your-Family)[Tips from other caregiversShare experiences and get advice from fellow caregivers in support groups.](https://aic.sg/Caregiving-Support/Connecting-with-other-caregivers)[Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) LinkGet personalised advice on care services and support schemes for yourself and your loved one.](https://www.aic.sg/about-us/aic-link)[Caregiver Support Action PlanLearn about the framework that MOH has introduced to support caregivers.](https://aic.sg/Caregiving-Support)

Seek help for caregiving

Asking for help or taking a break takes courage, but it could be the best thing you can do for yourself and your loved one.

[Eldercarer Foreign Domestic Worker SchemeHire a domestic helper who will undergo training in eldercare before they arrive at your home.](https://www.aic.sg/caregiving/eldercarer-foreign-domestic-worker-scheme)[Senior community care servicesFind suitable senior care services to support you in your caregiving duties.](https://aic.sg/Caregiving-Support)[Community mental health resources and servicesFor those who are caring for a loved one with a mental health condition.](https://aic.sg/Caregiving-Support/Mental-Health-Support)
