Caregiving expenses

If you’re a caregiver, you may be eligible for grants, levy concession, and tax relief.

[Home Caregiving Grant (HCG)Monthly cash payout to offset the cost of caring for a loved one with permanent moderate disability.](https://www.aic.sg/financial-assistance/home-caregiving-grant)[Foreign Domestic Worker (FDW) Levy ConcessionConcessionary levy rate for FDW employers caring for loved ones with mild disability.](https://www.aic.sg/financial-assistance/foreign-domestic-worker-levy-concession)[Caregivers Training GrantAnnual subsidy for caregivers to attend approved courses to better care for their loved ones.](https://www.aic.sg/financial-assistance/caregivers-training-grant)[Tax relief for caregiversFor those who are supporting their parents, grandparents, parents-in-law or grandparents-in-law.](https://www.iras.gov.sg/irashome/Individuals/Locals/Working-Out-Your-Taxes/Deductions-for-Individuals/Parent-Relief-/-Handicapped-Parent-Relief/)

Disability support

Explore other options besides CareShield Life and ElderShield.
