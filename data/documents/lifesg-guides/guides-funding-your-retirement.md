# CPF savings

CPF savings

Learn how your CPF savings can help fund your basic needs after you retire.

[CPF LifeMonthly payouts that start between age 65 and 70, for as long as you live.](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/retirement-income/monthly-payouts/cpf-life)[Retirement Sum SchemeMonthly payouts that start between age 65 and 70 until you turn 90.](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/retirement-income/monthly-payouts/non-cpf-life)[Top up your CPF Retirement AccountFor attractive interest rates, higher monthly payouts and tax relief.](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/growing-your-savings/saving-more-with-cpf/top-up-to-enjoy-higher-retirement-payouts)

Supplement your savings

Explore ways to increase your retirement savings for a more comfortable lifestyle.

[HDB Lease Buyback SchemeSell part of your lease for higher CPF payouts and a cash bonus.](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/retirement-planning/monetising-flat-for-retirement/lease-buyback-scheme-lbs/understanding-the-lbs)[Silver Housing BonusDownsize your flat and receive a cash bonus of up to S$30,000.](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/retirement-planning/monetising-flat-for-retirement/silver-housing-bonus)

Health insurance

Protect your retirement fund from unexpected medical bills.

[MediShield LifeHelps with large hospital bills and selected treatments.](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/medishield-life)[ElderShieldMonthly payouts for severe disabilities, for up to 6 years.](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/eldershield)[CareShield LifeMonthly payouts for severe disabilities, for as long as you live.](https://www.careshieldlife.gov.sg/careshield-life/about-careshield-life.html)[CareShield Life and ElderShield supplementsUpgrade your coverage with supplement plans from private insurers.](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/getting-supplementary-coverage)
