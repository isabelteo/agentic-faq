# Help In Finding A New Job

- [Home](https://www.life.gov.sg/)
- [Guides](https://www.life.gov.sg/guides)
- [Retrenchment benefits and measures](https://www.life.gov.sg/guides/retrenchment-benefits-and-measures)
- Help in finding a new job

In the case of retrenchment, you can get help from unions and agencies to:

find opportunities for new jobs

explore training courses and learn new skills

prepare for your resume, CV or interviews

find information on schemes and benefits

If you’re recently retrenched, you can get career advice from Career Grit at WSG and mental wellness support from mindline.sg.

Read more about support for your job search.

Please note that mindline.sg maintains the anonymity of users and does not collect any Personally Identifiable Information.
