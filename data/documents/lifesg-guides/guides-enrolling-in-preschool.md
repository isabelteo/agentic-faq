# Preschool education

Preschool education

It’s not compulsory to enrol your child in a preschool, but most parents do.

[Preschool: how it works and why it’s goodFind out how activities at preschool can support your child’s development.](https://familiesforlife.sg/parenting/Young-Children/Pages/YoungChildrenEducation/YoungChildrenGetting_Ready_for_School/Young_Children_How_Why.aspx )[Starting preschoolHelp your child get ready, especially if it’s their first time away from family.](https://familiesforlife.sg/parenting/Young-Children/Pages/YoungChildrenEducation/YoungChildrenGetting_Ready_for_School/Young_Children_Starting_Preschool.aspx)

Types of preschool

Child care, kindergartens, and selected operators that receive government funding to keep their fees affordable.

[Choosing a preschool for your childLearn about types of preschool available for children between 2 months and 7 years old.](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/choosing-a-preschool/choosing-a-preschool-for-your-child)[Anchor Operator (AOP)AOP preschools have a fee cap of of $720 for full-day child care and $1,275 for full-day infant care.](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/choosing-a-preschool/aop)[Partner Operator (POP)POP preschools have a fee cap of $760 for full-day child care and $1,330 for full-day infant care.](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/choosing-a-preschool/pop)

Financial and community support

Subsidies, financial assistance, and other support to give your child a good start in life.

[Child care and infant care subsidiesHigher subsidies for parents who are working, and additional subsidies for those in lower-income families.](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/preschool-subsidies/infant-and-childcare-subsidy-scheme)[Kindergarten Fee Assistance Scheme (KiFAS)For lower-income families with children in MOE kindergartens or preschool anchor operators.](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/preschool-subsidies#KIFAS)[Childminding for infantsFind a professional childminder to care for your infant (2 to 18 months old) in a home or community space.](https://www.ecda.gov.sg/parents/other-services/childminding-pilot-for-infants)[Child concession cardFor children under 7 who are above 0.9m tall to travel for free.](https://simplygo.com.sg/concession-monthly-travel-cards/child/child-concession-card/)

Early intervention programmes

If your child is not meeting developmental milestones, these subsidised programmes can help.

[Development Support (DS) and Learning Support (LS) ProgrammeFor K1 or K2 students with mild developmental or learning needs.](https://www.enablingguide.sg/im-looking-for-disability-support/therapy-intervention/development-support-and-learning-support)[Early Intervention Programme for Infants and Children (EIPIC)For children under 6 who are at risk of developmental, intellectual, sensory or physical disabilities.](https://www.enablingguide.sg/im-looking-for-disability-support/therapy-intervention/early-intervention-programme-for-infants-children)[Enhanced Pilot for Private Intervention ProvidersSubsidies for parents whose child has been referred to EIPIC, but choose to enrol in selected private intervention centres.](https://www.enablingguide.sg/im-looking-for-disability-support/therapy-intervention/enhanced-pilot-for-private-intervention-providers)
