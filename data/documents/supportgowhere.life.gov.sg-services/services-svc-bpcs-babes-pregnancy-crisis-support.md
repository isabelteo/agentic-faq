About this support

Babes supports pregnant teens through:

- Casework and counselling

- 24-hour helpline

- Practical support (befriender, baby essentials, employment & back to school assistance etc)

- Information & resources

- Outreach and youth engagement events


Who is this for?

- Pregnant teenager who needs help; Anyone who needs information about teenage pregnancy

- Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents


What to expect?

- Teenagers who require help with their pregnancy and/or anyone who needs information about teenage pregnancy may contact Babes via the 24-hour helpline (call/text).

- When an enquiry is attended to, the Helpliner will also assess if the teenager is eligible to be a case under Babes. Should the assessment be one that Babes can assist, Babes will arrange for a meeting with the teenager and/or her family/partner within 3-5 working days to assess the needs and provide the necessary support and guidance.


How to apply?

If you are a pregnant teen who needs help, know of one who needs help or need information about teenage pregnancy, please contact Babes' 24-hour helpline at 6206 6641 or text via WhatsApp at 8111 3535 to speak to the caseworkers.
