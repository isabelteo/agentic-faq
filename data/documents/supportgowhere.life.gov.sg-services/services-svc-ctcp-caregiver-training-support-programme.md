About this support

CTSP consists of the Caregivers-to-Caregivers (C2C) Programme and Engagement-to-Empowerment (E2E) Programme. C2C aims to provide caregivers with the knowledge and skills regarding caregiving so that they can better cope with their caregiving duties.

C2C provides various types of training courses that range from 8 to 12 sessions and in 3 different languages, to cover general or specific mental health conditions. Through the C2C training courses, caregiver leaders are also identified and trained to volunteer as caregiver leaders to support other caregivers in subsequent C2C classes and /or outreach and advocacy work.

E2E consists of the following components:

- Caregiver-for-Caregiver (C4C) Support Programme: 12 support group sessions over a year, with the aim of increasing support networks for caregivers. The first six sessions will be held in-person and will aim to provide support and equip caregivers with coping skills. Subsequent sessions may be a mix of online or in-person sessions, including self-run activities by caregivers with support from Mindfull Community Limited staff, to gather fellow caregivers for social activities or a sharing session.

- Train-the-Trainer (TTT): Caregivers who indicated interest to contribute and identified by staff as having potential are coached and trained as Volunteer Trainers and/or to support advocacy and outreach work through “Train-the-Trainer” modules so they can volunteer to support CAL’s programmes and activities.


Who is this for?

- Caregivers of Persons with Mental Health Issues (PMHIs).

- This can range from spouses, partners, parents, siblings, grandchildren, friends, neighbours, etc of someone who is experiencing a mental health condition.
