About this support

The GRIT Academy (TGA) aims to provide individual mentoring support for youths-at-risk aged between 11 to 17 years old residing or studying in schools in Jurong; and serve as the primary relationship holder to follow through with the youths as they transit to other youth programmes.

Who is this for?

The target clientele is youths-at-risk aged between 11 to 17 years old residing or studying in schools in Jurong (i.e. Jurong West, Jurong East and Taman Jurong).

Applicants must fulfil at least one of the following criteria to be eligible for the programme:

1. Youths who lack meaningful engagements such as those not attending Co-Curricular Activities, as well as youths who often loiter at public areas such as playgrounds and void decks.

2. Youths with underprivileged background such as youths from low-income families, students receiving the MOE Financial Assistance Scheme and youths staying in rental flats.

3. Youths exhibiting at-risk behaviour such as poor affiliation to schools, minor behavioural issues, socio-emotional issues and/or peer issues.


What to expect?

Screening for potential service users will take approximately 2 weeks from the referral/youth sign-up. Adjustment to the screening time frame will be considered based on the assessed urgency.

How to apply?

Complete the form on [https://lakeside.org.sg/our-services/the-grit-project/](https://lakeside.org.sg/our-services/the-grit-project/) and email it to [thegritproject@lakeside.org.sg](mailto:thegritproject@lakeside.org.sg).
