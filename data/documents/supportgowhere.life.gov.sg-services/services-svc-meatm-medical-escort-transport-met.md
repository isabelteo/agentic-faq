About this support

Provides transportation services with trained care aides to help frail seniors travel to and from their homes for medical appointments, and to accompany them during the appointment if needed.

Who is this for?

Senior who needs help to move around, and has no caregiver, or whose caregiver who is unable to provide support because of their own health and physical condition.

What to expect?

Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test](https://aic.sg/Care-Services/Meals-on-wheels#faq) result.

- Only applicable to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents


How to apply?

You can apply:

- Through the hospital, polyclinic or GP who is familiar with your loved one’s condition and needs

- At any of the [AIC Links](https://www.aic.sg/Contact-Us#map)



If you are interested to view the list of service providers, visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Medical-Escort-and-Transport).



For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

  - Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.

  - Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.
