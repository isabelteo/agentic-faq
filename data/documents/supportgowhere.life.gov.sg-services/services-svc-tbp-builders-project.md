About this support

The Builders Project is an early intervention programme targeting Primary 4-5 school children that aims to promote positive school climate and culture that are conducive for achieving social capital and social discipline. The programme works with the parents and teachers of the identified students which leads to better school outcomes such as lower chronic school absenteeism, premature withdrawal from school and other adolescent risk behaviours.

Who is this for?

P4 to P6 children who:

- Are in mainstream primary schools

- Have been assessed to have higher need


What to expect?

- The one year programme is available in selected partner schools.

- Lutheran Community Care Services (LCCS) will administer a pre-screening tool to students referred by teachers and social workers.

- Based on the results, LCCS and teachers will jointly decide on the identified students who are suitable for further support.


How to apply?

- Schools will refer students to the programme.
