About this support

- Facilitates the process for eligible foreigners to reside in Singapore on a permanent basis

- Permanent Residents aged 15 and above will be issued the Singapore blue Identity Card (IC)


Who is this for?

Available for:

- Spouse of a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident

- Unmarried child (aged below 21) born from a legal marriage to / have been legally adopted by a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident

- Aged parent of a Singapore Citizen

- Employment Pass or S Pass holder

- Student studying in Singapore

- Foreign investor in Singapore


Please note that the above criteria are not exhaustive. Individual eligibility is still subject to ICA’s approval.

What to expect?

- Processing time for each application is at least 6 months

- You may check your application status online through [MyICA](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/icsbip)

- A $100 non-refundable processing fee is payable for each applicant at the point of submission of application

- If application is successful, you will need to complete permanent residency formalities personally at the ICA office


How to apply?

- Refer to [ICA's explanatory notes](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/PR/apply) and prepare the required supporting documents

- All permanent residence applications must be submitted online using the [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/epr) using your valid Singpass account, or your Foreign Identification Number and immigration pass issuance date if you're a student

- For more information on e-Service accessibility, please refer to the [Frequently Asked Questions](https://ask.gov.sg/ica) and [Step-by-Step guide](https://www.ica.gov.sg/docs/default-source/ica/eservices/epr/step-by-step-guide.pdf) in the e-Service

- For further assistance, please contact ICA at [6391 6100](tel:6391-6100) or [submit an enquiry](https://go.gov.sg/ica-pr-enquiry)
