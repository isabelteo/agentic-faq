About this support

A weekly centre-based programme that helps seniors with dementia improve their cognitive functions and stay socially engaged, through:

- Activities that simulate the brain and memory functions, e.g. word and memory games

- Physical exercises, e.g. resistance training, silent disco, etc

- Social group-based activities, e.g. art and music


Who is this for?

- Singapore Citizen (SC) or Permanent Resident (PR)

- Senior who is diagnosed by a doctor to have mild to moderate dementia and is suitable for group-based activities


What to expect?

- CIP runs for 7 weeks, with 3-hour sessions held twice a week.

- Subsidies are available depending on the [means-test result](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Cognitive-Intervention-Programme#faq).

  - Only applicable to SCs and PRs
- Transport to and from the centre and home is available at an additional cost.


How to apply?

- Visit the [AIC’s website](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Cognitive-Intervention-Programme) to search for service providers bypostal code, address or your device’s current location.

- Contact the service provider directly to find out more.


For further assistance, you can contact AIC via the hotline [1800 650 6060](tel:18006506060) or [submit an enquiry](mailto:enquiries@aic.sg).

- Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines. Calls are free of charge only if made from regular land lines in Singapore.

- Calls from AIC Hotline may be reflected as 6650 6060 on your caller ID.
