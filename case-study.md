A Singapore government agency wants a single place where citizens can ask questions that span several agencies.

Today, a question like “I'm a new permanent resident, turning 55 next year, and buying a resale flat — what do I need to sort out?” touches ICA,
HDB, CPF and IRAS. The citizen has to visit four websites and reconcile four separate answers themselves.

The sponsor is a director, not an engineer. They have asked us for a proof of concept. Build something that demonstrates the idea is viable, using
publicly available agency content.
You are not expected to solve all of this. Choose how many agencies and what kind of questions to cover, and be ready to defend the cut.

We are more interested in your reasoning than in coverage.

Agency FAQs and public guides are all on public websites. How you get that content into your prototype is up to you — scraping, manual copy, or
synthetic content that stands in for the real thing. If you take a shortcut there, tell us.
